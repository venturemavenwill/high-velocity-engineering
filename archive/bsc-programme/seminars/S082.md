# S082 · Stakeholders, Power and the Person Not in the Room

**Module:** [M17](/archive/bsc-programme/modules/M17.md) · **Quarter:** [Q9](/archive/bsc-programme/quarters/Q9.md) · **Complexity class:** 6
**Strands:** PE · SG · all
**Whitepaper:** [WP-082](/archive/bsc-programme/whitepapers/WP-082.md)
**Depends on:** S038, S078, S081
**Platform anchor:** Azure AI Search document-level access control over the substrate's corpus, and Purview's data risk assessment as an instrument with a published sampling frame · **MCP servers:** Azure MCP Server (read) · Microsoft Learn MCP Server

---

## What this day is for

[S081](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S081.md) put the organisation inside the boundary and named three people the service depends on. All three were reachable. All three were in the institution. All three could be asked.

**The parties this day is about are the ones that construction cannot find.**

At [S078](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S078.md) the cohort enforced authorisation at retrieval time and learned that a relevant document may be one this user may not have. [S060](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S060.md) point 5 left the other half of that finding open and named this day for it: **the person whose documents were never indexed.** They are not blocked by a permission check. They are absent from the corpus, so the system has no opinion about them, so no measurement of the system will ever surface them, and the more thoroughly the cohort evaluates, the more confident everyone becomes about a population that has a hole in it.

The same shape produces the day's other subject. A stakeholder analysis is a consultation, and **a consultation samples the reachable.** The parties who most need representing are systematically the ones a thorough process omits, because thoroughness is measured in people contacted and the omitted parties have no channel to contact. This is instrumentation rule 3 turned on the students: their stakeholder map is evidence with a sampling frame, and they have never been asked to state it.

And there is the case [S069](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S069.md) point 4 named and refused to answer. The three escalation conditions require somebody else with the authority to act. **When the only person with that authority is the person whose decision your finding contradicts, the first condition fails and the procedure has nothing left to say.** [WP-069](/archive/bsc-programme/whitepapers/WP-069.md) §8 argued that this is not an edge case for a graduate two years into a career; it is the normal case, and the day that taught the procedure supplied it for the other one.

The target: **absence is produced rather than accidental, and the two questions that matter about any party are whether they can refuse and whether they can be harmed without recourse.**

## Phase 1 · Pretest and prediction (25 min)

Five items, **sealed and opened at phase 8.**

1. A substrate deployment from the cohort's own record is supplied. **List everyone affected by it.**
2. **Of the people on your list, who can stop it? Who cannot complain about it?**
3. You hold a finding. The only person with the authority to act on it is the person whose decision the finding contradicts. **What do you do?** Write what you would actually do, not what you would recommend.
4. **Predict: how many parties on your item 1 list will still be on it after phase 5?**
5. **Copilot prediction, sealed.** You will shortly ask the agent to produce the same list as item 1 from the substrate itself — role assignments, index permission filters, gateway consumers, assessment coverage. **Write down what it will produce, and name the party it structurally cannot return.**

Item 3 is [S069](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S069.md) point 4's unanswered case taken cold, four weeks after the procedure was taught. **It is a different question from [S069](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S069.md) item 3 — that one asked at what point telling one person discharges the duty — and the two are reported side by side at phase 8 and are not treated as two administrations of one instrument.**

**Item 5's honest answer is available to any student who thinks about it for a minute and the design expects most of them not to**: a party set assembled from a tenant contains everyone with an identity in the tenant, so the party it cannot return is the one who has no account, no ticket history and nothing in the corpus — which is the day's whole subject arriving as a property of a tool.

**Warrant.** Pretesting an enumeration that students will produce competently and a power question they will not have asked of it. Item 2 is the day's whole apparatus administered before any of it exists, and the expected result is that the two sets barely overlap and that most students have never noticed. Item 4 makes the phase 5 revision numeric in both directions, since the informative movement is additions rather than deletions. Item 5 is sealed at phase 1 and does not migrate, per instrumentation rule 8.

Free recall, no notes.

- [S038](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S038.md): the four vocabulary failure modes, and which one the scope statement said this day supplies.
- [S078](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S078.md): what makes a document unavailable to a user, and what the system does when it is.

- **Bridge:** [S081](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S081.md) said the boundary you draw determines which failures are yours. **Who chose the boundary you inherited?**

The room reaches it. Whoever commissioned the work, and their boundary excludes the parties whose harm would have made the work more expensive to justify. **The second half comes from the room too, and it is the one the day needs: not from malice.** A commissioner's boundary is shaped by exactly the four forces [S036](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S036.md) named on a request — what they think is possible, what they think will be agreed, what they have already tried, and who they have had to persuade — operating at organisational scale, and every one of those forces narrows the affected set without anybody deciding to narrow it.

**Warrant.** Expanding-interval retrieval at gaps of two years, one quarter and one week, closing with a bridge that converts [S081](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S081.md)'s boundary point from a design decision into a political fact. The bridge is answered by the room, and the good-faith half is required from the room rather than supplied, because a room that reaches the first half alone leaves the day cynical.

## Phase 3 · Contrasting cases and generation (45 min)

**The fifth map first, executed (6 min).** Each group produces a party set from the substrate itself, with Copilot in agent mode through the Azure MCP Server: role assignments on the resource group, the search index's permission filters, the gateway's per-consumer subscriptions, and the sites Purview's default assessment covered this week. Call it **M5**. It takes four minutes, it returns more entries than most of the maps that follow, and every entry has an object identifier. **M5 stays on the table for the rest of the phase and is ranked with the others.**

Then groups receive **four stakeholder maps of the same deployment**, each produced by a different party at the time, each with the deployment's eventual outcome attached and the harm that actually occurred. **These four are demonstrated rather than operated** — they are historical documents from a partner institution and there is nothing to run them against — **and they are not assessed.** M5 is executed and is assessed, at step 8.

**Sampling is disclosed**, per instrumentation rule 3: the deployment is a real one from a partner institution, the four maps are the four that were actually produced rather than a selection from a larger set, and the outcome was known to the staff who chose the deployment. Students are told this before they begin.

- **M1** — produced by the engineering team. Twenty-two entries, exhaustive on systems and interfaces, three entries on people, all of them named individuals who had raised tickets.
- **M2** — produced by the sponsor. Eleven entries, every one of them a party with a budget line or a reporting relationship to the sponsor. Nobody who performs the work appears.
- **M3** — produced by consulting the affected parties themselves, over six weeks, exhaustively. Thirty-four entries. It is the longest, the most careful, the one the room will rank first, **and every party on it was reachable.** The parties it omits are precisely those with no channel into the consultation, and the harm that occurred fell on one of them.
- **M4** — **the payload.** Four entries. It was produced in an afternoon by asking one question — *who can stop this, and who cannot complain* — and it names the party the harm fell on. It is the shortest map, it omits most of the organisation, and it predicted the outcome that M3's thirty-four entries did not.

Groups rank all five and commit. **Then the outcome and the harm are supplied.**

The payload is **M4**, and it defeats the room's expectation about what a stakeholder analysis is. The room expects enumeration and rewards completeness. M4 is not an enumeration; it is a two-axis power question, and the reason it beats M3 is not diligence or insight — **it is that M3's method has a sampling frame and M4's has an inference.**

**M5 is the same finding with the frame written down.** Its entries are the parties who hold an identity in the tenant. That is not a judgement anybody made, it is not a defect, and it is not repairable by running the prompt better: a party with no account, no ticket history and nothing in the corpus cannot appear in an artefact assembled from accounts, tickets and corpora. **The machine's map has M3's blind spot for a reason you can read off the tool**, which is the only version of this finding the day can put in front of a student in six minutes.

Task after: **for each of the five maps, name a party who was affected and is not on it, and say what produced their absence.** Not who forgot them — what produced it.

**Warrant.** Contrasting cases across the producers of an analysis rather than across its objects, with the real harm as adjudicator. M4 is the payload because it inverts the criterion the room brought and does so on the room's own preferred evidence: the shortest map was right. M3 is placed third and is deliberately excellent, because a payload that beats a weak alternative teaches nothing — the finding the day needs is that a genuinely thorough consultation has a blind spot its thoroughness cannot close. **M5 is the executed case and is placed first so that the room's own artefact is on the table before the historical ones**, which makes the task-after's demand for a mechanism a question about a tool the students just ran rather than about somebody else's judgement two years ago. The task-after's demand for a mechanism rather than a name is what makes the phase produce point 3 rather than a list of oversights.

## Phase 4 · Consolidating instruction (50 min)

1. **A stakeholder is anyone whose work, standing or interests change if this is deployed.** Not anyone who was consulted, which is M3's error, and not anyone with a budget line, which is M2's. The definition is cheap and the room already holds it; what it does not hold is that the definition and the method produce different sets.

2. **Two axes, not one list: can they refuse, and can they be harmed without recourse.** Four quadrants, and each requires something different. *Can refuse and cannot be harmed* — negotiate. *Can refuse and can be harmed* — the ordinary case, and the one consultation handles well. *Cannot refuse and cannot be harmed* — inform. **Cannot refuse and can be harmed without recourse — represent, because nobody else will, and this is the only quadrant where the duty is yours by default.** M4's one question is the two axes compressed.

3. **Three ways to be absent, and only one of them is fixed by a longer list.** *Unidentified* — you did not know they existed; the remedy is a better method. *Identified and not invited* — somebody decided; the remedy is to ask who and why. *Invited and unable to speak* — they were in the room and the cost of speaking exceeded the benefit; the remedy is not another invitation. **This enumeration omits absence produced by the timing of the decision — parties who would have been present had the decision been made three months later or earlier — which is common in procurement and reorganisation and is not addressed anywhere in this programme.**

4. **Consultation samples the reachable, and so does a corpus.** Instrumentation rule 3 turned on you: your stakeholder map is evidence, it has a sampling frame, and you must state it. **This is [S060](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S060.md) point 5's second half, which that day named this one for.** [S078](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S078.md) answered the authorisation half — a relevant document may be one this user may not have — and the other half is that **the person whose documents were never indexed is absent from your corpus for the same reason they are absent from your consultation: neither method reaches parties without a channel into it.** The system has no opinion about them, no evaluation will surface them, and the confidence interval around your relevance estimate is silent on the question because they contributed nothing to it.

   **The material for this point is the cohort's own.** [S078](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S078.md) named this day as the response occasion for its per-class recall measurement and its phase 2 [S060](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S060.md) point 5 recall rate. **Both are returned to the room here.** The per-class figures show that the cohort's own control served the least-privileged class worst, by a margin the students measured themselves — which converts *filtering degrades recall most for whoever has the least access* from a finding about retrieval into a finding about who is in the room. And the point 5 recall rate shows **how many of this cohort could name the corpus selection at all when asked, three quarters after being told about it.**

   **The branch is stated to the room, per the obligation [S078](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S078.md) placed here.** Where that recall rate was low — where most students named only the relevance criterion — this point is delivered in full from first principles rather than as a retrieval, the phase 2 bridge is not relied on to reach it, and **step 3's absent party is supplied as a prompted candidate rather than left to the student to find.** Where the rate was high, point 4 is a consolidation of something the cohort already holds and step 3 remains unsupported. The room is told which arm is in force and why.

5. **Interests that will not be stated.** [S039](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S039.md) point 3's omitted constraint kind, arriving where it belongs. A constraint protecting an interest nobody will state does not move under evidence, and pressing for its purpose — which is [S039](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S039.md)'s method and a good one — damages the relationship the negotiation depends on. **What to do instead: name the interest yourself, in the room, as a hypothesis you are inviting correction on, in a form you would be willing to have repeated to its holder.** You will sometimes be wrong and the cost of being wrong is real. **Safe where you hold a working relationship, where the hypothesis can be said without accusation, and where being wrong costs you standing rather than costing somebody else their position. Unsafe where the interest concerns a person's employment, conduct or protected characteristics, where a hypothesis stated aloud is an allegation regardless of your framing, and where the correct move is to state the constraint's effect and not its cause. This day does not teach the second case and does not have a home for it.**

6. **Vocabulary is a power fact.** [S038](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S038.md) point 4's omitted failure mode: two groups of domain experts who both believe they are speaking the domain's language. Whose word wins is not a modelling decision and the model cannot be neutral. **Record both usages and name which one the interface uses**, because a model that silently adopts one department's language has taken a side and the other department will experience the system as wrong rather than as foreign.

7. **When escalation terminates in the person your finding contradicts.** [S069](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S069.md) point 4, answered as far as this design can answer it. The three conditions still hold and the first one fails: there is no other party with the authority to act in time. **Four moves, with their costs, and none of them is a procedure.** *Put it in writing to the person, accept the decision, and record it* — cheapest, preserves the relationship, and leaves the harm. *Find the second authority* — regulatory, safety, audit, customer, or the affected party themselves — available only where one exists, and it converts a disagreement into an escalation the person will experience as an attack. *Decline to do the work* — available, costly, and effective only if somebody else will not simply do it. *Leave* — the last one, and it changes nothing about the finding. **The day's position is that the choice is yours, the cost is real, and anyone who tells you there is a correct answer has not been in the position.** Safe as a map of the moves; **unsafe as a theory of professional courage, which this programme does not supply and should not pretend to.**

8. **A criterion that exists to make a system look successful is a stakeholder artefact.** [S046](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S046.md) point 2's other omission. It has an owner and the owner has an interest, and [S047](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S047.md)'s validation will not detect it, because it is valid: it measures exactly what it was built to measure. The detection is not statistical. **It is asking who chose it and what would have happened to them had it shown the other result** — which is point 5's move applied to a number.

**Points 9 and 10 are the platform instance and come after the argument, per this module's convention.**

9. **Where the machine's frame is written down, and what it does with a document that is not there.** Azure AI Search captures permission metadata **at indexing time** and enforces it **at query time** against the caller's Entra token. Three consequences, and the third is today's. First, enforcement is against indexed metadata rather than live source permissions, so a lag between source and index is inherent and the documentation acknowledges it. Second, the generally available approach — security filters, a simple string comparison — is called a **workaround** in the vendor's own documentation, while the properly authenticated options are preview, so **the mature option is not the correct one and choosing correctness here means accepting preview terms.** Third, and today's: **a document that was never indexed has no permission metadata and therefore no behaviour at all. It is not denied. There is nothing to deny.** That is point 4 stated as a property of a system rather than of a consultation, and it is why step 3 asks for the party rather than for the permission. **Safe** for reasoning about who can see what within a corpus you indexed, at the freshness your indexer runs at. **Unsafe** for reasoning about anybody whose material is outside it, and unsafe as a claim that the index reflects the source's permissions now. **Perishable:** which approaches are preview, their names, the header the query-time check uses. **Durable:** absence and denial are different states, and only one of them produces a log line.

10. **Two defaults the platform chose for you, both with a person on the end.** The access-control documentation recommends **group access over individual user access "for ease of management"** — a reasonable default whose power consequence is that a party's access becomes a property of a group somebody else administers, and the person who cannot complain is frequently the one whose membership was set by somebody who has left. And Purview's item-level remediation offers **Remove sharing link**, documented as an action **"to be used sparingly because it could prevent people from accessing the item legitimately"**, after which the site or item owner must set a less permissive link for authorised users — while the **owner notification email is not customisable**. Read the two together and the day's subject arrives as a product: **the platform will tell somebody their access has changed, in words you did not write and cannot change, and it will not tell the person whose access was legitimate and is now gone.** Nobody chose that; it is what the defaults do. Note also what the documentation does not say. It states the remediation actions, the caps and the latencies precisely and **states no rate at which any of them is wrong.** There is no published false-positive figure for this and nothing in the tool would produce one.

**Warrant.** Consolidation indexed to phase 3's five maps. Point 2 names M4's question. Point 3 names the mechanisms the task-after produced and carries its own omission. Point 4 discharges [S060](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S060.md) point 5's second half; point 5 discharges [S039](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S039.md) point 3's; point 6 discharges [S038](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S038.md) point 4's; point 8 discharges [S046](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S046.md) point 2's second omission. Point 7 discharges [S069](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S069.md) point 4's deferral and carries the strong-form scope statement that matters most in the day, because the material invites a certainty the design does not have. **Point 9 is reached from M5's task-after entry rather than asserted and carries its own strong-form scope statement; point 10 is the [Copilot Practice Standard](/archive/bsc-programme/program/12-Copilot-Practice-Standard.md)'s required naming of a default the platform chose, and it is chosen so that the default's documented cost is a named person's inconvenience rather than a metric.**

## Phase 5 · Whole-task learning task (90 min)

**Class 6. The party set is the student's, and one finding leaves the room.**

Students work on the cohort's own service and on the [S081](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S081.md) boundary they redrew last week.

1. **Place the parties on both axes** — can refuse, can be harmed without recourse — with the evidence for each placement. Not the four quadrants as a diagram; a placement with a reason.
2. **State your sampling frame:** how you identified these parties, and who that method cannot reach. **Name at least one party you believe exists and cannot name**, and say what you would have to do to find them.
3. **Identify one party whose documents, records or requests are not in the substrate's corpus.** Use the [S078](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S078.md) authorisation model, that day's **per-class recall figures for this cohort's own control**, and the [S060](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S060.md) index. State what the system does to them, and whether anyone would find out. **Where the party you find is in the class your own control served worst, say so** — that is not a coincidence and the two measurements are of the same thing from two sides.
4. **For one party who cannot refuse and can be harmed without recourse, write what you would tell them** — in their words, not yours, at the standard [S050](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S050.md) set — **and name the person who will tell them.**
5. **Name one constraint on this deployment that you believe protects an interest nobody has stated.** Write the interest as a one-sentence hypothesis in a form you would be willing to have repeated to its holder. **Mark it as a hypothesis.** If you cannot write one you would stand behind, write that instead and say what stopped you.
6. **Take one finding to the person whose decision it contradicts.** The finding is real — from the cohort's own [S070](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S070.md) decision records or the [S079](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S079.md) incident record — and the person is the staff member who made that decision. Prepare it against [S069](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S069.md) point 4's three conditions. **Then state, before you go in, which of point 7's four moves you would take if they decline, and what it costs you.**
7. **Record the outcome in [S034](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S034.md)'s three-part form**, including a refusal: what each party may now assume, what each will observe, and what happens if it is not met.
8. **Ship the coverage check.** Thirty minutes, with Copilot in agent mode through the Azure MCP Server in read configuration. Produce a check that answers, for one named party, **whether anything of theirs is in the substrate's corpus at all — and that distinguishes *denied* from *absent*.** A query returning zero results has told you nothing until it can say which of the two it found. Ship it with M5 attached, and with **the frame written in one sentence: what a party must have in order to appear in this output at all.**

**Step 8 is performed first in the room and is listed last because it is the artefact's appendix rather than its argument.** Its output is step 3's evidence, and a step 3 entry supported only by a zero-result query fails.

**The staff member whose decision is contradicted is present for the last thirty minutes and is briefed to decline where the merits warrant it.** Six minutes each. They are not playing a role.

**The build is bounded and the encounter is not.** Step 8 is timeboxed to thirty of the ninety minutes. **The staff member's thirty minutes are timetabled first and the build is what compresses**, because a check can be finished afterwards and an encounter cannot be re-run.

**The artefact carries three further elements**, per the [Copilot Practice Standard](/archive/bsc-programme/program/12-Copilot-Practice-Standard.md).

- **Prompt record.** The instructions you gave, in order, including the ones you abandoned, each with why.
- **Intervention record.** Every correction and what it was. **An intervention record with no entries fails**, unless the artefact demonstrates that the design could have detected an intervention-worthy error — a check that fails closed when it cannot establish whether a zero result is a denial or an absence is the obvious example and is accepted.
- **Frontier statement.** One thing the agent did faster than you could have and one thing it did badly that you had to catch, both from this build. **The agent will write the query quickly and will conflate the two states unless told not to**, and a student whose frontier statement names that specifically has produced the day's finding in the machine register.

**Fading:** step 1's two-axis grid is supplied; step 6's preparation reuses [S069](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S069.md) point 4's three conditions and [S050](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S050.md)'s actionable-language standard. **Steps 2 and 5 are unsupported.**

**Warrant.** Whole-task at class 6 with a real counterparty entitled to refuse, which is [S069](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S069.md) step 7's construction with the authority relationship inverted: there the student proposed an arrangement to somebody who could decline it, and here the student contradicts a decision that person has already made. **Step 6 is this day's discharge of the deferral [S069](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S069.md) point 4 placed here, and it is an artefact element rather than a discussion**, which is what [WP-069](/archive/bsc-programme/whitepapers/WP-069.md) §8 said it could not check. **Step 3 is the discharge of [S060](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S060.md) point 5's second half.** Step 2 is unsupported because stating what your own method cannot reach is the day's central discipline and is not scaffoldable — a supplied frame would be answered by filling it in. Step 5's escape clause exists because the honest answer for some students is that they cannot write a hypothesis they would stand behind, and a day that forces the sentence produces the allegation point 5's scope statement warns against. **Step 8 exists because [S060](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S060.md) point 5's second half has been a claim for three quarters and this is the first occasion on which a student can execute it**: the difference between a party who was refused and a party who was never there is checkable against a real index in about four minutes, and nobody has been asked to check it before.

## Phase 6 · Error clinic (40 min)

Instructor first: a substrate change consulted widely and well. Every affected team was contacted, a written comment period ran, the objections raised were addressed, and the record of it is a model of the practice. It harmed a group of external users reading an exported format, who had no relationship with the institution, no ticket history, and no way to know a consultation was happening. Attention is directed at one fact: **the consultation was exemplary and the omission was produced by the consultation's own method.** Nobody skipped a step.

Then the agent case: a Copilot-written access review that was **confidently wrong and passed review**. Asked to report who could see a set of documents, it enumerated the index's permission filters, resolved every group to its members, produced a clean per-document access table, and reported full coverage. **It was correct about every document in the index.** Four of the documents the question was actually about had failed the last indexer run and were not in it, and the table did not have a row for them because a table built from an index cannot have a row for a document that is not in the index. Attention goes to why review did not catch it: **the reviewer checked that every row was right, and every row was right.** The failure is in the frame, not in the values, and no test of the values can find it.

Then students, in threes: **a decision that affected you that you found out about afterwards.** What would it have cost the decider to have asked you?

**Warrant.** Error management targeting point 4, with a case that contains no procedural failure at all, which is the only construction that prevents the room concluding that better process would have caught it. **The agent case is the [Copilot Practice Standard](/archive/bsc-programme/program/12-Copilot-Practice-Standard.md)'s required addition and is deliberately the same failure in the machine register as the instructor's is in the human one** — an exemplary method with a frame nobody stated — and it is the fail condition step 8 exists to prevent, demonstrated before the specification states it. The student disclosure puts every student in the absent party's position, which is the day's affective content and is the only part of it that cannot be reached by analysis. The second question is asked because the answer is nearly always *very little*, and the room discovering that about their own case is what makes step 4's named teller a duty rather than a formality.

## Phase 7 · Peer articulation (30 min)

Pairs. Each student presents **their step 5 hypothesised unstated interest**, with the constraint it attaches to.

The partner's task: **construct the strongest good-faith account of why that constraint exists** — an account in which nobody is protecting anything and the constraint is simply correct. They must produce one, not evaluate the student's.

The pair then records **whether the hypothesis survived the good-faith account**, and which specific evidence, if any, distinguishes them. Both students perform both roles.

Per specification rule 3, the outcome is recorded in the artefact, including a hypothesis that did not survive, which is a result about the hypothesis rather than an absence of one.

Per instrumentation rule 5 as amended at [WP-054](/archive/bsc-programme/whitepapers/WP-054.md) §8, this structure is a variant of the mechanism [S069](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S069.md)'s arrangement A4 isolated — requiring the party who wants a conclusion to argue the other side — whose first use is at [S070](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S070.md) phase 3 item 8. No day has run, so **this reuse is contingent on [S070](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S070.md)'s reported outcome**, and a reported failure there retracts it here.

**Warrant.** Interactive-tier articulation with bilateral production, aimed squarely at the module's worst failure mode. A cohort taught to see unstated interests will find them everywhere and become unfalsifiable; requiring the good-faith reconstruction is the only countermeasure the design has that operates on the student's own hypothesis rather than on a supplied one. The structure is deliberately not the absent-party one-question form, which has run at [S039](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S039.md) and [S069](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S069.md) and would be measuring familiarity on a third administration.

## Phase 8 · Calibration and commitment (20 min)

- **Sealed items opened.** Item 1's list against step 1's placement: **how many parties were added, how many removed, and how many students' final sets contain a party who can be harmed without recourse and was absent from item 1.** The last figure is the day's headline.
- Item 2's two sets shown as an overlap, cohort-wide.
- **Item 3's answers read back anonymously against point 7's four moves**, with the proportion who wrote a move that requires a second authority that does not exist. **The [S069](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S069.md) item 3 responses are shown beside them and are not compared with them**: the two questions differ, and neither is a second administration of the other.
- Item 4's predicted list movement against the actual.
- **Item 5 opened.** The sealed Copilot predictions against M5 as produced. **Two rates, reported separately: how many predicted the agent would return a longer list than they did, and how many named the frame — that a party must hold an identity, a ticket or a document to appear at all.** The second rate is the day's frontier-calibration measure and the design expects it to be low. The room is told that predicting the tool would be good is not a prediction about the tool.
- The step 2 unnamed-party statements, aggregated: **how many students could name a category of person they could not name a person in.**
- The step 8 coverage checks: **how many distinguished absent from denied on first run, and how many did so only after the student intervened.**
- **The step 6 outcomes:** how many findings the decision-owner accepted, how many they declined, and on what grounds — reported by the staff member, in the room.
- The phase 7 hypothesis survival rate.
- Each student names **one party affected by something they are currently building who has no way to hear about it.**
- Retrieval schedule: next morning, day 3, week 1, week 3.

## Assessment

**One specification item**, in PE390 with SG390. Per [Assessment Architecture](/archive/bsc-programme/program/03-Assessment-Architecture.md) rule 1, the checked elements are: the party placements on both axes with the evidence for each; the sampling frame with who it cannot reach and one named unfindable category with what would find them; the party absent from the corpus with what the system does to them and whether anyone would find out; the message to a party who cannot refuse, in their words, with a named teller; the hypothesised unstated interest marked as a hypothesis, or the statement of what stopped the student writing one; the escalation encounter with the pre-stated move and its cost; the outcome in three-part form; the phase 7 result; **the shipped coverage check with its absent-versus-denied distinction and its one-sentence frame; the prompt record; the intervention record; and the frontier statement.** **Nothing else is assessed.**

**A stakeholder map with no party in the cannot-refuse-and-can-be-harmed quadrant fails**, unless the student states positively why the deployment has none and the assessors accept it. This is the day's single hard requirement and it exists because that quadrant is the only one where the duty is the student's by default.

**A move named at step 6 that requires an authority the student cannot name fails.** "Escalate to compliance" without a compliance function is not a move; it is the procedure [S069](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S069.md) supplied for the other case, restated.

**A coverage check that cannot distinguish absent from denied fails**, and so does a step 3 entry evidenced only by a zero-result query. This is point 9 bound to the artefact.

**An intervention record with no entries fails**, unless the artefact demonstrates that the design could have detected an intervention-worthy error.

**A refusal at step 6 is a pass.** A finding the decision-owner declined, recorded with the grounds and with the move the student said in advance they would take, is the expected outcome for a substantial share of the cohort. **A finding recorded as accepted when it was not is a fail**, and the staff member's own record is the check.

**The four historical maps are not assessed**, because they are demonstrated rather than operated. M5 is executed and is assessed at step 8.

No entrustment judgement. The module's is at [S085](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S085.md).

**Deferral discharges.** Six earlier days named this one. **Two are discharged as artefact elements and four as taught content**, and [WP-082](/archive/bsc-programme/whitepapers/WP-082.md) §8 argues that the difference matters and that this day is the receiving end of a rule the design has been satisfying in the wrong direction.
- **[S069](/archive/bsc-programme/seminars/S069.md) point 4** deferred the escalation path that terminates in the person whose decision the finding contradicts. **Discharged at phase 4 point 7 and as an artefact element at phase 5 step 6, with a real counterparty entitled to decline, a pre-stated move, and a checked cost.**

**Rule-6 discharge.** One obligation lands on this day and **the element exists.**

## What to take from this day

**The claim to hold.** Absence is produced rather than accidental. A consultation samples the reachable and so does a corpus, so the parties who most need representing are the ones a thorough process omits — and the two questions about any party are **whether they can refuse and whether they can be harmed without recourse.** Absence and denial differ.

**What the sources license, and what they do not.** Stakeholder identification, power mapping and the position that a consultation's reach is a property of its method are verified at [research/05-fde-craft/beyer-holtzblatt-contextual-design.md](/research/05-fde-craft/beyer-holtzblatt-contextual-design.md) and [research/05-fde-craft/palantir-fde-practitioner-writing.md](/research/05-fde-craft/palantir-fde-practitioner-writing.md), at the depth those notes record; that an evaluation is silent about material absent from its collection follows [research/02-technical-foundations/barnett-rag-failure-taxonomy.md](/research/02-technical-foundations/barnett-rag-failure-taxonomy.md). **No magnitude is asserted for how often escalation terminates badly or what proportion of corpora carry coverage gaps.** The remediation tooling publishes **no rate at which any of its actions is wrong.**

**Guidance for your own work.**

- **State your sampling frame** — how you found these parties and who the method cannot reach — and name one party you believe exists and cannot name.
- **Make a zero result say which zero it is.** A check that cannot separate *denied* from *absent* has told you nothing.
- **Decide which move you will make if the decision-owner declines**, and what it costs you.
- **A control is not evidence.** Per the [Agent Configuration Standard](/archive/bsc-programme/program/13-Agent-Configuration-Standard.md), a permission filter licenses a claim about enforcement and none about who is missing from the corpus it enforces over.

**Where this is easy to get wrong.** Rewarding completeness. The most careful map in the room consulted everybody reachable, and the harm fell on a party its method could not reach.
