## Complexity classes

Sessions carry a complexity class from 1 to 6, rising across the arc. The class describes **how much of the method the learner supplies**, not how hard the subject is.

| Class | The learner is given | Stages |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The method and the standard | Q1 |
| 2 | The method; the standard is argued | Q2–Q3 |
| 3 | The problem; the method is chosen | Q4–Q5 |
| 4 | The problem; the evidentiary standard is supplied by the learner | Q6–Q7 |
| 5 | The situation only | Q8 |
| 6 | The situation, and the obligation to say what it did not establish | Q9 |

## Where the stages sit relative to the source

**Seven stages are built from the practice corpus. Two are written past it and say so on every page.**

[Q1 · Discover](/wiki/quarters/Q1.md) is a qualification stage that can decline the work, and the corpus refuses work exactly once, in a note attached to a slide. It has no declined engagement, no failure narrative that is not synthetic, and no exclusion criteria. **The canonical lifecycle is silent here too — it opens at Discover and has no refusal.**

[Q9 · Compound](/wiki/quarters/Q9.md) exists because the cycle is implied throughout and stated as a model nowhere. **It replaced a stage called Withdraw** after a challenge that the material pointed to growth rather than exit — a challenge that survived checking, and by a wide margin.

[Q8 · Operate](/wiki/quarters/Q8.md) is half-supported. Its estate-hygiene half is verified documented practice; **its capability-transfer half is built on an absence** — the corpus voices the aspiration once, as a reflection inside a business narrative, with no mechanism, measure or evidence.
