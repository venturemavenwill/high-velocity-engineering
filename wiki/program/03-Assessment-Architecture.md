# Assessment Architecture

> **Eighteen occasions, one closing each module.** None is an examination. Every one is an artefact produced under constraint and defended to somebody who can push back.

## The shape

| | |
|---|---|
| **Occasions** | 18, marked ⊘ in the [Programme Map](/wiki/program/01-Programme-Map.md) |
| **Specification checks** | 15 — a named artefact against a closed list of checked elements |
| **Entrustment decisions** | 3 — [S080](/wiki/seminars/S080.md), [S085](/wiki/seminars/S085.md), [S090](/wiki/seminars/S090.md) |
| **Graded scores** | none |

## Specification checks

A specification check lists what is examined and closes with the sentence **"Nothing else is assessed."** That sentence is load-bearing. It exists so that a learner can tell the difference between what the occasion measures and what the assessor happens to prefer, and so that an assessor cannot import a standard the learner was never shown.

**What is checked is the reasoning, not the outcome.** A learner whose build failed, who noticed, who corrected it and recorded the correction has produced a better artefact than one whose first attempt worked and who recorded nothing. That inversion is stated in advance because it is not the one learners expect.

## Entrustment decisions

The final three occasions are not checks. They are judgements about **how much supervision this person now needs**, formed from accumulated observation and written as narrative rather than as a score.

Entrustment is a decision about a unit of work, not a rating of a person. It answers *would I let them do this unsupervised, and what would I still want to see?* — and it is revisable in both directions.

**This is the weakest-warranted machinery in the programme and the best-warranted in the source literature.** The entrustment and narrative-assessment notes in [research/08-assessment-epas/](/research/08-assessment-epas/) are genuine full readings; whether the machinery works *here*, on this cohort, is untested.

## A defect in this architecture, recorded rather than fixed

**Four consecutive occasions in Stages 6 and 7 used the same device** — an offer the learner was expected to decline — and two further patterns recur across the programme: a condition written before the event ([S005](/wiki/seminars/S005.md), [S079](/wiki/seminars/S079.md), [S080](/wiki/seminars/S080.md), [S085](/wiki/seminars/S085.md), [S090](/wiki/seminars/S090.md)) and a fixed published closing question in every Stage 9 session.

The repetition was found by the authors of Stages 7 and 9 during construction, and the second author corrected it at [S085](/wiki/seminars/S085.md) and [S090](/wiki/seminars/S090.md) rather than conceding it again. **The earlier four are unamended and this page records that.**

The general risk is worth naming, because it applies to any assessment scheme: **a device a learner can anticipate stops measuring judgement and starts measuring pattern recognition.** Nothing here establishes how many repetitions it takes for that to happen.

## What no occasion does

- **No occasion assesses the output of an agent.** Prompt records, intervention records and frontier statements are assessed for presence and specificity, never for the quality of what the tool produced.
- **No occasion assesses adherence** to a plan the learner authored. Assessing adherence converts an autonomy-supporting artefact into a compliance instrument.
- **No occasion produces a number** that is compared across learners.
