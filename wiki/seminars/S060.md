# S060 · Shipping Retrieval Into Production ⊘

**Module:** [M12](/wiki/modules/M12.md) · **Quarter:** [Q6](/wiki/quarters/Q6.md) · **Complexity class:** 4
**Strands:** IS · SY · ME · PE
**Whitepaper:** [WP-060](/wiki/whitepapers/WP-060.md)
**Depends on:** S035, S056, S057, S058, S059
**Re-tests:** S045
**Platform anchor:** the whole Azure AI Search retrieval path — index, vector profile, operating point, hybrid ranking — serving the substrate's real users · **MCP servers:** Azure MCP Server (Search, write) · Foundry MCP Server · Microsoft Learn MCP Server

⊘ **Assessment-bearing day, the quarter's only entrustment judgement, and the closest precursor yet to EA-1.**

---

## What this day is for

Four days ago the cohort was told that the lexical baseline is not the naive option. Since then they have written a relevance criterion, measured how far two people diverge applying it, trained a retriever and found that its negatives defined it, chosen an operating point and discovered what the approximation ate, and re-read a published comparison until it stopped supporting the claim they had been making with it.

Every one of those days ended with a finding. **Today ends with a system serving people.**

The system is not a metaphor and it is not a prototype. It is a search index the students built over four days by prompting an agent, on a service the substrate already pays for, with an operating point they chose, a compression setting they chose, a ranking configuration they chose, and a set of defaults they did not choose and can now name. **Today it starts answering the substrate's queries**, and the queries do not stop when the seminar does.

The gap between a finding and a release is the whole of the day. A finding can be qualified indefinitely; a release cannot. At some point the interval is what it is, the ceiling is what it is, the operating point is chosen, and somebody has to say *this goes out, to these people, and here is what it will do badly.* The evidence never becomes sufficient. The decision is made anyway, by a person, who is then answerable for it.

**The target is not the decision.** Most students will reach a defensible one. The target is the harder thing: to ship into a population you have not observed, with a promise the users can check, a failure-mode statement that names something you did not want to be true, and a stop signal owned by somebody who is not you.

## The entrustment judgement, stated before phase 1

> Today's judgement is on: **ship a retrieval system into production and own the consequence.** It is the closest precursor the programme has to the first entrusted activity, EA-1, and it is not that activity — EA-1 is a ship / no-ship decision under a cost position and a deadline set by other people, and today the deadline is the timetable.
>
> Two assessors judge independently before conferring, and **form their level from your release record before the defence begins**, per rater discipline rule 7. The defence tests the level; it does not create it. The expected level is **2b, and 3a is available.**
>
> **The [S030](/wiki/seminars/S030.md), [S040](/wiki/seminars/S040.md), [S045](/wiki/seminars/S045.md) and [S050](/wiki/seminars/S050.md) judgements are not carried forward.** Each occasion is judged on its own evidence, and a level awarded five months ago is not evidence about today.
>
> **Five failure conditions are named in advance.**
>
> 1. Evidence cited without stating what it licenses is below 2b, whatever the decision.
> 2. **A failure-mode statement that does not name one property your system selects for that nobody asked for has not performed the activity.**
> 3. A promise the user cannot check is below 2b, however good the system behind it.
> 4. A withdrawal signal with no named person who acts on it fails.
> 5. **Declining to ship is a fully available answer and is not a safe one.** A student who declines and cannot state what evidence would change that is held to the same standard as one who ships and cannot state what would make them withdraw.
>
> **The five conditions are unchanged and no sixth has been added.** Your release record now carries a prompt record, an intervention record and a frontier statement, per the [Copilot Practice Standard](/wiki/program/12-Copilot-Practice-Standard.md). They are read by both assessors before the defence and they are checked specification elements. **They do not create a new way to fail the entrustment judgement.** They exist because a record of what you asked for and what you abandoned is evidence about your reasoning at the time you reasoned, which is the one thing a prospective decision record has never been able to supply — a gap [WP-060](/wiki/whitepapers/WP-060.md) §8 names and this addition partly closes.
>
> **The production result at phase 5 is not assessed.** That is stated here, before you decide, so that it is a commitment rather than a practice.

## Phase 1 · Pretest and prediction (25 min)

Five items, sealed and opened at phase 8.

1. **Write your decision as one sentence,** in the form you would actually send it.
2. **What in your evidence is weakest — and would you say so unprompted, or only if asked?**
3. **Which of your failure modes will occur first, and to whom?**
4. Predict your supervision level.
5. **Copilot prediction, sealed.** Over four days you have directed an agent to build this system. **Name the one thing it did that you did not catch until later, and the one thing you are still not sure it got right.** Opened at phase 8 against your own intervention record and against phase 5's production result.

Item 2 is the day's honest item. It is checked at phase 4 against whether the student in fact raised it before an assessor did.

Item 5 is the module's closing frontier item and it is deliberately asked at the point of maximum consequence. It is the only occasion in the programme where a student is asked to name an outstanding uncertainty about their own agent-directed work **while that work is about to go to users**, and the design's expectation is that the answers are markedly more specific than at [S056](/wiki/seminars/S056.md).

**Warrant.** Pretesting the position before the consequence exists, so that movement is measurable rather than remembered. Item 2's second clause is the construction that matters: every student will identify their weakest evidence correctly, and the difference between those who volunteer it and those who wait to be asked is the difference the day is judging. Item 5 is sealed here and opened at phase 8, per instrumentation rule 8, and it may not be revised after phase 5's results are seen.

## Phase 2 · Cumulative module retrieval (30 min)

Free recall, no notes, then paired reconstruction.

- [S056](/wiki/seminars/S056.md): the four terms in order of size, and your own disagreement rate.
- [S057](/wiki/seminars/S057.md): negatives as the decision boundary, and shared construction as a leak.
- [S058](/wiki/seminars/S058.md): two criteria rather than one, concentration of loss, and the four promise forms.
- [S059](/wiki/seminars/S059.md): what a benchmark fixes, and the ceiling as a floor on what may be claimed.
- **[S035](/wiki/seminars/S035.md):** blast radius as a quantity estimated before and measured after, and the five-part report.
- **[S045](/wiki/seminars/S045.md):** the six parts of a deployment decision, and why not deploying is a result.
- **Bridge:** [S035](/wiki/seminars/S035.md) said rollback is a decision made by somebody else, because a person inside an experiment is the worst-placed to judge when to stop it. **Who stops this one?**

The answer wanted is: a named person, named in the release record, before the release, and not the student.

**Warrant.** Cumulative retrieval across the whole module and back two quarters, closing on the one element students consistently omit. The bridge is not rhetorical — its answer is a required field in the artefact produced ninety minutes later, and a release record without it fails.

## Phase 3 · The release (60 min)

**The entrusted activity. Assessors observe and do not intervene.**

Teams built the system across [S056](/wiki/seminars/S056.md)–[S059](/wiki/seminars/S059.md). **The judgement is individual.** Each student owns one named decision — the retriever, the index operating point, the evaluation, or the user-facing promise — and produces the release record for it. The release proceeds with whatever its owners approved.

The record has eight parts and is finalised in this phase:

1. **The decision** — ship, do not ship, or ship under stated conditions — for the component you own.
2. **The evidence, with what each piece licenses**, in [S041](/wiki/seminars/S041.md)'s form and bounded by [S059](/wiki/seminars/S059.md)'s ceiling.
3. **The failure-mode statement.** Three failure modes, each with who experiences it and how often. **One must be a property your system selects for that nobody asked for**, evidenced on your own materials. **At least one must be drawn from your [S050](/wiki/seminars/S050.md) category 3** — a failure you did not anticipate last time.
4. **The user-facing promise.** One sentence, in one of [S025](/wiki/seminars/S025.md)'s forms, **checkable by the user**, with what happens when it is not met.
5. **The withdrawal signal, its threshold, and the named person who acts on it.**
6. **The blast-radius estimate**, per [S035](/wiki/seminars/S035.md): who, how many, for how long, and what they experience.
7. **The three records**, per the [Copilot Practice Standard](/wiki/program/12-Copilot-Practice-Standard.md), covering the four days of the build rather than this phase.

    - **Prompt record.** What you asked the agent for, in order, **including what you abandoned and why.** The abandoned instructions are the part that matters here: they are the alternatives you considered and rejected, recorded at the time you rejected them.
    - **Intervention record.** Every point at which you corrected it, and what the correction was. **An intervention record with no entries fails the specification**, unless the artefact demonstrates that the system's own design could have detected an intervention-worthy error.
    - **Frontier statement.** One thing the agent did across these four days that you could not have done as fast, and one thing it did badly that you had to catch.

8. **Then the release goes out**, to all users, with the disclosure obligation [Q6](/wiki/quarters/Q6.md) placed on this quarter attached to it.

**Warrant.** The entrusted activity, performed once, on a real system, with real users, and with the consequence arriving inside the same day. Part 3's unintended-selection requirement is this day's discharge of the obligation [S054](/wiki/seminars/S054.md) placed on it under instrumentation rule 6, and its category-3 clause discharges [Q6](/wiki/quarters/Q6.md)'s reporting commitment on [S050](/wiki/seminars/S050.md). Part 4 is the artefact element [S058](/wiki/seminars/S058.md) named. Part 5's third clause is phase 2's bridge made binding. **Part 7 is the [Copilot Practice Standard](/wiki/program/12-Copilot-Practice-Standard.md)'s three elements, placed in the release record rather than in a separate build because this day has no build phase**; its prompt record is the closest thing the design has to the rejected-alternative field [WP-060](/wiki/whitepapers/WP-060.md) §8 names as the fix for a prospective decision's evidential poverty, and it is read as such.

## Phase 4 · The defence (60 min)

Assessors have formed a level from the release record before this phase begins. The defence tests it; it does not create it.

- **Which piece of your evidence licenses the least, and did you rely on it?**
- **Which published claim does this decision rest on, and what did its per-dataset breakdown show?**
- **Name one default in this system that you did not choose, and say what accepting it committed you to.** Asked of every student.
- **Which instruction did you give the agent and then abandon, and what made you abandon it?**
- What did you decline to measure, and what would it have cost?
- Who experiences your first failure mode, and have you told them in words they can act on?
- If your withdrawal signal fires at three in the morning, what happens?
- **Name the part of this decision you are least willing to own.**

The last is asked of every student. **Declining to name one is recorded as an answer**, not as a refusal, and is discussed rather than penalised — but it is a datum about calibration and the assessors treat it as one.

**Warrant.** Defence-after-artefact, per rater discipline rule 7. The questions are diagnostic rather than evaluative. The second is the artefact element [S059](/wiki/seminars/S059.md) named under instrumentation rule 6, and it is asked of every student rather than as a follow-up, because a cohort that changed its conclusions on its own ablations and not on published tables will answer it in a characteristic way. **The third is the [Copilot Practice Standard](/wiki/program/12-Copilot-Practice-Standard.md)'s defaults requirement in its assessed form** — a student who shipped a search index and cannot name one default they inherited has operated the service without understanding it, and this day is the last place that could go unnoticed before Q7 profiles the same system. **The fourth reads the prompt record's abandoned entries**, and is the question this design believes comes closest to observing reasoning in a record that is otherwise entirely about the future.

## Phase 5 · The production check (35 min)

**The system has been serving real users since phase 3.**

- **Measured tail latency against the promised budget**, per component owner.
- **The queries the system actually failed on**, characterised, **and compared against each student's [S058](/wiki/seminars/S058.md) step 3 prediction of which documents the approximation would lose.**
- **The blast-radius estimate against what was observed.**
- The first user reports, unedited.

Individual results are given to each student privately. The cohort sees the aggregate.

**No assessment attaches to this result, and that is stated before phase 3 as well as here.** If the check were assessed, the failure-mode statements at part 3 would become strategic — students would name every conceivable failure to avoid being wrong — and the considered prediction the programme wants would stop being produced. This is the same commitment [S050](/wiki/seminars/S050.md) phase 5 made about its category 3 and it is made for the same reason.

**Warrant.** The programme's fourth long-horizon prediction check and its first with a gap of hours rather than weeks — which is the point. [S045](/wiki/seminars/S045.md) and [S050](/wiki/seminars/S050.md) reported consequences a quarter later, to students who had moved on. Here the student is in the room when their prediction about which documents would be lost meets the log. **The instrument [S058](/wiki/seminars/S058.md) named responds here**, and the comparison is the only occasion in the module where a geometric prediction meets real traffic.

## Phase 6 · Consolidating instruction (35 min) — assessors absent

1. **A ship decision is a claim about a population you have not observed.** [S041](/wiki/seminars/S041.md)'s licensing sentence at the point where it stops being an epistemics exercise: you are making the claim anyway, and the honest form names the population you did observe.

2. **The four terms, one last time.** Corpus, query population, relevance criterion, method — and you now own three of the four. Every complaint you receive in the next four weeks will be about one of the three, and almost none will be about the fourth.

3. **You will be held to what you promised, not to what you measured.** [S025](/wiki/seminars/S025.md) point 2 and [S058](/wiki/seminars/S058.md) step 7, arriving where they cost something.

4. **Not shipping is a result.** [S045](/wiki/seminars/S045.md)'s position, restated, and the cohort's split shown unattributed. A student who declined for stated reasons has performed the activity.

5. **Every retrieval system selects for something nobody asked for.** Your relevance criterion selected for a kind of answer; your corpus selected for **whose documents are findable at all**. The second is the one with ethical weight, it is the one your users cannot see, and **it is not answered today** — it returns at [S078](/wiki/seminars/S078.md), where a relevant document may be one this user may not have, and at [S082](/wiki/seminars/S082.md), where the person whose documents were never indexed is the person not in the room.

6. **What you did not think to say is larger than what you said wrongly.** This is the second time the programme has measured that quantity on you, after [S050](/wiki/seminars/S050.md), and it is why part 3 required a category-3 failure mode rather than three new ones.

7. **What you own includes decisions you did not make, and the list is finite. Marked perishable.** Between your relevance criterion and the result a user just received sit: two term-weighting parameters you did not set, a shard boundary you cannot see, a chunk size and overlap chosen by a starting-point recommendation, a graph link count that defaults to the bottom of its own range, an oversampling factor that is fetching extra candidates on your behalf right now, a fusion constant justified by unnamed experiments, and a reranking window that decides what the second stage is even allowed to see. **You can name all of them. In four days you did.** That is the difference between operating a service and running one, and it is the only part of this list worth keeping — every value in it will change, the names will change, and some of these decisions will move to a different layer entirely.

    **The durable form is a habit and it is one sentence: before you promise anything about a system, enumerate the decisions inside it that somebody else made.** On this platform there were eight. On the next one there will be a different number, and the enumeration is the same act.

8. **You own something now.** The system runs, the users are real, and [S061](/wiki/seminars/S061.md) to [S064](/wiki/seminars/S064.md) profile, batch and budget it. **This is the first artefact in the programme with no scheduled end**, and the quarter that follows exists because of that.

**Warrant.** Consolidation placed after the performance and after the consequence, so that every point indexes something the student has just done and then watched happen. Point 5 is the day's most important and is deliberately left open, with both homes named per the deferral register in the [Whitepaper Standard](/wiki/program/05-Whitepaper-Standard.md). **Point 7 is the [Copilot Practice Standard](/wiki/program/12-Copilot-Practice-Standard.md)'s perishable-platform and inherited-defaults requirement in its terminal form** — the module's whole platform layer collapsed into one enumeration and then explicitly discarded in favour of the habit that produced it. Assessors are absent from this phase and the next.

## Phase 7 · Error clinic (40 min) — assessors absent

Instructor first: a substrate retrieval release that was correct in every measured respect, kept its promise, and broke a workflow nobody had described. A group of users had built habits on the *ordering* of the old system's results — always second, always below the changelog — and had never described that to anyone because it was not a feature and nobody had promised it. The new ranking was better by every measure taken and destroyed the habit. Attention is directed at two facts: **nothing in the release record was wrong, and the users could not have told you, because they did not know they were relying on it.**

Then the agent case, and it is the module's last and quietest. Over four days a team directed an agent to build a retrieval system. Every individual instruction was reviewed, every output was read, and every correction was recorded — the intervention record ran to eleven entries and was the best in the cohort. **Nobody could say who had decided the chunk size.** It appeared in a skillset the agent generated on day two, in response to a prompt that did not mention chunking, at the value the documentation recommends as a starting point. It was read, it was reasonable, and it was never a decision. It then determined what a vector represented, what a relevance judgement attached to, what recall meant, and what the users got. Attention goes to a distinction the day exists to install: **review catches wrong output. It does not catch an unmade decision, because an unmade decision produces output that is not wrong.**

Then students, in threes: **a time you replaced something and found out afterwards what people had been doing with the old one** — or a value in the system you shipped ninety minutes ago that nobody in your team decided.

**Warrant.** Error management targeting the limit of point 3. The instructor case contains a kept promise and a correct release, which is the only construction that makes the lesson about the unpromised surface rather than about diligence. It is placed after the production check so that students who have just read their own first user reports have somewhere to put them. **The agent case is the [Copilot Practice Standard](/wiki/program/12-Copilot-Practice-Standard.md)'s required addition and is deliberately the case where review was excellent** — the module's other agent cases fail against inattentive review, and this one fails against attentive review, which is the finding worth carrying into Q7.

## Phase 8 · Calibration and commitment (20 min)

- Sealed items opened. **Item 2 — your weakest evidence, and whether you volunteered it — against the phase 4 record.** The room's volunteering rate is reported.
- Item 3's first-failure prediction against phase 5's observed.
- **Item 5 opened**, against each student's own intervention record and against phase 5's production result. **The tally is of how many of the still-unsure items in fact appeared in the production check**, and it is the module's closing frontier datum.
- Predicted supervision level against awarded. Distribution shown without names.
- **Phase 5's production results in aggregate, with the prediction-match rate on lost documents stated plainly.**
- **The [S010](/wiki/seminars/S010.md) psychological-safety instrument, seventh administration.** The full series across six quarters is shown to the cohort, per the instrument's own adoption conditions.
- Each student writes **one thing they will watch for over the next four weeks**, and keeps it.
- Retrieval schedule: next morning, day 3, week 2, week 6.

## Assessment

**Entrustment judgement.** Activity: *ship a retrieval system into production and own the consequence.* Ordinal level from the scale in [Assessment Architecture](/wiki/program/03-Assessment-Architecture.md): 1, 2a, 2b, 3a, 3b, 3c, 4a, 4b. **Expected 2b; 3a is available.** Precursor to EA-1.

**Narrative substantiation is mandatory** and must cite specific content from the release record and specific moments from the defence, not impressions of either.

Per rule 7, **the level is formed from the release record before the defence begins**, and the defence is used to confirm, revise or overturn it.

**The [S030](/wiki/seminars/S030.md), [S040](/wiki/seminars/S040.md), [S045](/wiki/seminars/S045.md) and [S050](/wiki/seminars/S050.md) judgements are not carried forward.**

**Named failure conditions**, stated before phase 1 and repeated here: evidence cited without what it licenses; a failure-mode statement with no unintended selection property; a promise the user cannot check; a withdrawal signal with no named actor; and a declining decision with no statement of what evidence would change it. **Five, unchanged; no sixth has been added by the three records.**

**One specification item**, in IS203 jointly with PE202. Per rule 1, the checked elements are: the decision; the evidence with what each piece licenses; three failure modes with populations and frequencies, including one unintended selection property and one drawn from [S050](/wiki/seminars/S050.md) category 3; the user-facing promise with its failure behaviour; the withdrawal signal with threshold and named actor; the blast-radius estimate; **the prompt record including abandoned instructions; the intervention record; the frontier statement**; the phase 4 least-willing-to-own answer or an explicit declining; **and the phase 4 named inherited default with what accepting it committed you to.** **Nothing else is assessed.**

**An intervention record with no entries fails the specification**, unless the artefact demonstrates that the system's own design could have detected an intervention-worthy error. This is a specification failure and not an entrustment failure condition, and the distinction is deliberate: the record is evidence the assessors read, and it is not a sixth way to be judged unready.

**Phase 5's result is not assessed.** Stated in this specification so that the decoupling is a written commitment and not a practice.

**Error-clinic firewall.** Assessors are absent for phases 6 and 7 and receive no account of them. Per rater discipline rule 5, nothing surfaced by a student in phase 7 may enter any assessment judgement about that student, ever.

**Token economy.** This day is within the quarter's individual allocation. A deferred attempt costs one token and is re-run against the next substrate release, not against a re-staged version of this one.

**Rule-6 discharges.** Four obligations were placed on this day and all four are built.

- [S054](/wiki/seminars/S054.md) named this day's **failure-mode statement** as the response occasion for its step 3-against-step 6 prediction accuracy. **The element exists, is assessed, and is a named failure condition: part 3 requires one property the system selects for that nobody asked for, evidenced.** The obligation is discharged.
- [S058](/wiki/seminars/S058.md) named this day's **phase 3** as the response occasion for its step 7 promise checkability. **The element exists and is a named failure condition: part 4 is a user-facing promise the user can check, and an uncheckable promise is below 2b.** The obligation is discharged.
- [S058](/wiki/seminars/S058.md) named this day's **phase 5** as the response occasion for its step 3 document-level predictions. **The element exists: the production check compares each student's prediction of which documents the approximation would lose against the queries the system actually failed on under real traffic.** The obligation is discharged.
- [S059](/wiki/seminars/S059.md) named this day's **phase 4** as the response occasion for its phase 1 conclusion-change gap. **The element exists: every student is asked which published claim their decision rests on and what its per-dataset breakdown showed.** The obligation is discharged.

**[Q6](/wiki/quarters/Q6.md)'s reporting commitment** on the [S050](/wiki/seminars/S050.md) category-3 results is discharged at part 3 of the release record.

**Instrumentation under rule 6.** Two instruments with named response occasions, both in Q7:

- **The phase 5 prediction-match rate** — how often a student's predicted lost documents matched the queries that actually failed. **Response occasion: [S068](/wiki/seminars/S068.md), phase 5, where each student builds a detector for the failure class they did not anticipate here.**
- **The phase 3 decision split — ship, conditional, decline — against awarded levels.** **Response occasion: [S070](/wiki/seminars/S070.md), phase 3, where the same students make a release decision with a real audience, and the design checks whether declining here predicts anything about conduct there.**

**Frontier instrumentation.** Item 5's sealed still-unsure statements, coded against what phase 5 in fact surfaced, complete the module's frontier series and are carried to the [Durable and Perishable Register](/wiki/program/09-Durable-and-Perishable-Register.md) per instrumentation rule 9. Per rule 3 the sample is stated: one cohort, one substrate, one service, one agent version, over a four-day build whose task was chosen by staff. **The series describes this module's tasks, not the agent**, and it is read by the following year's cohort on that understanding.
