## The situation

Everything in this stage so far has made it easier to decline. This session makes it expensive.

If the work does not happen, something continues. Somebody keeps paying for it, usually in time and usually without anyone recording that they are. Until that cost is on the page next to the cost of proceeding, a refusal is a preference.

Answer these in writing before anything is taught.

1. If this work does not happen, what specifically continues to happen? To whom, and how often?
2. Who is currently paying that cost, and do they know they are?
3. What is the baseline, and who measured it?
4. If the engagement succeeds, who will establish that the change was caused by it?
5. What does the discovery work itself cost, and who bears that?

**Item 4 has no answer in almost any engagement, and it is not a hard question.** It is simply one that nobody has been assigned.

## What you must produce

A **do-nothing statement**: what continues, who pays it, what the baseline is or an explicit declaration that there is none, and the named person or function who would establish that any observed change was caused by the work.

## What good looks like

- The continuing cost is an event with a frequency and a bearer, **not a category with an adjective attached.**
- The baseline is either measured, with its instrument named, or explicitly declared absent — and declaring it absent, with the person who would have to produce one, is a complete answer.
- Someone is named as responsible for attribution. If nobody can be, that is written down as the finding it is.
- The cost of the engagement itself appears on the same page, including the cost of the discovery you are currently doing.

## How it goes wrong

Four failure modes, each with the warning sign that precedes it.

- **The unowned number** — a figure appears with no instrument behind it. *Warning sign:* you cannot say who would produce it again next quarter, or how.
- **Attribution by assumption** — the benefit is defined as the change attributable to the work, which presupposes exactly the thing that needed establishing. *Warning sign:* your definition contains the word "attributable" and no method.
- **The costless refusal** — declining is treated as the zero option. *Warning sign:* your do-nothing column is empty, or contains the word "nothing".
- **Baseline by recollection** — a figure somebody remembered in a meeting becomes the reference point. *Warning sign:* the source of the baseline is a person rather than an instrument, and nobody has noticed.

## Do it

Work from [Brief A](/wiki/quarters/Q1.md). Records are returned by the assessor and reworked; nobody can say how often, and nobody has looked. That is not a gap in the brief. It is the normal condition, and the session's work is to write it down as such rather than to fill it in.

Method is yours. **You will not be told what counts as a baseline**, and a learner who declares that none exists, names who would have to produce one, and states what that would cost has answered correctly.

## Show it

Read the do-nothing column aloud, and then read the attribution line.

If the attribution line names nobody, say so out loud rather than moving past it. **A claim whose attribution is assigned to nobody is not a claim**, and this is the session where saying that costs nothing.

## What this does not establish

The framework this session is built against is not exaggerated; it contains no magnitudes at all. It instructs the reader to establish a baseline and never exhibits one being established, catalogues metric definitions and never reports one being measured, and recommends a simulation method for quantities it never quantifies.

**A framework that generates numbers while asserting none is harder to check than one that over-claims**, because there is no proposition to check the numbers against. That diagnosis is recorded from a complete reading of the material and is the strongest thing this session rests on. Everything else here — that pricing the alternative improves decisions, that a named attributor changes behaviour — is design reasoning with no external warrant.
