# S065 · The Cost Argument You Present to Someone Who Pays ⊘

**Module:** [M13](/wiki/modules/M13.md) · **Quarter:** [Q7](/wiki/quarters/Q7.md) · **Complexity class:** 5
**Strands:** ME · IS · SY
**Whitepaper:** [WP-065](/wiki/whitepapers/WP-065.md)
**Depends on:** S029, S061, S062, S063, S064
**Re-tests:** S050
**Platform anchor:** The capacity stack of S061–S064, read through Microsoft Cost Management · **MCP servers:** Azure MCP Server (read) · Microsoft Learn MCP Server

⊘ **Assessment-bearing day, the quarter's first entrustment judgement, and the module's only occasion with a counterparty who can refuse.**

---

## What this day is for

Four days have produced a cost model, a gateway policy, a reservation discipline and a capacity commitment. Every one of them is defensible. Every one of them is running or deployable. **None of them has been said out loud to anybody who controls the money.**

Today somebody does, and they are not persuaded by the thing the students are proudest of.

The payer does not want the model. They want to know what they are being asked for, what it buys, what happens if they say no, and when they will find out whether the person asking was right. [S020](/wiki/seminars/S020.md) established that nine quarters ago and the cohort has not had to act on it since, because every audience between then and now has been technical.

**The ask today is a specific one and the specificity is what makes the occasion real.** It is a quantity of capacity, of a named kind, under a named commitment shape, for a named period, with a date. Each of those four is a decision the student made on one of the four preceding days and can be traced back to a measurement. **And the platform will not let the ask be vague**, because there is no configuration in which somebody buys "more capacity": you buy a quantity, of a type, in a region, under an hourly meter or a term commitment, and every one of those choices has a consequence the payer can be told about.

**The target is not honesty and it is not arithmetic.** Both are already present. The target is the composite act: **ask for a specific amount of money, from a person who can refuse, on evidence you produced, having first exhausted what is already paid for, having named who bears the cost of your proposal, and having stated in advance the date and the number that will show you were wrong.**

And then, at phase 5, the bill for the last five weeks arrives.

---

## The entrustment judgement, stated before phase 1

> Today's judgement is on: **present a cost argument to someone who pays for it.** It is a precursor to the first entrusted activity, EA-1, and it touches the third, EA-3 — and it is neither of them. EA-1 is a ship or no-ship decision with an evaluation result attached, which is [S070](/wiki/seminars/S070.md). EA-3 is a risk communication to a non-technical decision-maker on a live decision, which is [S083](/wiki/seminars/S083.md). **Today's decision is real, its counterparties are real, and its scope is one quarter of the substrate's serving spend.**
>
> Two assessors judge independently before conferring, and **form their level from your artefact and your two encounters before the defence begins**, per rater discipline rule 7. The defence tests the level; it does not create it. The expected level is **2b**, and 3a is available and will be rare.
>
> **The S030, S040, S045, S050 and S060 judgements are not carried forward.** Each occasion is judged on its own, and this one concerns a decision whose consequence has not yet arrived.
>
> **Five failure conditions are named in advance. The first two are symmetric.**
>
> 1. **Asking for money you do not need.** Requesting spend for capacity the system already holds and is not using — the reserved-and-unused figure you measured at [S063](/wiki/seminars/S063.md) — is below 2b.
> 2. **Not asking for money you do need.** A recommendation that fits the budget by silently accepting a degradation the payer was not told about is also below 2b. **This is the one this cohort is at risk of**, because it looks like frugality and is rewarded everywhere else.
> 3. **Presenting capacity, quota and budget as one number, or as three numbers without three owners**, is a failure to perform the activity.
> 4. **A figure presented without its confidence and provenance in the same field** is below 2b, per specification rule 2. **On this day provenance includes the retrieval date of every commercial rate the figure depends on**, because a rate quoted without one cannot be re-derived on the day the recommendation becomes checkable.
> 5. **A recommendation with no date at which it becomes checkable and no number that would show it wrong** is a failure to perform the activity.

## Phase 1 · Pretest and prediction (25 min)

Five items, sealed and opened at phase 8.

1. **Write your ask as one sentence**, in the form you would actually say it.
2. What in that sentence will your payer want to be different?
3. **Which number in your argument are you most likely to soften, and to which of the two payers?**
4. Predict your supervision level.
5. **Copilot prediction, sealed.** You will shortly ask the agent to draft your one-sentence ask from your own artefact. Write down what it will produce, and **name what it will leave out.** Sealed with items 1 to 4 and opened at phase 8.

Item 3 is the day's honest item and is [S050](/wiki/seminars/S050.md) item 3's construction with a number in place of a claim. The repeat is deliberate and is named at phase 8; the two rates are reported side by side and are **not compared**, per instrumentation rule 2.

Item 5 is the module's closing frontier datum and it is deliberately the easiest prediction in the module to make and the hardest to make specifically. The design's expectation, from four days of evidence, is that the agent will produce a fluent, confident, well-formed sentence containing a number and containing **no owner, no expiry and no refusal branch** — and that a majority of students will predict *something will be missing* while a minority will name which of the three.

**Warrant.** Pretesting the position before the pressure arrives, so that movement under pressure is measurable rather than remembered. Item 1 is written before the artefact is finalised, which means the sentence a student would actually say is captured before it has been polished into something they would not. Item 5 is administered here rather than later, per instrumentation rule 8, and it closes the module's frontier series.

- [S062](/wiki/seminars/S062.md): the three-part latency promise, and the fact that every policy chooses who waits.

- [S064](/wiki/seminars/S064.md): the four quantities, their owners and their cadences.
- **[S029](/wiki/seminars/S029.md):** point 1 — start from the decision, not the question — and point 8's honest form of a limits statement.

- **Bridge:** S029 said start from the decision rather than the question, and that an instrument better than the decision requires is waste. **Your payer has a decision. What is it, and what is the smallest thing you could give them that would settle it?**

The room answers: the decision is whether to release the money, and the smallest sufficient thing is the binding quantity, its margin, and what would change the answer. That is [S020](/wiki/seminars/S020.md)'s four parts minus the model — and the model is the part the students most want to present.

**Warrant.** Cumulative retrieval across the whole module and across two earlier modules, closing with a bridge that produces the day's hardest instruction as a consequence of a measurement principle the cohort already holds. Students who reach it themselves spend the day economising rather than being told to.

## Phase 3 · The cost argument (60 min)

**The entrusted activity. Assessors observe and do not intervene.**

**This day departs from the [Seminar Day Design Pattern](/wiki/program/04-Seminar-Day-Design-Pattern.md), as assessment-bearing days are permitted to: phases 5 to 7 are replaced by an entrustment occasion, so there are no contrasting cases and no learning task.** The [Copilot Practice Standard](/wiki/program/12-Copilot-Practice-Standard.md)'s build requirement is therefore met differently. **Nothing new is built today. The four builds of S061 to S064 are what is being argued about, and their prompt records, intervention records and frontier statements travel with the artefact into the room** — which is a harder test of them than any specification, because the assessors can ask who wrote the number.

**The artefact is committed before this phase begins** and contains seven elements:

1. **The ask**, in currency per unit time, with what it buys. **Specific enough to act on**: a quantity, a kind of capacity, a commitment shape, a period, and the region or routing scope it applies to.
2. **The four quantities separately** — demand, capacity, quota, budget — each with its owner, its as-of date and its update cadence.
3. **The cheapest capacity available**, measured, and why it is not sufficient — or the statement that it is sufficient and no spend is required. **Three sources are expected and each is a measurement from a prior day**: capacity declared and not used, from [S063](/wiki/seminars/S063.md); the cache rate you are not achieving, from [S061](/wiki/seminars/S061.md); and any commitment coverage the organisation already holds and is not consuming.
4. **Who bears the latency cost** of the proposed operating point, and whether they have been told.
5. **Every figure with its confidence, its provenance and the retrieval date of every commercial rate it depends on, in the same field.**
6. **The recommendation, its check date, and the number that would show it wrong.**
7. **What you will do if the ask is refused.**

**The three records travel with it and are not part of the seven.** The prompt records, intervention records and frontier statements from S061 to S064 are appended, unedited. They are not assessed as artefact elements today — they were assessed on their own days — but **they are available to the assessors and to payer B**, and a student whose numbers were produced by an agent they did not direct will find that out in the second encounter rather than in a mark.

Two encounters of twenty-five minutes, with ten minutes between to reset.

- **Payer A · the person who signs for the substrate's serving spend.** A real staff member with real authority to refuse, and no technical background. Underneath every question they ask is one question: *what do I get, and what happens if I do not.*
- **Payer B · the person whose budget it comes out of.** Technically competent, has another use for the money, and **will attack the cost model rather than the recommendation.** Briefed to ask, at least once, where a specific number came from and who produced it.

The order is fixed. **A is first**, because the student must state a plain formulation in public before it is attacked, which is the professional sequence and the one that makes the attack informative.

**The same body of evidence must produce two materially different and simultaneously true arguments.** This is the competence [WP-013](/wiki/whitepapers/WP-013.md) named as assessed here, and it is what the assessors are watching for at phase 4 question 2.

**Warrant.** The task-class variability plan for M13 varies the payer: one body of evidence, two parties, two incompatible interests. **The counterparties are the real ones, per instrumentation rule 1** — [WP-024](/wiki/whitepapers/WP-024.md) §8 identified that a real requester was available at this day and had not been used, and named that as the more interesting of the two omissions it recorded. It is used here. Payer B exists because a payer who agrees is not an assessment; payer A exists because a technical interrogation is one the cohort has already survived four times. **The records are present rather than assessed** because their assessment already happened, and because the one thing an entrustment occasion can test that a specification cannot is whether the student can answer for work an agent did on their behalf.

## Phase 4 · The defence (60 min)

Assessors have formed a level before this phase begins. The defence tests it; it does not create it.

- What are you asking for, in currency per unit time, and what does it buy?
- **Which of your numbers is a measurement, which is somebody else's decision, and which is your commitment?**
- What is the cheapest capacity you did not take, and why not?
- **Who waits, under your recommendation, and did you tell them?**
- What date does this become checkable, and what number shows you were wrong?
- If I halve the ask, what breaks first, and when?
- **Point to a number in this argument that an agent produced. What did you check about it before you used it?**
- **Name the number in your argument you are least willing to defend.**

The last is asked of every student. **Declining to name one is recorded as an answer**, not as a refusal, and is discussed rather than penalised — but it is a datum about calibration and the assessors treat it as one.

**Warrant.** Defence-after-artefact, per rater discipline rule 7. The questions are diagnostic rather than evaluative, and each is constructed to surface a discrimination the two encounters may have concealed — particularly the second, which separates a student who holds the four quantities apart from one who has listed them. **The agent question is the [Copilot Practice Standard](/wiki/program/12-Copilot-Practice-Standard.md)'s requirement met in the register available to an assessment-bearing day**: the standard asks for a frontier statement from a build, and the build is four weeks of the student's own work, so the honest test is whether they can say what they checked rather than what they produced.

## Phase 5 · The bill (35 min)

**[S061](/wiki/seminars/S061.md) committed that this day would report the realised cost against every model built there.** That commitment is discharged here.

The substrate's serving spend for weeks 61 to 65 is retrieved **from Cost Management, in front of the room, by whoever asks for it** — the cohort has had read access since week 61 and this is the first time most of them will have opened it. Each student's S061 cost model and [S064](/wiki/seminars/S064.md) commitment are sorted into three categories:

1. **Costs modelled, which occurred** roughly as modelled and inside the stated divergence.
2. **Costs modelled, which did not occur.**
3. **Costs that occurred and were not modelled.**

Individual results are given to each student privately. The cohort sees the aggregate, and **category 3 is additionally split into costs the S061 scope statement had named as omitted and costs nobody named at all.**

**No assessment attaches to this result, and that was stated at S061 as well as here.** A student whose category 3 is large is not marked down. If the check were assessed, S061's limits statements would become strategic — students would list every conceivable cost to avoid category 3 — and the considered statement the module wants would stop being produced.

**Warrant.** The programme's fourth long-horizon prediction check, after S020, S040 and S050, and the first whose subject is money. Category 3 is the payload: it is the module's only measurement of what a student did not think to cost, which is a different and larger quantity than what they costed wrongly. The split within category 3 is [WP-061](/wiki/whitepapers/WP-061.md) §9 prediction 4's instrument and distinguishes a modelling failure from a scope statement that was delivered and not acted on.

## Phase 6 · Consolidating instruction (35 min) — assessors absent

1. **A number without its provenance will travel without it.** Specification rule 2 is not a formatting convention; it is a claim about what happens to figures inside organisations. The two-payer construction demonstrates it directly — the figure that reaches the decision is the one payer B repeated to payer A.

2. **Two true arguments from one body of evidence.** [S013](/wiki/seminars/S013.md) phase 7's question in its cost form: the arguments to A and to B differed in what was selected and both were true. **Selection is legitimate when the omitted material is available on request and the selection serves the audience's decision rather than yours.** The boundary between that and misrepresentation is not settled today and returns at [S088](/wiki/seminars/S088.md).

3. **The cheapest capacity first.** An ask that has not exhausted what is already paid for will be granted once, and remembered.

4. **A cost argument that names nobody who waits has hidden a party.** [S062](/wiki/seminars/S062.md) point 5's distributive decision arrives at the moment money changes hands, and this is the only occasion in the quarter where someone with authority hears it.

5. **The expiry condition is the professional part.** A recommendation with no date and no falsifying number is an opinion with arithmetic attached. This is the competence [WP-012](/wiki/whitepapers/WP-012.md) named as assessed here and again at [S088](/wiki/seminars/S088.md).

6. **"What if I refuse" is part of the ask.** An engineer with no prepared refusal branch is asking for permission rather than making a recommendation, and every payer can tell the difference.

7. **The payer's real question is almost never the number.** [S020](/wiki/seminars/S020.md) phase 5 point 4, nine quarters on, and confirmed today by the person who actually signs: they want the margin and the assumption. The model is what you needed in order to produce them, and it is not what they need to hear.

8. **Every number you presented today has an expiry you did not set.** The day's perishable platform-specific point, verified for this offering on **2026-07-26**. The rate you priced with is published on a page that changes without notice. The unit you bought in yields different amounts of service on different models, so **a model change re-prices your commitment**. The discount you argued for is a term commitment against a meter, not a purchase of anything, and it does not shrink when your deployment does. And the capacity you asked for may not exist when the money is approved, because availability moves through the day with other customers' demand. **Not one of those is an argument against making the ask.** They are the reason the ask carries a date, and they are why element 5 requires a retrieval date rather than merely a provenance — a figure you cannot re-derive on the day it becomes checkable is a figure you cannot be held to and cannot be vindicated by.

9. **Defaults are opinions, and today's was made by the organisation rather than the vendor.** Somebody decided, before any of you arrived, that this service would run on shared capacity billed per token with no latency guarantee. **That was a decision. It was reasonable. It was not written down anywhere, and until this quarter nobody in this room could have told you it had been made.** The general form is the one worth carrying: an operating position that nobody argued for is still an operating position, and the first person to notice it is usually the person asking to change it. **An engineer who cannot state what was chosen for them does not understand their own system**, and today you were the one who had to state it to somebody who signs.

**Warrant.** Consolidation placed after the performance so that every point indexes an encounter that has just happened. Point 2 answers the cost half of a question left open at S013 and names where the remainder goes, per the deferral register in the [Whitepaper Standard](/wiki/program/05-Whitepaper-Standard.md). Point 7 is the day's most durable sentence and it is the one the cohort will have just experienced being true. **Point 8 is the day's perishable platform-specific point and point 9 is the day's defaults point**, both required by the [Copilot Practice Standard](/wiki/program/12-Copilot-Practice-Standard.md) and both placed here rather than at phase 3 because this day's expository phase is phase 6.

## Phase 7 · Error clinic (40 min) — assessors absent

Instructor first: a substrate cost saving, taken deliberately, correctly argued and correctly approved. Six weeks later, an incident. **Nobody connected the two** — the decision was recorded in one quarter's spend review, the incident in another quarter's incident log, and no artefact joined them. Attention is directed at the joining rather than at the decision: **the saving may well have been right, and nobody can now say.**

Then the agent case, which closes the module's series and is deliberately the mildest of the four. A cost summary drafted by Copilot in agent mode for a real spend review, from real telemetry, correct in every figure. **It was circulated, read by four people, and quoted back six weeks later with one number detached from its qualification.** The agent had written the qualification — in the sentence after the number, which is where a competent writer puts it and where specification rule 2 says it must not go. Attention goes to the fact that **the error was in the artefact's shape rather than its content**, that no reviewer could have been expected to catch it by reading, and that the one thing that would have caught it is a rule this programme has taught since Q1 and the agent has never been told.

Then students, in threes: **a decision you made whose consequence you never found out**, or a number you circulated in a shape that let it travel alone.

**Warrant.** Error management targeting the module's class-5 property directly. The instructor case contains no error of judgement that anyone can identify, which is the point — the failure is that the record could not support the question, and it is the failure this quarter's whole structure is built to prevent. **The agent case is the [Copilot Practice Standard](/wiki/program/12-Copilot-Practice-Standard.md)'s required addition and is chosen for its mildness**: after three days of expensive agent failures, the closing one is a formatting decision, and it is the one most likely to happen to every student in their first job. The student disclosure is the softest in the programme by design, because it asks for an absence rather than a mistake.

## Assessment

**Entrustment judgement.** Activity: *present a cost argument to someone who pays for it.* Ordinal level from the scale in [Assessment Architecture](/wiki/program/03-Assessment-Architecture.md): 1, 2a, 2b, 3a, 3b, 3c, 4a, 4b. **Expected 2b; 3a is available and will be rare.**

**Narrative substantiation is mandatory** and must cite specific moments from the two encounters, not impressions of them.

Per rule 7, the level is formed from the artefact and the two encounters, and the defence is used to test it.

**Named failure conditions**, published before phase 1 and repeated here: asking for spend without exhausting the cheapest capacity; fitting the budget by silently accepting an untold degradation; presenting capacity, quota and budget as one number or without three owners; a figure without its confidence, provenance and rate retrieval date in the same field; and a recommendation with no check date and no falsifying number. **A student who satisfies both payers on the first encounter has either been fortunate in the assignment or has changed the ask, and the assessors check which.**

**One specification item**, in IS301 jointly with ME301. Per rule 1, the checked elements are: the seven artefact elements as listed at phase 3; **the appended prompt records, intervention records and frontier statements from S061 to S064, present and unedited**; for each of the two payers, what they wanted the argument to say and what was said instead; the phase 4 self-identified least-defensible number or an explicit declining; **the phase 4 answer on what was checked about an agent-produced figure**; and one number the student will not present again without its provenance. **Nothing else is assessed.**

**The three records are checked for presence and integrity, not for quality.** They were assessed on their own days and are not re-assessed here; what is checked is that they arrived, that they were not rewritten after the fact, and that the student can answer for what is in them. **A record edited after its day fails**, and this is the only assessment consequence attaching to them at this occasion.

**No commercial figure, product name, deployment category, commitment shape or platform mechanism is assessed.** All are perishable under the [Durable and Perishable Register](/wiki/program/09-Durable-and-Perishable-Register.md). What is assessed is the structure of the ask, the separation of the quantities, the provenance discipline, and the expiry condition.

**Phase 5's result is not assessed.** Stated in this specification and in [S061](/wiki/seminars/S061.md)'s, so that the decoupling is a written commitment made before the artefact rather than a reassurance offered after it.

**Error-clinic firewall.** Assessors are absent for phases 6 and 7 and receive no account of them.

**Token economy.** This day is within the quarter's individual allocation; a deferred attempt costs one token and is re-run against a different cost position, not the same one.

**Rule-6 discharges.** Three obligations placed on this day are discharged in the entrustment artefact itself.

- [S062](/wiki/seminars/S062.md) named this day as the response occasion for its step 5 waiting-class statement, requiring that the cost argument name who bears the latency cost of the proposed operating point. **The element exists as artefact element 4, it is a checked element, and failing to state whether that party has been told is examined at phase 4.** The obligation is discharged.
- [S063](/wiki/seminars/S063.md) named this day as the response occasion for its step 3 reserved-and-unused figure, requiring that the cheapest capacity available be stated before any spend is requested. **The element exists as artefact element 3 and is the first named failure condition of the entrustment judgement**, which is the strongest form a receiving element can take in this programme. The obligation is discharged.
- [S064](/wiki/seminars/S064.md) named this day as the response occasion for its phase 3 classification error rate, requiring capacity, quota and budget to be presented as three numbers with three owners. **The element exists as artefact element 2 and is the third named failure condition.** The obligation is discharged.

**Instrumentation under rule 6.** Two instruments with named response occasions, both outside this module:

- **The supervision-level distribution and the phase 4 least-defensible-number responses** measure whether the cohort can hold a cost position under pressure from a party with an interest. **Response occasion: [S070](/wiki/seminars/S070.md), where the same students make a release decision carrying a cost position in front of a real audience, and the two distributions are reported together.** This closes the forward link [WP-050](/wiki/whitepapers/WP-050.md) §8 recorded as missing at the equivalent occasion one quarter ago. **A second reading is taken at [S083](/wiki/seminars/S083.md) for the EA-3 half, and per instrumentation rule 2 the two readings are reported and not compared** — the elicitation is shared between the two occasions, so a difference between them would measure familiarity with the protocol as much as any change in the students.
- **The phase 5 category-3 composition** — what the cohort did not think to cost, split into named and unnamed omissions — measures the reach of the module's scope statements. **Response occasion: [S070](/wiki/seminars/S070.md), whose cost position must state which category-3 term from today it now accounts for and which it still does not.**

**Instrumentation rule 1 is satisfied by construction.** Both counterparties are real people present for their own reasons, and the observation of whether the argument worked is theirs rather than the student's or a peer's. [WP-024](/wiki/whitepapers/WP-024.md) §8 recorded that a real requester was available at this day and was not used; that omission is closed.

## What to take from this day

**The claim to hold.** **Ask for what you need and no more, and exhaust what is already paid for first.** The two failures are symmetric: requesting spend for capacity the system already holds, and fitting the budget by silently accepting a degradation the payer was never told about. The second looks like frugality and is rewarded almost everywhere else.

**What the sources license, and what they do not.** The requirement that a figure travel with its confidence and its provenance in the same field follows [research/03-measurement-evaluation/miller-adding-error-bars-to-evals.md](/research/03-measurement-evaluation/miller-adding-error-bars-to-evals.md), cited for **direction and mechanism only**: a quantity separated from its qualification is read as more certain than it is. The entrustment scale follows [research/08-assessment-epas/ten-cate-epa-framework.md](/research/08-assessment-epas/ten-cate-epa-framework.md). **No magnitude is asserted from either**, and every commercial figure in your ask is perishable by the week.

**Guidance for your own work.**

- **Put the qualification in the same field as the number.** The figure that reaches the decision is the one somebody repeated, and it travels alone.
- **Prepare the refusal branch.** An engineer with no answer to *what if I say no* is asking permission, and every payer can tell.
- **Name who waits** under your proposed operating point, and say whether they have been told.
- **Hold provenance and correctness apart**, per the [Agent Configuration Standard](/wiki/program/13-Agent-Configuration-Standard.md). Saying where an agent-produced number came from is not the same as having checked it, and the defence asks the second question.

**Where this is easy to get wrong.** Presenting the model. It is what you needed in order to produce the margin and the assumption, and it is not what the payer needs to hear.
