## The situation

This is the stage's second assessment occasion and it does not assess the same thing as [S045](/wiki/seminars/S045.md).

There, the boundary of the increment was marked. **Here the boundary of your understanding is marked**, and the standing prohibition from the source material is the spine of the whole occasion:

**Use the tooling to make each step better, not to skip steps.**

The assessment asks you to say which steps it made better and which ones it let you skip. **Both answers are expected. A candidate with no skipped steps has not audited, and a candidate with no improved steps has not delegated.**

Answer these in writing before you begin, and submit them.

1. Which step of your process did the tooling genuinely make better, and what specifically got better?
2. Which step did it let you skip, and what would show whether the skip cost anything?
3. Which region of your increment can you not explain, and what is it doing in a shipped build?
4. What did you configure that changed what the executor produced, and how do you know it was the configuration?
5. What in your account would stop being true if the pin moved?

**Item 4 is the one candidates get wrong.** A configuration you wrote and an output you liked is not a causal chain, and the honest answer is often *I do not know, because I changed it and never observed the version without it.*

## What you must produce

A **build defence**, presented to an assessor who has read your [S045](/wiki/seminars/S045.md) increment pack and your [S046](/wiki/seminars/S046.md) instruction set and nothing else.

- **The increment**, and the pin under which it was produced: commit, package version, host, model and date.
- **The instruction set**, with each constraint's conformance mode.
- **The plan**, with identifiers that resolve into the implementation record.
- **The comprehension audit** from [S048](/wiki/seminars/S048.md), with its opaque regions still classified opaque.
- **The confirmation record**, including standing permissions.
- **The inexplicability register** from [S049](/wiki/seminars/S049.md), with its stopping rules.
- **The check inventory**, with every entry labelled advisory or authoritative and each label's basis.
- **The improved-and-skipped ledger**: which steps the tooling improved, which it let you skip, and what evidence bears on each.

## What good looks like

These are the assessed elements. Each is checked as present and adequate, or not.

- **Every region of the increment is classified in the comprehension audit**, and the unexplained regions are named rather than absent.
- **A step the tooling made better is named**, with what specifically improved rather than a claim that things went faster.
- **A step that was skipped is named**, with what evidence would show whether the skip cost anything.
- **Each constraint in the instruction set states its conformance mode** — by execution, by review, or not at all — and the *not at all* entries are present.
- **A decision recorded during planning can be located from the implementation record by its identifier**, demonstrated live.
- **The inexplicability register is present and non-empty**, or its emptiness is defended with what was looked for and where.
- **Every check cited in the defence is labelled advisory or authoritative**, and the basis of the label is stated.
- **The pin is recorded in full**, and the candidate names which of their own conclusions would not survive it moving.
- **Nothing in the defence asserts a magnitude that was not measured by a named instrument.**

**Nothing else is assessed.** Whether the increment works is not marked. How much of it was generated is not marked. The ratio of generated to hand-written work is not marked. Speed, throughput and volume are not marked. The elegance of the code is not marked. Whether an agent was used at all is not marked. Whether the opaque regions were eventually explained is not marked. Whether the configuration in fact caused the output is not marked — only whether the candidate can say how they would find out.

## How it goes wrong

- **The delegated defence** — every difficult question is answered with *the agent wrote that*. *Warning sign:* your account of the increment has no author in it.
- **The unaudited green build** — everything passes, so everything is understood. *Warning sign:* your comprehension audit has no opaque class.
- **The improvement with no content** — the tooling made everything better, in no specified way. *Warning sign:* item 1's answer would fit any increment.
- **The confident causal chain** — the configuration produced the output, established by having written the configuration. *Warning sign:* you never saw the increment produced without it.
- **The reattributed shortfall** — everything that did not work is explained by the host, the model or the customer's environment. *Warning sign:* your account contains no mistake of your own. **This is the failure the source corpus itself institutionalises**, in a prepared answer that reattributes disappointing delivery to the customer's own constraints, and it is the one this occasion marks hardest against.
- **The unpinned defence** — the account is made about the tooling in general rather than about a revision. *Warning sign:* item 5 is blank.

## Do it

Assemble it, then attack it in this order.

**First, record the pin before anything else.** Commit, package version, host, model, date. Everything else in the defence is a claim conditioned on those five things, and a defence that does not state them is a claim about a moving object.

**Second, write the improved-and-skipped ledger honestly and in that order.** Improvements first, because they are easier and they calibrate the standard for specificity. Then the skips. **A skip is not a confession** — it is the professional content of this occasion, and the prohibition it stands against is the source material's own best rule, contradicted by that same material's proudest story.

**Third, rehearse item 3 out loud.** You are going to be asked to defend a region chosen by somebody else, and the region will not be the one you prepared. Practise on regions selected at random by another candidate.

**Fourth, establish one causal claim properly, or withdraw it.** Take the configuration change you believe altered the output. Produce the increment without it. If you cannot — because it is expensive, because the model has moved, because the increment is already shipped — **say that, and say what it would take.** An unestablished causal claim withdrawn with its cost stated scores better here than one asserted confidently.

Then have an agent argue that your defence is unfounded — that you cannot distinguish the configuration's effect from the model's default behaviour, and that your audit's *explained* class is optimistic. Keep what is better than your own reasoning; record what you rejected. **An agent that endorsed your defence was not used as an instrument**, which is [S001](/wiki/seminars/S001.md)'s claim closing the stage that finally delegated to it.

## Show it

**The assessor selects a region of your increment and asks you to defend it line by line.** You do not choose the region.

*The agent wrote that* is an accepted answer to the question of provenance and is not an answer to the question asked. The follow-up is always the same: **what did you do about it, and what is it doing in a build you shipped?**

A candidate who classifies the region as opaque, points to its entry in the audit, states the remediation price and says why it was not worth paying has answered completely. **The occasion does not require you to understand everything you shipped. It requires you to know what you did not understand, and to have decided about it on purpose.**

## What this does not establish

This occasion shows that you can account for a build produced partly by delegation. **It does not show that the delegation helped**, and nothing in the material behind this stage could show that.

The agent-configuration repository contains evaluation machinery and **no committed results** — a grader exists, a result corpus does not — and its own documentation states that its behaviour checks test output shape rather than correctness and that some of its checks are advisory. Every benefit word in its prose is unwarranted at the revision this stage pins. **The internal corpus offers approximately thirty quantitative assertions of which almost all measure activity rather than outcome, exactly one names a measurement instrument, and no figure from any of them is reproduced anywhere in this repository.** The open playbook offers no comparative evidence for any practice on its site.

**No cohort running this occasion will settle any of that**, and the design says so rather than implying that the defence is a measurement.

**The occasion cannot distinguish a candidate who understood their build from one who audited it well.** That is conceded at [WP-050](/wiki/whitepapers/WP-050.md) §8, and the design accepts it, because the alternative — marking comprehension directly — would be the assessor's opinion in a specification's clothing.

|---|---|
