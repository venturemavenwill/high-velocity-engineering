## The situation

You have a claim about where this engagement got to, a procedure for re-deriving it, and an adoption position and a re-run finding that either support the claim or do not.

Today somebody else executes your procedure, without you, against the estate. **You will not be in the room while they do it.**

Before that happens you lodge a prediction of what they will find, dimension by dimension, and the prediction is sealed. When they are finished, you reconcile the two in front of the assessors.

This is not a specification check. **It is a judgement about you** — whether you can be trusted to say what a claim about improvement actually rests on — and it is formed from everything observed across this module rather than from what you produce this morning.

Answer these in writing before the occasion opens. They are lodged with your claim and they are not revised afterwards.

1. What is your claim, in one sentence, with its scope and its date?
2. For each dimension: what position will the executor reach, and what will they inspect to reach it?
3. Which dimension will they fail to reach at all, and why will they fail?
4. Where do you expect them to reach a different position from yours, and which of you will be right?
5. What in your own evidence argues against your claim, and why did you make the claim anyway?

**Item 3 is the one the occasion is built on.** A procedure with no predicted failure is a procedure its author has not run.

## What you must produce

Three things, lodged together before the occasion, and one produced during it.

- **The claim.** What matured, over what interval, at what scope, with the name of whoever is responsible for establishing that the change was caused by the work.
- **The re-derivation procedure.** Per dimension: the artefact, the observation, the rule that turns the observation into a position, and the artefacts it deliberately does not use.
- **The sealed prediction.** Per dimension, the position you expect the executor to reach, and the dimensions you expect to defeat them.
- **The reconciliation**, written live: for every divergence between the prediction and the execution, which of three things happened — the evidence is not there, the rule was wrong, or the executor read it differently from you — and what follows.

## The material

### A maturity claim with its counter-evidence attached is the only kind worth making

This is the module's closing claim and it is deliberately narrow.

A claim without counter-evidence is not a stronger claim. **It is a claim whose author did not look**, or looked and did not say, and a reader cannot tell which. Attaching the finding that damages your own position does two things at once: it tells a reader what you searched for, and it tells them the search was honest enough to report a result it did not want. **Both are properties of the claimant, and in the absence of any calibrated instrument they are the only properties a reader has to work with.**

The counter-evidence is not a caveat. A caveat is a general softening — *within limits*, *broadly*, *at this stage* — and it protects the author. **Counter-evidence is a specific finding that argues the other way**, and it exposes the author. The grammatical test is quick: a caveat cannot be checked, and counter-evidence can.

**This is class 3.** No source available to this programme establishes that claims carrying counter-evidence are more accurate, more trusted or more useful than claims without it. It is an argument about what a reader can do with a claim, and it is the argument this module ends on.

### Assembling three instruments, each with a different silence

The occasion's claim rests on three kinds of platform evidence, and the point of using three is that their weaknesses do not overlap.

**Posture findings tell you about configuration and sample the estate.** The governance surfaces split into data posture and workload posture, with a generative AI Bill of Materials on one side and oversharing assessment on the other ([research/06-microsoft-platform/purview-dspm-for-ai-and-defender-ai-spm-docs.md](/research/06-microsoft-platform/purview-dspm-for-ai-and-defender-ai-spm-docs.md)). Their four infrastructure-as-code checks — private endpoints, restricted endpoints, managed identity, identity-based authentication — are the cheapest re-derivable evidence available, because a configuration is either present or absent and a stranger can look. **And the assessment samples the estate rather than covering it**, on a periodic cadence, with a reporting delay measured in days and item-level scanning capped. **What you may claim is bounded by the scan scope, the cadence and the latency, in that order.**

**Gateway telemetry tells you about traffic and cannot tell you about use.** A dimensioned token metric attributes consumption to a receiving group, and the source's own summary is the useful one: **dimensions are what make a metric answerable** ([research/06-microsoft-platform/apim-ai-gateway-docs.md](/research/06-microsoft-platform/apim-ai-gateway-docs.md)). It establishes that a request was made under an identity. **It does not establish that a person read the output, kept it, or was helped by it**, and a claim treating a token count as evidence of value has laundered a volume into a benefit.

**Evaluation results tell you about scores and not about agreement with a person.** The evaluation surface computes, thresholds and reports — and **publishes no agreement rate with human judgement for any evaluator, no calibration data and no rationale for any default threshold.** Most of those evaluators are model-judged. **Assert no agreement figure for any of them, here or anywhere.**

**Three silences, none overlapping, and that is the design.** Posture is silent on accuracy. Telemetry is silent on value. Evaluation is silent on agreement. **A claim assembled from all three is bounded by the union of their silences, and the assembly's job is to state that union rather than to hope the three cover each other.**

**Limits carried:** the Purview page consulted describes itself as the classic version and states it has been replaced; the Defender material was read through search excerpts with structure reconstructed; agent-level capabilities move to a separate licence in 2026; **no detection or false-positive rate is published for either posture product and none is asserted**; gateway policy names, tier availability and preview status change frequently, and the gateway page recommends logging prompts and completions without discussing the privacy consequences.

### A difference smaller than its own noise is not evidence of anything

The claim you lodge is a claim about change, and change claims fail statistically before they fail rhetorically.

Reporting conventions in adjacent fields highlight a leading figure "but not necessarily to test that result for any kind of statistical significance", and two consequences bind directly ([research/03-measurement-evaluation/miller-adding-error-bars-to-evals.md](/research/03-measurement-evaluation/miller-adding-error-bars-to-evals.md)). **A comparison on a small set frequently cannot distinguish the two states at all**, so the correct move is to decide in advance what difference the instrument could have detected. And **where items arrive in related groups rather than independently, an uncorrected interval is too narrow**, which makes a measurement look more precise than it is — the normal condition of estate findings, which cluster by site, by team and by template.

**Your sealed prediction should therefore contain a dimension on which you expect no detectable difference**, and saying so in advance is worth more than any dimension you expect to win.

**Its limits:** analytical rather than empirical; illustrative magnitudes rest on stylised assumptions; and **statistical validity says nothing about construct validity — a tight interval around a meaningless dimension is still meaningless.** No figure from it is reproduced.

### Combining a small trusted sample with a large untrusted one, and the conditions that make it legal

There is a formal answer to the shape you are in — a large corpus of machine-produced judgements and a budget to check only a few of them by hand — and it is worth knowing because the informal answers are both wrong.

The naive move treats the model's judgements as labels and computes over everything: fast, and invalid, because any systematic error in the model propagates straight into the estimate. The conservative move discards the model's output and uses only the hand-checked subset: valid, and wasteful. **Prediction-powered inference resolves the trade by estimating the model's systematic discrepancy from the hand-checked sample and correcting the large-sample estimate with it**, propagating the correction's own uncertainty into the interval ([research/03-measurement-evaluation/angelopoulos-prediction-powered-inference.md](/research/03-measurement-evaluation/angelopoulos-prediction-powered-inference.md)).

The property that makes it usable is that validity does not depend on the model being good: the framework is stated as making **"no assumptions on the machine-learning algorithm that supplies the predictions"**, and model quality buys narrower intervals rather than correct ones. **A bad model gives you wide intervals, not wrong ones.**

**Its conditions are strict and every one of them is a way this can be misused.** The hand-checked labels must actually be gold standard — the method corrects the model's error against the labels and **cannot correct error in the labels themselves.** The checked subset must be a valid sample of the same population as the unchecked one; **convenience-sampled labels break the guarantee silently.** Sample-size requirements do not vanish. And it says nothing about whether the quantity you are estimating is worth estimating.

**One further limit is specific to this repository and must travel with the citation.** The note behind this material was verified **at abstract level only**: the section structure is reconstructed rather than transcribed, the term used here for the correction was not confirmed line by line as the authors' own, and **no coverage rate, interval width, sample size or per-domain result is recorded or asserted.** Use the shape. Do not quote the paper.

### Attaching counter-evidence has to be safe, or it will not happen

The occasion asks a learner to lodge, in writing, the evidence that argues against their own claim — and to do so before an execution that may embarrass them, in front of assessors, with no opportunity to revise.

**That is a voice behaviour before it is an analytical one.** The construct is the belief that the work environment is safe for interpersonal risk taking, and it is emphatically **not** niceness: the source is explicit that psychological safety "is about candor, about making it possible for productive disagreement and free exchange of ideas", that it "does not imply ease or comfort", and that it is not an environment where people are not expected to meet high standards ([research/04-professional-formation/edmondson-psychological-safety.md](/research/04-professional-formation/edmondson-psychological-safety.md)). Safety and standards are orthogonal, and the dangerous common case is high standards with low safety.

The mechanism is a calculation rather than a disposition: the cost of speaking is immediate and certain, the benefit is deferred and uncertain, so **silence is rational under the payoff structure** and moralising about it does not work. The lever is the response — the true test is what happens when somebody does speak up.

**The consequence for this occasion is uncomfortable and is stated rather than solved.** If attaching counter-evidence is conditional on safety, then assessing it is partly assessing the room. The design's only available answer is structural: the counter-evidence must be in the *lodged* artefact rather than produced under questioning, so that it is written before the room exists.

**Its limits:** the leader's tool kit in that source is drawn partly from consulting experience and **is offered without evaluation**; the theoretically load-bearing findings sit in an earlier paper this repository has not read; and **no magnitude from it crosses into this wiki**, which is a namespace rule rather than a verification one.

### Why the judgement is a narrative and not a score

The last piece is about the assessment itself, and it has the best warrant in the session.

Assessors reading accumulated evidence **"mainly relied on narrative feedback to inform their judgment… Grades and qualifications were merely used to confirm impressions based on narratives"** ([research/03-measurement-evaluation/govaerts-narrative-assessment.md](/research/03-measurement-evaluation/govaerts-narrative-assessment.md)). The numbers were not the evidence; they were a check on a conclusion already formed from the prose.

Two findings from the same work shape this occasion's rules directly. **Assessors reaching similar final judgements differed substantially in their reasoning** — same outcome, different route — which is why more than one assessor records level *and* narrative independently before any discussion, and why divergence is investigated rather than averaged. And **assessors "were inclined to stick to their initial judgments even when confronted with seemingly disconfirming evidence"**, with final judgements more elaborate but not substantially different from initial ones. That is anchoring, in the people doing the assessing, and it is the reason the lodged prediction is read aloud by somebody else before the learner speaks.

**Its limits:** built on two open-access studies deliberately substituted for several paywalled works that were not consulted; the studies are in a different professional domain; and the note records the substitution rather than concealing it.

### An entrustment decision is about an activity, not about a person

The frame the whole occasion sits in comes from the entrustment literature, and its discipline is what stops the judgement becoming a rating.

An entrustable professional activity is a bounded unit of practice with a beginning and an end that a supervisor could delegate whole; entrustment is expressed as **an ordinal supervision level rather than a score**; and a summative decision "should include an agreement between multiple observers that this entrustment is justified, to avoid subjective bias, and multiple observations" ([research/08-assessment-epas/ten-cate-epa-framework.md](/research/08-assessment-epas/ten-cate-epa-framework.md)).

Three of its details are used here directly. **The activity's description must state its limitations** — what is and is not covered — which is why the claim's scope is narrower than the engagement. **The probing conversation must include what-if questions**, because retrospective explanation shows only that the learner understood the case they met and counterfactual questioning is the only evidence about the cases they did not. And **entrustment can expire**: a decision made about somebody who has not practised the activity for a long time may lapse. **A supervision level is a judgement with a date on it, which is the same discipline this module has been applying to claims.**

**Its limits:** a conceptual primer by the concept's originator, synthesising rather than evaluating; its empirical items are reported from prior studies rather than generated; **and whether the machinery works on this cohort, in this domain, is untested.**

## The workflow

**Assemble one claim about one named capability from three instruments, attach its counter-evidence, and seal a prediction before anybody executes it.**

You are assembling, not rebuilding. The re-derivation procedure is [S081](/wiki/seminars/S081.md)'s, the adoption position is [S082](/wiki/seminars/S082.md)'s, the drift register is [S083](/wiki/seminars/S083.md)'s and the iteration record is [S084](/wiki/seminars/S084.md)'s.

**Step 1 — name one capability, and make the sentence narrow enough to be wrong.** Not *our AI governance improved*. Something of the form: *the retrieval path serving the finance workspace enforces the caller's authorisation at query time, and did not at the start of the engagement.* **A claim about everything is a claim about nothing.**

**Step 2 — pull the posture evidence and write its bounds above it.** The four infrastructure-as-code checks per serving endpoint; the discovery inventory against your own list; the data risk assessment findings on the sites backing the indexes in scope. **Then the three resolution properties — scan scope, cadence, latency — at the top, before any finding.**

**Step 3 — pull the telemetry and say what it cannot say.** Per-group traffic across the interval, with the unattributed share reported as a headline. **Then one sentence: this establishes traffic under an identity and does not establish that anybody was helped.**

**Step 4 — pull the evaluation results and record the silence.** Scores, thresholds, and the fact that **no agreement rate with human judgement is published for any evaluator here.** If you hand-checked a subset, say how it was sampled and whether it is a valid sample of the same population as the rest — **because a convenience-sampled subset breaks the correction silently, and silently is the problem.**

**Step 5 — go looking for the counter-evidence, deliberately, before you assemble.** Set a timer. Search each instrument for the finding that damages your claim most: an endpoint missing a check, a group whose traffic fell to zero, a dimension where the difference is smaller than the instrument could detect. **A search that returns nothing means the search was not competent**, not that the claim is clean.

**Step 6 — write the claim file, and lodge it.**

```yaml
# maturity-claim.lodged.yaml  — SHAPE, NOT SCHEMA.
# Lodged BEFORE execution. Not revised afterwards. The reconciliation is a
# separate document. Every product name, blade and policy below is a fact
# about ONE date — re-derive, never copy.

claim:
  capability: >
    <one sentence, narrow enough to be false>
  scope: "<narrower than the engagement; name what is excluded>"
  interval: "<from, to>"
  asserted_on: "<YYYY-MM-DD>"
  causation_owner: "<named person responsible for establishing that the work
                     caused the change>"          # never 'the team'

evidence:
  - instrument: "posture findings"
    what_it_establishes: "configuration present or absent, on sampled scope"
    silence: "no detection rate and no false-positive rate is published"
    resolution: { scan_scope: "<bounded subset>", cadence: "<periodic>",
                  latency: "<days; results expire>" }
    findings: ["<...>"]
  - instrument: "gateway telemetry"
    what_it_establishes: "a request was made under an identity"
    silence: "does NOT establish that anybody read, kept or was helped by the output"
    unattributed_share: "<report as a headline, not a footnote>"
    findings: ["<...>"]
  - instrument: "evaluation results"
    what_it_establishes: "a score against a threshold"
    silence: "no agreement rate with human judgement is published for any evaluator"
    hand_checked_subset:
      sampled_how: "<must be a valid sample of the SAME population>"
      gold_standard: "<the correction cannot fix error in the labels themselves>"
    findings: ["<...>"]

union_of_silences: >
  <what NONE of the three instruments can tell you. This bounds the claim.>

counter_evidence:               # searched for deliberately, on a timer
  - finding: "<the one that damages the claim most>"
    instrument: "<which>"
    why_i_made_the_claim_anyway: "<in plain words>"
  - finding: "<second>"
search_for_counter_evidence:
  looked_for: ["endpoint missing a check", "group whose traffic fell to zero",
               "dimension where difference < detectable", "item regenerating forever"]
  outcome: "<what the search returned>"
  note: "A search returning nothing means the search was not competent."

sealed_prediction:              # per dimension, before anyone executes
  - dimension: "<name>"
    executor_will_reach: "<position>"
    they_will_inspect: "<artefact>"
  - dimension: "<name>"
    executor_will_fail_to_reach: true
    because: "<the reason — a procedure with no predicted failure has not been run>"
  - dimension: "<name>"
    no_detectable_difference_expected: true
    detectable_difference_decided_in_advance: "<what would have counted>"
```

**Step 7 — use the agent to attack your own claim, not to assemble it.** One prompt: *argue that this capability claim is false, that my counter-evidence section omits the strongest available objection, and that at least one of my three instruments does not establish what I have said it establishes.* Read it for the second clause. **Add whatever it finds to the counter-evidence section rather than to a rebuttal**, keep what is better than your own reasoning, and record what you rejected. **Record also that this instrument shares a model family with the evaluation tooling in your evidence and is therefore not independent of it** — an agent that endorsed your claim was not used as an instrument.

**Step 8 — lodge, and then write nothing.** Hand the claim, the procedure and the sealed prediction to the executor. **The interval while they work is deliberately empty**, because the temptation this occasion exists to remove is the one where an author walks a re-deriver through their own reasoning and calls the result a re-derivation.

**What this makes observable that prose cannot.** Whether the procedure survives its author's absence.

Everything else in this module can be produced by somebody arguing well. **This cannot.** Within minutes of the executor starting, it is visible whether the steps are executable by a stranger, and the failure is specific rather than general: they stop at a particular line, for a particular reason, and that line is usually an imperative with no object. **A learner who has watched somebody else stop at their own instruction does not write that instruction again.**

## What good looks like

- **The procedure is executable by somebody who does not know your engagement.** That is the single property the occasion is arranged to test, and it is visible within minutes.
- The prediction is specific enough to be wrong. *They will find it difficult* is not a prediction; *they will reach a lower position on this dimension because the only evidence is a document we wrote* is.
- The claim's scope is narrower than the engagement. A claim about everything is a claim about nothing, and the dimensions it cannot reach are already named.
- **Divergences are diagnosed, not resolved.** Deciding you were right is a conclusion; saying which of the three causes produced the difference is the work.
- Where the executor reached a higher position than you predicted, that is examined as carefully as the other direction. **Understating a claim and overstating it are the same defect**, and a learner who only guards against one has a habit rather than a method.
- The evidence against your own claim is in your lodged answers, not produced under questioning.

## How it goes wrong

- **The procedure that only its author can run** — every step is executable if you already know the answer. *Warning sign:* a step in your procedure begins with the word *check*.
- **The prediction adjusted** — the reconciliation quietly restates what you predicted so that it matches. *Warning sign:* your account of your prediction is more nuanced than your prediction.
- **Divergence read as executor error** — every difference is attributed to the person who ran it. *Warning sign:* your reconciliation contains no case in which you were wrong.
- **The unfalsifiable claim** — the claim is stated so that no execution could disturb it. *Warning sign:* you predicted no failures.
- **The safe understatement** — the claim is pitched low enough to be certain, and the occasion becomes about nothing. *Warning sign:* your claim would have been true before the engagement started.
- **The caveat mistaken for counter-evidence** — the document softens rather than exposing. *Warning sign:* your counter-evidence section contains no artefact, no date and nothing a stranger could check.
- **The instruments treated as covering each other** — three sources are cited and their silences are never stated. *Warning sign:* your document has no sentence beginning *none of these can tell you*.
- **Traffic reported as value** — a token count is offered as evidence that the capability helped somebody. *Warning sign:* your adoption evidence is a volume.
- **The convenience-sampled check** — a hand-checked subset was chosen because it was easy to reach. *Warning sign:* you cannot say how the subset was sampled, and you assumed that made no difference.
- **Noise reported as movement** — a dimension improved and the difference is smaller than the instrument could have detected. *Warning sign:* you did not decide in advance what would have counted.

## Exercises

**The exercises below are not assessed. The lodged artefacts, the execution and the reconciliation are the occasion, and they are assessed as described in the next section.** Work these before you lodge.

**1 · Sort six statements into caveat, counter-evidence and neither.** For each, say what a reader could do with it.

- *These findings should be read with appropriate care.* · *Two of the seven serving endpoints do not have a private endpoint configured, as of the assessment that ran on this date.* · *The picture is broadly positive.* · *The second site's dimensioned traffic fell to zero four weeks ago and has not returned.* · *Results may vary depending on context.* · *The dimension where I claim most improvement shows a difference smaller than what this instrument could have detected.*

> **Model answers.** The second, fourth and sixth are **counter-evidence**: each names an artefact, a moment and a finding that argues against the claim, and each is checkable. The first, third and fifth are **caveats**: they soften and protect, and no observation could contradict them. **The grammatical test is quick — a caveat cannot be checked and counter-evidence can.** The sixth is the most valuable of the three and the least likely to be written, because it concedes on the dimension the author cares about most.

**2 · Assemble and state the union of the silences.** Using the workflow, pull all three instruments and write the sentence beginning *none of these can tell you…*

> **What you should find.** That the three silences do not overlap and therefore do not cover each other: posture is silent on accuracy, telemetry is silent on whether anybody was helped, evaluation is silent on agreement with a person. **A claim assembled from all three is bounded by the union, not the intersection**, and a document that never states the union has quietly assumed the opposite.

**3 · Go back to your own item 3, then break it.** Find what you wrote for opening item 3 — which dimension the executor will fail to reach, and why. **Do not rewrite it yet.** Put it beside the criterion below and write one sentence naming the difference. Then write the honest version, and lodge that one.

> **Criterion.** A pass names **the step at which the executor will stop and the reason the step is unexecutable by somebody without your context** — no artefact exists; the artefact is one your team wrote; the evidence lives outside the procedure's reach; the instruction assumes knowledge you did not write down. **A pass is available only by naming a step and a reason.** *"They will find dimension four difficult"* fails, because it predicts a feeling. *"They will stop at step three because it says to check the configuration and does not say where"* passes. **A procedure with no predicted failure is a procedure its author has not run.**
>
> **Why the comparison step is mandatory rather than encouraged.** The research this session's structure rests on tested exactly this: showing learners a correct answer next to their own wrong one produced no benefit, and only the group *instructed to compare them* learned more ([research/04-professional-formation/sinha-kapur-productive-failure.md](/research/04-professional-formation/sinha-kapur-productive-failure.md)). Reading the criterion is not the exercise. Naming the difference between it and what you wrote is.

**4 · Run the counter-evidence search on a timer.** Twenty minutes, three instruments, one instruction: find the finding that damages your claim most.

> **What to notice.** How much harder it is to search *against* yourself than *for* yourself, and that the difficulty is procedural rather than emotional — you know where the supporting evidence lives because you assembled it, and you have to construct the query for the opposing evidence from scratch. **A search that returned nothing means the search was not competent**, and the honest record of that is *I looked here, here and here and found nothing*, not *there is nothing*.

**5 · Predict a null.** Choose one dimension on which you expect the executor to find no detectable difference, and write what difference would have counted, before you look.

> **Scoring criterion.** A pass states the detectable difference in advance and in terms of the instrument. A fail predicts a null without saying what would have counted, which is unfalsifiable in the flattering direction. **A prediction of no difference, lodged in advance, is worth more than any dimension you expect to win**, because it is the only part of the sealed prediction that cannot be read as advocacy.

**6 · Write the sentence you would rather not lodge.** From your own evidence, take the finding that most damages your claim and write it as the first line of the counter-evidence section, with the plain-words reason you made the claim anyway.

> **What to notice.** That the second half is harder than the first. Naming the damaging finding is a search problem; **saying why you made the claim anyway is a judgement you have to own in front of people**, which is why the occasion requires it in the lodged document rather than under questioning. A claim whose author can state its strongest objection and their reason for proceeding is the most a reader can be given in a field with no calibrated instrument.

## Show it

The reconciliation is short and live. Your lodged prediction is read out first, by somebody else, before you speak.

You will be asked one question at the end, whatever else is asked: **which dimension of your claim is now weaker than you thought, and what would it take to strengthen it?**

## Assessment

**This is an entrustment decision, not a specification check.** There is no checklist here and nothing in this section is one. What is recorded is an ordinal supervision level with a mandatory narrative, against a single judgement:

> **Can this learner be trusted to say what a claim about improvement actually rests on?**

**How the level is formed.** From accumulated observation across [S081](/wiki/seminars/S081.md)–[S084](/wiki/seminars/S084.md), by more than one assessor, on more than one occasion, with each assessor recording level and narrative independently before any discussion. The lodged artefacts and the execution supply the evidence; the reconciliation tests it. Divergence between assessors of more than one sub-level is investigated rather than averaged. See [Assessment Architecture](/wiki/program/03-Assessment-Architecture.md) for the scale and the rater rules.

**What the assessors are reading for**, none of which is a threshold to be met.

- Whether the procedure was executable by a stranger, and whether the learner had predicted where it would not be.
- Whether the sealed prediction was honest — specific, falsifiable, and containing at least one expectation that reflected badly on the learner's own claim.
- Whether divergences were diagnosed by cause rather than resolved by authority, and whether the learner could distinguish absent evidence from a bad rule.
- Whether overstatement and understatement were treated with equal suspicion.
- Whether the claim named who is responsible for establishing causation, and held that position under questioning.

**A narrative is mandatory and names a specific observed action.** A level recorded without one is rejected by moderation rather than by the assessor.

**What may not enter the judgement.** The executor's competence. A procedure defeated by a careless execution and a procedure defeated by its own defects look similar from outside, and the assessors resolve that by reading the reconciliation rather than by scoring the execution. **Nor may the executor's own level be affected by what they found**, in either direction; an executor who reports being unable to proceed has produced the occasion's most useful observation, and a programme that penalises that report will not receive it again.

## What this does not establish

**That the learner's claim is true.** A re-derivable claim is one whose provenance can be inspected. Whether the thing it describes actually improved is a question no source behind this programme answers for any system it describes.

**And nothing here validates the instrument.** No calibration of any maturity instrument against human judgement exists anywhere in this material — no gold standard, no held-out sample, no inter-rater statistic, no record that any produced position was ever compared with an expert's reading of the same system. The scales, their boundaries and their weights are asserted constants presented in the visual language of measurement. **This occasion tests whether a learner can re-derive a position on a scale nobody has shown to mean anything.** That is worth doing and it is less than it sounds.

**No staged maturity model is asserted at this occasion or anywhere in this module**, and no source available to this programme accompanies such a model with evidence that progression through its stages produces better outcomes. **A claim about one named capability, with its evidence and its counter-evidence, is what replaces it** — and that replacement is this design's argument rather than a finding.

**The platform instruments are silent in three different directions and none covers the others.** Posture assessment publishes no detection rate and no false-positive rate, samples the estate rather than covering it, and reports after a delay. Gateway telemetry establishes traffic under an identity and establishes nothing about whether anybody was helped. **The evaluation surface publishes no agreement rate with human judgement for any evaluator, no calibration data and no threshold rationale** — assert none, here or anywhere. **Their union bounds the claim; their intersection does not.**

**The statistical machinery is cited at abstract level and must not be quoted.** The correction that combines a small trusted sample with a large untrusted one is used here for its shape and its conditions only: the note behind it was verified from the abstract, its section structure is reconstructed, the term used for the correction was not confirmed as the authors' own, and **no coverage rate, interval width or sample size from it is recorded or asserted.**

**The safety condition is real and this occasion does not remove it.** Attaching counter-evidence is a voice behaviour, and voice behaviour is conditional on the room. **Assessing it is therefore partly assessing the room**, and the design's only answer is to require the counter-evidence in the lodged artefact rather than under questioning — which is a mitigation and not a solution. The source for that construct offers its leader's tool kit without evaluation, and its theoretically load-bearing findings sit in an earlier paper this repository has not read.

**The entrustment machinery is the best-warranted apparatus in the programme and the least tested here.** The primer behind it is conceptual guidance by the concept's originator, synthesising prior work rather than evaluating this use of it, from a different professional domain. **Whether the machinery works on this cohort is untested**, and this repository's own assessment architecture says so.

**The simulation limits are the same as everywhere in this programme and are not reduced by the occasion's structure.** The estate is fictional, the executor is a peer with no stake, the claim carries no commercial consequence, and nobody's next engagement depends on the answer. **Every pressure that makes an honest maturity claim difficult in practice is absent.**
