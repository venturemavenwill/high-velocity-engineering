## In plain terms

Every paper in this wiki ends by sorting its claims into four classes. This session applies the same apparatus to an engagement.

The reason is that engagements produce accounts of themselves, and those accounts travel further and last longer than any artefact. Somebody who was not present is scoping the next piece of work now, and what they will have is a story. **Nobody is going to ask for a register of what was not established, and its absence will not be noticed by anybody.**

The failure this guards against is not lying. It is the ordinary drift by which activity becomes outcome. **The corpus behind this programme is the clearest available specimen, and it is a specimen because it is good rather than because it is sloppy.** Its organising thesis is excellent — everything short of realised benefit is activity, including the team, the architecture, the fundamentals and the deployment — and it is put to an engineering audience deliberately, because it is uncomfortable. Then almost every figure the corpus offers in its own support measures activity: elapsed time, headcount, proportions of code generated, minutes to add a feature, backlog readiness. It devotes space to externally published measurement frameworks and reports no measurement from any of them. **It fails its own test in the same documents that state it, in the same register, without noticing.**

That is what happens to a practice that never writes down what it did not establish. This session is the countermeasure, and there is no evidence that countermeasures of this kind work.

## Abstract

This paper defends closing an engagement with a register of its limits, sorted into the same four evidence classes this programme applies to its own papers. The argument runs in four parts. **First**, the account of an engagement outlives and outtravels its artefacts, and is produced under conditions that select for units of activity. **Second**, the drift from activity to outcome is not a lapse but the default, and the corpus behind this programme is the clearest available specimen precisely because it is good: an excellent organising thesis, supported almost entirely by activity measures, in the same documents, without noticing. **Third**, the class that carries the value is the fourth, because what documentation omits is what the next team will assume — and the platform this programme teaches supplies a complete, precise evaluator catalogue that publishes no agreement with human judgement, no calibration data and no threshold rationale for any evaluator it ships. **Fourth**, the discipline is not distributed evenly across organisations, because writing down what you did not establish is a voice behaviour, and voice behaviour is conditional on whether admitting error is survivable.

The session produces a limits register with two mandatory entries — the scope exclusions and at least one result undermining the learner's own position — and requires each unestablished claim to name who bears the cost of its being assumed. **It then requires the same instrument to be turned on this programme**, whose closing concessions are stated in §10 and are not gated. The paper concedes that no source behind this programme produces such a register; that the apparatus is this repository's own turned outward; that the register's costs and benefits fall on different people; and that the session's most likely failure is a register honest about everything except the things that matter.

## 1. The problem this session must solve

**An account is the most portable artefact an engagement produces and the one with the least evidence attached.**

It is also produced under conditions that select for a particular shape. Everyone involved is tired of the subject. The people who would raise objections have moved on. The commercial function needs something to say. And the natural unit of a summary is what was done, which is precisely the unit the corpus's own thesis calls activity.

Four specific corruptions are worth naming because they are committed by capable people.

**Frameworks as furniture.** A recognised measurement approach is named, its authority is borrowed, and no measurement from it is ever reported. The approach functions as credibility rather than as an instrument, in an account whose whole claim is that measurement is what counts.

**Specimens that become norms.** Illustrative figures — the kind that appear inside example answers, sample dashboards or template thresholds — are unattributed, plausible, and read as scales. **An engineer who has absorbed them will hear a real customer's number as high or low against something nobody validated.** These are the items to quarantine most aggressively, because they do their damage without ever being asserted.

**The unfalsifiable account.** Where every disappointing result is explained by the customer's readiness, and the readiness rubric is composed of customer dispositions, the evidence has been arranged so that a poor outcome confirms the thesis. **Nobody arranged it. It assembles itself out of reasonable individual moves**, which is why it needs a countermeasure rather than an accusation.

**Unstated exclusions.** A method that never says what it does not cover invites every reader to assume it covers everything, and the assumption is made silently and immediately.

## 3. Theoretical basis

**3.1 Load profile.** Intrinsic load is low and the affective load is the highest in the stage. The concepts are four and mutually exclusive; the work is admitting things about work the learner did. **The design spends its surplus on the mandatory undermining result and buys that by supplying the four classes rather than asking learners to derive a taxonomy.** A design judgement, no external warrant.

**3.2 Why four classes rather than a confidence scale.** A scale invites a position in the middle and produces no obligation. The classes differ in what they license and in what would refute them: a verified claim names an artefact, a cited claim licenses direction and mechanism only, a design claim is an argument that can be argued with, and a vendor claim carries a version and a silence. **A claim that could sit in two classes is two claims**, and separating them is where the work is.

**3.3 Why no magnitude survives the second class.** General knowledge licenses direction and mechanism. It does not license a proportion, a multiple, a range or a rate, and a magnitude carried into an account from a general belief acquires the account's authority while retaining none of its own warrant. **This rule is absolute in this repository and it is absolute here.**

The statistical literature supplies the sharper version of the same rule. Evaluation results are reported with a highest-number-is-best mentality in which a leading figure is highlighted **"but not necessarily to test that result for any kind of statistical significance"** ([research/03-measurement-evaluation/miller-adding-error-bars-to-evals.md](/research/03-measurement-evaluation/miller-adding-error-bars-to-evals.md)). Two consequences bind the register. **A comparison on a small set frequently cannot distinguish two systems at all**, and the correct response is to decide in advance what difference the instrument could have detected. And **where items arrive in related groups, an uncorrected interval is too narrow**, making a measurement look more precise than it is. **An apparatus that emits a number and no interval has not told you whether it found anything.**

**Its limits:** analytical rather than empirical, with most illustrative magnitudes resting on stylised assumptions; valid only where the items genuinely sample the population you care about; and **statistical validity says nothing about construct validity — a tight interval around a meaningless metric is still meaningless.** **No figure from it is reproduced here.**

**3.4 Why the vendor class records a silence, and the worked example that proves it.** What documentation omits is what the next team will assume. A version without a silence is a citation; a version with one is a warning.

The evaluation documentation for this platform is the cleanest available demonstration ([research/06-microsoft-platform/agent-framework-and-evaluations-docs.md](/research/06-microsoft-platform/agent-framework-and-evaluations-docs.md)). It is precise about what the tooling computes — three core types, two evaluator tiers, three split strategies, repetitions to detect non-deterministic behaviour, a four-category catalogue, pass-or-fail output carrying a `reason`, a `threshold` and a `passed` field — and it states the load-bearing warning explicitly: **"How you split determines *what you're evaluating*."** **And it publishes no agreement rate with human judgement for any evaluator, no calibration data and no rationale for any default threshold**, while most of those evaluators are model-judged. **Assert no agreement figure for any of them, in this paper or anywhere.**

The same shape appears one layer further out. A first-party engineering blog describes a **"golden dataset"** of questions, approved answers and best source articles, and before-and-after tests on indexing changes — **and publishes no result from any of it** ([research/06-microsoft-platform/learn-mcp-server-docs.md](/research/06-microsoft-platform/learn-mcp-server-docs.md)). **The apparatus of measurement is described in detail and its outputs are withheld.** The general-availability announcement's stated basis is that usage **"grew without issues"**, which is an availability-and-adoption argument that says nothing about whether the answers were right.

**Both sources' limits:** first-party throughout, definitional in authority and evidential nowhere. The evaluation pages were read through search excerpts rather than full fetches, most evaluators are marked preview, and one consulted page is an explicitly superseded variant. The Learn family is extremely perishable, had collapsed sections that were not read, and **carries outcome claims in its associated repository that are marketing and are prohibited in this wiki in every form.**

**3.5 Why the undermining result is mandatory, and why the requirement has to be compulsory.** A register composed of limits the author had already priced in is a document about the author's comfort. The requirement is a structural countermeasure to a motivational problem, and it works only because it is compulsory — an invitation to include one would be declined by exactly the people who most need to.

The organisational-behaviour literature explains why. Recording what you did not establish is speaking up when silence is cheaper, which is the construct Edmondson studies and Frazier synthesises ([research/04-professional-formation/edmondson-psychological-safety.md](/research/04-professional-formation/edmondson-psychological-safety.md); [research/04-professional-formation/frazier-psychological-safety-meta-analysis.md](/research/04-professional-formation/frazier-psychological-safety-meta-analysis.md)). Edmondson's mechanism is a quick, not entirely conscious risk calculation involving **discounting the future** — underweighting the consequence that plays out slowly and overweighting the response that arrives immediately. **The cost of the undermining result is immediate and lands on its author; the cost of its absence is delayed and lands on somebody they will never meet.**

**The consequence for this design is uncomfortable and is stated rather than solved.** If the disposition is conditional on the environment, then a session that installs it and says nothing about where it is survivable has given a learner a capability they may not be able to exercise. That is the same concession [WP-001](/wiki/whitepapers/WP-001.md) makes about refusal, arriving ninety sessions later about honesty, and this paper does not resolve it either.

**Their limits:** the Edmondson note covers a 2019 trade book read in full and a 1999 paper that was **not read**, with case studies almost entirely secondary; the Frazier note is written from the official abstract of a closed-access meta-analysis. **Direction and mechanism only; no magnitude from either appears here.**

**3.6 Why an externally borrowed number does not fill the gap.** The figure a learner reaches for to compensate for a partial baseline carries its own provenance problem, one layer further away and harder to inspect. An audit of the de facto public ranking of frontier models found scores distorted **by undisclosed platform policy rather than by any technical flaw**, producing **"biased Arena scores due to selective disclosure of performance results"** ([research/03-measurement-evaluation/singh-leaderboard-illusion.md](/research/03-measurement-evaluation/singh-leaderboard-illusion.md)). **Its allocation figures are external estimates by its own account, its findings are specific to one platform at one time, and no figure from it is reproduced here.**

**3.7 Why each entry names who bears the cost.** A limit with no bearer reads as modesty. A limit whose bearer is the next delivery team, or the operator's back-office team leader, or the assessor, is a professional communication. **This is the attribution requirement from [S070](/wiki/seminars/S070.md) applied to an absence rather than to a number.**

**3.8 The control estate, where the coverage-versus-efficacy structure is at its largest and its most consequential.**

§§3.2 to 3.4 argue the four classes from two specimens: an evaluation catalogue and a documentation service. **Both are small objects. The class-4 discipline earns its place on a larger one — the set of controls a closing report names when it says an estate is governed.**

Read five platform documentation families for those controls and one structure recurs without exception. **Each states, frequently with unusual candour, exactly what it covers. None states how well it works.** The enumeration, drawn from [research/06-microsoft-platform/agent-365-and-agent-identity.md](/research/06-microsoft-platform/agent-365-and-agent-identity.md), [research/06-microsoft-platform/foundry-control-plane-and-governance.md](/research/06-microsoft-platform/foundry-control-plane-and-governance.md), [research/06-microsoft-platform/foundry-agent-build-and-runtime.md](/research/06-microsoft-platform/foundry-agent-build-and-runtime.md) and [research/06-microsoft-platform/microsoft-iq-intelligence-layer.md](/research/06-microsoft-platform/microsoft-iq-intelligence-layer.md), is: **no detection rate, false-positive rate, precision or recall for any agent threat detection; no measure of registry coverage; no discovery latency; no measure of tracing completeness; no evaluator agreement with human judgement; no before-and-after incident data, availability target or service-level commitment; no published record that any stated notice period was honoured; and no statement of what falls outside every discovery mechanism at once.**

**The conclusion §4 requires a learner to write, in these words, is that the estate is instrumented and is not proven safe.**

**Three things keep that from being a complaint, and all three are load-bearing.**

**First, the coverage boundaries are genuinely good and are the reliable content.** These sources state which agents a real-time control does not cover, which interaction types a data-loss control omits, that an agent is not informed when it is blocked, that generated content does not inherit its source's protection, that a synchronisation is manual, that a dashboard lags its source, that tracing is off until enabled, and that a governance console has no programmatic interface. **A vendor publishing its own blind spots has done something most do not**, and §4 requires those boundaries to be quoted rather than paraphrased, because paraphrase loses the precision that makes them usable.

**Second, the gap is structural rather than concealment.** Definitional authority is what product documentation has; evidential authority is not what it is for. **Recognising the genre is the skill**, and it generalises: a practitioner who can read one of these families correctly can read most vendor security documentation correctly, because the shape recurs.

**Third, and this is why §4 teaches a question set rather than the list, the list is not stable.** Any absence in it may be published tomorrow, at which point the correct act is to move the item into class 1 with its page and date and report the correction. **What is durable is the five-question form** — what does it cover, what does it explicitly not cover, at what latency, over what population, with what measured effect. **Four are answerable from documentation and the fifth almost never is**, and a closing report that answers four and lets the fifth be inferred is precisely the drift §3.3 describes, committed by people who could recite the difference.

**And the limit that binds this section hardest.** Every absence enumerated above is **an absence in what was read, on a date**, and substantial portions of several of these families were read as search-returned excerpts rather than full page fetches. **An absence is evidence only if the search was competent** — this paper's own rule, applied to this paper's own material — which is why §4 requires the register to record where the learner looked and at what depth, and why a published measurement discovered later falsifies this section on that point rather than embarrassing it.

## 4. Session design and its warrant

**The session starts from the account, not from the artefacts.** The learner writes the three sentences somebody on their side would say about the engagement in a room, and sorts those. Working forwards from artefacts produces a register of things nobody was going to claim; working backwards from the account puts the classification exactly where the flattering claims are.

**The baseline is constructed to be partial and late**, and its treatment is the session's hardest requirement: the learner must write, in a form a scoping conversation could quote, what the baseline permits anybody to say. **The alternative is that somebody quotes the number and drops the qualification**, which is the ordinary mechanism by which a limitation becomes a benchmark.

**The exclusions are asked as three questions rather than one** — what did nobody look at, whose interests were never represented, what would have shown up — because a single question about scope produces a paragraph about scope and the three produce a list.

**The closing question is fixed:** who would be harmed if this document were never written, and would they know? The second half is the one that matters. **Most of the harm from an unwritten limits register is invisible to the person harmed**, which is why the document has to be produced by somebody with no incentive to produce it.

## 5. Predicted failure modes and countermeasures

**Activity reported as outcome.** *Countermeasure:* the account-first ordering, and the class definitions, which have no home for a delivered artefact as an outcome.

**The framework as furniture.** *Countermeasure:* the register requires the result from any approach named, and a named approach with no result becomes an entry in the second class or is struck.

**The specimen that became a norm.** *Countermeasure:* partial. The session names the mechanism, and a learner who has already internalised an illustrative figure cannot easily notice it. **This is the failure mode most likely to survive the session.**

**The unfalsifiable account.** *Countermeasure:* the requirement to state what result would have made the learner conclude the work had not succeeded, which the corpus's own question bank never asks anybody.

**The comfortable register.** *Countermeasure:* the mandatory undermining result, and the assessment position that its absence is the finding.

## 7. Assessment and evidence generated

No specification item and no entrustment level. This session generates observation for the entrustment decision at [S090](/wiki/seminars/S090.md), and it is the strongest single input to that occasion.

**What assessors record**, as narrative: whether the verified class was smaller than the account; whether any magnitude appeared in the cited class; whether the vendor class recorded a silence rather than only a version; whether the undermining result was genuinely undermining; whether exclusions named an interest rather than a topic; and whether each entry named a bearer.

## 8. Objections and rebuttals

**Objection 1: the register is a document nobody asked for, which will not be read, and whose only reliable effect is to damage the engagement's account of itself.**

At its strongest: the commercial function will not circulate it, the customer did not request it, the next scoping team will read the case study rather than the caveats, and the delivering organisation is competing for the next piece of work against parties who produce no such document. **The register's costs fall entirely on the party producing it and its benefits fall on somebody who will never know it existed.** A profession does not sustain a practice on those terms, and telling learners otherwise is teaching them a habit their first employer will remove.

**This is conceded in substance.** The incentive analysis is correct, and this paper cannot show that any organisation would receive such a document well. The rebuttal is narrower: **the register's first reader is the learner**, and the act of sorting an account into classes changes what the author is willing to say afterwards, whether or not anybody else reads the document. **The fix that was considered and declined** was to fold the limits into the engagement's normal closing report, where they would be read. It was rejected because a limit inside a report about success is edited towards the report's register, and the ones that survive are the ones that do not disturb it. **The objection is entitled to say that a document written for its author is a training exercise rather than a professional practice, and that this paper has just conceded as much.**

**Objection 2: the four classes are this repository's apparatus, and applying them to an engagement is exporting a documentation convention as though it were an engineering method.**

At its strongest: the classes were invented to keep a wiki honest about its sources. An engagement is not a wiki. Its claims are made to different people, for different purposes, under different obligations, and the classes' boundaries — particularly between a verified claim and a design claim — do not map cleanly onto delivery work. **A learner sorting an engagement into these boxes is performing a ritual imported from a document about documents.**

The rebuttal concedes the origin and defends the transfer narrowly. The classes are not proposed as the right taxonomy; they are proposed as **a taxonomy that forces a claim to name what would refute it**, which is the property the account of an engagement lacks. Any taxonomy with that property would do. **Whether these particular four survive contact with a real engagement is untested, and no source behind this programme reports anything comparable being attempted.**

**Objection 3: requiring learners to turn the apparatus on this programme is a rhetorical move that immunises the programme rather than testing it.**

At its strongest, and this is the objection this paper finds hardest: a design that concedes its own lack of evidence, loudly and in its own closing session, has purchased something. It has made the concession *part of the teaching*, which converts a weakness into a demonstration of rigour. **A reader who admires the honesty has been persuaded by the absence of evidence rather than despite it** — and that is a rhetorical structure, not an epistemic one. Worse, the move is unfalsifiable in the same shape the session criticises elsewhere: an objection that the programme is unevidenced has been pre-answered by the programme, so raising it confirms rather than disturbs.

**Conceded, and this paper has no rebuttal that does not exhibit the structure it is conceding.** The most that can be said is procedural. The concessions in §10 are stated as **deficiencies with named consequences** rather than as caveats — that most predictions will never be measured is written as the design's largest weakness, and §10's final paragraph names what a reader should conclude from it. **The declined fix was to remove the self-audit exercise entirely** and let the programme be judged from outside, and it was declined because a learner who can perform the four-class sort on a vendor and not on the material teaching them has learned a manner rather than a method — which is a real pedagogical cost, and is a smaller claim than the objection needs answering. **A reader who concludes that the self-audit is partly ornamental is making a defensible call, and §9's fifth prediction is written so that a cohort could show it.**

## 9. Falsifiable predictions and instrumentation

**Prediction 1.** On a first attempt the verified class will be the longest of the four in most registers. *Instrument:* the registers, with entries counted by class.

**Prediction 2.** Magnitudes will appear in the cited class in a noticeable share of registers, most often as a range rather than as a point. *Instrument:* the cited-class entries, coded for any quantity.

**Prediction 3.** Undermining results will more often concern the measurement than the system. *Instrument:* the mandatory entry, classified by subject. **If the undermining result is nearly always about the baseline, the requirement is being satisfied by the easiest available admission** and the standard needs a second clause.

**Prediction 4.** Exclusions will more often name topics than interests. *Instrument:* the exclusion list, coded topic or interest, against the three prompting questions.

**Prediction 5.** In the self-audit exercise, a majority of learners will place this repository's prediction register in the correct class — and a majority of the same learners will place at least one of their own engagement's activity measures in the verified class in the same sitting. *Instrument:* exercise 4's placement against the learner's own register, scored by the same reader in one pass. **This predicts the failure of the session's central countermeasure**, on the hypothesis that the sort is easy to perform on somebody else's material and hard on your own, which is the whole claim the session makes and the one it has never tested. **If it holds, the self-audit exercise is a comprehension check rather than a transfer instrument**, and §8's third objection is substantially correct.

**Prediction 6.** Vendor-class entries will record a version and a date far more often than a silence, and the `does_not_say` field will be the most frequently empty field in the register. *Instrument:* the vendor entries, coded for the presence of a stated absence and of a search record. **If this holds, the class that carries the most value for the next team is the one the session's design least successfully produces.**

**None of these has been measured.** No cohort has run this session, and no instrument named above has been applied to anybody. **See §10.**

## 10. What this programme did not establish

**This section is numbered because it is not an appendix.** The session requires learners to turn the four classes on their own engagement, and it would be indefensible for the programme to demand that and exempt itself. What follows is this repository's own limits register, in its own classes.

**Nothing in this programme has been measured.** Every paper in this wiki closes with falsifiable predictions, each naming an instrument, each with its coding scheme specified and its artefacts defined. There are hundreds of them. **Not one has been resolved, because no cohort has run.** The apparatus is complete and it is machinery. **It is not a result**, and a reader who mistook the existence of the prediction register for evidence about this programme's effectiveness would be making exactly the error [S068](/wiki/seminars/S068.md) and this session exist to prevent.

**Stage 1 and Stage 9 are class 3 throughout, and both are written past their source.** Stage 1 teaches qualification from a corpus that records a single refusal and no declined engagement; it infers a practice from an absence, and says so in every session. Stage 9 teaches compounding from a cycle the corpus implies in hundreds of scattered references and states as a model nowhere. **Both are arguments and neither is a finding.**

**The pedagogical design rests on studies of school and university learners.** Every study behind the generation-first pattern used in all ninety sessions is a school, university or laboratory study. **Its author states that the cycle works in corporate training and adult education and cites nothing in his own reference list for that sentence** ([research/04-professional-formation/sinha-kapur-productive-failure.md](/research/04-professional-formation/sinha-kapur-productive-failure.md)). This programme's learners are working professionals on multi-week engagements. The convergent warrant from error-management training, which is an adult workplace literature, is a partial mitigation and not a rescue. **This is the most serious methodological objection to the programme's entire teaching pattern.**

**The practice corpus behind the programme reports no declined engagement, no non-synthetic failure and no outcome data of any kind** — no exclusion criteria, no cost side, no defect or incident data, no sustainment evidence, and not one reference or citation anywhere in it.

**And the prediction this programme makes about itself: most of its own predictions will never be measured.** Not some. Most. **That is the design's largest weakness and it should be read as a weakness rather than as charming humility.** A body of work that specifies its own instruments and never applies them has built the most elaborate possible apparatus for not finding out — which is the same object as a golden dataset described in detail with no published result, and the same object as an evaluation harness that emits scores with no agreement data behind them. **This programme is a specimen of the pattern it teaches. It says so here rather than being caught at it, and saying so does not make it less of one.**

## What a learner should take from this paper

**What the design claims.** That a method which never states its exclusions is read as having none; that an account in which no result could disappoint you is not an account; and that what documentation omits is worth more to the next team than what it contains.

**What the evidence licenses.** The description of how the corpus fails its own thesis is verified as description. The published silences in the platform documentation are verified as absences. **The register, the classes and their application to an engagement are this design's own apparatus**, and there is no evidence that producing one changes anything.

**The concession worth remembering.** §8 concedes that this document's only reliable reader is its author, and that its costs and benefits fall on different people. That is an uncomfortable place to close a session about honesty, and it is where the honest reading leaves it. **And §10 above is not gated, is not an appendix, and is the reason this session exists.**

**The silences that matter, and they are the reason this session exists.** **No agreement rate with human judgement is published for any evaluator, no calibration data, and no rationale for any default threshold** — which is a significant gap given that most of those evaluators are model-judged, and it means a team can stand up a complete harness, produce a dashboard, and have no basis for believing the scores correspond to what a competent human would say. **Assert no agreement figure for any evaluator, here or anywhere.** No measure of retrieval quality is published anywhere consulted in the documentation family — no recall figure, no ranking metric, no groundedness score, **and no result from the golden dataset whose existence and use are described in detail.** No indexing lag is bounded. No rate limit value, window or retry guidance is published. **No evidence is published anywhere that grounding through that server improves answer accuracy**, and the strongest claims to that effect are marketing.

**And the general position, stated once.** **Vendor documentation across this industry describes what a product does and is silent on what it does not do**; scope exclusions, known blind spots and populations a feature was not validated against are published by almost nobody, and where a limitation is published it is usually attached to a preview status rather than to the page a practitioner reads. **Governance material published across the industry defines what must be assessed before a system is deployed and stops at deployment**, which is the silence recorded at [WP-080](/wiki/whitepapers/WP-080.md) and observed here for the third time in three stages. A register of what an engagement did not establish is, among other things, the place where a practitioner records the silences their vendors did not. If any vendor publishes a scope-exclusion statement, an evaluator agreement rate or a calibration dataset for a feature this programme uses, the relevant session acquires a stronger anchor and its paper records the version and what is still omitted.
