## In plain terms

This is the stage's second assessment occasion and it does not assess what [S045](/wiki/seminars/S045.md) assessed.

There, the boundary of the increment was marked. **Here the boundary of the candidate's understanding is marked**, and the spine is the source corpus's own standing prohibition:

**Use the tooling to make each step better, not to skip steps.**

The occasion asks which steps it made better and which it let you skip. **Both answers are expected.** A candidate with no skipped steps has not audited; a candidate with no improved steps has not delegated.

Five elements are unusual and they are the ones that matter.

**The pin is an assessed element**, and so is naming which conclusions would not survive it moving. Everything a candidate observed about their executor is conditioned on a commit, a version, a host and a model. **The pin does not preserve any of it. It makes the decay visible**, which is the only property available — the alternative is a claim about a moving object that reads as true long after it stopped being true, and which nobody can check. **A defence without a pin is not a weaker defence; it is a different kind of statement.**

**The assessor selects the region to be defended, and the candidate does not.** A prepared region measures preparation.

**Half the questioning is counterfactual.** Retrospective explanation shows the candidate understood the case they met; **what-if questioning is the only available evidence about the cases they did not**, and an entrustment decision is an acceptance of risk about exactly those.

**A causal claim may be withdrawn with its cost stated, and that scores better than asserting one you cannot establish.** *I changed the configuration and the output improved* is not a causal chain, and the honest position is usually that the increment was never produced without the change.

**Reattributing every shortfall to the host, the model or the customer is the failure this occasion marks hardest against.** It is the failure the source corpus itself institutionalises, in a prepared answer that explains disappointing delivery by the customer's own constraints. **A model whose failure explanation is always somebody else's readiness has arranged its evidence to be unfalsifiable**, and a candidate who does the same has learned the wrong thing from a stage that spent ten sessions on it.

## Abstract

This paper defends closing the build stage with an assessment of a candidate's account of a delegated build rather than of the build. The argument has four parts. That delegating the work does not delegate the defence, so the object of assessment is the delegator. That the assessable competence is knowing what you did not understand and having decided about it deliberately, rather than understanding everything, because the second standard would be failed by everybody or satisfied by nobody honestly. That a claim about a configured executor is conditioned on a pin and is unbounded without one, so the pin is an element rather than a preamble — and that pinning buys visibility of decay rather than protection from it. And that once an assessment is oral, the artefact stops being the evidence, which is what permits the specification to exclude how much of the increment was generated. The occasion installs an eight-part build defence assembled from four prior artefacts, a live identifier resolution, an assessor-selected region defended line by line with mandatory counterfactual questioning, and an improved-and-skipped ledger. It closes its specification with the statement that nothing else is assessed. The paper concedes that the occasion cannot distinguish a candidate who understood their build from one who audited it well, that no source establishes any benefit from any of the practices assembled, that the assessor is an instrument whose documented idiosyncrasy this occasion cannot afford the published remedy for, and that the pin makes the occasion's platform half obsolete on the vendor's schedule rather than the programme's.

## 1. The problem this session must solve

A candidate arrives with a working increment, part of which they did not write, and every incentive present favours presenting the increment.

**Three defences are available and all three are wrong.** *It works* — which the specification does not mark and which nothing in this stage establishes as evidence of anything. *The agent wrote that* — which answers a question about provenance that nobody asked. And *the configuration made it do this* — which is a causal claim requiring a comparison the candidate almost certainly never ran.

The deeper problem is that **the module's four sessions produce artefacts that can each be satisfied in form.** An instruction set can be written and never attached. A plan can carry identifiers nobody resolves. An audit can classify everything as explained. A register can be empty. **Each of those failures is invisible in the artefact and visible only when somebody points at a specific thing and asks.**

And there is a fourth problem that is specific to this module and is why the anchor is a citation standard rather than a product. **Every interesting thing a candidate observed during the stage is true of a configuration rather than of the world.** The instruction arrived; the check blocked; the boundary held; the mode refused. All four are properties of a commit, a version, a host and a model on a day. **A defence that states them as though they were general is making claims nobody can check, and the candidate will not notice, because the sentences read identically.**

The occasion's response is to make the pointing part of the assessment: identifiers resolved live, a region chosen by the assessor, a check inventory whose labels must state their basis, and a pin against which every claim is scoped.

## 2. Entry state and prior knowledge

**Entry state: this-programme-only.** The occasion assembles four artefacts and cannot be attempted without them: the instruction set from [S046](/wiki/seminars/S046.md), the plan from [S047](/wiki/seminars/S047.md), the comprehension audit and confirmation record from [S048](/wiki/seminars/S048.md), and the register and check inventory from [S049](/wiki/seminars/S049.md). The increment pack from [S045](/wiki/seminars/S045.md) is what the assessor holds.

The prior the occasion works against is that a defence of delegated work is a defence of the delegate. **It is a defence of the delegator**, and the specification is arranged so that no element can be satisfied by describing the tool.

## 3. Theoretical basis

**3.1 Load profile.** The stage's evaluative peak, and its difficulty is unlike [S045](/wiki/seminars/S045.md)'s. There the candidate defended a decision they made; here they account for work they did not do and remain answerable for.

**3.2 What a pin is, and why it is an element rather than a preamble.**

The instruction comes from the corpus this module is built on, stated about itself: **pin the revision, never teach it live, record commit, package version, host, model, enabled collections and date, and treat any of those changing as a re-verification trigger** ([research/06-microsoft-platform/hve-core-repository.md](/research/06-microsoft-platform/hve-core-repository.md)). Two further statements from the same note constrain what a candidate may say. **Host and model dependence is total** — every artefact is inert without a host that loads it, and **a result observed on one client and model is not a result about the corpus.** And **absence at one commit is not proof of absence in the project**, which is the limit candidates drop first when asked whether something is possible.

**So a pin is a scope declaration and not a citation format.** It says: this claim is about this thing at this moment on this equipment. **A claim without one is not more confident; it is unbounded, and an unbounded claim cannot be checked and therefore cannot be defended.** Making it an element rather than a convention is the only way to keep it from being dropped at assembly, and item 5 — what would stop being true if the pin moved — is what converts it from a header into a piece of reasoning.

**The property being bought is visibility of decay rather than protection from it.** Nothing about recording a commit slows the movement of the thing recorded. What it does is let a reader see, from the record alone, that a term has passed and a version has moved, and work out which conclusions that touches. **A candidate who leaves believing a pinned claim is a durable claim has learned the opposite of the occasion's argument**, which is why exercise 1 sorts claims into pinned and durable and expects most to be pinned.

**3.3 What a citation of an unpinned moving target looks like.** The openly published handbook is the demonstration. It is live, contributable and **has no single date**: its pages carry individual footer dates spread across several years, roughly a fifth of it is stubs or link lists, and **it contradicts itself in at least a dozen places without noticing** — its branch-naming convention differs across three pages, its trade-study template disagrees with the page introducing it, and its testing comparison marks one test type manual while three other pages assume it automated ([research/05-fde-craft/microsoft-ise-engineering-playbook.md](/research/05-fde-craft/microsoft-ise-engineering-playbook.md), pages read 2026-07-30). **None of those are errors of fact; they are the normal residue of a document written by many hands over several years with no reconciliation pass**, and the practical consequence is the rule this repository applies: cite by page and by the date the page was read, never as a whole. **It also asserts four unsupported magnitude claims, recorded here as specimens and reproduced nowhere.**

**3.4 Why provenance and comprehension are separated, and where the separation is already written down.** The same handbook contains the only account in this repository's reading of what an engineering organisation does about generated code in a commit. In the **agentic** case the agent is the recorded author and the supervising human is recorded with a co-authorship trailer; in the **assistant** case it recommends a team convention using **a deliberately different trailer key**, on the stated grounds that the co-authorship field expects an address and **that authorship should not be confused with assistance.** **That is bookkeeping, and it is useful as bookkeeping.** It records who supervised and nothing about whether anybody understood, which is precisely the distinction this occasion marks — and a candidate who offers provenance when asked for comprehension has answered a different question.

**3.5 Why knowing what you did not understand is the assessed competence.** Requiring full comprehension of a shipped increment would either be failed by everybody or satisfied by nobody honestly. **The achievable and useful standard is a deliberate decision about each region**, which is what an engineer can actually be held to and what a successor actually needs. It also has a name in the assessment literature: **humility**, defined behaviourally as the ability to observe one's own limitations and the willingness to ask for help, alongside **integrity** — truthfulness in reports to a supervisor — and **reliability** — stable, predictable, conscientious working ([research/08-assessment-epas/ten-cate-epa-framework.md](/research/08-assessment-epas/ten-cate-epa-framework.md)). **The opaque class in the audit is the humility item made into an artefact**, which is why an empty opaque class is treated here as a defect rather than as an achievement.

**3.6 Why half the questioning is counterfactual.**

The form of the conversation is taken from the entrustment literature rather than invented. An **entrustment-based discussion** is short, follows the activity, and should probe deep understanding of what was done — **but should include what-if questions**, to establish whether the candidate would know what to do in unexpected or unfamiliar situations. The reason the counterfactual half is mandatory is stated plainly and is the strongest argument available for this occasion's shape: **an entrustment decision implies the acceptance of risk, as it can never be fully predicted what the candidate will encounter.** Retrospective explanation is evidence about the cases the candidate met. **Counterfactual questioning is the only available evidence about the cases they did not**, and those are the cases the decision is actually about.

**Its limits are recorded and heavy.** It is a clinical model from a domain with patients, legally responsible supervisors and a licensure endpoint, and the transfer to engineering has to be argued rather than assumed. It is guidance from the concept's originator and **reports adoption rather than effectiveness.** **The full evidence standard it specifies — multiple trained observers producing documented, deliberated summative judgements — is expensive, and this occasion runs a reduced version and says so at §8.**

**3.7 Why an oral form permits the specification's largest exclusion.**

The specification does not mark how much of the increment was generated, and that is not leniency — it is what an oral assessment makes affordable. The clearest published statement of the mechanism comes from a long-running course that recommends its AI tooling almost without restriction and carries the weight in two clauses instead: **you are solely responsible for the correctness of the solution**, and **using code generation tools without understanding the generated code will likely create challenges when answering questions** ([research/08-assessment-epas/cmu-oral-defense-and-token-economy-patterns.md](/research/08-assessment-epas/cmu-oral-defense-and-token-economy-patterns.md)). The same course permits collaboration on practical work **including outright copying**, for one stated reason: the explanation must be the candidate's own. **Once the assessment is oral, the artefact stops being the evidence, and policing its provenance stops being necessary.**

That course also supplies the form this occasion borrows: **oral verification as a rubric item discharged in ordinary contact time within a bounded window**, rather than as a separate set-piece event — which is what makes it affordable at cohort scale. **Its limits: this is course policy documentation from an offering that has run for years, not a study of assessment. No effectiveness data is published — no grade distributions, no comparison against a prior examination-based version, and no measurement of whether oral verification detects understanding that written work misses.** It is well-tested practice, which is a real form of evidence, and it is not an evaluated intervention.

**3.8 Why the assessor selects the region, and why that is not enough.**

Candidate selection measures preparation; random selection measures the audit, which is the artefact the module produced. That much is straightforward. **What is not straightforward is that the assessor is also an instrument, and a poorly characterised one.**

Studies of how assessors judge accumulated evidence report three findings that bear directly on this occasion. **Assessors relied mainly on narrative feedback and used grades and qualifications merely to confirm impressions already formed from the prose** ([research/03-measurement-evaluation/govaerts-narrative-assessment.md](/research/03-measurement-evaluation/govaerts-narrative-assessment.md)). Having formed a view through a cyclical process of acquiring, organising and integrating evidence, **they were inclined to stick to their initial judgements even when confronted with seemingly disconfirming evidence** — later judgements were more elaborate and not substantially different. And **assessors reaching similar outcomes differed in the reasoning behind them**, driven by different beliefs about which evidence is trustworthy, different implicit theories of competence, and different inferences from the same evidence. The underlying rater literature reports that **idiosyncratic rater effects account for a substantial share of variance in performance ratings, that this is unrelated to rater expertise**, and that standardising instruments and training raters has met with mixed success at best.

**The remedy that literature proposes is not a better scale.** It is substantiated judgements and group deliberation, so that divergent reasoning becomes visible and discussable. **This occasion adopts what it can afford** — a closed specification fixed in advance, a written substantiation from the assessor, and assessor-chosen regions — and **does not run the deliberation, which is the part the literature actually recommends.** §8's third objection is about that gap.

**Its limits:** medical education, single institution, mock portfolios, assessors who did not know the students; think-aloud methods cannot capture fast or automatic processing and participants may construct plausible explanations for the researcher. **The transfer is an argument made explicitly here rather than a given.**

**3.9 Why an unestablished causal claim should be withdrawn with its price.** Establishing that a configuration changed an output requires producing the increment without it, which is frequently unaffordable and sometimes impossible once the model has moved. **A candidate who names the comparison, prices it and declines it has reasoned correctly**; one who asserts the effect has produced the same failure the source corpus commits with its benefit vocabulary.

**3.10 Why reattribution is marked against.** The corpus's prepared answer to *your tooling improved and our delivery did not* reattributes the shortfall to the customer. Paired with a readiness rubric composed of customer dispositions, that arrangement makes a disappointing result confirm the thesis. **This repository's reading calls it the single most teachable thing in the collection**, and the occasion is the last opportunity in the stage to make a candidate notice themselves doing it.

## 4. Session design and its warrant

The seven movements run situation, artefact, standard, failure modes, work, review, limits.

**Opening item 4 is the instrument.** *What did you configure that changed what the executor produced, and how do you know it was the configuration?* The design's expectation is that most candidates assert the chain and a minority say they cannot establish it, and **the minority answer is the better one** — which the standard states in advance so that honesty is not gambled on.

**Opening item 5 is the second instrument and it is the anchor's.** *What in your account would stop being true if the pin moved?* A blank answer means the candidate has not separated what they observed from what they concluded, and an answer of *nothing* means they have concluded that a configuration is a principle.

**The pin is recorded before anything else is assembled.** The work movement is explicit about the ordering, for the same reason [S040](/wiki/seminars/S040.md) writes its cost ledger first: an artefact assembled before its conditions are stated will be described in terms that outrun them.

**4.1 Why the pin is recorded in full rather than in the short form.** The abbreviated commit form is convenient and ages badly — it is ambiguous across a growing history and cannot be resolved once a mirror moves. The requirement costs a candidate nothing and is the difference between a record somebody can act on in a year and one they cannot.

**The improved-and-skipped ledger is written improvements-first.** Improvements are easier and they calibrate the standard for specificity, which the skips then have to meet. **A skip is named in the session as the professional content of the occasion rather than as a confession**, because the prohibition it stands against is the source's own best rule and is contradicted by that source's own best story.

**4.2 Why the counterfactual half is rehearsed separately.** Candidates rehearse explanation and almost never rehearse *what if*. The session therefore requires practice on a region chosen at random by another candidate **and** on a stated change of condition, because §3.6's argument is that the second is where the evidence actually is and it is the half nobody prepares.

**The specification is closed and its exclusions are long**: not whether the increment works, not how much was generated, not the ratio of generated to written work, not speed or volume, not elegance, not whether an agent was used at all, not whether the opaque regions were later explained, and **not whether the configuration in fact caused the output** — only whether the candidate can say how they would find out.

**The agent's brief is to argue the defence is unfounded** — that the configuration's effect cannot be distinguished from the model's default behaviour, that the audit's *explained* class is optimistic, that the check labels were read rather than observed, and that at least one claim is stated as though unbounded. **[S001](/wiki/seminars/S001.md)'s claim closes the stage that finally delegated to it**, which is the module's intended shape and the last time the programme states it in Stage 5. The candidate is also required to record that this instrument shares a model family with their executor and is therefore not independent of it.

## 5. Predicted failure modes and countermeasures

**The delegated defence.** The likeliest failure. *Countermeasure:* the assessor's follow-up, stated in the session — *the agent wrote that* is accepted as provenance and is answered with *what did you do about it, and what is it doing in a build you shipped?*

**The unpinned claim.** A statement about the tooling made as though it held generally. *Countermeasure:* the pin element and item 5, which are the two most mechanically checkable parts of the specification, and exercise 1's sorting.

**The unaudited green build.** *Countermeasure:* the element requiring every region classified, and the standard's warning sign — your audit has no opaque class.

**The improvement with no content.** *Countermeasure:* the element's requirement to say what specifically improved rather than that things went faster, which is also enforced by the stage's prohibition on unmeasured magnitudes.

**The confident causal chain.** *Countermeasure:* the explicit exclusion in the specification, and the rule that a priced withdrawal scores better than an assertion.

**The rehearsed region.** The candidate steers toward the region they prepared. *Countermeasure:* assessor selection, and the counterfactual half, which cannot be prepared for a region the candidate has not been given.

**The reattributed shortfall.** *Countermeasure:* the standard's warning sign — your account contains no mistake of your own. **This is a warning sign rather than an element and §8 concedes the asymmetry.**

## 6. Retention architecture

Retrieved by [S057](/wiki/seminars/S057.md).

**[S057](/wiki/seminars/S057.md) reopens the authority statement** where a governance scope test has to survive agentic delivery, and the defence's own account of what a check licenses is the material that test needs. The module's artefacts are carried forward with it: the audit is what a system model must be built over if it is not to be a guess with a diagram, and the register is what an incident review starts from.

The occasion also serves the whole module backwards, which is its more important direction. **Each of the four preceding sessions produces an artefact satisfiable in form**, and this is the only place where each is exercised against a specific question chosen by somebody else. **Without it the module's artefacts are four documents; with it they are four claims somebody checked.**

## 7. Assessment and evidence generated

**This is an assessment occasion.** It generates specification evidence and no entrustment judgement — the programme's entrustment decisions begin at [S080](/wiki/seminars/S080.md).

Nine elements, each checked present and adequate or not: every region of the increment is classified in the comprehension audit and the unexplained regions are named rather than absent; a step the tooling made better is named with what specifically improved; a step that was skipped is named with what evidence would show whether the skip cost anything; each constraint in the instruction set states its conformance mode and the *not checked at all* entries are present; a decision recorded during planning can be located from the implementation record by its identifier, demonstrated live; the inexplicability register is present and non-empty, or its emptiness is defended with what was looked for and where; every check cited in the defence is labelled advisory or authoritative with the basis of the label stated; the pin is recorded in full and the candidate names which of their conclusions would not survive it moving; and nothing in the defence asserts a magnitude that was not measured by a named instrument.

**Nothing else is assessed.** Whether the increment works is not marked. How much of it was generated is not marked. The ratio of generated to hand-written work is not marked. Speed, throughput and volume are not marked. The elegance of the code is not marked. Whether an agent was used at all is not marked. Whether the opaque regions were eventually explained is not marked. Whether the configuration in fact caused the output is not marked — only whether the candidate can say how they would find out.

Nothing else is assessed.

## 8. Objections and rebuttals

**Objection 1: the occasion cannot distinguish a candidate who understood their build from one who audited it well, and it rewards the second.**

At its strongest: every element is satisfiable by a careful writer. A candidate who understood almost nothing, classified honestly, priced one region and wrote a clean register scores well. A candidate who understood a great deal but classified conservatively and investigated by instinct rather than by rule scores worse. The occasion marks the paperwork of comprehension and calls it comprehension, and its own §3.5 admits the substitution.

**Conceded, and it is the sharpest objection to this occasion.** Three partial answers. The assessor-selected region is a live test a good auditor cannot pass by writing — a candidate who classified a region as *explained* and cannot defend it line by line has failed that element in front of the assessor, and the selection is not theirs. **The counterfactual half is a second live test and a harder one**, because a written audit contains no answer to *what would you do if this had to handle a partial upstream failure*. And the specification's honest position is that the audit is what a successor inherits: **a truthful map of what nobody understands is more useful to the next engineer than an untruthful claim to have understood it all.** **The declined fix is to add an element marking demonstrated comprehension across the increment.** It is declined because comprehension across a whole increment is not observable in a defence of any workable length, and an element that sampled it would be the assessor's opinion in a specification's clothing — the same reason [WP-040](/wiki/whitepapers/WP-040.md) §8 and [WP-045](/wiki/whitepapers/WP-045.md) §8 declined their analogues. **The programme has now declined this class of fix three times, and the accumulation is itself worth noticing.**

**Objection 2: the pin makes half the occasion obsolete on the vendor's schedule, so the programme is assessing something it cannot maintain.**

At its strongest: a commit, a package version, a host and a model will all have moved before a second cohort runs this. The elements referring to them will have to be rewritten, and a specification that requires annual rewriting is not a specification — it is a lesson plan. Meanwhile the durable elements would work without any of it.

**Conceded on the maintenance and rejected on the conclusion.** The maintenance burden is real and [Q5](/wiki/quarters/Q5.md) states it in the stage's own terms: this is the most perishable content in the programme, and **pinning does not slow the decay, it makes the decay visible.** The elements themselves are stated in terms that survive — *the pin is recorded in full*, *every check is labelled with its basis* — and it is the cohort's own pin that changes, not the element. **The declined fix is to drop the platform-bearing elements and assess only the durable ones.** It is declined because the durable elements are unfalsifiable without the perishable ones: a candidate can claim to know what a check licenses without ever having established one, and the only way to close that is to require an observation made on a real configuration on a stated date.

**Objection 3: the assessor is a documented source of variance and this occasion runs the reduced version of every remedy its own sources recommend.**

At its strongest, and this is the objection the design finds hardest because its own citations supply the ammunition. The assessment literature this occasion rests on reports that **idiosyncratic rater effects account for a substantial share of variance in performance ratings, that this is unrelated to rater expertise, and that standardising instruments and training raters has met with mixed success at best.** It reports that assessors **anchor early and then seek confirmation**, that later evidence elaborates rather than overturns the initial judgement, and that **assessors reaching the same outcome frequently reason to it incompatibly.** Its recommended remedies are explicit: multiple observers, multiple observations, substantiated judgements, and group deliberation on high-stakes decisions. **This occasion runs one assessor, on one occasion, with a substantiation and no deliberation.** By the standard of its own sources it is producing what that literature would call an ad-hoc judgement and presenting it as a specification outcome. The closed specification does not repair this: **an assessor who has decided within two minutes that a candidate is thorough will read every element through that decision**, and a fixed element list constrains what is marked without constraining how it is read.

**Conceded, and this is the concession that most limits what the occasion's result means.** Three mitigations, none sufficient. The elements are **presence-and-adequacy checks against artefacts the assessor holds**, which is a narrower judgement than a global impression and is harder to anchor on. The assessor's written substantiation exists precisely so that divergent reasoning is recorded rather than invisible, which is the literature's own first recommendation. And the occasion is **not an entrustment decision** — the programme's entrustment judgements begin later, under a different standard — so the result carries the weight of a specification check rather than of a certification.

**The declined fix is to run the occasion with two assessors and a documented deliberation.** It is declined on cost, and the cost is not hypothetical: the programme has assessment occasions across nine stages, the module already carries the heaviest platform setup burden in the programme, and doubling assessor time here would be paid for by removing it somewhere the evidence standard is higher. **The design's position is that a reduced instrument honestly labelled is better than a full instrument that cannot be staffed** — but that is a resourcing argument, not an epistemic one, and it should not be mistaken for the second. **A cohort able to afford two assessors should use two**, and prediction 5 is written so that the cost of not doing so is measurable rather than assumed.

## 9. Falsifiable predictions and instrumentation

**Prediction 1.** Most candidates will assert a causal chain in answer to opening item 4 rather than withdrawing one. *Instrument:* the item-4 answers, coded *asserted* / *withdrawn with a stated comparison* / *absent*, by a second reader.

**Prediction 2.** Most candidates will answer opening item 5 with fewer pinned claims than their defence actually contains. *Instrument:* the item-5 answer against a second reader's classification of every platform claim in the defence as pinned or durable. **This is the test of the anchor's own teaching**, and a large gap means the pin is being recorded as a header rather than understood as a scope.

**Prediction 3.** Assessor-selected regions will be defended less well than candidate-selected ones, where both are collected — and the gap will be larger on counterfactual questions than on retrospective ones. *Instrument:* a paired defence of one region of each kind per candidate, marked by the same assessor, with retrospective and counterfactual questions scored separately.

**Prediction 4.** Improved-and-skipped ledgers will list more improvements than skips, and the improvements will be less specific. *Instrument:* the ledger, with each entry coded *specific* / *generic* by a second reader, split by kind.

**Prediction 5.** Where two assessors independently mark the same defence, they will agree on the outcome more often than on which elements were weak. *Instrument:* paired element-level marks from two assessors on a subset of defences, with agreement computed separately for the overall result and for the per-element pattern. **This is the direct test of §8's third objection**, and a confirmation means the single-assessor arrangement is producing a defensible outcome by an undocumented route — which is the condition the literature warns about and the one this occasion cannot currently detect.

**Prediction 6.** Candidates who name a mistake of their own in the defence will score no differently on other elements than those who do not. *Instrument:* element-level marks paired against the presence of a self-attributed failure. **The design expects this prediction to hold**, and if it fails — if self-attribution correlates with lower marks — the reattribution warning sign is punishing honesty and must be removed.

## What a learner should take from this paper

**What the design claims.** That delegating the work does not delegate the defence; that the assessable competence is knowing what you did not understand and deciding about it on purpose; that every claim about a configured executor is conditioned on a pin; and that a pin buys visibility of decay rather than protection from it.

**What the evidence licenses.** Nothing about whether delegation helped. **The configuration corpus has graders and no results; the practice collection has approximately thirty quantitative assertions of which almost all measure activity and exactly one names an instrument; the handbook has no comparative evidence for anything on its site; the oral form is well-tested practice with no published effectiveness data; and the entrustment model reports adoption rather than effectiveness.** No cohort running this occasion will settle any of it.

**The concession worth remembering.** This occasion marks the paperwork of comprehension and says so, and it is marked by one assessor where its own sources recommend several. **Both trades are stated at §8 rather than smoothed over**, and you should be able to say whether either was worth making.

## Evidence status

**Verified in this repository.** That the practice corpus states, as a headline takeaway, that the tooling is to be used to make each step better rather than to skip steps; that its anticipated-objection material prepares most heavily for the objection that the tooling improved while delivery did not, and answers it by reattributing the shortfall to the customer's own organisational constraints; that this is paired with a readiness rubric composed almost entirely of customer dispositions, so that a disappointing result confirms rather than challenges the thesis; that it contains no reference, footnote, study, dataset or citation anywhere; and that of approximately thirty quantitative assertions across its delivery material, exactly one names a measurement instrument. Recorded at [research/05-fde-craft/microsoft-accelerated-delivery-practice.md](/research/05-fde-craft/microsoft-accelerated-delivery-practice.md), from a complete reading of access-controlled material **that no public reader can check. No figure from it is reproduced here or anywhere in this repository.**

That the open handbook offers no comparative evidence that its practices outperform alternatives and must not be taught as though it did; that it has no single date and its page footers span several years; that it contradicts itself in at least a dozen places, including on branch naming across three pages; and that its only treatment of generated code is a commit-authorship convention distinguishing the agentic case, where the agent is the author and the human is recorded as co-author, from the assistant case, where a **deliberately different trailer key** is recommended because the co-authorship field expects an address and authorship should not be confused with assistance — recorded at [research/05-fde-craft/microsoft-ise-engineering-playbook.md](/research/05-fde-craft/microsoft-ise-engineering-playbook.md), from a complete reading of all 243 pages on 2026-07-30. **It asserts four unsupported magnitude claims, recorded there as specimens and reproduced nowhere.**

That an entrustment-based discussion is short, follows the activity, probes deep understanding and **must include what-if questions**, on the stated ground that an entrustment decision implies the acceptance of risk because it can never be fully predicted what the candidate will encounter; and that integrity, reliability and humility are defined behaviourally as truthfulness in reports to a supervisor, stable and conscientious working, and the ability to observe one's own limitations and ask for help — recorded at [research/08-assessment-epas/ten-cate-epa-framework.md](/research/08-assessment-epas/ten-cate-epa-framework.md), from a complete reading of the open-access full text. **It is a clinical model reporting adoption rather than effectiveness, and its full multiple-observer evidence standard is not met here.**

That a long-running course states the candidate is solely responsible for the correctness of the solution and that using code generation tools without understanding the generated code will create challenges when answering questions; that it permits collaboration on practical work including copying because the explanation must be the candidate's own; and that it discharges oral verification as a rubric item within a bounded window rather than as a set-piece event — recorded at [research/08-assessment-epas/cmu-oral-defense-and-token-economy-patterns.md](/research/08-assessment-epas/cmu-oral-defense-and-token-economy-patterns.md), **from published course policy documentation which reports no effectiveness data of any kind.**

That assessors reading accumulated evidence relied mainly on narrative and used grades merely to confirm impressions already formed; that they were inclined to stick to initial judgements when confronted with seemingly disconfirming evidence; that assessors reaching similar outcomes differed in the reasoning behind them; that idiosyncratic rater effects account for a substantial share of variance in performance ratings and that this is unrelated to rater expertise; and that the recommended remedy is substantiated judgement and group deliberation rather than better scales — recorded at [research/03-measurement-evaluation/govaerts-narrative-assessment.md](/research/03-measurement-evaluation/govaerts-narrative-assessment.md), from open-access full texts. **Single-institution medical-education studies using mock portfolios and think-aloud methods; the variance figures are reported there and are deliberately not reproduced here.**

**Cited from general knowledge, not verified here.** That an example chosen by the person being examined measures preparation rather than competence; that a causal claim requires a comparison and not merely a sequence; that a defence assembled before its conditions are stated will be phrased in terms that outrun them; and that an unbounded claim cannot be checked. **Direction and mechanism only; no magnitude is asserted.**

**Design reasoning with no external warrant.** The eight-part defence and the nine assessed elements; the requirement that the pin be recorded in full rather than abbreviated; item 5 as the operational form of the scope-declaration argument; the improved-and-skipped ledger and its ordering; the rule that a priced withdrawal of a causal claim scores better than an assertion; the assessor-selected region and the stated follow-up to *the agent wrote that*; the separate rehearsal of the counterfactual half; the treatment of reattribution as a warning sign rather than an element; the requirement to record the attacking instrument's lack of independence; and all six predictions in §9.

**Grounded in vendor documentation, with its version and its silence recorded.** The agent-configuration corpus was read at commit `cf29fb457b0fe62745bb71592ec9394b834957f4`, published package version 3.3.101, on the date recorded in this repository's note, and every structural claim is as of that revision. **Its architecture, configuration and self-described limits are warranted; every benefit word in its prose — accelerate, prevents, proven — is not established by anything in it.** It contains distinct evaluation lanes and **no committed result corpus**; its own documentation records that behaviour checks test output shape rather than correctness, that some checks are advisory by default, and that human oversight remains necessary because it ships no model and no safety classifier and because host behaviour varies. **At the revision read, one job failed while most were skipped; the revision is not all-green and that is part of the evidence.** Its own teaching guidance instructs that the revision be pinned and never taught live, recording commit, package version, host, model, enabled collections and date, with any change treated as a re-verification trigger — **which is the standard this occasion assesses.**

**The silences that matter.** The corpus **publishes no measure of whether any of its controls change what is produced**, and no comparison of work made with and against them; a reader has machinery and no results. The vendor documentation for the host **publishes no measure of how often the self-correction loop converges, and none of how often the agent reports completion on work that is wrong** — so a candidate cannot establish, and neither can an assessor, whether an anomaly in their register was rare. **And nothing anywhere in this repository's platform reading publishes a rate at which a configured constraint changes an executor's output**, which is the single figure that would make the causal element of this defence answerable rather than merely priceable. **The occasion is arranged around that absence rather than despite it**, and a cohort that finds any of those measures published should treat this specification as due for revision.

The host is GitHub Copilot agent mode as recorded in [WP-001](/wiki/whitepapers/WP-001.md)'s reading of 2026-07-30. **That reading has not been repeated for this paper and is carried forward as read**: agent mode determines its own steps and iterates rather than stopping after one response; its confirmation before a terminal command or a non-built-in tool is **a dismissible default rather than a boundary, auto-confirmable per session, per solution or permanently**, with the vendor's own quickstart instructing the reader to select the autopilot option, which removes approvals entirely; and the agent-mode page carried two product version ranges simultaneously. **Every element of this specification that refers to the platform is conditioned on the cohort's own recorded pin and licenses nothing about any other.**
