## In plain terms

**The category is not the lesson. The decision is.**

Classifying a use as sensitive is a lookup, and a learner who classifies and stops has produced a label. What makes the classification worth anything is that it forces somebody to decide something and record it, and most regimes are far clearer about how to classify than about who decides.

There are three decisions and no fourth. **Change the design. Proceed with a recorded deviation. Decline.** *Escalated* and *under review* are states, not decisions, and a record that ends in one of them has resolved nothing.

The middle option is the one worth dwelling on. **Adding a documented, approvable way to say *we cannot comply yet* is the clearest maturity signal in the regime this module draws on**, and the reason is behavioural rather than administrative: a compliance regime with no legitimate way to say that does not stop people shipping. **It stops people recording.** Making deviation visible rather than merely absent is what converts an aspiration into a control — which is the same argument [S010](/wiki/seminars/S010.md) made about engagements, and the same one this stage has made about unmitigated threats.

Two smaller things carry disproportionate weight.

**Sensitive use usually lives where a person is the control**, which means it lives in exactly the region [S052](/wiki/seminars/S052.md)'s binding rule pushed out of the threat registry. That is what the exclusion register is for, and this is the session where it earns its place.

**And the dodge check is the session's most uncomfortable requirement.** Could a slightly different description of your system have avoided the classification? If yes, that is not a loophole to use — **it is a finding about the test you wrote at [S057](/wiki/seminars/S057.md)**, and it belongs in the record.

Two things frame the whole session and this paper states both rather than implying them.

**The first is evidential.** This is the one place in the entire practice corpus where the answer to a customer request is no — **one refusal across approximately ninety documents**, in a collection with no exclusion criteria of any kind and no recorded failure. That refusal is real and correct and **one instance is a shape rather than a base rate.** It supports a form to imitate. It supports no claim about how often refusal is warranted, whether it was accepted, or what followed. **A programme that built a general stance on it would be doing what this repository exists to prevent**, and §8's second objection concedes the point rather than deflecting it.

**The second is about who is being asked to do this.** Stating a refusal to a room that has decided to build something is a **voice act** — speaking up when silence is cheaper — and voice is conditional on an environment the learner does not control. **If that is right, teaching refusal to an individual is treating an environmental variable as a personal skill**, and this paper concedes it at §8's third objection without solving it.

## Abstract

This paper defends teaching sensitive use as a decision-forcing device rather than as a taxonomy, defends compressing the available responses into three, and defends anchoring the session's technical work in a workload-inventory surface rather than in a policy document.

The argument runs in five steps. **First**, a classification which forces no decision is a label, and the commonest failure in this area is correct classification followed by nothing. **Second**, a regime offering no legitimate way to declare an inability to comply produces silence rather than compliance: people under delivery pressure do not stop delivering, they stop generating the record. **Third**, sensitive use predominantly occupies the human process around a system, which is exactly the region a bound threat model excludes by construction — a claim this programme can support from a worked pipeline that loses those threats systematically and declines to measure the loss. **Fourth**, the property that makes a determination possible at all is an honest inventory, and the workload-posture surface supplies both the form of one and the two moments a model touches proprietary data by construction: grounding and fine-tuning. **Fifth**, a decision recorded as a verdict has recorded the least informative part of itself, because assessors converge on outcomes for incompatible reasons and only substantiation makes the divergence visible.

The session installs a hand-built inventory diffed against a discovery tool, a located determination, a decision drawn from three, a named decision-maker with the material they saw, substantiated reasoning, an expiry and revisit condition on any deviation, a reviewable written refusal carrying the three properties of the one recorded refusal, and a dodge check filed against the scope test.

The paper concedes that requiring a named individual will meet real organisational resistance and may expose a person to blame for a collective decision; that teaching from a single recorded refusal is reasoning from one case in a programme that spends nine stages objecting to exactly that; that refusal is conditional on a psychological-safety environment the programme cannot supply and whose leadership prescriptions are untested; and that the posture surface publishes no effectiveness data of any kind.

## 1. The problem this session must solve

A learner who identifies a sensitive use will classify it and consider the work done, because classification is what the form asks for and because the next step is uncomfortable.

**The next step is uncomfortable because it makes somebody responsible.** A decision has an owner, a date and a consequence; a classification has none of those, and an organisation can hold classifications indefinitely without anything changing. **The commonest failure is not misclassification. It is correct classification followed by nothing.**

The second problem is that the regime's options are usually stated less crisply than they need to be, so the practical answer becomes *escalate*. **Escalation is a handoff and it terminates in the same place**, one level up, where the same three options are available and the same discomfort applies.

The third is the incentive one. **The description of a system is written by the party who benefits from the classification going one way**, and a scope test that can be avoided by rewording will be avoided by rewording — usually without anybody experiencing it as dishonesty, because a narrower description is always defensible on its own terms.

## 2. Entry state and prior knowledge

**Entry state: this-programme-only.** The session needs [S051](/wiki/seminars/S051.md)'s components and flows to locate a determination, [S052](/wiki/seminars/S052.md)'s exclusion register to find the places the threat model excluded, [S057](/wiki/seminars/S057.md)'s scope test to run the dodge check against, and [S058](/wiki/seminars/S058.md)'s instrumentation to give a deviation somewhere to expire.

It retrieves [S010](/wiki/seminars/S010.md) directly. **A refusal you can defend is the same artefact at a different scale**, and a learner who wrote one in week two writes a better one here.

**The prior it works against is that governance categories are somebody else's vocabulary.** They are, and the decision they force is not.

## 3. Theoretical basis

**3.1 Load profile.** Low intrinsic, highest interpersonal load in the stage. The session's work makes a named person responsible and asks the learner to write the naming down. The inventory step is placed first partly as ballast: it is concrete, technical and finishable, and a session that opened on the affective work would lose the room.

**3.2 The opening items are a Productive Failure generation phase, and item 5 is the one the release rule protects.**

Learners answer before instruction, and the design wants two different failures. Item 4 — *your regime has no legitimate way to say we cannot comply yet, what happens?* — is a question most experienced people can answer and have never said aloud, and saying it is what makes the deviation mechanism legible as a control rather than as leniency. Item 5 — the dodge check — is answerable honestly **only before the learner knows it will be read.**

The binding condition is that **the generation phase must not be scaffolded**, because guidance that reduces the failure rate reduces the learning ([research/04-professional-formation/sinha-kapur-productive-failure.md](/research/04-professional-formation/sinha-kapur-productive-failure.md)). The second condition is why exercise 3 is a comparison prompt: **presenting a correct answer beside a learner's incorrect one achieves nothing unless they are instructed to compare them.**

**Limits.** School, university and laboratory evidence; **no adult-professional study in the reference list**; a trade book in which anecdote and evidence sit in adjacent paragraphs; and an **Unproductive Failure** quadrant in the author's own model — low performance and low learning — which is what an unassembled generation phase produces.

**3.3 Why the decision set has three members.** A set with an implied fourth member collapses into it, because the fourth is always cheaper. **Naming exactly three, and stating explicitly that escalation is not among them, is the only way to keep the expensive options live.** The compression is this programme's, not the source's, and §8 does not defend it as the only possible one.

**3.4 Why the absence of a legitimate deviation produces silence rather than compliance.** People under delivery pressure do not stop delivering when a rule cannot be met; they stop generating the record that would show it. **A regime that forbids saying *not yet* converts a visible gap into an invisible one**, which is worse for everybody including the regime. The corpus's own maturity signal is exactly this addition ([research/05-fde-craft/microsoft-accelerated-delivery-practice.md](/research/05-fde-craft/microsoft-accelerated-delivery-practice.md)), and it is the clearest thing in the collection.

**3.5 Why a deviation needs an expiry.** An unbounded deviation is a permanent exception, and the organisational learning is worse than the original gap: it teaches that the mechanism is a formality. **The expiry is what distinguishes the maturity signal from the loophole.**

**3.6 Why sensitive use hides in the human process, and why this programme can say so with a worked example.**

The place where an output changes what happens to a person is usually a decision made by a human holding the output — a process step rather than a component. **A bound threat model excludes it by construction**, and this programme has watched a good one do exactly that.

The pipeline's discipline is that **a threat survives only if it can be bound to at least one named component and at least one numbered flow**, with anything else discarded and a reason logged ([research/05-fde-craft/microsoft-agentic-threat-modelling.md](/research/05-fde-craft/microsoft-agentic-threat-modelling.md)). That rule is genuinely good — it makes the model about *this* system. **It also removes everything about the operator, the supply chain, human process and out-of-band administration, each with an entirely plausible logged justification that nobody reads. Recall is unmeasured and the design declines to measure it**: the evaluator explicitly refuses to deduct for threats that are missing, listing them separately as unscored suggestions.

The same source supplies the session's sharpest distinction. **There is no approval gate anywhere in the chain.** Human decisions exist as inputs; the sign-off placeholders — reviewer, date, disposition — are **a form to fill in rather than a precondition for anything**; and the one unavoidably present human authenticates a write to a live tracking system, **being asked for a credential rather than for a judgement.** That is what an out-of-scope determination looks like from the inside, produced by people who plainly knew what they were doing.

**Limits.** Internal and access-controlled; paraphrased and never quoted; **it cannot supply an external warrant for any claim.** No outcome evidence exists for any of it, and its implied observed failures are nowhere recorded. **The source contains operational attack detail that is not reproduced here and should not be.**

**3.7 Why the inventory is the technical spine, and what the posture surface contributes.**

A determination cannot be located in a system nobody has enumerated. The workload-posture surface exists in the session because it makes the enumeration a first-class artefact: it builds a **generative AI bill of materials** — "application components, data, and AI artifacts from code to cloud" — discovered automatically and continuously across several first-party and third-party model services, scanning source for infrastructure misconfigurations and container images for known vulnerabilities in generative-AI library dependencies ([research/06-microsoft-platform/purview-dspm-for-ai-and-defender-ai-spm-docs.md](/research/06-microsoft-platform/purview-dspm-for-ai-and-defender-ai-spm-docs.md)). Its named minimum baseline for a hosted model endpoint — **private endpoints, restricted endpoints, managed identity, identity-based authentication** — is mostly about removing keys and network exposure.

Two of its ideas do real work in a determination. **Attack path analysis targets "toxic combinations"**, individually acceptable configurations that compose into a breach — **which is the normal shape of a sensitive use**, where a fine model, a fine data source, a fine workflow step and a person downstream who treats the composite as authoritative produce something nobody approved. **And it names grounding and fine-tuning as the two moments a model touches proprietary data**, flagging that data might be exposed during each; those are therefore the two moments data can leak **by construction rather than by attack**, and the first two places to look for a person's information entering a system that will act on it.

**Limits, and they are why this is an anchor rather than an authority.** **Read through search-returned excerpts rather than a full page fetch**, so the structure is reconstructed and should be re-verified. **No detection rates, no false-positive rates and no coverage measurements are published — none is asserted here or in the session.** Agent-level discovery and posture are mid-licence-migration. Single-vendor: concepts transfer, mechanisms and portal paths do not. Substantial administrative prerequisites that a course lab may not satisfy.

**One further discipline belongs here.** A learner will be tempted to ask the system whether its own use is sensitive. The planning literature holds that these models cannot self-verify **"because self-verification is after all a form of reasoning"**, and that correctness must come from an **external** verifier ([research/02-technical-foundations/kambhampati-llms-cant-plan.md](/research/02-technical-foundations/kambhampati-llms-cant-plan.md)). **A sensitivity determination is a verification claim about the system's own effects**, so the external verifier here is a person with a name — which is the same requirement the session already imposes, arriving from a second direction. A contested position paper scoped to auto-regressive models as of early 2024; **the architectural discipline transfers.**

**3.8 Refusal is a voice act, and voice is conditional on something the learner does not control.**

This is the part of the design with the strongest evidence and the least comfortable implication.

What the session asks a learner to rehearse is speaking up when silence is cheaper. The construct is defined as **"the belief that the work environment is safe for interpersonal risk taking"**, with the interpersonal risks being appearing **"ignorant, incompetent, or disruptive"** ([research/04-professional-formation/edmondson-psychological-safety.md](/research/04-professional-formation/edmondson-psychological-safety.md)). The mechanism is a calculation rather than a disposition: **voice benefits the organisation, after a delay, with low certainty; silence benefits oneself, immediately, with high certainty.** **Silence is rational under that payoff structure**, which is why exhortation does not move it and why the session asks learners to price the refusal rather than to resolve to be braver.

Four corrections from the same source are load-bearing because the popular version inverts them. **It is not about being nice** — "you could say it's the opposite", and it is "about candor, about making it possible for productive disagreement and free exchange of ideas." **It is not comfort.** **It is not lowered standards**: safety and performance standards are separate dimensions rather than a trade-off, and the quadrant the author names as the one that keeps her up at night is **high standards with low safety.** And **sanction sits inside the model**: a fair, thoughtful response to a genuinely blameworthy act is held to reinforce safety rather than damage it.

For synthesis rather than advocacy the meta-analytic review is the better instrument ([research/04-professional-formation/frazier-psychological-safety-meta-analysis.md](/research/04-professional-formation/frazier-psychological-safety-meta-analysis.md)). It aggregates a large body of independent samples, **ranks antecedents by relative strength rather than listing them**, tests **incremental validity** against named comparators — positive leader relations and work engagement — which is the sceptic's question and the right one, and tests **homology** across individual and group levels rather than assuming it.

**Limits, and they are why this paper does not claim more.** The popular account's **leadership framework is untested**: no trial appears in the book, the author does not claim one, and it is drawn partly from consulting experience. Its narrative review is not a systematic review and reports no disconfirming study. **Its magnitudes may not cross into this wiki and none does.** The meta-analytic note here rests on the paper's **abstract**, reports **no numerical effect sizes**, and its pooled evidence is **overwhelmingly cross-sectional and self-report, so causal direction is not established by it.**

**3.9 Why the decision is recorded as reasoned narrative rather than as a verdict.**

When assessors judge complex evidence they **rely on narrative to form the judgement and use scores only to confirm it** — grades were "merely used to confirm impressions based on narratives" ([research/03-measurement-evaluation/govaerts-narrative-assessment.md](/research/03-measurement-evaluation/govaerts-narrative-assessment.md)). Two further findings bear on a sensitive-use record. **Assessors anchor early and then seek confirmation**, sticking to initial judgements even against seemingly disconfirming evidence, with final judgements more elaborate but not substantially different. **And convergence on an outcome conceals divergence in reasoning** — assessors reached similar decisions for incompatible reasons, invisible in the outcome.

**A record stating only the decision has therefore recorded its least informative part.** The remedy the same literature recommends is that high-stakes decisions **come from group discussion** rather than individuals, and that judgements be **thoroughly substantiated**, because substantiation is what makes divergent reasoning visible and discussable.

**Limits.** Studies of clinical assessors judging portfolios and trainee performance, in a single institutional setting, using think-aloud methods that cannot capture fast or automatic processing and that risk confabulated explanations. **The transfer to an engineering governance decision is this design's inference, not the authors'.**

## 4. Session design and its warrant

The movements run situation, artefact, material, workflow, standard, failure modes, exercises, limits.

**4.1 Opening item 4 is the instrument.** *Your regime has no legitimate way to say we cannot comply yet — what happens?* Every experienced person in the room knows the answer and most have never said it aloud, and saying it is what makes the deviation mechanism legible as a control rather than as leniency.

**4.2 Opening item 5 is asked before instruction because it cannot be asked afterwards.** Once a learner knows the dodge check is read, the honest answer becomes harder to give. **This is the strictest release-rule dependency in the module.**

**4.3 The inventory is built by hand before any tool is opened, and the order is the design.** A learner who starts from a generated bill of materials has been handed a completed artefact and has learned to read it. **A learner who enumerates first and diffs second learns two things a generated inventory cannot teach**: what they did not know about their own system, and — more usefully — what they know that the tool does not cover. **The second list is where sensitive uses live**, because tools discover components and sensitive use lives in process.

**4.4 Grounding and fine-tuning are marked on the learner's own inventory.** They are the two moments a model touches proprietary data, and marking them converts an abstract caution into two coordinates on a diagram the learner drew.

**4.5 The workflow sends the learner to the exclusion register**, which is the module's designed payoff: an artefact built in [S052](/wiki/seminars/S052.md) for one reason turns out to be the input to a different problem three sessions later. **A cohort that resented producing it meets it here as the only place the answer lives.**

**4.6 The refusal example is given with its shape named rather than summarised**, because a refusal is a genre and the genre is learnable: what the humans catch, why the consequence class matters, and declining the customer's own proposal rather than a hypothetical. **The scarcity of the example is stated alongside it** — one instance in approximately ninety documents — because a learner who takes it as a base rate has drawn the wrong lesson from the right case.

**4.7 The toxic-combination exercise is checked back against the learner's own scope test.** If any component of the combination is individually out of scope under [S057](/wiki/seminars/S057.md)'s test, the learner has found a hole in their own test **with a live example attached** — which is worth considerably more than the hypothetical they wrote a session earlier, and which is why the exercise is placed here rather than there.

**4.8 The record requires substantiation rather than a verdict**, for §3.9's reason. The reasoning field is written to be disagreed with: enough that a reader who takes the other view knows exactly what to take issue with.

**4.9 The agent attacks the decision from the side the learner cannot see.** A learner who declined is asked to have it argue the refusal is unnecessary; one who deviated or redesigned gets the mirror. **The output is treated as an argument to answer rather than a verdict to accept**, and the record notes separately that a system being governed is not an independent judge of whether it should be.

**4.10 The exercises ask the learner to price the refusal for themselves.** Not to resolve to make it — to write down what it would cost, to whom, in front of whom. **The design's position is that a cost you have written down is a cost you can decide about, and one you have not is a cost that decides for you.**

## 5. Predicted failure modes and countermeasures

**The category exercise.** *Countermeasure:* the standard's warning sign — your artefact ends with a label — and the decision set stated as an artefact requirement rather than as advice.

**The escalation as decision.** *Countermeasure:* the explicit exclusion of escalation from the decision set.

**The absent decider.** The likeliest failure. *Countermeasure:* the requirement to record what the decision-maker saw, which a function name cannot satisfy. §8 concedes the resistance this will meet.

**The credential mistaken for a judgement.** *Countermeasure:* the worked pipeline example, where the only unavoidable human supplies access rather than agreement, and the standard's warning sign — the person you named was asked for access rather than for an opinion. **This is the design's newest countermeasure and §9's prediction 5 measures it.**

**The permanent deviation.** *Countermeasure:* the expiry and revisit condition as named elements, plus the closing exercise's second question.

**The unrecorded refusal.** *Countermeasure:* [S010](/wiki/seminars/S010.md)'s form, retrieved rather than re-taught, plus the three properties drawn from the recorded case.

**The self-classified system.** *Countermeasure:* §3.7's discipline, stated in the material — a sensitivity determination is a verification claim, and these systems cannot self-verify.

**The scope dodge.** *Countermeasure:* the dodge check, asked before instruction. **This is a disclosure rather than a prevention**, and the design has nothing better.

## 7. Assessment and evidence generated

No entrustment evidence. ## 8. Objections and rebuttals

**Objection 1: requiring a named individual is a design that organisations will refuse, and that exposes a person to blame for a decision the organisation made.**

At its strongest: committees exist precisely to distribute accountability for decisions no individual should carry, and in regulated settings the decision genuinely belongs to a body. Requiring a name produces one of two outcomes — a fictitious owner, or a real person carrying personal exposure for a collective judgement they had limited power to shape. **Personal accountability without personal authority is scapegoating**, and a curriculum should not be manufacturing it.

**Conceded, and the design accepts an adjustment rather than defending the strict form.** **A named body, with a named chair and named papers, satisfies the element.** What does not satisfy it is a function — *Governance*, *the review board*, a mailbox — because a function cannot be asked what it was looking at, and **the material the decision-maker saw is the element carrying the real information.** A decision-maker shown a one-page summary decided about a one-page summary, and if that summary was produced by the process under assessment then [S054](/wiki/seminars/S054.md) applies. **The declined fix is to accept a function name.** It is declined for that reason. **The session states the stricter form deliberately**, because a learner offered the looser form first will always take it, and this paper records the adjustment rather than silently widening the session's text.

**Objection 2: teaching from the corpus's single recorded refusal is reasoning from one case, in a programme that spends nine stages objecting to exactly that.**

At its strongest: this programme's standing criticism of its own source material is that it generalises from experience without evidence. Here it takes the one refusal in an entire collection, holds it up as a template, and names three features of it as though they were established properties of good refusals. **That is a sample of one, presented as a genre.**

**Conceded.** Two things narrow the damage without removing it. The session uses the case as a **shape rather than as a base rate**, and says so explicitly in its situation and again in its limits — one case tells you what a well-formed refusal can look like and nothing about how often refusal is right. And the three features named are defensible on their own terms without the case: a refusal that says what the humans catch is checkable, one that names the consequence class is proportionate, and one that declines the customer's own proposal rather than a hypothetical is addressed to the actual decision. **The declined fix is to remove the case and describe a well-formed refusal abstractly.** It is declined because an abstract description of a refusal has no traction — learners produce refusals that meet every stated property and would persuade nobody — and because **the scarcity of the case is itself part of the teaching: one refusal in an entire body of professional practice is a finding about the practice.** The residual risk, that a cohort over-fits to a single domain as the paradigm of sensitive use, is real and the session does not counter it. **§9's prediction 6 is the instrument for it.**

**Objection 3: refusal is conditional on an environment the programme cannot supply, so teaching it to individuals equips them with a capability they cannot exercise and may harm them by encouraging its use.**

At its strongest, and this is the objection the design finds hardest. If speaking up is a voice behaviour and voice is conditional on psychological safety, then **a programme that installs the disposition and says nothing about the environment has asked a learner to take a risk it has not helped them assess.** A junior engineer who declines work in an unsafe team pays a real cost, immediately and certainly, for a benefit that accrues elsewhere, later, uncertainly. **The programme will not be there when the bill arrives.** And the evidence base makes this worse rather than better: the construct is well supported, the *prescriptions* for changing it are not, and the leadership framework the popular account offers has never been trialled.

**Conceded, and it is conceded more completely here than anywhere else in the module.** The design has one mitigation and it is not a solution. **The session requires the refusal to be written so that somebody else could carry it** — located, substantiated, reviewable by a person who disagrees — which converts a personal act into a transferable artefact and is the only move available to somebody with no authority over their room. It also requires the learner to **price** the refusal rather than to resolve to make it, because a written cost can be decided about. **The declined fix is to teach the environment instead of the act** — to run this session as an exercise in building psychological safety rather than in exercising it. It is declined on two grounds. Most learners have no authority to change their team's norms, so a session about doing so would be a session about somebody else's job. And **this repository is hostile to prescriptions without warrant**, and the available prescriptions are exactly that: untested, drawn partly from consulting experience, and reported by an advocate. **The cost of declining is that the session teaches a disposition whose precondition it does not supply**, which is the same accepted cost [WP-001](/wiki/whitepapers/WP-001.md) records for [S001](/wiki/seminars/S001.md), arriving here in its sharpest form.

**Objection 4: anchoring a session about human judgement to a security posture product is a category error, and the inventory work will crowd out the decision work.**

At its strongest: the session's subject is a decision that a person makes and records. Its anchor is a discovery tool that enumerates infrastructure. **Those are different problems**, and giving learners a concrete, technical, satisfying task at the start of a session whose real work is uncomfortable and unbounded guarantees that the technical task expands to fill the time. **A cohort will leave with a good bill of materials and a determination written in the last ten minutes**, which is the opposite of the intended allocation.

**Partly conceded, and the reasoning for the placement is stated rather than assumed.** The inventory is not decorative: **a determination cannot be located in a system nobody has enumerated**, and the commonest failure in the pre-revision session was determinations located nowhere — asserted rather than bound to a component, a flow or a named process step. The anchor supplies two things the policy material does not: a worked form for the enumeration, and the observation that grounding and fine-tuning are the two by-construction data-exposure moments, which converts *where should I look* from a matter of taste into two coordinates. **The declined fix is to drop the platform work and spend the whole session on the decision.** It is declined because a session composed entirely of affective and procedural work has nothing a learner can check, and because the diff between the hand-built inventory and the discovered one is where the session's most reliable surprise lives. **The cost is a real risk of misallocation**, and §9's prediction 7 measures it directly rather than assuming the design won the argument.

## 9. Falsifiable predictions and instrumentation

**Prediction 1.** Most records will name a function rather than a person, or a body with a chair and papers. *Instrument:* the submitted record, coded *named person* / *named body with chair and papers* / *function* by a second reader.

**Prediction 2.** Most deviations will lack an expiry at first attempt. *Instrument:* a presence check for expiry and revisit condition on each deviation submitted.

**Prediction 3.** Most dodge checks will be answered in the negative. *Instrument:* the learner's answer, paired with a second reader's independent attempt to construct a narrower description of the same system that avoids the classification.

**Prediction 4.** Sensitive uses will be located inside the system more often than in the human process around it. *Instrument:* the location coded against the categories in the learner's [S052](/wiki/seminars/S052.md) exclusion register. **If this holds, the module's claim that the exclusion register is the right input here is not being realised in practice, and the workflow's ordering should be reconsidered.**

**Prediction 5.** Where a decision-maker is named, a substantial share will turn out to have been asked for an authorisation rather than for an opinion. *Instrument:* the named decision-maker interviewed, or the record's *what they saw* field coded *judgement artefact* / *access request* / *unclear* by a second reader. **This is the instrument for §5's newest countermeasure**, and a confirmation means the session is recording approvals and calling them decisions.

**Prediction 6.** Cohort determinations will cluster around review, approval and eligibility workflows — the domain of the single recorded refusal — and under-represent sensitive uses in monitoring, prioritisation and resource allocation. *Instrument:* determinations coded by domain and compared against the domain distribution of the cohort's actual systems. **This is the instrument attached to §8's second objection**, and a confirmation means the single case is functioning as a template for what counts rather than for how to write it.

**Prediction 7.** Learners will spend more time on the inventory and diff than on the determination and its reasoning, and the reasoning field will be the shortest substantive field in most records. *Instrument:* self-reported time allocation, plus field-length and substantiation quality coded by a second reader. **This predicts the failure §8's fourth objection describes**, and a confirmation means the anchor should move later in the session rather than being removed.

**Prediction 8.** Learners asked to price their own refusal will describe a cost and then decline to state whether they would pay it. *Instrument:* the priced-refusal exercise, coded *cost stated and decision stated* / *cost stated only* / *neither*. **If the second category dominates, the exercise is producing awareness without decision**, which is the pattern the psychological-safety literature predicts and which the session has no countermeasure for.

## What a learner should take from this paper

**What the design claims.** That a classification forcing no decision is a label; that a person asked for a credential has not been asked for a judgement; that a regime with no legitimate way to say *not yet* produces silence rather than compliance; and that a deviation without an expiry teaches an organisation that the mechanism is a formality.

**What the evidence licenses.** Almost nothing about outcomes. **The regime publishes no evidence that any determination ever changed a system, and the surrounding corpus contains exactly one recorded refusal across its entire collection.** A single case is a shape, not a rate. The posture surface publishes **no detection rate, no false-positive rate and no coverage measurement.** The psychological-safety construct is well supported and **its prescriptions are not**: the leadership framework is untested, its author does not claim otherwise, and the meta-analytic evidence is overwhelmingly cross-sectional so **causal direction is not established.** The narrative-assessment findings come from clinical assessors and their transfer here is an inference.

**No magnitude appears anywhere in this paper.** That is a rule of this repository rather than an accident of what was read.

**The concession worth remembering.** This session asks you to write down who decided, what they saw, and whether you could have described the system differently to avoid the question. **All three are uncomfortable and the third is the one you will be tempted to skip.** It is also the only one that tells anybody anything about the regime itself. And §8's third objection concedes the thing the session cannot fix: **the environment that decides whether your refusal is survivable is not one this programme can give you.**

**The silences that matter.** **No detection rate, no false-positive rate, no measurement of how much oversharing or misconfiguration exists in a typical estate, and no evaluation of any control's effectiveness is published anywhere in this documentation.** None is asserted here or in the session, and a cohort that finds one should treat this paper as due for revision. **The documentation names toxic combinations and publishes nothing about how often they occur or how many are found**, so the concept is definitional rather than empirical. **It says nothing about who decides**, which is the whole subject of this session — discovery and posture are technical surfaces and the determination they inform is a human act the documentation never describes. **And it does not address the human process around a system at all**, which is where this session argues sensitive use predominantly lives; that argument is the design's, supported by a worked internal specimen and by nothing the vendor publishes.

**This session enumerates no sensitive-use categories and no thresholds, and nothing in it is legal or regulatory guidance.** Which uses are heightened, who may approve a deviation and what must be retained belong to the learner's organisation, their customer, their regulator and their jurisdiction — **none of which this programme knows, and none of which it should be read as describing.**
