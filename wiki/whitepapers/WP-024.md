## In plain terms

Code says what was done. It never says what was rejected, and it never says why.

Six months later, an engineer who was not present looks at something and asks why it is like that. The people who know have moved on. What is left is an artefact that records the winner of an argument nobody can reconstruct.

The published specification this session uses is unusually good and its field list is not the reason. **Two properties do the work.**

**The record enters review as a proposal, before the thing is built.** That is a claim about timing rather than content, and it is the difference between reasoning that could still change something and a minute of a decision already taken. A record written after the code is not a decision record; it is an explanation.

**The context section must be value-neutral — an account of the forces at play, written so that a reader cannot tell which way the decision went.** This is genuinely difficult to produce, it is mechanically checkable by handing the section to somebody, and it catches the commonest defect in written engineering judgement: **reasoning composed after the conclusion, to fit it.**

The session marks against that and against very little else.

## Abstract

This paper defends teaching the architecture decision record as a specification rather than as a habit, and marking it on the value-neutrality of its context and the exercise of its status vocabulary. The argument is that a record's engineering value comes from entering review as a proposal and from stating what got worse, and that both properties are checkable while the quality of the decision is not. It requires one record in every set to be superseded by another that names it, on the grounds that a status vocabulary nobody exercises is decoration — a defect visible in the source's own published examples. The paper concedes that value-neutrality cannot be distinguished from careful omission by the test it proposes, and that the specification's own worked examples do not meet it, which the session teaches as material rather than resolving.

## 1. The problem this session must solve

Most decisions in delivery are not taken; they accrete.

Somebody tries something under time pressure. It works well enough. Nobody objects, because objecting requires an alternative and nobody has one ready. Within weeks the thing is load-bearing, and by the time anyone calls it a decision it has no author, no rejected alternative and no stated cost. **It cannot be revisited because there is nothing to revisit** — a decision with no recorded reasoning can only be reversed by re-deriving the whole situation, which nobody has time for, so it is not reversed.

There is a second problem the session addresses in the same movement, and it is about writing rather than about process. **Engineers asked to record a decision write advocacy.** The context section explains why the chosen option was obviously right, because by the time the record is written the author believes it and believes it was always obvious. The record then documents a conclusion and its supporting argument, which is exactly the artefact that cannot help a later reader — the later reader needs the forces, not the verdict.

## 2. Entry state and prior knowledge

**Entry state: either.** Practitioners have usually met decision records and have usually met them as a compliance artefact filled in at the end. That prior is the session's principal adversary and it is stronger than ignorance would be.

The session retrieves [S021](/wiki/seminars/S021.md), [S022](/wiki/seminars/S022.md) and [S023](/wiki/seminars/S023.md) because the decisions it records are the ones those sessions forced. **A learner arriving without a requirement set will write records about nothing**, which is why this session is fourth in the module rather than first.

## 3. Theoretical basis

**3.1 Load profile.** Deliberately moderate, and lighter than [S023](/wiki/seminars/S023.md). It is placed fourth so that the assessment at [S025](/wiki/seminars/S025.md) follows a session the cohort found manageable rather than one that exhausted them. The load that exists is affective: writing a value-neutral context for something you already believe is uncomfortable in a small, particular way.

**3.2 Why timing rather than content is the primary property.** A record that enters review before the work can change the work. A record written afterwards cannot, whatever its quality. **This is the same argument [S012](/wiki/seminars/S012.md) makes about readiness gates and [S020](/wiki/seminars/S020.md) makes about stopping rules — a criterion formed after the outcome is known cannot discriminate between outcomes.** Three appearances, one claim, and the design says so each time.

**3.3 Why value-neutrality is checkable and quality is not.** Whether the decision was correct depends on facts nobody has yet. Whether a reader can guess the decision from the context is a property of the text and can be established in a minute. **The session prefers the checkable property**, and this paper concedes in §8 what that preference costs.

**3.4 Why a superseded record is required.** A status vocabulary exists to be exercised. **Neither of the published specification's own worked examples ever reaches the superseded state**, and a status nobody uses is a field, not a mechanism. Requiring one supersession per set forces the learner to discover that superseding is a piece of writing rather than a deletion — the old record stays, its status changes, and the new one names what it replaced.

**3.5 Why consequences must include something that got worse.** Every real decision costs something, and a consequences section listing only benefits is unusable to a later reader deciding whether the trade still holds. **This is design reasoning; the specification requires a consequences section and does not require a concession in it.**

## 4. Session design and its warrant

Seven movements: situation, artefact, standard, failure modes, work, review, limits.

**Opening item 2 is the instrument.** Naming a decision nobody wrote down is easy; naming the person who took it is frequently impossible, and the impossibility is the session's opening content. Accretion is invisible from inside and becomes visible the moment somebody asks for an author.

**The work movement requires two records for structural reasons rather than for practice.** One documents a decision already taken, which exposes the advocacy habit. One documents a decision not yet taken, which is the use the specification is actually for and the use most teams never make. **Writing both in one session is what makes the difference between them legible**, and neither alone would produce it.

**The supersession is a third piece of writing and is required.** It is placed after the two records so that the learner has something to supersede and so that the act is performed on their own reasoning rather than on a supplied example.

**The review movement withholds the decision on purpose.** The context section is handed over alone and unlabelled, and the reviewer is asked which way the decision went. **This is the session's only mechanical check and everything else rests on it.** It is cheap, it is fast, and §8 records what it cannot distinguish.

**The specification is taught as a specification that its own examples do not meet.** The published records do not exercise the full status vocabulary and do not refer to a documented comparison although the parent page instructs that a data-driven decision should. Pages read 2026-07-30. **That is handed to learners as material**, on the same reasoning [M03](/wiki/modules/M03.md) used for the contradiction it found in the internal corpus.

## 5. Predicted failure modes and countermeasures

**The advocacy context.** The likeliest failure by a wide margin. *Countermeasure:* the review movement, and the standard's instruction that a comparative adjective in the context is a warning sign.

**The fait accompli.** The record documents something already built and enters review with nothing at stake. *Countermeasure:* the requirement that one of the two records concerns a decision not yet taken. **This does not prevent the failure; it makes the learner experience the alternative once.**

**The consequence-free record.** *Countermeasure:* the standard requires a sentence beginning with a concession. Weakly enforced — a concession can be written about something that does not matter.

**The unexercised status.** *Countermeasure:* the required supersession, which is the strongest countermeasure in the session because it cannot be satisfied without producing a third artefact.

**The orphan record.** Well written, attached to nothing, in a folder nobody opens. *Countermeasure:* the standard requires the record to be tied to the work it came from, which is the specification's own collision rule repurposed as a findability rule.

**The manufactured decision.** The learner writes a record for a choice that was never genuinely open, because those are easier to write. *Countermeasure:* none available at this session. **[S029](/wiki/seminars/S029.md) supplies the related discipline** — that a comparison should not be run where the choice is already clear — and this session's parallel case is not checked.

## 7. Assessment and evidence generated

**No entrustment evidence.** One specification item at [S025](/wiki/seminars/S025.md): at least one decision record whose context does not reveal which way the decision went, checked by handing the section to the assessor.

**The supersession is not separately assessed at [S025](/wiki/seminars/S025.md)** and that is a deliberate narrowing. It is required in the session and marked nowhere, on the grounds that the specification at the assessment should be short enough to be applied consistently. **The cost is that the session's strongest countermeasure has no assessment behind it.**

## 8. Objections and rebuttals

**Objection 1: the value-neutrality test cannot distinguish neutrality from careful omission.**

At its strongest: a learner who understands the test will write a context that conceals the decision by leaving out the force that determined it. The reviewer cannot guess, the item passes, and the record is now worse than an honest advocacy piece — it has the appearance of balance and is missing the thing a later reader most needs. **The test rewards a skill adjacent to the one it is meant to develop**, and there is no version of the test that separates them, because both produce the same observable.

**Conceded without qualification, and it is the stronger objection in this paper.** The rebuttal is only about the base rate: the observed default is advocacy rather than concealment, so a test that catches advocacy catches most of what is there, and a learner sophisticated enough to conceal has at least understood what a context section is for. **The declined fix is to have the reviewer read the context and then the decision, and judge whether the context accounts for it.** It is declined because that check requires a reviewer with domain knowledge of the decision, which the session cannot guarantee, and a check that only works with an expert reviewer will silently become a check nobody runs.

**Objection 2: decision records are an artefact of organisations that keep them, and teaching the specification teaches an artefact the learner will have no authority to introduce.**

At its strongest: the specification assumes a repository the team controls, a review process the team runs, and a culture where a proposal can be argued with before the work happens. **Forward-deployed engineers frequently have none of these.** They work inside somebody else's process, on somebody else's repository, under a delivery plan that has already committed to the decisions the record would examine. A graduate arriving with a decision-record habit will find nowhere to put one.

**Substantially conceded.** The rebuttal is narrow and is about the writing rather than the process: the value-neutral context and the concession in the consequences are properties of a paragraph and can be produced in a project wiki, a comment, or an email, in an organisation that has never heard of the specification. **The declined fix is to teach the writing without the apparatus.** It is declined because the apparatus is what makes the timing property real — a paragraph can be written after the fact and a proposal entering review cannot — and the timing property is the one this paper argues carries the value. **That is a genuine tension and it is not resolved.**

## 9. Falsifiable predictions and instrumentation

**Prediction 1.** Most first-draft context sections will allow a reviewer to guess the decision correctly. *Instrument:* the review movement's guess, recorded per record, before revision.

**Prediction 2.** Learners will find the record for the decision already taken harder to write than the record for the decision not yet taken, and will say so when asked. *Instrument:* a single question put after both are written, coded *already taken harder* / *not yet taken harder* / *no difference*.

**Prediction 3.** Consequences sections will contain concessions about matters the author does not care about more often than about matters they do. *Instrument:* the concessions, coded by a second reader against the record's own stated decision. **If concessions are evenly distributed, the countermeasure is working better than this paper expects and the warning sign should be withdrawn.**

**Prediction 4.** Where a cohort is not required to supersede a record, none will. *Instrument:* status distributions in any cohort where the requirement is relaxed, compared against one where it is not.

## What a learner should take from this paper

**What the design claims.** That a record entering review as a proposal can change the work and one written afterwards cannot; that a context revealing its own conclusion is advocacy; and that a status vocabulary nobody exercises is decoration.

**What the evidence licenses.** Nothing about outcomes. The field list is a specification from a page read on 2026-07-30, from a publication offering no comparative evidence for any of its practices and claiming none. Nothing establishes that teams keeping these records decide better, or that later engineers read them.

**The concession worth remembering.** The session's only mechanical check cannot tell a balanced account from a carefully incomplete one, and this paper does not pretend otherwise.
