> ## Vocabulary used here without definition
>
> | Term | Meaning |
> |---|---|
> | **Forward brief** | This occasion's artefact: a written statement to whoever supervises the learner next, of what they can and cannot be trusted with, and the observation supporting each line |
> | **Calibration comparison** | Placing the learner's own reading of their limits beside the assessors' independently formed levels, with neither side visible to the other beforehand |
> | **Firewall** | The standing rule that nothing a learner disclosed about their own error may be used against them |
> | **Entrustable professional activity** | A bounded unit of practice with a beginning and an end that a supervisor could delegate whole. Describes work, never a person |
> | **Supervision level, and sub-level** | The ordinal scale on which entrustment is expressed, from observe-only to supervising others, with the middle band split by how much a supervisor still double-checks |
> | **Entrustment-based discussion** | A short post-activity conversation that must include what-if questions, because retrospective explanation is evidence only about the cases the learner met |
> | **Assessment versus evaluation** | Gathering data, and interpreting it into decisions and actions. **A programme can do the first and skip the second** |
> | **Expiry** | The lapse of an entrustment decision through disuse. Most academic schemes assume permanence; the model this occasion uses does not |

## In plain terms

This is the last occasion in the programme, and it asks the version of the first question that is about the learner.

At [S001](/wiki/seminars/S001.md) they were asked what would have to be true for the right answer to be *do not build this*, and most people could not answer it concretely. **That gap was the programme's opening finding.** The closing one is the same shape: what should you not yet be trusted to do without supervision, and what did somebody observe that shows it?

The instrument is a comparison and nothing else. The learner lodges a forward brief before hearing anything. The assessors have independently formed levels across the whole programme. Both go on the table at once. **What is assessed is the fit, in both directions** — a learner who understated their limits and a learner who overstated them have made the same error, and it is the error this programme has been about from the first week.

**There is no challenger, no adversary, and nothing the learner is expected to decline.** That is a deliberate correction and §8 records why.

And the occasion closes the programme by doing to itself what it has spent ninety sessions doing to its sources. **Nothing in this programme is evidence that any of it works.** The occasion states that as its final act, in front of the people it was performed on.

## Abstract

This paper defends closing the programme with an entrustment decision formed around a calibration between a learner's own account of their limits and assessors' independently accumulated levels. The argument is that the professionally consequential capability is the accurate location of one's own competence in a form somebody else can act on, that it is directly comparable against an independent reading, and that both directions of divergence are defects. The occasion produces a forward brief addressed to a future supervisor, lodged before the assessors' levels are revealed, and a short live account of the divergence. The paper records the removal of an assessment device this programme had used on consecutive occasions and had begun conceding rather than correcting, notes two further devices the design now repeats, concedes that the entrustment vocabulary is borrowed from settings this one does not resemble, and closes by stating that the programme reports no outcome, has never been run, and holds that most of its own predictions never will be measured.

## 1. The problem this occasion must solve

A practitioner leaves a programme and joins work where the supervision they receive is set by somebody who has never seen them.

That person will decide, quickly and on thin evidence, what to assign. **They will be substantially wrong in one direction or the other, and the fastest available correction is an honest account from the practitioner themselves** — which is exactly the account a practitioner has the least incentive to give accurately.

Two pressures act in opposite directions and both are ordinary. **Overstatement** is rewarded at the point of assignment and punished later, in private, by somebody else. **Understatement** is safe, socially attractive, and produces an engineer who is never given the work that would develop them. Neither is dishonest and neither is available to the person's own inspection, because self-assessment is performed with the same instrument being assessed.

The third problem is form. Most accounts of one's own limits are dispositional — *I can be overconfident*, *I am not strong on infrastructure* — and dispositions cannot be acted on. **A supervisor needs an activity at the granularity of a task somebody is assigned, with a supervision position and an observation behind it.** That is a different artefact from a reflection and learners produce the reflection by default.

**Entry state: this-programme-only.** The occasion assumes the whole programme, and specifically the position from [S001](/wiki/seminars/S001.md), the attribution requirement from [S070](/wiki/seminars/S070.md), the transfer decision from [S080](/wiki/seminars/S080.md), the maturity occasion from [S085](/wiki/seminars/S085.md), and the limits register from [S088](/wiki/seminars/S088.md).

## 3. Theoretical basis

**3.1 Load profile.** Intrinsic load is minimal and affective load is the highest in the programme. **The design spends everything on the comparison and deliberately removes every other source of pressure** — no challenger, no offer to decline, no unseen material. §8's second objection records why. A design judgement, no external warrant.

**3.2 Why the unit is an activity and not a competence.** The frame is not invented here. An entrustable professional activity is a bounded unit of practice — **"activities with a beginning and an end"** that **"are only entrusted to trained personnel"** — and the granularity test is behavioural: could a supervisor delegate this whole thing and walk away, and would the learner know when they had finished ([research/08-assessment-epas/ten-cate-epa-framework.md](/research/08-assessment-epas/ten-cate-epa-framework.md)). **That test is why the forward brief names tasks rather than topics, and why a line whose subject is a quality rather than an act is returned as incomplete.**

The same source supplies three further design constraints this occasion obeys. **Entrustment is expressed on an ordinal supervision scale, not a score**, and the sub-levels are where a programme's actual progression lives — the gap between *all findings double-checked* and *key findings double-checked* is real development that a five-point scale cannot see. **The summative standard requires multiple observers in agreement and multiple observations**, which is why a single assessor would not be defensible. And **entrustment expires through disuse**, which most academic assessment schemes never admit and which the brief's expiry field records.

It also supplies the shape of the live conversation. An entrustment-based discussion **"should probe the student's deep understanding of what was done, but should include 'what-if' questions, to grasp whether the learner would know what to do what unexpected or familiar situations would arise"**, because **retrospective explanation is evidence about the cases the learner met and counterfactual questioning is the only available evidence about the cases they did not.** The fixed closing question is that requirement in its shortest form.

**Its limits, and they are the objection in §8 stated in advance.** A clinical model with patients, legally responsible supervisors and a licensure endpoint; **a programme has to construct the stakes artificially and the construction is the hardest part of the transfer.** The primer is guidance from the concept's originator and **reports adoption rather than effectiveness**; it makes no empirical claim that entrustment-based assessment outperforms what it replaced. **The full evidence standard is expensive and this programme's version is reduced**, and the source's own instruction is that a reduced version should be described as one.

**3.3 Why the judgement is narrative, and why one assessor would not do.** This is the best-evidenced part of the design and the evidence is uncomfortable.

Assessors reading competency portfolios while thinking aloud **"mainly relied on narrative feedback to inform their judgment"**, and **"Grades and qualifications were merely used to confirm impressions based on narratives"** ([research/03-measurement-evaluation/govaerts-narrative-assessment.md](/research/03-measurement-evaluation/govaerts-narrative-assessment.md)). **The numbers were not the evidence; they were a check on a conclusion already formed from the prose.** That is the warrant for making narrative mandatory rather than optional.

Two further findings determine the mechanics. **Assessors anchor**: they reached judgements cyclically and then sought confirming or disconfirming evidence, and **"assessors were inclined to stick to their initial judgments even when confronted with seemingly disconfirming evidence"**, with final judgements more elaborate than preliminary ones **but not substantially different**. **That is the entire warrant for the double-blind lodging order** — it is not fairness theatre, it is the only available defence against an anchor on either side. And **agreement on the outcome conceals disagreement in the reasoning**: assessors reached similar pass-or-fail judgements while differing in assessment beliefs, in performance theories that **"tended to deviate from the formal assessment criteria"**, and in the inferences drawn from identical evidence. **A substantial share of rating variance is attributable to the rater rather than the performance, and it does not improve with rater experience.** **No figure from that source is reproduced here.**

The authors' remedy is the one §7 adopts: **high-stakes decisions should come from group discussion, with assessors thoroughly substantiating their judgements**, because substantiation makes divergent reasoning visible and therefore discussable.

**Its limits are substantial.** Single-institution medical education; mock portfolios; assessors who did not know the students; think-aloud methods cannot capture fast or automatic processing and participants may construct plausible explanations for a researcher; **narratives are not self-validating and can carry bias more easily than numbers.** Critically, these studies **establish that narrative-based judgement is what actually happens, not that it is more valid than score-based judgement.** The note also records that it substitutes two open-access papers for the author group's most-cited paywalled works.

**3.4 Why the defence is oral and unassisted, and why that settles the tool question.** The operational pattern comes from a course that removed the final exam and made oral verification structural: labs are graded in session by **"showing source code, demoing executions, and verbally answering a few questions"**, and collaboration is permitted **including outright copying**, because **"you will have to present and explain your solution to the TA on your own"** ([research/08-assessment-epas/cmu-oral-defense-and-token-economy-patterns.md](/research/08-assessment-epas/cmu-oral-defense-and-token-economy-patterns.md)).

**Once the assessment is oral, the artefact stops being the evidence and policing its provenance stops being necessary.** That is why the same course recommends AI tools without restriction and prohibits them in exactly two places, one of which is **"live conversations with course staff, whether over Zoom or in person"**. This occasion inherits the structure exactly: the brief may be prepared with any tool and the workflow requires one to be turned against it; **the defence is unassisted, and that is where the evidence is collected.**

**Its limits:** course policy documentation from a research university, not a study. **No effectiveness data is published** — no grade distributions, no comparison against the exam-based version, no measurement of whether oral verification detects understanding that written work misses. **Longevity is not measurement.** The policy describes no calibration among its assessors, and raises equity questions for non-native speakers and for learners with anxiety or speech-related disabilities that it does not address; **any adoption needs an accommodation path designed alongside it, and this programme's is not described here.**

**3.5 Why both directions of divergence are defects, and why understatement is not the safe option.** Efficacy beliefs are built primarily through mastery experience, and their formation is asymmetric in a way that bears directly on this occasion: **"It is more difficult to instill high beliefs of personal efficacy by social persuasion alone than to undermine them"**, and disbelief in one's capabilities **"creates its own behavioral validation"** by constricting the activities that would have developed the capability ([research/04-professional-formation/bandura-self-efficacy.md](/research/04-professional-formation/bandura-self-efficacy.md)). The same source warns that **"If people experience only easy successes they come to expect quick results and are easily discouraged by failure."**

**So a learner who understates is not being safe.** They are lodging a claim that will be received, may be believed, and will shape what they are given to do — which is the mechanism by which understatement becomes self-confirming. **Treating only overstatement as an error would produce a cohort competing at modesty, and modesty is not calibration.** This rule appears three times in the programme — at [S080](/wiki/seminars/S080.md) about a recipient, at [S085](/wiki/seminars/S085.md) about a claim, and here about oneself — and it is the same rule each time.

**The limits are important for this source.** The note covers a 1977 paper that was **not read** — paywalled — and a 1995 edited volume read in full, and **the two must not be conflated**; the author wrote the preface and one chapter, and a claim from a contributed chapter is that contributor's. The volume is a conference collection rather than a systematic review. **Direction and mechanism only; no magnitude appears here.**

**3.6 What a competency claim requires, and what this programme has therefore not done.** The accreditation standard separates two obligations more crisply than this repository does: **assessment** is "one or more processes that identify, collect, and prepare data to evaluate the attainment of student outcomes"; **evaluation** is "one or more processes for interpreting the data and evidence accumulated through assessment processes" and **"results in decisions and actions regarding program improvement"** ([research/07-accreditation-exemplars/abet-cac-criteria-and-student-outcomes.md](/research/07-accreditation-exemplars/abet-cac-criteria-and-student-outcomes.md)).

**Applied to this programme the verdict is immediate: it has designed an assessment process and performed neither assessment nor evaluation, because no cohort has run.** §10 states the consequence.

The standard also determines the *form* of what this occasion produces. Its required outcomes are verbs applied to situations, and three of the five are not primarily technical — communication, professional responsibility and teamwork sit alongside analysis and design as separately assessed obligations ([research/01-course-structure/abet-cac-and-cs2023.md](/research/01-course-structure/abet-cac-and-cs2023.md)). Its proposed changes add a definition — **"Professional dispositions are behaviors desired in the workplace"** — and require a comprehensive project that **"enables the application of appropriate professional dispositions."** **The terseness is the point: a disposition named as a behaviour must be observable, which means the curriculum must contain occasions where somebody watches.** The disciplinary guidelines make the argument with more force, asserting parity between dispositions and knowledge while conceding the field **"has not yet widely accepted the benefits of professional dispositions to the same extent"** ([research/07-accreditation-exemplars/cs2023-competency-and-disposition-materials.md](/research/07-accreditation-exemplars/cs2023-competency-and-disposition-materials.md)).

**All three sources' limits:** consensus committee products, not studies. **None offers evidence that its requirements improve outcomes and none claims to.** The accreditor's authority is procedural; the guidelines' is disciplinary consensus with no empirical validation against graduate outcomes; the proposed changes are proposals with a stated first-application cycle. **Cite them for what the field requires, never as evidence that a structure works.**

**3.7 Why the brief must be addressed to somebody.** An artefact addressed to an assessor is written for assessment. An artefact addressed to a future supervisor who will act on it is written for use, and the difference is visible in the granularity. **The addressee is a design device rather than a fiction.**

**3.8 Why lodging precedes revelation.** A brief written after hearing the assessors' reading is a response; one written before is an instrument. **§3.3's anchoring finding is the warrant**, and the ordering is enforced administratively rather than by anybody's restraint.

**3.9 Why at least one unsupervised claim is required.** Without it the occasion has no exposed surface and the learner risks nothing. **The claim is the only line an assessor can disagree with at cost to the learner**, and it is therefore the only place the calibration is genuinely tested.

**3.10 Why the firewall does not lapse on the last day.** A programme that has spent nine stages asking learners to report their own errors, and then uses those reports in its terminal judgement, has taught its last lesson and it is the wrong one. **The firewall is stated in the paper and published to learners, because a rule that is not visible is not a protection.**

## 4. Session design and its warrant

**Neither side sees the other before both are recorded.** The assessors' levels are formed from accumulated observation and written down before the brief is opened; the brief is lodged before the levels are revealed. Both orderings are enforced administratively rather than by anybody's restraint.

**The learner speaks first, briefly, on the divergence only.** Not a summary of the brief, which has been read, and not a response to the programme's opinion of them. **The narrowness is the design**: an unbounded closing statement becomes a valediction, and a valediction cannot be assessed.

**The closing question is fixed and published in advance:** what is the first thing you will get wrong, and who will notice? Publishing it does not weaken it. A learner who has prepared an answer has done the thinking the question exists to provoke, and the second half — who will notice — is the part that distinguishes a rehearsed humility from a working model of one's own error.

**There is no offer, no challenger and no trap.** The occasion produces its evidence from a comparison that happens whether or not the learner performs well, which is a property the design now values more highly than it did earlier in the programme, for reasons in §8.

## 5. Predicted failure modes and countermeasures

**Modesty performed.** Limits chosen for how they sound. *Countermeasure:* the calibration comparison, which reads understatement as a defect, published to learners in advance.

**The unclaimed competence.** *Countermeasure:* the mandatory unsupervised claim.

**Collapse on divergence.** The assessors read differently and the learner immediately adopts their reading. *Countermeasure:* none available beyond observation, and it is read directly as evidence — the same treatment given to conceding a defensible position at [S080](/wiki/seminars/S080.md).

**The disposition instead of the episode.** *Countermeasure:* each line requires an occasion, and a line whose evidence is a trait is returned as incomplete before the occasion rather than judged during it.

**The unusable brief.** Accurate, honest, and pitched where no supervisor could act. *Countermeasure:* the addressee, and the granularity requirement.

**The valediction.** The learner uses the closing minutes to summarise the programme. *Countermeasure:* the narrow speaking brief, enforced by the chair.

## 6. Retention architecture

**There is no later session to retrieve this.** The forward brief leaves the programme with the learner, which is the only artefact in ninety sessions of which that is true.

What is retrieved is the programme's own output into [Q1](/wiki/quarters/Q1.md): the reuse ledger from [S086](/wiki/seminars/S086.md), the limits register from [S088](/wiki/seminars/S088.md), and the amendment from [S089](/wiki/seminars/S089.md). **The arc closes into a cycle rather than ending, and the artefacts that cross the join are three documents and no people.**

## 7. Assessment and evidence generated

**An entrustment decision, and it is the programme's terminal judgement. No checklist, and nothing in this section is one.**

**The judgement.** Can this learner be trusted to judge the limits of their own competence, in a form somebody else can act on?

**How the level is formed.** From accumulated observation across the whole programme, by more than one assessor, on more than one occasion, with each assessor recording level and narrative independently before any discussion **and before the forward brief is opened**. The comparison tests that reading; it does not form it. Divergence of more than one sub-level between assessors is investigated by re-observation where possible and never resolved by averaging. The scale and the rater rules are at [Assessment Architecture](/wiki/program/03-Assessment-Architecture.md).

**What assessors read for**, none of which is a threshold:

- Fit between the brief and the accumulated record, **in both directions.** This is the judgement.
- Whether each line rests on an observation of an act rather than on a disposition.
- Whether at least one activity was claimed unsupervised, and whether it was held or given up for a reason.
- Whether divergence was engaged with rather than deferred to.
- Whether the brief would be usable by a supervisor who knew nothing else about the learner.

**Narrative is mandatory and must name a specific observed action.** A level recorded without one is rejected by moderation rather than by the assessor.

**The firewall.** Nothing the learner disclosed about their own error, anywhere in the programme, in any debrief or report designed to elicit it, may enter this judgement. It has been in force since [S079](/wiki/seminars/S079.md) and it does not lapse on the last day. **If it is breached once, it is dead for the cohort and for every cohort they speak to.**

**What the level does not record.** Whether the learner is competent. Whether they will do any of this in a real engagement. Whether the programme improved them. **It records that this learner, observed on these occasions, described the limits of their own competence in a way that matched what people watching them saw.**

**What is assessed, stated exhaustively.** The fit between the forward brief and the accumulated record, in both directions. Whether each line rests on an observation of an act rather than on a disposition. Whether at least one activity was claimed unsupervised, and whether it was held or given up for a reason. Whether divergence was engaged with rather than deferred to. Whether the brief would be usable by a supervisor who knew nothing else about the learner. **Nothing else is assessed.**

## 8. Objections and rebuttals

**Objection 1: an entrustment level formed on simulated occasions is a judgement about how somebody performs in a programme, presented in the vocabulary of professional supervision.**

At its strongest, and it is the same objection [WP-080](/wiki/whitepapers/WP-080.md) §8 concedes: entrustment scales were developed where a supervisor watches a trainee perform the real activity, with real consequence, repeatedly, over a long period. Here the activities are fictional, the recipients are role-players, the consequence is a record, and the occasions are few. **Borrowing the vocabulary imports a credibility the setting has not earned**, and this level in particular — the terminal one, the one a reader will treat as the programme's verdict — will be read as meaning what it means in the settings the scale came from.

**Conceded without qualification, and the concession is worse here than at [WP-080](/wiki/whitepapers/WP-080.md), because this is the level that leaves the building.** The mitigations are real and insufficient: the level is coarse, the narrative is mandatory and names an observed action, the programme publishes that rater variance in this class is large, and this paper states the limit where a reader will meet it. **The fix that was considered and declined** was to record no terminal level at all and let the module-level records stand. It was rejected because a programme that assesses nine stages and refuses to say anything at the end has pushed the summary judgement onto whoever reads the transcript, who will form one with less evidence and no narrative. **The objection is entitled to say that this prefers an overreach the programme controls to an inference it does not.**

**Objection 2: the design changed its terminal instrument to stop repeating itself, and it is still repeating itself elsewhere.**

At its strongest: a parallel author reported that four consecutive assessment occasions in earlier stages turn on the same device — an offer the learner is expected to decline — and that the design had begun conceding the repetition in its papers rather than correcting it. **This occasion and [S085](/wiki/seminars/S085.md) remove the device.** That is a correction and it is narrow, because the same design now repeats two other devices and this paper is the place to say so.

**First: the condition written before the event.** A disqualifying condition at [S005](/wiki/seminars/S005.md), an intervention condition at [S079](/wiki/seminars/S079.md), a refusal that must carry an observer and a date at [S080](/wiki/seminars/S080.md), a sealed prediction at [S085](/wiki/seminars/S085.md), and a brief lodged before revelation here. **Second: the fixed closing question**, which now appears in every session of Stage 9 and in both terminal occasions. Both are defensible as a spine and both are, on the evidence of their frequency, becoming a house style — which is what the repeated-device criticism was about in the first place.

**Conceded, and recorded rather than fixed.** The design's position is that the pre-commitment device is load-bearing in a way the decline-the-offer device was not: it changes what evidence exists rather than what pressure is applied, and its repetition across stages is the mechanism by which it is learned. **That is an argument and it is exactly the argument somebody would make to avoid correcting a habit.** The fix that was considered and declined was to vary the terminal occasion's instrument again, away from pre-commitment. It was rejected because the alternative instruments available all reintroduce an adversary, which is the thing being corrected. **A later reader who finds this unpersuasive should raise it at [S089](/wiki/seminars/S089.md), which is the mechanism this programme built for exactly that purpose, and which has now been used once — here, on itself, by its own author.**

**Objection 3: the closing concessions purchase credibility from the absence of evidence, which is a rhetorical structure rather than an epistemic one.**

At its strongest, and it is the objection this paper is least able to answer: a programme that states its own lack of evidence loudly, in its final paper, in front of the people it was performed on, has converted a weakness into a demonstration of rigour. **A reader who admires §10 has been persuaded *by* the absence of evidence rather than despite it.** The structure is also self-sealing in exactly the shape [S088](/wiki/seminars/S088.md) criticises elsewhere: an objection that the programme is unevidenced arrives pre-answered, so raising it confirms the design's self-account rather than disturbing it. **And the confession is cheap** — it costs the author nothing at the point of writing and it is paid for by whoever adopts the programme on the strength of how honest it sounded.

**Conceded, and this paper has no rebuttal that does not exhibit the structure it is conceding.** What can be offered is procedural rather than argumentative. §10 states the deficiencies **as deficiencies with named consequences** — that most predictions will never be measured is written as the design's largest weakness, not as humility, and §10 closes by naming what a reader should conclude. The predictions in §9 are written so that a cohort could falsify the design's central claims. **And the amendment mechanism at [S089](/wiki/seminars/S089.md) is real, is public, and is the route by which this objection would be lodged against a later revision.** **The fix that was considered and declined** was to move §10 out of the paper entirely and publish it as a separate, unattributed statement of limitations, so that it could not function as part of the argument. It was declined because a limits statement detached from the design it limits is read by nobody, which is the precise failure [S088](/wiki/seminars/S088.md) documents about every other practice this programme examined. **The cost of that decision is that the concession does double duty as an argument, and a reader who discounts §10 for that reason is making a defensible call.**

## 9. Falsifiable predictions and instrumentation

**Prediction 1.** Most forward briefs will contain more lines claiming limitation than lines claiming unsupervised competence, and the unsupervised claims will be pitched at narrower activities. *Instrument:* the briefs, with lines counted by type and coded for activity granularity.

**Prediction 2.** Divergence between the learner's reading and the assessors' levels will fall predominantly in the direction of learner understatement. *Instrument:* paired readings, with direction of divergence recorded per learner. **If divergence is predominantly overstatement, §1's account of the two pressures is wrong for this population** and the occasion's framing must be rewritten rather than its instrument.

**Prediction 3.** Learners who engaged with divergence rather than deferring to it will more often have named an occasion in every line of their brief. *Instrument:* the live record coded for deferral, against the briefs coded for occasion completeness.

**Prediction 4.** Answers to the fixed closing question will more often name what the learner will get wrong than who will notice. *Instrument:* the answers, coded for both halves.

**Prediction 5.** The double-blind lodging order will not prevent convergence: assessors who record a level before opening a brief will nonetheless revise their narrative after reading it more often than they revise their level, and the revised narratives will move towards the learner's language. *Instrument:* assessor narratives captured at both points, compared for level change and for lexical overlap with the brief. **This predicts the failure of the occasion's central countermeasure**, and it follows directly from §3.3's anchoring finding applied to the assessors rather than to the learner. **If it holds, the comparison measures the learner's writing and the assessors' susceptibility to it**, and the occasion's claim to measure calibration is unsupported.

**Prediction 6.** Learners will place their activities at whole supervision levels rather than at sub-levels in most briefs, despite the instruction, and the sub-level distinctions will appear more often in the assessors' records than in the learners'. *Instrument:* both sets of records, coded for scale granularity. **If this holds, the sub-level machinery §3.2 imports is doing work for the assessors and none for the learner**, which would mean the brief's design has borrowed a scale it does not teach.

**None of these has been measured, and the wider statement belongs here rather than in a footnote.** Every paper in this wiki closes with predictions naming an instrument, and not one of those instruments has been applied to anybody. No cohort has run this programme. **The design's own position, stated where it is least convenient, is that most of these predictions will never be measured at all** — and a body of work that predicts its own instrumentation will not be executed has conceded something about itself that its sources never conceded about theirs. **See §10.**

## 10. What this programme did not establish

**This section is numbered because it is not an appendix, and it is not gated.** The occasion requires a learner to state the limits of their own competence in a form somebody else can act on. A programme that demanded that and exempted itself would be teaching the opposite of its subject on the last day.

**Nothing in this programme has been measured.** Every paper closes with falsifiable predictions naming an instrument — hundreds of them, with coding schemes specified and artefacts defined. **Not one has been resolved, because no cohort has run.** By the accreditation standard's own vocabulary in §3.6, this programme has designed an assessment process and performed **neither assessment nor evaluation**. The apparatus is complete and it is machinery; it is not a result. **And the design predicts that most of those predictions will never be measured at all. That is its largest weakness, and it should be read as a weakness rather than as charming humility** — a body of work that specifies its own instruments and never applies them has built the most elaborate possible apparatus for not finding out.

**Stage 1 and Stage 9 are class 3 throughout, and both are written past their source.** The first infers a qualification practice from a corpus recording a single refusal and no declined engagement. The last infers a cycle the corpus implies in hundreds of scattered references and states as a model nowhere. **Both are arguments and neither is a finding.**

**The pedagogical design rests on studies of school and university learners.** Every study behind the generation-first pattern used in all ninety sessions is a school, university or laboratory study, and **its author's claim that the cycle works in corporate training and adult education is uncited in his own reference list** ([research/04-professional-formation/sinha-kapur-productive-failure.md](/research/04-professional-formation/sinha-kapur-productive-failure.md)). This programme's learners are working professionals on multi-week engagements. The convergent warrant from error-management training is a partial mitigation, not a rescue. **This is the most serious methodological objection to the programme's entire teaching pattern.**

**The practice corpus behind the programme reports no declined engagement, no non-synthetic failure and no outcome data of any kind** — no exclusion criteria, no cost side, no defect or incident data, no post-deployment record, no sustainment evidence, and not one reference or citation anywhere in it.

**And this occasion's own machinery is borrowed and reduced.** The entrustment primer reports adoption rather than effectiveness. The narrative-assessment studies establish what assessors do, **not that narrative judgement is more valid than score-based judgement**. The oral-verification pattern is course policy with no published effectiveness data and no described assessor calibration, and its equity implications are unaddressed there and here. The accreditation standards are consensus committee products offering no evidence that their requirements improve outcomes, and claiming none.

**What a reader should conclude.** Not that the design is dishonest, and not that it is admirable for saying so — §8's third objection is right that admiring the confession is a mistake. **Conclude that this is a designed and undemonstrated programme**, that its epistemic discipline is its strongest claim and its subject matter its weakest, and that the first cohort to run it will produce more evidence about it in one term than its authors produced in all of it.

## What a learner should take from this paper

**What the design claims.** That the professionally consequential form of self-knowledge is a statement somebody else can act on; that understating your own competence and overstating it are the same defect; and that a limit you inferred about yourself is a hypothesis while one somebody watched you hit is evidence.

**What the evidence licenses.** Nothing. Not about you, not about the programme, and not about the practice it describes.

**The closing statement, which is the programme's own.** The practice corpus this material rests on reports no outcome, no evaluation, no comparison and no measurement of the standard it proposes; no failure, no exclusion criterion, no cost side, no quality data, no post-deployment record and no sustainment evidence; and not one reference, study or citation anywhere. The change-management sources are commercially interested practitioner accounts with no independent evaluation between them. The published engineering handbook offers no comparative evidence that its practices outperform alternatives, and says so. **This programme has added an apparatus to that material and has not added evidence to it.**

What you have been assessed on is whether you can hold a claim and its warrant apart, in a practice that mostly does not, using an apparatus that has never been shown to produce that ability in anybody. **The apparatus is an argument. You are the first people it has been made to.** If it is wrong, [S089](/wiki/seminars/S089.md) is where to say so, and saying so is the last thing this programme asks of you.

**Cited from general knowledge, not verified here.** That self-assessment is performed with the instrument being assessed and is therefore unreliable in both directions; that rater variance in judgements of this class is large and a single observation by a single assessor is not defensible; that assessors given both narrative and level reason from the narrative; that assessors anchor early and elaborate rather than revise; and that a judgement lodged before a revelation is a different instrument from one offered after. That efficacy beliefs are built by mastery over obstacles and are easier to undermine than to instil. **Direction and mechanism only; no reliability figure, agreement statistic, rate or magnitude is asserted here or anywhere in this wiki.**

**Design reasoning with no external warrant.** The forward brief and its addressee; the calibration comparison and the double-blind lodging order; the reading of understatement and overstatement as the same defect; the mandatory unsupervised claim; the requirement that every claim carry an evidence class and a named silence; the narrow speaking brief; the fixed published closing question; the removal of the decline-the-offer device and the reasoning in §8; the concession that two other devices are now repeated; the application of an entrustment scale to simulated occasions at this frequency; the judgement in §8's third objection that a detached limits statement would be read by nobody; §10's characterisation of the unmeasured prediction register as the design's largest weakness; and all six predictions in §9.

**Grounded in vendor documentation, with its version and its silence recorded.** **The platform anchor of this occasion is not a product. It is the four-class discipline itself, applied by the learner to their own account** — which means the vendor material in scope is whatever the learner's own engagement relied on, and the standard they are held to is the one recorded at [research/06-microsoft-platform/agent-framework-and-evaluations-docs.md](/research/06-microsoft-platform/agent-framework-and-evaluations-docs.md): a complete, precise, definitional catalogue read through search-returned excerpts rather than full page fetches, with most evaluators marked preview and one consulted page an explicitly superseded variant. **It publishes no agreement rate with human judgement for any evaluator, no calibration data and no rationale for any default threshold**, while most of those evaluators are model-judged. **Assert no agreement figure for any of them, in this paper or in any learner's brief.**

**The silence that closes the programme is the aggregate one, and it is the reason this occasion exists.** **No vendor documentation known to this programme publishes what a person must be able to do before being trusted to operate, review, oversee or withdraw from an AI-bearing system they helped build.** Product documentation describes capabilities and configuration. Governance material defines what must be assessed before deployment and stops there. Delivery material describes what a team does while it is present. **Nothing published by any party with a commercial interest describes the competence required of the human in the loop, or the evidence that would establish it** — which is the largest silence this programme found, and is the reason its final occasion is about a person rather than about an artefact.

**And the silence closest to home.** The assessors forming a level today are an instrument with **no published agreement data, no calibration and no independent validation**, judging a learner in a programme that spent ninety sessions teaching what that means about somebody else's evaluator. The mitigations — more than one assessor, independent recording, mandatory substantiation, discussion before decision — are the ones the narrative-assessment literature proposes and are more than the platform's evaluator catalogue has. **They are not a calibration and this paper does not present them as one.** If any vendor publishes a competence or entrustment criterion for the people operating its systems, or an agreement rate for any evaluator it ships, this occasion acquires a stronger anchor and this paper records its version and what it still omits.
