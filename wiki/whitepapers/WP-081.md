# WP-081 — Maturity as an Evidenced Level

**Seminar day:** [S081](/wiki/seminars/S081.md) · **Module:** [M17](/wiki/modules/M17.md) · **Quarter:** [Q9](/wiki/quarters/Q9.md)
**Strands:** PE · ME · **Complexity class:** 6
**Platform anchor:** Purview DSPM for AI and Defender AI-SPM posture findings — a maturity claim that names the evidence behind it, against one that names a stage

> ## Release rule
>

> ## Vocabulary used here without definition
>
> | Term | Meaning |
> |---|---|
> | **Maturity instrument** | An ordinal scale across several dimensions, applied to an engagement or a system, producing a recorded position |
> | **Re-derivation** | Reaching the same position from the same artefacts, by somebody who did not produce them |
> | **Capability claim** | A sentence describing what a system does, at a stated scope, on a stated date — which can therefore be false |
> | **Instrument resolution** | The three properties every tool-derived finding inherits: what was scanned, how often, and how long results took to appear |
> | **Self-supply** | The defect in which an earlier step of a process produces the value a later step counts as evidence |
> | **Satisfiable proxy** | A check that reduces to whether some text mentions a thing, and is therefore passable by mentioning it |
> | **Evidence-derived closure** | Status established by searching the estate for the thing, rather than by somebody asserting it — taught at [S062](/wiki/seminars/S062.md) |
> | **Class 3** | Design reasoning with no external warrant — see [Whitepaper Standard](/wiki/program/05-Whitepaper-Standard.md) |

## In plain terms

Organisations that deliver work like to record how mature the result was. The instruments they use look like measurements: several dimensions, an ordinal position on each, sometimes a name for the overall band and a colour.

**They are almost never measurements, and the reason is structural rather than careless.** Whoever enters the position is usually on the delivering side, working from memory, at the moment everybody wants the engagement to be finished. Some dimensions are scored against documents the delivering team wrote. Some are scored against controls that exist in a plan and nowhere else. Nobody re-derives any of it, because the instrument does not ask anybody to.

This session does not try to fix the instrument. It asks a smaller and more answerable question: **what would somebody who does not trust you inspect, to reach the same position?** Where that question has an answer, the position has a provenance. Where it does not, the position is a claim about the claimant.

One prohibition governs the whole module and it lands hardest here. **This programme asserts no staged maturity model — not the vendor's, not anyone's — and the reason is that no staged model in any source available to it is accompanied by evidence that progression through its stages produces better outcomes.** That is a strong statement and it is meant literally. The models are proposed, described, illustrated with successful cases, and never compared against anything. So the session teaches maturity as an evidenced claim about a specific capability, and treats the ladder as a thing to be named and declined.

The platform makes the alternative concrete rather than merely arguable. Posture-management findings are the cheapest re-derivable evidence available to most engagements: a configuration is either present or absent, and a stranger can look. **They also come with bounds** — the assessment samples the estate rather than covering it, runs periodically rather than continuously, and reports after a delay — and a claim built on them inherits all three. Writing those bounds above the findings, rather than beneath them, is the single habit this session exists to install.

## Abstract

This paper defends replacing the defence of a maturity position with the specification of a procedure for re-deriving it, and defends teaching maturity without endorsing any staged model.

The argument runs in four parts. **First**, a level's substantive property is not its accuracy — which nobody can establish, because no maturity instrument known to this programme has ever been compared against an expert's reading of the same system — but its provenance, which anybody can inspect. **Second**, the ordinary failure of such instruments is not exaggeration but *self-supply*, in which an earlier step of a process produces the value a later step counts as evidence, together with *satisfiable proxies* that can be passed by naming a technology nobody deployed. **Third**, the standing prohibition on asserting any staged maturity model is not a stylistic constraint but the correct reading of the evidence: the sources contain staged models and contain no evaluation of them, and a programme that asserted one would be doing exactly what it teaches learners to detect. **Fourth**, the platform workflow exists to make one durable property physically observable — that every tool-derived finding carries a scan scope, a cadence and a latency, and that a claim resting on it is bounded by all three.

The paper concedes that no maturity instrument known to this programme has been calibrated against human judgement in any form; that the scales and their boundaries are asserted constants presented with the visual language of measurement; that a re-derivable position is not thereby a true one; and that a respectable position holds the honest response to an uncalibrated instrument to be refusal to feed it. This design chose otherwise and §8 says why.

## 1. The problem this session must solve

A recorded level is consumed by people who were not present, at a distance, as though it were an observation.

It travels further than anything else the engagement produced. A transfer manifest is read by two people; a level is read by whoever is scoping next, by whoever is deciding whether the model works, and by whoever is writing the case study. **It carries the least evidence of any artefact in the engagement and it is the most portable**, which is the worst possible combination.

Four specific defects make it worse than a casual estimate would be.

**Self-supply.** Where a process generates the material it is later assessed against, the assessment measures the process's willingness to assert rather than the system's state. In the specimen this programme uses, an early stage is instructed to infer missing security properties from good practice, forbidden from marking those inferences in the table, and told to resolve conflicts in the more secure direction; a later stage then scores the very cells that stage filled in. **A guess enters as an assumption, loses its marker by design, and is counted as evidence — with the bias pointing optimistic.**

**Satisfiable proxies.** Where a check reduces to whether some text somewhere mentions a thing, the check can be passed by mentioning the thing. In the same specimen, the evaluator's own operative definition of a sufficiently specific mitigation is that it names a concrete technology. **Naming a technology you have not deployed therefore scores fully**, and any team that learns the rubric can raise its position without touching the system.

**Silence on recall.** What the instrument did not consider is not deducted for. In the specimen, items that could not be bound to a named component and a numbered flow are dropped with a logged reason, which systematically removes everything about operators, supply chains, human process and out-of-band administration — and the evaluator then explicitly declines to deduct for anything missing. That is a defensible choice, and it means **the position is silent on the most consequential failure mode of the thing producing it.**

**Unstated resolution.** Where a dimension rests on a scanning tool, the position inherits the tool's scan scope, cadence and latency — and those three are almost never written down beside the finding. A posture assessment that samples a bounded subset of an estate on a recurring cadence with multi-day reporting latency produces a posture *estimate*. **Reported without its bounds it reads as a statement about the estate, and the difference between those two readings is where every governance argument in the following year will be lost.**

The session's response is to stop arguing about the position and start specifying its reproduction.

**Entry state: this-programme-only.** The session assumes evidence-derived closure from [S062](/wiki/seminars/S062.md), the number-generating-apparatus argument from [S068](/wiki/seminars/S068.md), the defended measurement and its attribution requirement from [S070](/wiki/seminars/S070.md), and the unmitigated list from [S055](/wiki/seminars/S055.md).

It also assumes the learner has met, at [S054](/wiki/seminars/S054.md), the general shape of a value that changes status without changing substance. **This session is that shape applied to an assessment rather than to a data flow**, and a learner who recognises the isomorphism will find the session easy in a way that should make them suspicious: the recognition is not the work, and the procedure is.

One platform prerequisite is real rather than nominal. The learner must be able to read a posture-management surface — an AI inventory and a data risk assessment — or must run the procedure against a sample tenant and record that they did. **The procedure is the assessed artefact, not the finding**, so an absent tenant is an inconvenience and not a blocker.

## 3. Theoretical basis

**3.1 Why provenance rather than accuracy.** Accuracy requires a criterion the field does not have. No maturity instrument known to this programme has been compared against an expert's reading of the same system, so there is no standard against which a position could be accurate. Provenance requires only that the derivation be inspectable, which is achievable, and it is what a downstream reader actually needs in order to discount the claim correctly.

**3.2 Why no staged model may be asserted, stated as a reading of the evidence rather than as a rule.**

The programme's standing prohibitions are recorded at [09-Durable-and-Perishable-Register](/wiki/program/09-Durable-and-Perishable-Register.md), and one of them forbids asserting a named staged maturity model. It would be easy to treat that as a house style. It is not.

The practice corpus behind this programme contains maturity matrices among its artefacts ([research/05-fde-craft/microsoft-accelerated-delivery-practice.md](/research/05-fde-craft/microsoft-accelerated-delivery-practice.md)). The same corpus reports **no failure of any kind** — every case study succeeds and the single failure narrative is a synthetic composite, fictional by its own admission — **no cost side**, **no quality evidence**, and **no sustainment evidence**: whether any delivered system survived, was maintained by the customer, or was still running months later is not reported once. Its own organising thesis is that only measurable benefit counts and everything short of it is activity.

That is the whole argument. **An instrument for scoring maturity, published by an organisation that has never observed an outcome, is a description of what its authors consider good.** It may well be excellent as a description. It licenses nothing about progression.

Two consequences bind the session's design. The first is that a staged model may be *named* — refusing to name what learners will meet next week is not honesty, it is avoidance — but must be named as something the programme declines to assert, with the reason attached. The second is that the productive move is downwards: from the stage to the capability, from the capability to the artefact, from the artefact to the observation. **Each step down loses generality and gains falsifiability, and the exchange rate is the lesson.**

**3.3 Why the instrument's resolution is a first-class part of the claim.**

The posture documentation this session anchors on is unusually good at stating its own operational bounds and unusually silent about its accuracy ([research/06-microsoft-platform/purview-dspm-for-ai-and-defender-ai-spm-docs.md](/research/06-microsoft-platform/purview-dspm-for-ai-and-defender-ai-spm-docs.md)). It publishes item ceilings, site caps, exclusions, assessment cadences, reporting delays and result expiry. It publishes **no detection rate and no false-positive rate**, and none is asserted here or anywhere in this wiki.

That asymmetry is consistent enough across the platform sources to be a property of the genre rather than an oversight by any author: every source states a latency or a sampling bound and none states an accuracy. The design consequence is direct. **A claim built on a sampled, delayed instrument is bounded by the sampling and the delay, and is silent on accuracy in both directions.** The session therefore requires the three resolution properties to be recorded above the findings, where a reader meets them first.

The documentation also supplies the session's best single argument, and it is worth stating in the paper rather than only in the session. Generative AI **"amplifies the problem of oversharing data"** because a retrieval system reads, instantly and on anyone's behalf, the over-permissioned content that has sat unread for two decades. **A latent misconfiguration becomes an active leak on the day the assistant is switched on**, which makes an AI deployment a security event rather than a feature launch. A maturity dimension about data governance that was scored before deployment is a dimension scored against a different world.

**3.4 Why a composite is treated as a defect rather than a convenience, and why a difference may not be one.**

Combining ordinal judgements into a single position produces arithmetic precision from inputs that have none. The computation may be reproducible and the formulas may be published — and where every input is a judgement, the precision is spurious. **A reproducible calculation on unwarranted inputs is more dangerous than an unreproducible one, because it invites checking of the part that is sound.**

The statistical literature sharpens this from a different direction. Reporting conventions in adjacent fields highlight a leading figure "but not necessarily to test that result for any kind of statistical significance", and two consequences follow that bear directly on maturity claims: a comparison on a small evaluation set frequently cannot distinguish the two things being compared at all, and where items arrive in related groups rather than independently an uncorrected interval is too narrow ([research/03-measurement-evaluation/miller-adding-error-bars-to-evals.md](/research/03-measurement-evaluation/miller-adding-error-bars-to-evals.md)). Estate findings cluster by site, by team and by template, which is exactly the condition that makes an interval too narrow.

**A maturity claim resting on a difference smaller than its own noise is not a claim.** The source is analytical rather than empirical, its illustrative magnitudes rest on stylised assumptions, and it is explicit that statistical validity says nothing about construct validity — a tight interval around a meaningless dimension is still meaningless. **No figure from it is reproduced.**

**3.5 Why claimed standing diverges from measured standing, and what that predicts about self-entered levels.**

There is a published worked example of a scoring system distorted not by technical error but by who controlled disclosure. Private testing with the ability to retract unfavourable results produced **"biased Arena scores due to selective disclosure of performance results"**, and participants were found to be **"overfitting to Arena-specific dynamics rather than general model quality"** ([research/03-measurement-evaluation/singh-leaderboard-illusion.md](/research/03-measurement-evaluation/singh-leaderboard-illusion.md)).

**A best-of-many reported as a single draw is a bias with a computable shape**, and it arises wherever the scored party chooses which attempts are disclosed. A self-entered maturity level has precisely that structure: the delivery team chooses the dimensions, the artefacts and the day, and nobody sees the attempts that were not entered. The mechanism transfers even though the domain does not, and it predicts optimism without requiring anybody to be dishonest.

**Its limits:** the audit's allocation figures are external estimates by its own account, its findings are specific to one platform at one time, and it establishes nothing about whether models improve. **No figure from it is reproduced.**

**3.6 Why the opening items are unscaffolded, and why exercise 3 exists.**

The opening items are a Productive Failure generation phase: learners generate answers to a problem they have not been taught, fail, and are then taught ([research/04-professional-formation/sinha-kapur-productive-failure.md](/research/04-professional-formation/sinha-kapur-productive-failure.md)). Three of the design's stated conditions bind here.

**Do not scaffold the generation phase** — which is why the release rule gates §§4, 5 and 9, and specifically why §4's enumeration of the routes to a free high score is post-session reading. **The consolidation phase must explicitly prompt comparison** — presenting a correct answer beside a learner's wrong one achieves nothing on its own, and only the condition instructed to compare them learned more, which is why exercise 3 requires the learner to return to their item-3 answer and name the difference before rewriting it. **The norms are part of the design** rather than a preamble to it, which matters in a session whose honest output is frequently a lower position than a colleague recorded.

**The warrant's limits are real and are conceded in §8.** Every study behind the design is a school, university or laboratory study; the source is a trade book; and no adult-professional study appears in its reference list.

**3.7 Why the re-deriver must be a stranger.** A procedure written by somebody who knows the answer will contain steps executable only by somebody who knows the answer. The commonest form is the imperative *check* with no object — a step that reads as an instruction and functions as a reminder. **Handing the procedure to a person without the context is the only reliable way to find those**, and this session is a rehearsal for [S085](/wiki/seminars/S085.md), where that actually happens.

**3.8 Why unreachable dimensions are the assessed part.** A procedure that reaches every dimension has almost certainly widened a rule until it fits. The unreachable list is where the honest work is, and it is the only part of the artefact that tells a reader something they could not have guessed.

**3.9 Why an agent is used adversarially here.** The session's claim is that a procedure must survive a re-deriver lacking the author's context. An agent supplied with the artefacts and the scale and nothing else is a cheap approximation, available before the real occasion. **It is a rehearsal instrument and not evidence:** an agent's disagreement locates an ambiguity and does not establish which reading is right. It also shares a model family with much of the tooling under assessment, and is therefore not independent of it — which the learner is required to record.

## 4. Session design and its warrant

**4.1 The movement sequence.** Situation, artefact, material, workflow, standard, failure modes, exercises, limits.

**The opening items are the instrument.** Item 3 — how you would have scored a dimension high without changing anything about the system — is the one the design cares about. Items 1, 2 and 5 are not filler: they are answered fluently, and the contrast between that fluency and the silence on item 3 is what makes the gap legible to the learner who wrote it.

**4.2 The dimensions in the supplied situation are constructed to be defeating.** Two are scored against documents the delivery team wrote and one against a control named in a plan and deployed nowhere. **This is not a trick and it is not unusual** — it is the ordinary way such a position is arrived at, and the situation reproduces it rather than inventing it.

**4.3 The three routes to a free high score**, which §4 exists to withhold until after the generation phase: score the dimension against an artefact your own side authored; satisfy the check by naming a technology rather than deploying one; or narrow the scope silently until the evidence you have covers it. All three are available in most real instruments, none requires dishonesty, and the third is the one learners find hardest to see because narrowing scope feels like precision.

**4.4 The session assesses the procedure, not the position.** A learner who concludes that the recorded level was correct and specifies how a stranger would establish that has done the work. A learner who concludes it was wrong and cannot say how anybody would find out has not.

**4.5 The deletion-versus-downgrade decision is deliberate and has no right answer.** A dimension that cannot be evidenced may be removed from the instrument, scored at its floor, or marked unreachable. All three are defensible, they have different consequences for whoever reads the position next, and the session requires the consequence to be stated rather than the choice to be justified.

**4.6 What the platform workflow is for.** It is not a tooling tutorial. It exists to make two things physically observable that no amount of prose achieves.

The first is the **inventory gap**. A learner writes down every AI workload they believe exists, then runs discovery, and the tool returns endpoints they did not list. **The gap is a fact about the learner's knowledge rather than about the estate**, and a maturity claim about an estate nobody enumerated is a claim about the remembered part.

The second is the **resolution shock**. Writing scan scope, cadence and latency above a list of findings changes how the findings read, permanently and in one direction. This is the cheapest durable habit the module installs and the only one that survives every platform change, because the three properties exist for every scanning instrument that will ever be built.

**4.7 The four infrastructure-as-code checks are chosen deliberately as the worked dimension.** Private endpoints, restricted endpoints, managed identity, identity-based authentication. Each is a configuration fact rather than a judgement; each is verifiable by a stranger without a conversation; and three of the four are about eliminating keys and network exposure, which makes them a coherent capability rather than a checklist. **A dimension whose evidence is a configuration is the easiest possible case, and a learner who cannot write a re-derivation rule for the easy case will not manage the hard ones.**

## 5. Predicted failure modes and countermeasures

**The imperative with no object.** Steps beginning *check*, *verify*, *confirm*. *Countermeasure:* the adversarial re-derivation in step 7, which fails on exactly these and fails visibly.

**The widened rule.** A dimension is reached by loosening what counts until something qualifies. *Countermeasure:* the requirement that at least one dimension be marked unreachable, which makes the honest option available and cheap.

**Defence of the recorded position.** The learner argues the level rather than specifying its reproduction. *Countermeasure:* the artefact's fixed structure, which has a column for the observation and none for the argument.

**Resolution recorded and then ignored.** The learner writes scan scope, cadence and latency at the top of the document, and then reports the findings beneath as though they characterised the estate. *Countermeasure:* the *therefore* sentence in exercise 4, which forces the bound into a consequence rather than leaving it as a preamble. **This countermeasure is the one §9 predicts will fail.**

**Agent used as a confirmer.** The learner supplies their own summary and receives their own position back. *Countermeasure:* the instruction is explicit that the agent receives artefacts and scale only, that it is asked to attack rather than review, and that the record of what it was given is part of the artefact.

**The band restored at the end.** The dimensions are re-derived honestly and then summarised into one overall position, which is the only sentence anybody will quote. *Countermeasure:* the standard forbids it explicitly, and the failure-modes list names it. **Neither is likely to be sufficient**, because the summarising sentence is what the reader downstream will ask for.

**The instrument critiqued instead of used.** A learner who has understood §1 too well produces an essay about why maturity instruments are worthless and no procedure. *Countermeasure:* none available in the session, and it is the failure this paper is most likely to cause. It is addressed at [S085](/wiki/seminars/S085.md), where a critique cannot be executed by anybody and the absence is visible within minutes.

## 6. Retention architecture

Retrieved by [S084](/wiki/seminars/S084.md), where the same evidence discipline is applied to a closure re-run, and by [S085](/wiki/seminars/S085.md), where the procedure is executed by somebody else without the author present. Also retrieved at [S088](/wiki/seminars/S088.md), where the position becomes one of the claims sorted into evidence classes.

**The knowledge retrieved is not the instrument.** It is the question *who could reach this without me*, which is the same question asked of a handover at [S080](/wiki/seminars/S080.md) and of a measurement at [S070](/wiki/seminars/S070.md), in a third costume.

The resolution habit retrieves differently and more widely. **Scan scope, cadence and latency are properties of every instrument the learner will meet for the rest of their career**, and the session deliberately installs them against a governance product because that is where they are published rather than because governance is the point.

## 7. Assessment and evidence generated

No specification item and no entrustment level. This session generates observation feeding the entrustment decision at [S085](/wiki/seminars/S085.md).

**What assessors record**, as narrative rather than as score: whether the procedure was written for a stranger; whether an unreachable dimension was named honestly or approximated; whether the learner distinguished what the estate contains from what a plan says it contains; whether the instrument's resolution was stated and then used; and whether a lower re-derived position was reported early or deferred to the end.

The decision to record this as narrative rather than as a mark follows the assessment literature this programme's architecture rests on: judgements of this kind are better recorded as reasoned narrative, because the useful information is *why* the assessor judged as they did, and because assessors reaching the same outcome frequently reach it by different reasoning that a score conceals ([research/03-measurement-evaluation/govaerts-narrative-assessment.md](/research/03-measurement-evaluation/govaerts-narrative-assessment.md)).

**Nothing else is assessed.**

## 8. Objections and rebuttals

**Objection 1: this replaces an unreliable instrument with an expensive procedure that produces the same unreliable position.**

At its strongest: re-derivability is not truth. A procedure can be perfectly executable by a stranger and produce a position that means nothing, because the scale it lands on has no derivation, its boundaries are asserted, and no evidence exists that a system at one position behaves differently from one at another. **The session therefore adds cost and a reassuring appearance of rigour to an instrument that should probably be discarded**, and a learner who has produced a beautiful procedure for scoring nothing has been taught to launder an unwarranted number.

**This is conceded in substance.** The scales are asserted constants, the bands have no derivation, and nothing in this programme establishes that any position means anything. The rebuttal is narrower than the objection: a reader who receives a position with an inspectable derivation can discount it correctly, and a reader who receives one without cannot discount it at all. **The fix that was considered and declined** was to teach learners to refuse to produce a maturity position at all. It was rejected because such positions will be produced whether or not this cohort participates, and an engineer who can only refuse has no move available in the room where the level is being entered. **The objection is entitled to say that this trades a clean refusal for a sophisticated complicity**, and this paper does not have an answer to that beyond the judgement that the trade is worth making.

**Objection 2: the self-supply defect is a property of one specimen and is being generalised without warrant.**

At its strongest: the inference-laundering path this paper describes — a value inferred, its marker stripped, its justification dropped at assembly, then the value counted as evidence — is documented in one internal agent pipeline read once. Generalising from it to maturity instruments as a class, or to the recorded position in the supplied situation, is exactly the move this programme criticises elsewhere.

The rebuttal is partial. The specimen is one artefact and the generalisation is this design's own. What supports it is weaker than a finding and stronger than nothing: the same corpus contains maturity instruments that are self-assessed by construction; the recorded position in the supplied situation is invented to have the defect; and the session teaches the learner to *look for* self-supply rather than asserting that it is present. **Whether it is common is unknown to this programme and unknown, as far as this programme can tell, to anybody.**

**Objection 3: refusing to assert any staged model leaves the learner unable to speak to the people who use them, which is everybody.**

At its strongest: staged maturity models are the lingua franca of the rooms this programme's graduates will work in. Procurement uses them. Governance boards use them. Partners are assessed on them. A graduate who has been taught that they license nothing will either say so and be ignored, or say nothing and be useless. **Worse, the prohibition is itself unfalsifiable in the form this paper states it** — "no source available to this programme" is a claim about a reading list, and reading lists are choices.

**Partially conceded, and the second half is conceded without qualification.** The prohibition is scoped to the sources this programme holds, and a staged model accompanied by an evaluation of whether progression improves outcomes would overturn it. The session says so, and exercise 5 is constructed so that a learner who finds one produces the counter-evidence themselves.

On the first half the rebuttal is that the session does not teach refusal to speak. It teaches the downward move: from the stage to the capability, from the capability to the artefact. **A graduate who can say "here is what our position on that dimension rests on" is more useful in a governance room than one who can only agree or disagree with the position.** **The declined fix was to teach one named staged model as a shared vocabulary**, flagged as unwarranted, so that learners could at least speak fluently. It was declined because a model taught fluently is a model absorbed, the flag does not survive the first re-telling, and the programme's own experience of this failure — recorded across several sessions where a named framework's vocabulary was adopted along with its assumptions — is that the vocabulary wins. **The cost of that decision is that graduates will be less fluent in the rooms where these models are spoken**, and this paper records it as an accepted cost rather than a solved problem.

**Objection 4: the platform anchor is a security product, and this is not a security session.**

At its strongest: teaching maturity through posture management conflates two things. A posture finding tells you about a control's configuration. A maturity dimension may be about delivery practice, about operational readiness, about the customer's own capability — none of which any scanning tool can see. Anchoring the session on the one dimension that happens to be machine-checkable teaches learners that maturity is what a scanner reports, which is worse than the error it replaces.

**Conceded as a real risk and addressed structurally rather than rebutted.** The artefact's *unreachable dimensions* section exists precisely so that the scannable dimension is visibly the exception. §4.7 states plainly that the configuration dimension is the easy case and is chosen because it is easy. **The declined fix was to anchor the session on a dimension no tool can reach**, which would have made the point more honestly and would have left the workflow with nothing physically executable — and a session whose workflow cannot be run is the defect this brief exists to prevent.

## 9. Falsifiable predictions and instrumentation

**Prediction 1.** Most learners will produce at least one procedure step that a stranger cannot execute, and most of those steps will be an imperative with no object. *Instrument:* the procedures, coded step by step for executability by a reader without the brief, with the imperative-with-no-object form counted separately.

**Prediction 2.** Answers to opening item 3 will be markedly less specific than answers to items 1, 2 and 5. *Instrument:* the five written answers per learner, coded *specific* / *generic* / *absent* by a second reader who does not know which item is the target.

**Prediction 3.** Learners will more often mark a dimension unreachable when their adversarial re-derivation disagreed with them than when it agreed. *Instrument:* the unreachable list against the recorded agent transcript.

**Prediction 4.** Learners who report a lower re-derived position will place that report later in their artefact than learners reporting a higher one place theirs. *Instrument:* position of the finding within the document, against its direction.

**Prediction 5.** A majority of learners will record the instrument's scan scope, cadence and latency as instructed, and then write findings that generalise beyond those bounds without noticing. *Instrument:* the artefacts, coded for whether any finding statement's scope exceeds the recorded scan scope. **This predicts the failure of the session's own principal countermeasure**, and it is included because a countermeasure whose failure rate is unmeasured is an assumption. If it holds, the *therefore* sentence in exercise 4 should be moved from an exercise into the artefact's required structure.

**Prediction 6.** Learners instructed to compare their item-3 answer against the criterion (exercise 3) will produce more routes-to-a-free-score in their final artefact than learners who read the same material without the comparison prompt. *Instrument:* a within-cohort split on whether exercise 3 was completed, against the count of self-supply and satisfiable-proxy findings in the submitted procedure. **This is the session's only direct test of the mechanism §3.6 rests on**, and a null result would mean the comparison prompt is ceremony.

**Prediction 7.** A noticeable share of learners will produce a critique of maturity instruments in place of a procedure. *Instrument:* the artefacts, classified as procedure, critique, or both. **If this share is large, §1 of this paper is doing the damage** and the session's opening should be moved after the workflow rather than before it.

**None of these has been measured.** No cohort has run this session, and the prediction register of this repository is machinery rather than a result — which is [S068](/wiki/seminars/S068.md)'s finding applied to this page.

## What a learner should take from this paper

**What the design claims.** That a position nobody else can re-derive is a claim about its claimant; that self-supply and satisfiable proxies are the characteristic defects of assessment instruments rather than exaggeration; that every tool-derived finding carries a scan scope, a cadence and a latency that bound anything built on it; and that the dimensions a procedure cannot reach are the part worth writing down.

**What the evidence licenses.** Nothing about whether maturity instruments measure anything. The description of one pipeline's assessment behaviour is verified as description. The platform's operational bounds are verified from first-party documentation whose accuracy claims are entirely absent. Everything about the procedure is this design's argument.

**Why the prohibition is not fastidiousness.** A programme that asserted a staged model would be doing, in public, the exact thing it teaches learners to detect: attaching the authority of a framework to a claim the framework never established. **The prohibition is the design taking its own medicine**, and it costs something — §8's third objection says what.

**No magnitude appears anywhere in this paper.** That is a rule of this repository rather than an accident of what was read, and it holds even where a source states a number in a full text that has been read.

**The concession worth remembering.** You are being taught to make a claim inspectable, not to make it true, and there is a respectable position holding that the honest response to an uncalibrated instrument is to decline to feed it. This design chose otherwise and §8 says why. Hold it to that.
