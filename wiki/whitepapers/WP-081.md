## In plain terms

Organisations that deliver work like to record how mature the result was. The instruments they use for this look like measurements: several dimensions, an ordinal position on each, sometimes a name for the overall band and a colour.

**They are almost never measurements, and the reason is structural rather than careless.** Whoever enters the position is usually on the delivering side, working from memory, at the point where everybody wants the engagement to be finished. Some dimensions are scored against documents the delivering team wrote. Some are scored against controls that exist in a plan and nowhere else. Nobody re-derives any of it, because the instrument does not ask anybody to.

This session does not try to fix the instrument. It asks a smaller and more answerable question: **what would somebody who does not trust you inspect, to reach the same position?** Where that question has an answer, the position has a provenance. Where it does not, the position is a claim about the claimant.

The best available model for the move comes from an unlikely place. Inside an agent pipeline that this programme uses as a specimen, one stage refuses to accept an assertion that a control is implemented and requires a file and a location instead — status re-derived from the workspace rather than ticked by a person. **That is the right shape, and it sits in the same pipeline as an assessment stage that grades a system by counting cells an earlier stage of the same pipeline filled in.** Both ideas are in one artefact. Telling them apart is the session.

## Abstract

This paper defends replacing the defence of a maturity position with the specification of a procedure for re-deriving it. The argument is that a level's substantive property is not its accuracy, which nobody can establish, but its provenance, which anybody can inspect — and that the ordinary failure of such instruments is not exaggeration but self-supply, in which an earlier step of a process produces the value that a later step counts as evidence. The session produces a re-derivation procedure, dimension by dimension, together with the dimensions the procedure cannot reach and the reason for each. The paper concedes that no maturity instrument known to this programme has been calibrated against human judgement in any form, that the scales and their boundaries are asserted constants presented with the visual language of measurement, and that a re-derivable position is not thereby a true one.

## 1. The problem this session must solve

A recorded level is consumed by people who were not present, at a distance, as though it were an observation.

It travels further than anything else the engagement produced. A transfer manifest is read by two people; a level is read by whoever is scoping next, by whoever is deciding whether the model works, and by whoever is writing the case study. **It carries the least evidence of any artefact in the engagement and it is the most portable**, which is the worst possible combination.

Three specific defects make it worse than a casual estimate would be.

**Self-supply.** Where a process generates the material it is later assessed against, the assessment measures the process's willingness to assert rather than the system's state. In the specimen this programme uses, an early stage is instructed to infer missing security properties from good practice, forbidden from marking those inferences in the table, and told to resolve conflicts in the more secure direction; a later stage then scores the very cells that stage filled in. **A guess enters as an assumption, loses its marker by design, and is counted as evidence — with the bias pointing optimistic.**

**Satisfiable proxies.** Where a check reduces to whether some text somewhere mentions a thing, the check can be passed by mentioning the thing. In the same specimen, the evaluator's own operative definition of a sufficiently specific mitigation is that it names a concrete technology. **Naming a technology you have not deployed therefore scores fully**, and any team that learns the rubric can raise its position without touching the system.

**Silence on recall.** What the instrument did not consider is not deducted for. In the specimen, items that could not be bound to a named component and a numbered flow are dropped with a logged reason, which systematically removes everything about operators, supply chains, human process and out-of-band administration — and the evaluator then explicitly declines to deduct for anything missing. That is a defensible choice, and it means **the position is silent on the most consequential failure mode of the thing producing it.**

The session's response is to stop arguing about the position and start specifying its reproduction.

## 3. Theoretical basis

**3.1 Why provenance rather than accuracy.** Accuracy requires a criterion the field does not have. No maturity instrument known to this programme has ever been compared against an expert's reading of the same system, so there is no standard against which a position could be accurate. Provenance requires only that the derivation be inspectable, which is achievable, and it is what a downstream reader actually needs in order to discount the claim correctly.

**3.2 Why the re-deriver must be a stranger.** A procedure written by somebody who knows the answer will contain steps that are executable only by somebody who knows the answer. The commonest form is the imperative *check* with no object — a step that reads as an instruction and functions as a reminder. **Handing the procedure to a person without the context is the only reliable way to find those**, and the session's structure is a rehearsal for the occasion at [S085](/wiki/seminars/S085.md) where that actually happens.

**3.3 Why unreachable dimensions are the assessed part.** A procedure that reaches every dimension has almost certainly widened a rule until it fits. The unreachable list is where the honest work is, and it is also the only part of the artefact that tells a reader something they could not have guessed.

**3.4 Why a composite is treated as a defect rather than a convenience.** Combining ordinal judgements into a single position produces arithmetic precision from inputs that have none. The computation may be reproducible and the formulas may be published — and where every input is a judgement, the precision is spurious. **A reproducible calculation on unwarranted inputs is more dangerous than an unreproducible one, because it invites checking of the part that is sound.**

**3.5 Why an agent is used adversarially here.** The session's claim is that a procedure must survive a re-deriver who lacks the author's context. An agent supplied with the artefacts and the scale and nothing else is a cheap approximation of that, available before the real occasion. It is a rehearsal instrument and not evidence: an agent's disagreement locates an ambiguity and does not establish which reading is right.

## 4. Session design and its warrant

**The dimensions in the supplied situation are constructed to be defeating.** Two are scored against documents the delivery team wrote and one against a control named in a plan and deployed nowhere. **This is not a trick and it is not unusual** — it is the ordinary way such a position is arrived at, and the situation reproduces it rather than inventing it.

**The session assesses the procedure, not the position.** A learner who concludes that the recorded level was correct and specifies how a stranger would establish that has done the work. A learner who concludes it was wrong and cannot say how anybody would find out has not.

**The deletion-versus-downgrade decision is deliberate and has no right answer.** A dimension that cannot be evidenced may be removed from the instrument, scored at its floor, or marked unreachable. All three are defensible, they have different consequences for whoever reads the position next, and the session requires the consequence to be stated rather than the choice to be justified.

**The closing statement is constrained to one sentence** — *this is the level, and this is what somebody who does not trust me would inspect to reach it* — because the failure mode is elaboration. A procedure that needs a paragraph of framing is a procedure whose first step is a conversation.

## 5. Predicted failure modes and countermeasures

**The imperative with no object.** Steps beginning *check*, *verify*, *confirm*. *Countermeasure:* the adversarial re-derivation in the working movement, which fails on exactly these and fails visibly.

**The widened rule.** A dimension is reached by loosening what counts until something qualifies. *Countermeasure:* the requirement that at least one dimension be marked unreachable, which makes the honest option available and cheap.

**Defence of the recorded position.** The learner argues the level rather than specifying its reproduction. *Countermeasure:* the closing sentence's fixed form, which has no room for an argument.

**Agent used as a confirmer.** The learner supplies their own summary and receives their own position back. *Countermeasure:* the instruction is explicit that the agent receives artefacts and scale only, and the record of what it was given is part of the artefact.

**The instrument critiqued instead of used.** A learner who has understood §1 too well produces an essay about why maturity instruments are worthless and no procedure. *Countermeasure:* none available in the session, and it is the failure this paper is most likely to cause. It is addressed at [S085](/wiki/seminars/S085.md), where a critique cannot be executed by anybody and the absence is visible.

## 7. Assessment and evidence generated

No specification item and no entrustment level. This session generates observation that feeds the entrustment decision at [S085](/wiki/seminars/S085.md).

**What assessors record**, as narrative rather than as score: whether the procedure was written for a stranger; whether an unreachable dimension was named honestly or approximated; whether the learner distinguished what the estate contains from what a plan says it contains; and whether a lower re-derived position was reported early or deferred to the end.

## 8. Objections and rebuttals

**Objection 1: this replaces an unreliable instrument with an expensive procedure that produces the same unreliable position.**

At its strongest: re-derivability is not truth. A procedure can be perfectly executable by a stranger and produce a position that means nothing, because the scale it lands on has no derivation, its boundaries are asserted, and no evidence exists that a system at one position behaves differently from one at another. **The session therefore adds cost and a reassuring appearance of rigour to an instrument that should probably be discarded**, and a learner who has produced a beautiful procedure for scoring nothing has been taught to launder an unwarranted number.

**This is conceded in substance.** The scales are asserted constants, the bands have no derivation, and nothing in this programme establishes that any position means anything. The rebuttal is narrower than the objection: a reader who receives a position with an inspectable derivation can discount it correctly, and a reader who receives one without cannot discount it at all. **The fix that was considered and declined** was to teach learners to refuse to produce a maturity position. It was rejected because such positions will be produced whether or not this cohort participates, and an engineer who can only refuse has no move available in the room where it is being entered. The objection is entitled to say that this trades a clean refusal for a sophisticated complicity.

**Objection 2: the self-supply defect is a property of one specimen and is being generalised without warrant.**

At its strongest: the inference-laundering path this paper describes — a value inferred, its marker stripped, its justification dropped at assembly, then the value counted as evidence — is documented in one internal agent pipeline read once. Generalising from it to maturity instruments as a class, or to the recorded position in the supplied situation, is exactly the move this programme criticises elsewhere.

The rebuttal is partial. The specimen is one artefact and the generalisation is this design's own. What supports it is weaker than a finding and stronger than nothing: the same corpus contains maturity instruments that are self-assessed by construction, the recorded position in the supplied situation is invented to have the defect, and the session teaches the learner to look for self-supply rather than asserting that it is present. **Whether it is common is unknown to this programme and unknown, as far as this programme can tell, to anybody.**

## 9. Falsifiable predictions and instrumentation

**Prediction 1.** Most learners will produce at least one procedure step that a stranger cannot execute, and most of those steps will be an imperative with no object. *Instrument:* the procedures, coded step by step for executability by a reader without the brief, with the imperative-with-no-object form counted separately.

**Prediction 2.** Learners will more often mark a dimension unreachable when their adversarial re-derivation disagreed with them than when it agreed. *Instrument:* the unreachable list against the recorded agent transcript.

**Prediction 3.** Learners who report a lower re-derived position will place that report later in their artefact than learners reporting a higher one place theirs. *Instrument:* position of the finding within the document, against its direction.

**Prediction 4.** A noticeable share of learners will produce a critique of maturity instruments in place of a procedure. *Instrument:* the artefacts, classified as procedure, critique, or both. **If this share is large, §1 of this paper is doing the damage** and the session's opening should be moved after the working movement rather than before it.

**None of these has been measured.** No cohort has run this session.

## What a learner should take from this paper

**What the design claims.** That a position nobody else can re-derive is a claim about its claimant; that self-supply is the characteristic defect of assessment instruments rather than exaggeration; and that the dimensions a procedure cannot reach are the part worth writing down.

**What the evidence licenses.** Nothing about whether maturity instruments measure anything. The description of one pipeline's assessment behaviour is verified as description. Everything about the procedure is this design's argument.

**The concession worth remembering.** You are being taught to make a claim inspectable, not to make it true, and there is a respectable position that says the honest response to an uncalibrated instrument is to decline to feed it. This design chose otherwise and says so in §8.

## Evidence status

**Verified in this repository.** That the practice corpus contains maturity instruments and that they are self-assessed; that a generated assessment in that material scores a system by counting properties an earlier stage of the same process supplied, with the marker for those inferences stripped by design and the bias set optimistic; that the evaluator's operative definition of a sufficiently specific mitigation is that it names a concrete technology, so a technology named and not deployed scores fully; that items which cannot be bound to a named component and a numbered flow are discarded with logged reasons, removing operator, supply-chain, human-process and out-of-band categories; that the evaluator explicitly declines to deduct for what is missing; that no calibration of any produced position against human judgement exists in any form — no gold standard, no held-out sample, no inter-rater statistic, no record of disagreement; that two assessing components in the same process produce positions on different scales and never reference each other; and that weights, allocations, band boundaries and partial-credit rules are asserted constants with no derivation. All recorded at [research/05-fde-craft/microsoft-agentic-threat-modelling.md](/research/05-fde-craft/microsoft-agentic-threat-modelling.md), on a complete reading. **These are verified as descriptions of what one organisation wrote. None is evidence that any of it is effective.**

**Cited from general knowledge, not verified here.** That a judgement stated before an observation is a different instrument from one offered after it; that combining ordinal judgements into a composite produces resolution the inputs do not carry; and that people asked to assess their own work under time pressure produce systematically different results from independent assessors. **Direction and mechanism only; no magnitude, rate or reliability figure is asserted here or anywhere in this wiki.**

**Design reasoning with no external warrant.** The re-derivation procedure as an artefact; the requirement that at least one dimension be marked unreachable; the treatment of unreachable dimensions as the assessed part; the deletion-versus-downgrade decision and the requirement to state its consequence rather than justify the choice; the use of an agent as an adversarial re-deriver; the fixed one-sentence closing statement; the generalisation of self-supply from one specimen to assessment instruments as a class; and all four predictions in §9.

**Grounded in vendor documentation, with its version and its silence recorded.** **This session has no platform anchor and relies on no vendor documentation for its content.** The silence worth recording is nevertheless specific and large: **no vendor documentation known to this programme publishes a derivation for any maturity or posture scale it ships, a comparison of any generated position against an expert reading of the same system, or a statement of what its instrument does not consider.** Scores are published with bands, names and severity colours and without any account of where the boundaries came from. If any vendor publishes a derivation or a calibration for such an instrument, this session acquires an anchor and this paper records its version and what it still omits.
