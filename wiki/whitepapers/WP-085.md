## In plain terms

This occasion asks one question and it is about the learner rather than about their claim: **can they be trusted to say what a claim about improvement actually rests on?**

The instrument is deliberately mechanical. The learner lodges a claim, a procedure for re-deriving it, and a sealed prediction of what somebody else will find when they run it. Then somebody else runs it, without the author present and without conversation. Then the two are put side by side.

**What that arrangement removes is the ordinary defence of a claim**, which is a conversation in which the claimant's fluency does most of the work. What it substitutes is a prediction the claimant made before they knew the answer, which is a different kind of evidence about the same person.

The claim itself is assembled rather than argued, and its shape is the module's closing position. **A maturity claim with its counter-evidence attached is the only kind worth making.** A claim without counter-evidence is not a stronger claim — it is a claim whose author either did not look or looked and did not say, and a reader cannot tell which. **The counter-evidence is not a caveat.** A caveat softens and protects the author; counter-evidence is a specific finding that argues the other way and exposes them. The grammatical test is quick: a caveat cannot be checked, and counter-evidence can.

**The platform makes the assembly honest by making its limits explicit.** Three instruments are used and their weaknesses deliberately do not overlap. Posture findings tell you about configuration and sample the estate. Gateway telemetry tells you about traffic and is silent on whether anybody was helped. Evaluation results tell you about scores and publish no agreement with human judgement. **A claim assembled from all three is bounded by the union of their silences, not by their intersection** — and stating that union is the part every assembled claim omits.

**The occasion is deliberately not a challenge dialogue**, and the reason is recorded in §8 rather than buried: this programme has used the same assessment device — an offer the learner is expected to decline — on consecutive occasions, and had begun conceding the repetition rather than correcting it. The correction is here.

## Abstract

This paper defends closing the maturity module with an entrustment decision formed around a re-derivation executed by a third party against a prediction lodged in advance, and defends requiring the claim under test to carry its own counter-evidence.

The argument runs in four parts. **First**, the substantive property of a maturity claim is whether anybody else can reach it; that is directly testable; and the ordinary defence format tests fluency instead, because a claimant who knows the estate is indistinguishable in a room from a claimant whose claim is sound. **Second**, a claim carrying its counter-evidence tells a reader both what was searched for and that the search was honest enough to report an unwanted result — which, absent any calibrated instrument, are the only properties a reader has to work with. **Third**, an assembled claim inherits the union of its instruments' silences: posture assessment is silent on accuracy and samples rather than covers; telemetry establishes traffic and not use; evaluation publishes no agreement with human judgement for any evaluator. **Fourth**, attaching counter-evidence is a voice behaviour before it is an analytical one, so the design requires it in the lodged artefact rather than under questioning — written before the room exists.

The occasion produces a claim, a re-derivation procedure, a sealed prediction and a live reconciliation classifying each divergence as absent evidence, a bad rule, or a different reading.

The paper defends the removal of the adversarial device that four prior occasions used; concedes that a re-derivable position on an uncalibrated scale is not a true one; concedes that the entrustment vocabulary is borrowed from settings this one does not resemble; and concedes that assessing whether somebody attaches counter-evidence is partly assessing the room they are in, which the design mitigates and does not solve.

## 1. The problem this session must solve

A maturity claim is defended in a room, by the person who made it, to people who were not there. Every property that makes such a defence go well is a property of the defender.

**Fluency helps. Confidence helps. Knowing the estate helps enormously, and it is the one thing the assessor cannot separate from knowing the truth.** A claimant who can answer any question about the system is indistinguishable, in that room, from a claimant whose claim is sound — because the questions are answered from the same knowledge that produced the claim.

The second problem is that a procedure written by somebody who knows the answer contains steps that only work if you know the answer, and its author cannot see them. This is not a failure of care. **It is the ordinary condition of any procedure that has never been executed by a stranger**, and it is why the occasion exists in this form rather than as a better conversation.

The third is that an assembled claim inherits limits its author did not choose. Three instruments are silent in three different directions and **none of them covers the others**; a document citing all three without stating what none of them can establish has quietly assumed that three partial views make a complete one. **They do not, and the arithmetic is a union rather than an intersection.**

The fourth is incentive. Everything about the close of an engagement rewards a claim pitched at the level that will be believed. Nothing rewards a claim pitched at the level that could be reproduced, and nothing at all rewards attaching the finding that damages it.

## 3. Theoretical basis

**3.1 Why entrustment rather than specification.** The property of interest is a judgement about the relationship between a claim and its evidence, exercised under a pull towards the flattering answer. Reducing that to a list would produce a checkable proxy and certify the proxy. Where the question is *how much supervision would this person need*, the answer is recorded as a supervision level with a narrative and never as a score.

**3.2 Why multiple observers and multiple occasions.** Rater variance in judgements of this class is large and a single observation by a single assessor is not defensible. Independent written judgement precedes deliberation, because assessors reaching the same level frequently reason from incompatible premises and deliberating first destroys the evidence that this has happened. Divergence beyond one sub-level is investigated rather than averaged. **Direction and mechanism only; no reliability figure is asserted here or anywhere in this wiki.**

**3.3 Why the prediction is sealed.** A prediction offered after an observation and a prediction lodged before it are different instruments, and only the second is evidence about the predictor. This is the same argument the programme makes about the pre-mortem at [S032](/wiki/seminars/S032.md) and about the intervention condition at [S079](/wiki/seminars/S079.md). **The occasion's contribution is to apply it to a claim about the claimant's own work.**

**3.4 Why the executor may not be helped.** The single most reliable way to destroy a re-derivation is to let the author explain their reasoning during it. The interval is therefore empty by rule, and the emptiness is the control. **A control that depends on the author's restraint is not a control**, which is why the arrangement removes the opportunity rather than prohibiting the act.

**3.5 Why divergence is classified rather than resolved.** Deciding who was right is a conclusion and takes a minute. Saying whether the difference was caused by absent evidence, a bad rule, or a different reading of the same evidence is the diagnostic work, and it is the only part that improves the procedure. **A reconciliation containing no case in which the author was wrong is read as a diagnosis that was not attempted.**

**3.6 Why understatement is a defect.** A claim pitched low enough to be certain is safe, defensible and useless, and it makes the occasion about nothing. Overstatement and understatement are read identically, which is the same rule applied to readiness at [S080](/wiki/seminars/S080.md) and to self-knowledge at [S090](/wiki/seminars/S090.md).

**3.7 Why counter-evidence rather than caveats, and why it must be lodged.**

A caveat is a general softening that protects its author and cannot be checked. **Counter-evidence is a specific finding that argues the other way and exposes its author**, and it can be checked. The distinction is grammatical before it is ethical, which is what makes it teachable.

The design's argument for requiring it is narrow. In a field with no calibrated instrument, a reader assessing a claim has almost nothing to work with: the scale is asserted, the bands have no derivation, and the claimant knows the estate better than the reader ever will. **What remains available is evidence about the claimant** — specifically, what they searched for and whether they reported a result they did not want. A claim carrying counter-evidence supplies both. A claim without it supplies neither, and the reader cannot distinguish a clean estate from an incurious author.

**Lodging matters more than attaching.** Counter-evidence produced under questioning is a concession; counter-evidence in the lodged document is a position. The design requires the second for a reason §3.10 gives.

**This is class 3.** No source available to this programme establishes that claims carrying counter-evidence are more accurate, more trusted or more useful than claims without it.

**3.8 Why three instruments, and why the arithmetic is a union.**

The occasion's claim rests on posture findings, gateway telemetry and evaluation results, chosen because their silences do not overlap.

**Posture findings establish configuration and sample the estate.** The four infrastructure-as-code checks — private endpoints, restricted endpoints, managed identity, identity-based authentication — are the cheapest re-derivable evidence available, because a configuration is present or absent and a stranger can look ([research/06-microsoft-platform/purview-dspm-for-ai-and-defender-ai-spm-docs.md](/research/06-microsoft-platform/purview-dspm-for-ai-and-defender-ai-spm-docs.md)). And the assessment runs over a bounded, usage-ranked subset, on a periodic cadence, with a reporting delay measured in days and item-level scanning capped. **Scan scope, cadence and latency bound anything built on it**, and **no detection rate and no false-positive rate is published — none is asserted here.**

**Gateway telemetry establishes traffic under an identity and nothing about use.** A dimensioned token metric attributes consumption to a receiving group, and the source's own formulation is the operative one: **dimensions are what make a metric answerable** ([research/06-microsoft-platform/apim-ai-gateway-docs.md](/research/06-microsoft-platform/apim-ai-gateway-docs.md)). It does not establish that anybody read the output, kept it, or was helped. **A claim treating a token count as evidence of value has laundered a volume into a benefit**, one layer below the error [S082](/wiki/seminars/S082.md) exists to remove.

**Evaluation results establish a score against a threshold and nothing about agreement.** The evaluation surface publishes no agreement rate with human judgement for any evaluator, no calibration data and no rationale for any default threshold, and most of its evaluators are model-judged. **Assert no agreement figure for any of them, here or anywhere.**

**The design consequence is one sentence and it is the section's whole content: an assembled claim is bounded by the union of its instruments' silences rather than by their intersection.** Posture is silent on accuracy, telemetry on value, evaluation on agreement. Three partial views do not compose into a complete one, and a document that never writes the sentence beginning *none of these can tell you* has assumed that they do.

**Limits carried:** the Purview page consulted describes itself as the classic version and states it has been replaced; the Defender material was read through search excerpts with structure reconstructed; agent-level capabilities move to a separate licence in 2026; gateway policy names, tier availability and preview status change frequently; and the gateway page recommends logging prompts and completions without discussing the privacy consequences.

**3.9 Why a null prediction is required, and how a small trusted sample may legally be combined with a large untrusted one.**

The lodged claim is a claim about change, and change claims fail statistically before they fail rhetorically. Reporting conventions highlight a leading figure "but not necessarily to test that result for any kind of statistical significance", and two consequences bind: a comparison on a small set frequently cannot distinguish the two states at all, and **where items arrive in related groups rather than independently an uncorrected interval is too narrow** ([research/03-measurement-evaluation/miller-adding-error-bars-to-evals.md](/research/03-measurement-evaluation/miller-adding-error-bars-to-evals.md)). Estate findings cluster by site, by team and by template, which is precisely that condition.

**The sealed prediction therefore requires a dimension on which no detectable difference is expected**, with the detectable difference stated in advance. It is the only part of the prediction that cannot be read as advocacy.

There is also a formal answer to the shape the learner is in — many machine-produced judgements, a budget to check few by hand — and both informal answers are wrong. Treating the machine's judgements as labels propagates its systematic error into the estimate; discarding them wastes the corpus. **Prediction-powered inference estimates the model's systematic discrepancy from the hand-checked sample and corrects the large-sample estimate with it**, propagating the correction's own uncertainty, and is stated as making **"no assumptions on the machine-learning algorithm that supplies the predictions"** ([research/03-measurement-evaluation/angelopoulos-prediction-powered-inference.md](/research/03-measurement-evaluation/angelopoulos-prediction-powered-inference.md)). Model quality buys narrower intervals rather than correct ones: **a bad model gives you wide intervals, not wrong ones.**

**Its conditions are strict and each is a way to misuse it.** The hand-checked labels must actually be gold standard, because the method corrects the model against the labels and **cannot correct error in the labels themselves.** The checked subset must sample the same population as the unchecked one; **convenience sampling breaks the guarantee silently.** Sample-size requirements do not vanish. And it says nothing about whether the estimated quantity is worth estimating.

**One limit is specific to this repository and travels with the citation.** The note was verified **at abstract level only**: its section structure is reconstructed rather than transcribed, the term used here for the correction was not confirmed line by line as the authors' own, and **no coverage rate, interval width, sample size or per-domain result is recorded or asserted.** Use the shape; do not quote the paper.

**3.10 Why attaching counter-evidence is a voice behaviour, and what that costs the assessment.**

The occasion asks a learner to write down, before an execution that may embarrass them, the evidence that argues against their own claim.

**That is speaking up when silence is cheaper**, which is the construct the psychological-safety literature studies. It is emphatically not niceness: the source is explicit that the construct "is about candor, about making it possible for productive disagreement and free exchange of ideas", that it "does not imply ease or comfort", and that it is not an environment in which people are excused from high standards ([research/04-professional-formation/edmondson-psychological-safety.md](/research/04-professional-formation/edmondson-psychological-safety.md)). Safety and standards are orthogonal dimensions, and the dangerous common case is high standards with low safety — which is a fair description of an assessment occasion.

The mechanism is a calculation rather than a disposition: the cost of speaking is immediate and certain and the benefit deferred and uncertain, so **silence is rational under the payoff structure** and exhortation does not change it. The lever is the response — the true test is what happens when somebody does speak up.

**The consequence for this occasion is uncomfortable and is stated rather than solved: if attaching counter-evidence is conditional on safety, then assessing it is partly assessing the room.** The design's only structural answer is to require the counter-evidence in the *lodged* artefact, written before the room exists. That is a mitigation, not a solution, and §8's third objection is where it is confronted.

**Its limits:** the leader's tool kit in that source is drawn partly from consulting experience and is **offered without evaluation**; the theoretically load-bearing findings sit in an earlier paper this repository has not read; and **no magnitude from it crosses into this wiki**, which is a namespace rule rather than a verification one.

**3.11 Why the judgement is narrative, and why the prediction is read aloud by somebody else.**

Assessors reading accumulated evidence **"mainly relied on narrative feedback to inform their judgment… Grades and qualifications were merely used to confirm impressions based on narratives"** ([research/03-measurement-evaluation/govaerts-narrative-assessment.md](/research/03-measurement-evaluation/govaerts-narrative-assessment.md)). The numbers were a check on a conclusion already formed from the prose.

Two further findings from the same work shape this occasion's rules directly. **Assessors reaching similar final judgements differed substantially in their reasoning** — which is why more than one assessor records level and narrative independently before any discussion, and why divergence is investigated rather than averaged, since deliberating first destroys the evidence that divergent reasoning occurred. And **assessors "were inclined to stick to their initial judgments even when confronted with seemingly disconfirming evidence"**, with final judgements more elaborate but not substantially different from initial ones. **That is anchoring in the assessors**, and it is the reason the lodged prediction is read aloud by a third party before the author speaks: the assessors' first impression should form on the prediction rather than on the performance.

**Its limits:** built on two open-access studies deliberately substituted for several paywalled works that were not consulted, in a different professional domain; the note records the substitution rather than concealing it.

**3.12 Why the frame is an activity rather than a person.**

An entrustable professional activity is a bounded unit of practice with a beginning and an end that a supervisor could delegate whole; entrustment is expressed as **an ordinal supervision level rather than a score**; and a summative decision "should include an agreement between multiple observers that this entrustment is justified, to avoid subjective bias, and multiple observations" ([research/08-assessment-epas/ten-cate-epa-framework.md](/research/08-assessment-epas/ten-cate-epa-framework.md)).

Three details are used directly. **An activity's description must state its limitations**, which is why the claim's scope is required to be narrower than the engagement and its excluded dimensions named. **The probing conversation must include what-if questions**, because retrospective explanation shows only that the learner understood the case they met and counterfactual questioning is the only evidence about the cases they did not — which is what the fixed closing question is. And **entrustment can expire** where the activity has not been practised: **a supervision level is a judgement with a date on it**, the same discipline this module applies to claims.

**Its limits:** a conceptual primer by the concept's originator, synthesising rather than evaluating; its empirical items are reported from prior studies rather than generated; **and whether the machinery works on this cohort, in this domain, is untested** — which this repository's own assessment architecture states.

## 4. Session design and its warrant

**The executor is a peer who worked a different part of the brief.** They receive the procedure and the estate and nothing else — no summary, no conversation, no clarification. If they cannot proceed they record where they stopped, and that becomes part of the finding rather than an interruption of it.

**The prediction is read aloud by somebody else before the author speaks.** This removes the commonest recovery, which is a restatement of the prediction that has quietly acquired the executor's result.

**The occasion is not adversarial and contains no offer to decline.** There is no challenger, no easy exit, and nothing the learner is expected to refuse. What produces the evidence is an execution and a comparison, both of which happen whether or not the learner performs well.

**Both directions of divergence are examined.** An executor who reaches a higher position than predicted has told the author something about their own pessimism, and a learner who investigates only the unflattering direction has a habit rather than a method.

**The closing question is fixed and published in advance:** which dimension of your claim is now weaker than you thought, and what would it take to strengthen it? Publishing it does not weaken it; a learner who has prepared an answer has done the thinking the question exists to provoke. **It is a what-if question in the entrustment sense** — it asks about a case the execution did not produce — and §3.12 is why it is the one question that is never omitted.

**What the assembly workflow is for.** It is not a tooling exercise, and a learner who leaves able to pull three dashboards and unable to state what none of them establishes has taken the wrong half.

It exists to make one property observable that argument cannot deliver: **whether the procedure survives its author's absence.** Everything else in this module can be produced by somebody who writes well. This cannot. Within minutes of the executor starting, it is visible whether the steps are executable by a stranger, and the failure is specific rather than general — they stop at a particular line, for a particular reason, and the reason is usually an imperative with no object. **A learner who has watched somebody else stop at their own instruction does not write that instruction again**, and no amount of being told about it produces the same effect.

**The counter-evidence search is timed deliberately.** Twenty minutes, three instruments, one instruction: find the finding that damages the claim most. The timing is not about efficiency. **Searching against yourself is procedurally harder than searching for yourself** — you know where the supporting evidence lives because you assembled it, and the opposing query has to be constructed from scratch — and a bounded interval makes that asymmetry visible rather than letting it dissolve into *I did look*.

## 5. Predicted failure modes and countermeasures

**The procedure only its author can run.** *Countermeasure:* the occasion itself, which fails on this immediately and visibly, and the rehearsal at [S081](/wiki/seminars/S081.md).

**The prediction adjusted.** *Countermeasure:* it is read aloud by a third party before the author speaks.

**Divergence attributed to the executor.** *Countermeasure:* the classification is mandatory per divergence, and executor error is a legitimate third class — so attributing everything to it is a visible pattern rather than a defensible move.

**The unfalsifiable claim.** Pitched so nothing could disturb it. *Countermeasure:* item 3 of the opening requires a predicted failure, and a prediction of none is itself the finding.

**The safe understatement.** *Countermeasure:* §7's rule that the two directions are read identically, published to learners before the occasion.

**The caveat substituted for counter-evidence.** The lodged document softens rather than exposing. *Countermeasure:* the artefact's `counter_evidence` block requires a finding, an instrument and a plain-words reason for making the claim anyway — three fields a caveat cannot fill.

**The instruments treated as covering each other.** Three sources cited, no union stated. *Countermeasure:* the `union_of_silences` field, which has to be written before the claim can be lodged. **Partial**, because a learner can populate it with a generality, and §9 predicts that many will.

**Traffic reported as value.** *Countermeasure:* the `silence` field on the telemetry instrument states the limit in the artefact itself, in the same place the findings are written.

**Executor discouragement.** A learner sent to execute a bad procedure concludes they have failed. *Countermeasure:* the firewall in §7 — the executor's own level may not be affected by what they found, in either direction — and the explicit position that an executor reporting they could not proceed has produced the occasion's most useful observation.

## 6. Retention architecture

This is the module's terminal occasion. Its artefacts are retrieved at [S088](/wiki/seminars/S088.md), where the claim is sorted into an evidence class, and at [S090](/wiki/seminars/S090.md), where this level is one input among several.

**The knowledge retrieved is not the maturity claim.** It is the sealed prediction as an instrument, which returns at [S090](/wiki/seminars/S090.md) in a different costume: a statement about oneself, lodged before the assessors' reading is revealed.

**The counter-evidence discipline retrieves more widely and outlives the programme.** Every claim a graduate makes to a customer, a governance board or a successor is a claim in a field with no calibrated instrument, and the only thing they can add that a reader can use is what they searched for and what they found that they did not want.

## 7. Assessment and evidence generated

**An entrustment decision. No checklist, and nothing in this section is one.**

**The judgement.** Can this learner be trusted to say what a claim about improvement actually rests on?

**How the level is formed.** From accumulated observation across [S081](/wiki/seminars/S081.md)–[S084](/wiki/seminars/S084.md), by more than one assessor, on more than one occasion. Each assessor records level and narrative independently before any discussion. The provisional level comes from the lodged artefacts and the execution; the reconciliation confirms, revises or overturns it. Divergence of more than one sub-level is investigated by re-observation where possible and never resolved by averaging. The scale and the rater rules are at [Assessment Architecture](/wiki/program/03-Assessment-Architecture.md).

**What assessors read for**, none of which is a threshold:

- Whether the procedure was executable by a stranger, and whether the learner had predicted where it would not be.
- Whether the sealed prediction was specific, falsifiable, and contained at least one expectation reflecting badly on the learner's own claim — including one dimension on which no detectable difference was expected, with the detectable difference stated in advance.
- Whether the lodged claim carried counter-evidence rather than caveats: a specific checkable finding, its instrument, and a plain-words reason for making the claim anyway.
- Whether the union of the instruments' silences was stated, and stated specifically rather than as a generality.
- Whether divergences were classified by cause rather than resolved by authority, and whether absent evidence was distinguished from a bad rule.
- Whether overstatement and understatement were treated with equal suspicion, **in either direction**.
- Whether the claim named who is responsible for establishing causation and held that under questioning.

**Narrative is mandatory and must name a specific observed action.** A level recorded without one is rejected by moderation rather than by the assessor. A conforming narrative states what the learner was asked to do, what they did including at least one specific observed action, what the assessor inferred, and what would have to change for the next level.

**The firewalls, of which there are two.** The executor's competence may not enter the author's judgement; a procedure defeated by carelessness and one defeated by its own defects look similar from outside, and the assessors resolve that by reading the reconciliation rather than by scoring the execution. **And the executor's own level may not be affected by what they found, in either direction.** An executor who reports being unable to proceed has produced the most useful observation available, and a programme that penalises that report will not receive it again. **If either firewall is breached once, the mechanism is dead for the cohort.**

**What the level does not record.** Whether the claim is true. Whether the engagement matured. Whether the instrument means anything. **It records that this learner, observed on these occasions, described what a claim rested on in a way that survived somebody else attempting to reach it.** The level carries a date and, following the entrustment frame, may expire where the activity is not practised.

**Nothing else is assessed.**

## 8. Objections and rebuttals

**Objection 1: the occasion certifies a procedure for producing a position on a scale that has never been shown to mean anything.**

At its strongest: no maturity instrument known to this programme has been calibrated against human judgement in any form. Its weights, band boundaries and allocations are asserted constants. Nothing establishes that a system at one position behaves differently from one at another. **An occasion that awards an entrustment level for the rigorous re-derivation of a meaningless number has taught its learners to be careful about nothing**, and has given them a professional vocabulary for doing it.

**This is conceded without qualification and it is the strongest objection in this module.** The mitigations are narrow: the occasion assesses the relationship between a claim and its evidence rather than the claim's truth, and that relationship is a general skill which happens to be exercised here on a maturity claim. **The fix that was considered and declined** was to move the occasion onto a claim with a real criterion — a defect count, an incident record, an assessor-return rate — where accuracy could in principle be established. It was rejected because every such criterion in the supplied situation is either unmeasured, partial, or arrives after the decision that needs it, which is itself the honest state of the engagement. **The objection is entitled to say that the design chose fidelity to a bad situation over the possibility of a meaningful assessment.**

**Objection 2: the design changed its assessment device to avoid repeating itself, which is a reason internal to the programme rather than a reason about the learner.**

At its strongest: four consecutive assessment occasions in earlier stages turn on the same move — an offer the learner is expected to decline — and a parallel reviewer reported that the design had begun conceding the repetition in its papers instead of correcting it. This occasion's instrument was chosen partly because it is different. **Novelty is not validity.** A device adopted because the previous one was overused has been selected on a criterion that has nothing to do with whether it measures what it claims to.

**This is conceded, and the concession is the point of recording it here.** The device was chosen for two reasons and only one of them is about the learner: the re-derivation directly tests the property the module is about, and it is not the repeated device. **The fix that was considered and declined** was to keep the challenge dialogue and vary its content. It was rejected because content variation does not change what a repeated device rewards, which is the ability to hold a position under social pressure — a real capability, already assessed three times, and not the capability this module is about. **Conceding a repetition and correcting it are different acts, and this programme had done the first three times.** The correction is recorded rather than announced, so that a later reader can see both what was repeated and what was done about it.

**Objection 3: requiring counter-evidence under assessment measures the room and calls it the learner.**

At its strongest, and it is the objection this paper finds hardest: attaching the finding that damages your own claim is a voice behaviour, and voice behaviour is conditional on whether speaking is safe. **A learner in a cohort where assessors have previously reacted badly to unflattering findings will lodge fewer of them, and the level they receive will record an environmental variable as a personal property.** Worse, the mechanism is invisible from inside — a cautious cohort looks like a cohort of cautious people — and the design has no instrument that could distinguish the two. **A programme that concedes elsewhere that teaching refusal without teaching its preconditions is close to negligent has here gone further and started grading the refusal.**

**Substantially conceded.** The design's structural mitigation is real but partial: the counter-evidence must be in the *lodged* artefact, written before the room exists, so that the immediate social cost at the moment of writing is lower than it would be under questioning. **That reduces the pressure; it does not remove it**, because the learner knows who will read the document.

Two further mitigations are worth naming and neither is sufficient. The narrative requirement forces an assessor to record a specific observed action rather than an impression, which makes a punitive reading legible to moderation. And the executor firewall protects the person most likely to produce an unwelcome finding. **The fix that was considered and declined was to assess only the procedure and not the counter-evidence**, which would have removed the objection entirely and would have removed the module's closing claim with it — because a maturity claim without its counter-evidence is precisely the artefact this stage exists to argue against. **The cost of the decision is that a cohort in an unsafe room will be assessed on a behaviour that room suppresses**, and this paper records that as an accepted cost rather than a solved problem. An assessor who suspects it is happening should say so in the narrative, which is the only route the design leaves open.

## 9. Falsifiable predictions and instrumentation

**Prediction 1.** Most executors will encounter at least one step they cannot perform, and most authors will not have predicted it. *Instrument:* the execution records against the sealed predictions, matched per step.

**Prediction 2.** Answers to opening item 3 will more often predict a difficulty than name a step and a reason. *Instrument:* the item-3 answers, coded *step and reason named* / *difficulty predicted* / *no failure predicted* by a second reader.

**Prediction 3.** Learners will classify divergences as absent evidence more readily than as a bad rule, even where the evidence exists and the rule is at fault. *Instrument:* the reconciliations coded per divergence, checked against the executor's record of what they inspected.

**Prediction 4.** Divergences in the flattering direction — where the executor reached a higher position — will receive shorter treatment in the reconciliation than divergences in the other direction. *Instrument:* the reconciliation, with treatment length and specificity coded by direction.

**Prediction 5.** A majority of lodged claims will populate the union-of-silences field with a generality rather than with a specific statement of what none of the three instruments can establish. *Instrument:* the lodged artefacts, with that field coded *specific* / *generic* / *absent* by a reader who has the three instrument definitions and not the claim. **This predicts the failure of the session's own principal countermeasure against treating three partial views as one complete one**, and it is included because a countermeasure whose failure rate is unmeasured is an assumption. If it holds, the field should require one sentence per instrument rather than one for the assembly.

**Prediction 6.** Counter-evidence sections will more often contain findings from the instrument the learner used least in building the claim than from the one they used most. *Instrument:* counter-evidence entries coded by source instrument, against the instrument distribution of the supporting evidence. **If this holds it is a finding about the search rather than about the estate** — that learners search where it is cheap rather than where the claim is weakest — and the timed search in the workflow should be re-specified per instrument.

**Prediction 7.** Independent assessor levels will diverge by more than one sub-level on a noticeable share of learners, and divergence will concentrate on learners whose procedure defeated their executor early. *Instrument:* paired independent judgements before deliberation, cross-tabulated with the execution's stopping point. **If divergence concentrates there, the occasion cannot distinguish a bad procedure from an incomplete observation** and the standard for an early stop needs writing.

**None of these has been measured.** No cohort has run this occasion, and the programme's own position is that most predictions in this wiki will never be measured at all.

## What a learner should take from this paper

**What the design claims.** That a claim only its author can reach is a claim about its author; that a prediction lodged before an execution is a different instrument from one offered after; that overstating and understating a claim are the same defect; and that **a maturity claim with its counter-evidence attached is the only kind worth making.**

**What the evidence licenses.** Nothing about maturity, and nothing about outcomes. The rater discipline is direction and mechanism from assessment literature in a different professional domain. The entrustment scale is borrowed from settings this one does not resemble, and that objection is conceded at [WP-080](/wiki/whitepapers/WP-080.md) §8 in terms that apply here unchanged. The three instruments are verified from first-party documentation that publishes no accuracy of any kind. The statistical correction is cited at abstract level and must not be quoted.

**No staged maturity model is asserted at this occasion or anywhere in this module**, and the substitute is a claim about one named capability with its evidence and its counter-evidence. **That substitution is this design's argument rather than a finding**, and a reader who wants a ladder will leave without one.

**No magnitude appears anywhere in this paper.**

**The concession worth remembering.** You are being assessed on the rigour with which you can reproduce a number that nobody has shown to mean anything, and partly on a behaviour whose availability depends on the room you are in. §8 concedes both and declines the available fixes, with the reasons. Hold the occasion to that reading.

**What the documentation does not say, recorded because it is the more important half.** **No vendor documentation known to this programme publishes a calibration of any generated score, grade, posture rating or quality tier against the judgement of a competent person assessing the same system**, and none publishes the derivation of the band boundaries such scores are reported against. Scores are shipped with names and severity colours — the visual language of measurement — attached to constants that are somebody's judgement. **The absence is uniform across every such surface this programme has examined**, which is why the occasion teaches re-derivation rather than trust.
