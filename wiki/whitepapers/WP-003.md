# WP-003 — The Problem Behind the Request

**Seminar day:** [S003](/wiki/seminars/S003.md) · **Module:** [M01](/wiki/modules/M01.md) · **Quarter:** [Q1](/wiki/quarters/Q1.md)
**Strands:** PE · ME · **Complexity class:** 1
**Platform anchor:** Microsoft Learn MCP Server with Copilot agent mode — generating alternative solutions to the customer's stated problem and testing which ones the documentation actually supports

> ## Release rule
>
> **§§4, 5 and 9 are post-session reading.** §4 explains why item 5 is left blank and why that is the design's intention, §5 names the failure the session is built to produce, and §9 states the predicted cohort behaviour. §§1, 2, 3 and 8 spoil nothing.
>
> **The reason is §3.2.** The opening items are a generation phase and the session's central item is meant to be unanswerable. A learner who reads §4 first will fill item 5 with something plausible and will not experience the emptiness the session is built around.

> ## Vocabulary used here without definition
>
> | Term | Meaning |
> |---|---|
> | **Premature zoom** | Investigating detail inside a problem category before establishing that the category matters to the informant |
> | **Behavioural question** | One answerable only by recounting something that happened, as against something the informant believes or predicts |
> | **Specimen answer** | An example response shipped alongside a question, which narrows the response set whether or not anyone intends it to |
> | **Satisficing** | Tolerating a poor arrangement because changing it costs more than living with it. A reported pain point may be one of these |
> | **Scooping model** | The mistaken picture in which researchers collect ready-made needs and engineers implement them |
> | **Mechanism versus configuration** | Two alternatives differ in mechanism if they fail for different reasons; if they differ only by a setting, they are one alternative |
> | **Stated operating envelope** | What a vendor publishes about the limits of its own product, as distinct from what a benchmark reports about its performance |
> | **Documented silence** | A question the first-party documentation does not address. A finding in its own right, and never a licence to infer a limit |

## In plain terms

A customer arrives with a request that names a product. The request is confident, detailed and internally consistent, and it is not evidence about a problem. It is evidence that a decision was taken somewhere you were not present, on grounds nobody has written down.

The move this session teaches is small and unglamorous: remove the technology from the sentence and see whether anything is left. If something is left, find the last time it happened, find who worked around it, and find out what that person did instead. If nothing is left, you have been handed a preference.

There is an obstacle, and it is the reason the session is placed this early. **Almost anyone will help you rank details inside a problem category once you have signalled that the category matters** — including people for whom the category matters not at all. The rank ordering they produce will be internally coherent, will feel like progress, and will be about nothing.

## Abstract

This paper defends teaching request-to-problem translation before any discovery technique, and defends teaching it *against* the practice corpus rather than from it. The argument runs in four parts. **First**, a stated solution is the normal form in which forward-deployed work arrives, and the accelerated tooling that makes this programme necessary changes the failure mode rather than the failure: solving the wrong problem quickly is characteristic rather than residual, because speed removes the interval in which the error used to become obvious. **Second**, the corpus's own discovery instrument systematically invites the customer to endorse the interviewer's solution category, in every block, and ships specimen answers alongside its questions — two structural properties visible on inspection and independent of whether any alternative works. **Third**, the countermeasure is cheap and checkable: anchor every claimed difficulty to a datable occurrence and a named workaround performer, and require that the restated difficulty admit at least three solutions differing in mechanism. **Fourth**, the platform workflow converts the third part from an assertion into an observation, because a restatement that yields only configurations of one product was written about a request.

The paper concedes that the countermeasure rests on practitioner literature with no controlled evidence behind it; that the datable-occurrence rule systematically discards chronic conditions; that generating alternatives early is in obvious tension with the session's own warning against premature solutioning; and that using an agent inside the learner's own generation phase risks contaminating the phase the design depends on.

## 1. The problem this session must solve

Forward-deployed work arrives as a solution more often than as a problem. The practice corpus behind this programme records the observation in its own voice: customers routinely arrive having chosen an approach without ever having articulated a difficulty; the real difficulty is often mentioned once, in passing, inside a long conversation about solutions; and some customers have genuinely lost track of what they were solving because it moved across handoffs.

An engineer who accepts the request as the problem is not lazy. They are doing the reasonable thing, which is to take the customer's account of their own situation seriously. **The session's whole difficulty is that the correction — treat the customer's account as weak evidence — sounds like arrogance and is not.** The customer owns the problem. The engineer owns whether the stated problem has been established.

There is a second reason the session sits here rather than later, and it is specific to this programme's subject. The same corpus names, from field experience, the failure the new tooling introduces: **solving the wrong problem quickly is the characteristic failure of accelerated delivery rather than a residual one.** Under the old economics, a misread brief revealed itself during the months it took to build the wrong thing. Under the new economics it reveals itself after delivery. A programme about velocity that did not open on problem identification would be teaching people to arrive at the wrong destination sooner.

## 2. Entry state and prior knowledge

**Either.** Some learners arrive having conducted user research and already hold the distinction between what somebody says they want and what they can be observed doing. Others arrive from environments where the requirement document was the beginning of thought.

The session equalises by making the artefact evidential rather than analytical. Both populations can produce a restatement; only one of them can produce a date, and the difference shows up on the page in a way it does not show up in discussion.

The workflow assumes [S001](/wiki/seminars/S001.md)'s configuration of the Learn MCP Server and nothing further. A learner who did not complete that step can complete it here; the instructions file is different and the server is the same.

## 3. Theoretical basis

**3.1 Load profile.** Intrinsic load is low and element interactivity is minimal. The surplus is spent on resisting a socially costly move — declining to accept a confident brief — which is the expensive part, and on a second demand the earlier draft of this session did not make: holding three candidate solutions open at once without committing to any. **This paragraph is a design judgement and carries no external warrant.**

**3.2 The opening items are a generation phase, and this one is multi-solution by design.**

Productive Failure asks learners to generate *multiple* solutions to a problem targeting a concept they have not been taught, fail, and then be taught ([research/04-professional-formation/sinha-kapur-productive-failure.md](/research/04-professional-formation/sinha-kapur-productive-failure.md)). The plurality is not decoration. A single attempt can be right by accident; several attempts expose the structure of the learner's understanding, including the parts that are wrong.

This session takes that structure literally rather than metaphorically. The opening items are the generation phase for the *pedagogical* concept, and the three-alternative requirement in the artefact is a generation phase for the *engineering* concept. Both are unscaffolded and both are meant to produce inadequate output.

Two of the design's binding conditions apply, and one of them was violated by the earlier draft. **Generation must not be scaffolded** — which is why the release rule gates §§4, 5 and 9, and why the session names the instrument item after the items rather than before. And **consolidation must explicitly prompt comparison**: presenting a correct answer beside a learner's own achieves nothing unless the learner is instructed to compare them. Exercises 3 and 5 now carry that instruction. **The evidence base is school, university and laboratory work, and its transfer to working professionals is an extrapolation this programme concedes rather than hides.**

**3.3 Why recall beats prediction, and why two anchors beat one.**

Asking what somebody did the last time a difficulty occurred yields different and generally more useful evidence than asking what they would do or what they usually do, because the first has a referent and the second two do not. Fitzpatrick's formulation places the defect in the question rather than in the respondent, and his usable-evidence list is entirely retrospective: current costs, time spent, allocated budget, existing workarounds, previous searches for a solution, switching costs, and the most recent occurrence ([research/05-fde-craft/fitzpatrick-the-mom-test.md](/research/05-fde-craft/fitzpatrick-the-mom-test.md)).

Recall is nevertheless selective and inaccurate, which is why the artefact requires a workaround and its performer as well as a date. **Two anchors are harder to confabulate than one**, and they fail differently: a date can be misremembered while a workaround has a person attached who can be asked.

The contextual-design literature supplies the deeper reason the customer's account is thin, and it is not a defect of the customer. People "know their own activities intimately but do not ordinarily articulate their structure"; familiar action becomes tacit, and "requests for features elicit solutions shaped by the tools people already know" ([research/05-fde-craft/beyer-holtzblatt-contextual-design.md](/research/05-fde-craft/beyer-holtzblatt-contextual-design.md)). Its four inquiry principles — context, partnership, interpretation, focus — describe an interviewer who proposes an interpretation and lets it be corrected while the relevant context is still present, which is a stronger move than any question in the session's own material.

Portigal adds the constraint that most often saves a restatement from being useless: a reported pain point may be tolerated because changing it costs more than living with it, and he rejects outright the "scooping model" in which researchers collect ready-made needs and engineers implement them ([research/05-fde-craft/portigal-interviewing-users.md](/research/05-fde-craft/portigal-interviewing-users.md)). **Insight requires inference. A restatement that required none is a transcription.**

**All three are practitioner texts and none of them measures anything.** Direction and mechanism only, and the citation limits are recorded in each note: no edition for one, first edition only for another, and a method rather than an evaluation for the third.

**3.4 Premature zoom is a property of instruments, not a lapse of attention.**

The construct this session is built to prevent is Fitzpatrick's: once an interviewer has signalled that a problem category matters, respondents will help rank details inside it even when the category is irrelevant to their priorities.

What makes it teachable here rather than merely nameable is that this repository holds a large, competent, real discovery instrument that does it structurally. The corpus's question bank asks the customer, in every section, where the interviewer's own solution category would help; it ships specimen answers with nearly every question; it contains no observation of any kind; and it has no consolidation step, offering instead a lookup table converting a single utterance from a single person into a prescribed intervention ([research/05-fde-craft/microsoft-accelerated-delivery-practice.md](/research/05-fde-craft/microsoft-accelerated-delivery-practice.md)).

**Those are structural properties visible on inspection**, and they would bias an answer whether or not the alternative this session teaches works. The instrument is not careless — it contains a mandatory breadth-first block that is an independent rediscovery of the anti-zoom rule, enforced rather than advised, which is better than the books manage. **That combination is why it is the right teaching object:** a serious instrument with a diagnosable defect teaches more than a straw one.

The corpus is internal and access-controlled. It is paraphrased throughout this programme, never quoted, never linked, and its organisation is not named.

**3.5 Why the alternatives must differ in mechanism.**

The artefact requires three alternatives, and the requirement is doing epistemic work rather than creative work. A difficulty that admits only the customer's original answer was not restated; it was renamed. The plurality is a test of the restatement, applied to the restatement.

The contextual-design method builds the same discipline in and is explicit about why: the team makes several visions before evaluating any of them so that early convergence does not suppress possibility, and it appoints a designated challenger to introduce alternatives when the group settles too quickly. Its crossing from evidence to invention is deliberately controlled — ideas are posted beside the observations that prompted them, and "Posted ideas are prompts for later design, not accepted requirements."

The same source supplies the rule that stops this collapsing into brainstorming, and it is the one this session leans on hardest: a user's proposed solution is evidence, not a specification. A requested handle may reveal a need to move the object rather than mandate a handle. **Every alternative generated in this session is a probe into whether the difficulty was understood, and none of them is a plan.**

**3.6 The division of labour with the agent is fixed, and the reason is structural.**

Retrieving a documented constraint and quoting it with a source is a task these systems suit. Judging which difficulty matters is not, and the argument that large language models do not plan in the sense the word usually implies bears directly on the boundary ([research/02-technical-foundations/kambhampati-llms-cant-plan.md](/research/02-technical-foundations/kambhampati-llms-cant-plan.md)). The agent proposes candidates and retrieves constraints; the learner decides which difficulty is real and supplies the occurrence, the workaround and the name — **none of which is in any corpus the model has read.**

There is a second reason the agent's output must be read sceptically. Capability in these systems is jagged: the boundary between competence and failure is uneven and does not track human-perceived difficulty, and outside it the failure mode is confident error rather than refusal ([research/04-professional-formation/dellacqua-jagged-technological-frontier.md](/research/04-professional-formation/dellacqua-jagged-technological-frontier.md)). **That note rests on an abstract; its shape is usable and no number in it is.**

**3.7 Why the feasibility test is a stated envelope rather than a benchmark.**

The workflow tests alternatives against what a vendor publishes about its own limits, not against performance comparisons. That choice is deliberate and it has a source. Published capability comparisons are systematically distorted by the conditions under which they are produced ([research/03-measurement-evaluation/singh-leaderboard-illusion.md](/research/03-measurement-evaluation/singh-leaderboard-illusion.md)), so a discovery check resting on standings would inherit the distortion.

A stated operating envelope is a different kind of evidence: definitional rather than comparative, and falsifiable by reading. The comparison family this session uses states some envelopes plainly — the initiation-model fork between agents that "rely on user-initiated interactions" and agents that "enable agents to trigger actions automatically, even without direct user input", and one product "designed for small-scale deployments, such as department-level solutions" ([research/06-microsoft-platform/microsoft-foundry-and-copilot-studio-docs.md](/research/06-microsoft-platform/microsoft-foundry-and-copilot-studio-docs.md)). **It states no scale envelope at all for most of the tools it compares**, which is why the instructions file makes `NOT ADDRESSED` a first-class classification rather than a gap to be filled by inference.

## 4. Session design and its warrant

**4.1 The opening sequence.** The five items narrow from the request to the stopping condition. Items 1 and 2 are mechanical and almost everyone completes them. Items 3 and 4 require evidence the learner does not have, which is intentional: the gap is what sends them looking.

**Item 5 is the instrument and it is designed to be left blank.** It asks what answer would make the learner abandon the problem category altogether. A learner who cannot name one has, without noticing, already accepted the category — which is exactly the condition under which a rank ordering of details feels like discovery.

**4.2 Why the artefact grew from three parts to five.** The earlier version of this session required a difficulty, an occurrence and a workaround. It produced restatements that were checkable and inert: nothing in the artefact could distinguish a genuine translation from a careful renaming, because a renamed solution also has an occurrence and a workaround.

The three-alternative requirement is the discriminator, and the documentation verdict is what stops the alternatives being wishes. **A restatement is now falsifiable by its own consequences** rather than by a reader's judgement of its wording.

**4.3 The commit-before-agent rule.** It is procedural, it is the same device used at [S001](/wiki/seminars/S001.md), and it is defeatable. It exists because a restatement written after reading the agent's cannot be distinguished from one written before, and the design would otherwise have no evidence about its own instrument. The design has no detector for a learner who ignores it, and §5 records that.

**4.4 Why step 4 withholds the original brief from the agent.** If the brief is supplied, the brief comes back. This is the same mechanism as the specimen-answer problem in §3.4, transposed: appending an example narrows the response set, and a stated solution is the most powerful example available. Withholding it is the only reliable way to find out whether the restated difficulty generates anything on its own.

**4.5 What the platform workflow is for.** It makes two things observable that prose can only assert. That grounding renders an answer accurate within a corpus and silent outside it, with nothing in an ungrounded output marking which side of the line you are on — the in-sentence citation rule is the apparatus that renders the silence visible. And that the number of *mechanisms* a restatement admits is a measurable property of the restatement rather than a matter of taste.

## 5. Predicted failure modes and countermeasures

**The renamed solution.** The most likely outcome: the learner removes the product name and leaves the solution intact underneath, so that "an agent that fills in the record" becomes "automatic completion of the record". *Countermeasure:* the three-alternative rule, which a renamed solution fails, because a renamed solution admits only configurations of itself.

**Alternatives that are one alternative.** The learner satisfies the rule with three settings of the same product. *Countermeasure:* the instructions file requires the alternatives to differ in mechanism and requires one to need no new software. This is a countermeasure applied by an executor to itself, which is the weakest kind, and §9's fourth prediction expects it to fail.

**Anchoring to the agent's framing.** A learner reads the agent's candidates and cannot afterwards recover their own. *Countermeasure:* the commit-first rule, which is procedural, defeatable, and for which the design has no detector. **Recorded as unsolved.**

**Interrogation.** A learner over-corrects, treats the sponsor as a hostile witness, and damages the relationship the engagement depends on. *Countermeasure:* none in this session. [S004](/wiki/seminars/S004.md) puts the same learner in a room they were invited into and makes the social cost visible; whether that is timely enough is unknown.

**The inferred limit.** A learner reads a limit published about one product as though it applied to another, and records `OUTSIDE ENVELOPE` where the honest answer is `NOT ADDRESSED`. *Countermeasure:* the instructions file forbids it explicitly and exercise 4 requires a documented silence to be produced. Both are instructions rather than enforcement.

## 7. Assessment and evidence generated

No entrustment evidence. One specification element at [S005](/wiki/seminars/S005.md): the problem stated without a technology in it, carrying a datable occurrence and a named workaround performer. **Assessed for presence and specificity, never for whether the restated problem is the right one** — which nobody in the room is in a position to know, because the brief is invented and no outcome exists.

The three alternatives and the documentation verdicts are **not** part of that specification element. They are the session's own instrument, kept out of the assessment deliberately, so that a learner is not rewarded for producing plausible-looking alternatives to a difficulty they have not established.

## 8. Objections and rebuttals

**Objection 1: the session teaches against its own primary source, on the strength of a different source with no better warrant.**

At its strongest: the practice corpus is a working instrument used by experienced engineers in real engagements; the interviewing literature this session prefers is a founder's handbook reporting no controlled comparison, no sampling method and no measured effect, whose own numerical guidance is explicitly anecdotal. Replacing one unevidenced practice with another is not an improvement, it is a preference.

**Conceded in part, and the concession is specific.** Neither source establishes an outcome, and this paper does not claim the substitution improves anything. What can be said without an effect claim is narrower: the corpus's question bank asks the informant to endorse the interviewer's solution category in every section, and ships specimen answers containing figures an engineer will hear as a scale. Those are structural properties of an instrument, visible on inspection, and they would bias an answer regardless of whether the alternative works. **The declined fix was to omit the corpus's discovery material entirely**, rejected because a learner who has never seen a leading question bank will not recognise the next one — and the next one will be their own organisation's.

**Objection 2: requiring a datable occurrence privileges problems that produce incidents and hides the ones that produce drift.**

At its strongest: chronic conditions — slow degradation, an accumulating backlog, a capability nobody has — have no last Tuesday. A rule that demands one will systematically discard them, and the discarded class contains most of the problems worth a multi-week engagement.

**Conceded without qualification, and no fix is offered in this session.** The workaround anchor partly compensates, because chronic conditions usually have elaborate workarounds even when they have no incidents — indeed the elaborateness of the workaround is often the only visible trace. That is a mitigation and not an answer. **The declined fix was to accept "a representative recent week" in place of a datable occurrence**, and it was declined because it reopens exactly the fluff channel the rule exists to close: a representative week is a generality with a date attached. The gap is left open here rather than papered over.

**Objection 3: requiring three alternative solutions before the problem is established is precisely the premature solutioning this session condemns.**

At its strongest, and it is the sharpest objection to the rebuild: the session spends its material arguing that a stated solution is weak evidence and that jumping to answers is the field's characteristic error — and then requires the learner to produce three answers before they have spoken to anybody. If a customer's single premature solution is a problem, three of the engineer's own are three problems.

**Not conceded, and the distinction matters enough to state precisely.** The alternatives are not proposals. They are a diagnostic applied to the restatement, in the sense the contextual-design method uses when it makes several visions before evaluating any: plurality exists to prevent premature convergence rather than to accelerate it. The session says explicitly that none of them is a plan and that a user's own proposal is evidence rather than specification. **But the objection identifies a real risk in delivery rather than in design** — a learner who leaves the session attached to alternative two has been harmed by this structure, and nothing in the session detects that. **The declined fix was to defer alternative generation to [S011](/wiki/seminars/S011.md)**, declined because it would leave the restatement unfalsifiable in the one session where it is written, which was the defect the rebuild set out to repair. §9's fifth prediction is where the harm would show.

**Objection 4: using an agent inside the learner's own generation phase contaminates the phase the design depends on.**

At its strongest: §3.2 requires generation to be unscaffolded, and an agent is scaffolding. A learner who asks a model for three mechanisms has outsourced the generation whose failure was supposed to teach them, and the commit-first rule is a procedural fig leaf with no detector behind it.

**Partially conceded.** The design's answer is that the two generation phases are separated in time and in object: the opening items are generated alone and committed before any tool is opened, and the agent enters only after the learner's own restatement exists. **The declined fix was to remove the agent from alternative generation and use it only for the documentation check**, declined because the documentation check alone reduces the session to a retrieval exercise and loses the observation that a restatement's mechanism-count is a property of the restatement. The concession is that the separation is enforced by instruction rather than by anything else, and §9's third prediction is the one that would catch it failing.

## 9. Falsifiable predictions and instrumentation

**Prediction 1.** Most learners will leave item 5 blank or answer it with a restatement of item 2. *Instrument:* the five written answers, coded *specific* / *generic* / *absent* by a second reader.

**Prediction 2.** Restatements written before the agent is invoked will share fewer nouns with the original request than restatements written afterwards. *Instrument:* a noun-overlap count between request and restatement, applied to a cohort split by sequencing.

**Prediction 3.** A substantial minority of learners will open the agent before committing their restatement, and will not report having done so. *Instrument:* file timestamps on the committed restatement against the first prompt in the session log. **This predicts the failure of the session's own countermeasure** — the commit-first rule is procedural and has no detector, and a rule whose violation rate is unmeasured is an assumption. If it holds, the rule should become a tool-enforced ordering rather than an instruction.

**Prediction 4.** A majority of first-attempt alternative sets will differ in configuration rather than in mechanism, despite the instructions file requiring otherwise, and the agent will not flag this about its own output. *Instrument:* the three alternatives, coded by a second reader as *distinct mechanisms* / *configurations of one*. **This is the second prediction of a countermeasure failing**, and it is included because an instruction an executor applies to itself is the weakest control in the session.

**Prediction 5.** Learners will more often revise their restated problem category when at least one alternative is classified `NOT ADDRESSED` than when all three are classified `SUPPORTED`. *Instrument:* the documentation verdict table against whether the restated category differs from the request's category, judged by a second reader. **If this fails, the documentation check is doing no discovery work** and belongs at [S011](/wiki/seminars/S011.md) as an architecture exercise instead of here.

**Prediction 6.** Learners who produce a datable occurrence will more often revise the problem category than learners who produce only a workaround. *Instrument:* the presence of a date in the artefact against whether the restated category differs from the request's, judged by a second reader. If this fails, the occurrence requirement is doing no diagnostic work and should be reduced to a recommendation.

**Prediction 7.** In the exercise 6 transcript coding, marks identifying a difficulty rather than a solution will cluster in the later half of the conversation. *Instrument:* position of each mark as a proportion of transcript length, aggregated across the cohort. **This tests a practitioner claim the corpus asserts and never measured**, and a null result would mean the buried-problem observation is folklore rather than a pattern.

## What a learner should take from this paper

**What the design claims.** That the normal form in which work arrives is a solution; that translating it back into a difficulty is an evidential act with a checkable output; and that a difficulty admitting only one mechanism was never translated.

**What the evidence licenses.** The interviewing moves are class 2 — direction and mechanism, no magnitude. The reading of the corpus's question bank is class 1, because the instrument's structure is recorded from a complete reading of it. The published capability boundaries are class 4, definitional and perishable. **The claim that any of this improves an engagement is class 3**, and no source behind this programme measures it.

**The concession worth remembering.** The rule that a difficulty must have a date will discard real problems that never produced an incident. Nothing in this session fixes that, and a learner working on a genuinely chronic condition should expect the artefact to fight them.

**No magnitude appears anywhere in this paper**, and none may be inferred from it.
