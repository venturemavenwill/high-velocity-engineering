## In plain terms

A customer arrives with a request that names a product. The request is confident, detailed and internally consistent, and it is not evidence about a problem. It is evidence that a decision was taken somewhere you were not present, on grounds nobody has written down.

The move this session teaches is small and unglamorous: remove the technology from the sentence and see whether anything is left. If something is left, find the last time it happened, find who worked around it, and find out what that person did instead. If nothing is left, you have been handed a preference.

There is an obstacle, and it is the reason the session is placed this early. **Almost anyone will help you rank details inside a problem category once you have signalled that the category matters** — including people for whom the category matters not at all. The rank ordering they produce will be internally coherent, will feel like progress, and will be about nothing.

## Abstract

This paper defends teaching request-to-problem translation before any discovery technique, and defends teaching it against the practice corpus rather than from it. The argument is that a stated solution is the normal form in which forward-deployed work arrives; that the corpus's own discovery instrument systematically invites the customer to endorse the interviewer's solution category; and that the countermeasure — anchoring every claimed difficulty to a datable occurrence and a named workaround performer — is cheap, checkable and teachable in one session. The paper concedes that the countermeasure rests on practitioner literature with no controlled evidence behind it, and that the session's use of an agent as a foil is an unmeasured demonstration.

## 1. The problem this session must solve

Forward-deployed work arrives as a solution more often than as a problem. The practice corpus behind this programme records the observation in its own voice: customers routinely arrive having chosen an approach without ever having articulated a difficulty; the real difficulty is often mentioned once, in passing, inside a long conversation about solutions; and some customers have genuinely lost track of what they were solving because it moved across handoffs.

An engineer who accepts the request as the problem is not lazy. They are doing the reasonable thing, which is to take the customer's account of their own situation seriously. **The session's whole difficulty is that the correction — treat the customer's account as weak evidence — sounds like arrogance and is not.** The customer owns the problem. The engineer owns whether the stated problem has been established.

## 2. Entry state and prior knowledge

**Either.** Some learners arrive having conducted user research and already hold the distinction between what somebody says they want and what they can be observed doing. Others arrive from environments where the requirement document was the beginning of thought.

The session equalises by making the artefact evidential rather than analytical. Both populations can produce a restatement; only one of them can produce a date, and the difference shows up on the page in a way it does not show up in discussion.

## 3. Theoretical basis

**3.1 Load profile.** Intrinsic load is low, element interactivity minimal. The surplus is spent on resisting a socially costly move — declining to accept a confident brief — which is the expensive part.

**3.2 Why recall beats prediction.** Asking what somebody did the last time a difficulty occurred yields different and generally more useful evidence than asking what they would do or what they usually do, because the first has a referent and the second two do not. Recall remains selective and inaccurate, which is why the artefact also requires a workaround and its performer: two anchors are harder to confabulate than one. **Direction and mechanism only; no magnitude is asserted.**

**3.3 Why the agent is used as a foil rather than a tool.** The session's second claim is that fluent restatement and genuine investigation are indistinguishable in finished prose. An agent produces the fluent version on demand, at no cost, which makes the claim demonstrable in minutes rather than assertable in a sentence.

## 4. Session design and its warrant

The five opening items narrow from the request to the stopping condition. Items 1 and 2 are mechanical and almost everyone completes them. Items 3 and 4 require evidence the learner does not have, which is intentional: the gap is what sends them looking.

**Item 5 is the instrument and it is designed to be left blank.** It asks what answer would make the learner abandon the problem category altogether. A learner who cannot name one has, without noticing, already accepted the category — which is exactly the condition under which a rank ordering of details feels like discovery.

The commit-before-agent sequencing is procedural and is the same device used at [S001](/wiki/seminars/S001.md). It exists because a restatement written after reading the agent's cannot be distinguished from one written before, and the design would otherwise have no evidence of its own instrument.

## 5. Predicted failure modes and countermeasures

**The renamed solution.** The most likely outcome: the learner removes the product name and leaves the solution intact underneath, so that "an agent that fills in the record" becomes "automatic completion of the record". *Countermeasure:* the artefact requires an occurrence and a workaround performer, and a renamed solution can supply neither.

**Anchoring to the agent's framing.** A learner reads the agent's restatement and cannot afterwards recover their own. *Countermeasure:* the commit-first rule, which is procedural and defeatable, and for which the design has no detector.

**Interrogation.** A learner over-corrects, treats the sponsor as a hostile witness, and damages the relationship the engagement depends on. *Countermeasure:* none in this session. [S004](/wiki/seminars/S004.md) puts the same learner in a room they were invited into and makes the social cost visible; whether that is timely enough is unknown.

## 7. Assessment and evidence generated

No entrustment evidence. One specification element at [S005](/wiki/seminars/S005.md): the problem stated without a technology in it, carrying a datable occurrence and a named workaround performer. **Assessed for presence and specificity, never for whether the restated problem is the right one** — which nobody in the room is in a position to know.

## 8. Objections and rebuttals

**Objection 1: the session teaches against its own primary source, on the strength of a different source with no better warrant.**

At its strongest: the practice corpus is a working instrument used by experienced engineers in real engagements; the interviewing literature this session prefers is a founder's handbook that reports no controlled comparison, no sampling method and no measured effect, and whose own numerical guidance is explicitly anecdotal. Replacing one unevidenced practice with another is not an improvement, it is a preference.

**Conceded in part, and the concession is specific.** Neither source establishes an outcome, and this paper does not claim the substitution improves anything. What can be said without an effect claim is narrower: the corpus's question bank asks the informant to endorse the interviewer's solution category in every section, and ships specimen answers containing figures that will be heard by an engineer as a scale. Those are structural properties of an instrument, visible on inspection, and they would bias an answer regardless of whether the alternative works. The design's fix is to teach the anchoring move and to hand learners the corpus's instrument as material at Stage 2 rather than hide it. **The declined fix was to omit the corpus's discovery material entirely**, rejected because a learner who has never seen a leading question bank will not recognise the next one.

**Objection 2: requiring a datable occurrence privileges problems that produce incidents and hides the ones that produce drift.**

At its strongest: chronic conditions — slow degradation, an accumulating backlog, a capability nobody has — have no last Tuesday. A rule that demands one will systematically discard them.

**Conceded without qualification, and no fix is offered in this session.** The workaround anchor partly compensates, because chronic conditions usually have elaborate workarounds even when they have no incidents. That is a mitigation and not an answer, and the gap is left open here rather than papered over.

## 9. Falsifiable predictions and instrumentation

**Prediction 1.** Most learners will leave item 5 blank or answer it with a restatement of item 2. *Instrument:* the five written answers, coded *specific* / *generic* / *absent* by a second reader.

**Prediction 2.** Restatements written before the agent is invoked will share fewer nouns with the original request than restatements written afterwards. *Instrument:* a noun overlap count between request and restatement, applied to a cohort split by sequencing.

**Prediction 3.** Learners who produce a datable occurrence will more often revise the problem category than learners who produce only a workaround. *Instrument:* the presence of a date in the artefact against whether the restated category differs from the request's category, judged by a second reader. **If this fails, the occurrence requirement is doing no diagnostic work** and should be reduced to a recommendation.

## What a learner should take from this paper

**What the design claims.** That the normal form in which work arrives is a solution, and that translating it back into a difficulty is an evidential act with a checkable output.

**What the evidence licenses.** The interviewing moves are class 2 — direction and mechanism, no magnitude. The reading of the corpus's question bank is class 1, because the instrument's structure is recorded from a complete reading. The claim that any of this improves an engagement is class 3.

**The concession worth remembering.** The rule that a difficulty must have a date will discard real problems that never produced an incident. Nothing in this session fixes that.

## Evidence status

**Verified in this repository.** That the corpus's discovery question bank asks the informant to endorse the interviewer's solution category in every block, ships specimen answers alongside its questions, contains no observation of any kind, and has no question asking what result would make the customer conclude the work had not succeeded, is recorded at [research/05-fde-craft/microsoft-accelerated-delivery-practice.md](/research/05-fde-craft/microsoft-accelerated-delivery-practice.md), which rests on a complete reading. That a request stated with unusual specificity signals that thinking happened elsewhere is recorded there as a practitioner judgement and not as a finding. The premature-zoom construct and the compliments-fluff-ideas taxonomy are recorded at [research/05-fde-craft/fitzpatrick-the-mom-test.md](/research/05-fde-craft/fitzpatrick-the-mom-test.md), which also records that the book reports no controlled comparison and no measured effect.

**Cited from general knowledge, not verified here.** That asking about a specific past episode yields different and generally more actionable evidence than asking about typical or future behaviour; that recall of a specific episode remains selective and should be corroborated; that attempting an answer before instruction improves later retention. **Direction and mechanism only; no magnitude is asserted.**

**Design reasoning with no external warrant.** The requirement that a restatement carry a datable occurrence and a named workaround performer; the claim that fluency and investigation are indistinguishable in finished prose; the use of an agent as a foil rather than an assistant; the commit-before-agent sequencing; the entire existence of Stage 1; and all three predictions in §9.

**Grounded in vendor documentation, with its version and its silence recorded.** The agent-mode behaviour this session relies on is the reading recorded at [WP-001](/wiki/whitepapers/WP-001.md) and not a fresh one: agent mode determines its own steps and iterates rather than stopping after a single response, and requests confirmation before running a terminal command or a tool that is not built in, with that confirmation being a dismissible default rather than a boundary, auto-confirmable per session, per solution or permanently. Read 2026-07-30, at which reading the agent-mode page carried two product version ranges simultaneously. **The documentation publishes no measure of how often the agent's self-correction loop converges, and none of how often it reports completion on work that is wrong** — which is precisely the property this session's foil depends on, and the reason the foil is read out rather than accepted.
