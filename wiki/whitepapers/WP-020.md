## In plain terms

This is the stage's class boundary. Everything before it supplied the criterion for done. This assessment does not, and the candidate must supply one.

The question is *when is discovery enough?* It has no general answer. It depends on the decision waiting on the discovery, on what being wrong would cost, and on whether something cheaper than more conversation could settle the uncertainty. **A candidate who states a rule tied to effort — a number of interviews, a number of weeks — has answered a different question**, and one whose rule appears after the evidence has written a description rather than a rule.

So the stopping rule is submitted separately, before anything else is opened, and the assessment examines its timing and its tie to a decision. **It does not examine whether the rule was right**, and it cannot, because nothing in the material behind this programme establishes when discovery is sufficient. Marking the content would mean asserting a threshold no source supplies.

The second thing assessed is routing. Whether people want the outcome, will fund it, have the authority and will adopt it are questions a conversation can move. Whether the system can be built, whether the data supports it, whether the model behaves, whether the integration holds — those are not, and no quantity of interviewing settles them. **A packet that answers a technical question with testimony has misrouted**, and that is the most common failure at this session.

The third thing is smaller and is the only part of the packet with an external referent. **Every platform claim carries a citation and a date**, produced by running the claims through grounded documentation. Most of the value is in the failures: a claim that comes back *not found* is not an embarrassment, it is an uncertainty, and an uncertainty is something this same assessment already requires you to route. **The claim that comes back unsupported is reliably one you were sure of**, which is why the instruction is to include the claims you believe.

## Abstract

This paper defends an assessment of discovery sufficiency that examines the existence, timing and decision-linkage of a stopping rule while explicitly declining to examine its content. The argument is that a sufficiency criterion produced after the evidence cannot discriminate; that the appropriate criterion is a function of the decision and the cost of error rather than of effort expended; and that no source behind this programme supplies a threshold, so a scheme marking the rule's content would be asserting one without warrant. The assessment additionally requires that each uncertainty be routed to a mechanism capable of settling it, and that absences be restored where the packet has become smoother than the engagement.

It also defends a platform movement that runs every claim about a platform capability through grounded documentation and records the empties. The argument is that **grounding makes an answer accurate within a corpus and silent outside it**, that an in-sentence citation requirement converts that silence into a recordable finding, and that a *not found* result is a routable uncertainty rather than a claim to delete — which makes it, in an assessment otherwise judged entirely against standards the design wrote, the packet's only externally checkable content.

The paper concedes that the scheme is nearly content-free by construction; that it cannot distinguish a well-reasoned rule from a lucky one; that the routing categories are not clean; that **nothing in it verifies that a citation supports the claim it is attached to**, so the requirement may reward the appearance of rigour rather than its substance; and that two of its three objections are answered partly by what the design cannot afford to check. It discloses one further defect: **this repository holds no verified note for the documentation server the workflow uses**, which is precisely the failure the assessment teaches a candidate to record.

## 1. The problem this session must solve

Discovery has no natural end. There is always another informant, another exception, another role that has not been heard.

Practitioners therefore stop for reasons that have nothing to do with the decision: the time allotted ran out, the sponsor grew impatient, the team felt they had enough, or somebody wanted to start building. **Each of those is a stopping event and none is a stopping rule**, and the difference matters because a rule can be wrong in a way that is visible afterwards while a stopping event cannot.

There is a second problem the session addresses in the same movement. Discovery expands to absorb uncertainties it cannot resolve. A team that has spent weeks interviewing about whether a model will perform adequately has spent weeks not finding out. **The uncertainty was misrouted, and the misrouting is invisible because the activity looked like diligence.**

The assessment's response is to require the rule first, and to require the routing explicitly.

## 2. Entry state and prior knowledge

**Entry state: this-programme-only, and the whole stage is load-bearing.** The packet is assembled from [S011](/wiki/seminars/S011.md), [S015](/wiki/seminars/S015.md), [S016](/wiki/seminars/S016.md), [S017](/wiki/seminars/S017.md), [S018](/wiki/seminars/S018.md) and [S019](/wiki/seminars/S019.md), and a candidate missing any of them submits a packet with a hole in a named place.

The prior it works against is that sufficiency is a property of effort. Candidates arrive expecting to be told how much discovery is enough, and the absence of that answer is the class boundary rather than a gap in the material.

## 3. Theoretical basis

**3.1 Load profile.** Evaluative rather than intrinsic. The packet's contents were produced in earlier sessions; the load is entirely in choosing and defending a criterion with no supplied standard, which is what complexity class 3 means in this programme.

**3.2 Why the rule must precede the evidence.** A criterion formed after an outcome is known cannot discriminate between outcomes. This is the same argument [S012](/wiki/seminars/S012.md) makes about readiness gates and [S066](/wiki/seminars/S066.md) makes about baselines. **One claim, three appearances, and the design says so each time.**

**3.3 Why the rule is tied to the decision rather than to effort.** The value of further discovery is the reduction in the probability of a costly wrong decision. A cheap wrong answer justifies a short discovery; an expensive one does not. **This is design reasoning; no source behind this programme supplies a threshold or a method for computing one.**

**3.4 Why routing is assessed.** Conversation can reduce uncertainty about whether people want an outcome, will fund it, have authority and will adopt it. It cannot establish that a technically difficult system will work. When the dominant risk is on the second side, the team must build or run a test earlier and accept that talking supplies nothing. **This distinction is the source's, the enterprise routing categories are this repository's inference from it, and neither is measured.**

The contextual design method supplies the generalisation that makes routing more than a taxonomy: its contribution is **locating each decision in the cheapest representation capable of answering it** ([research/05-fde-craft/beyer-holtzblatt-contextual-design.md](/research/05-fde-craft/beyer-holtzblatt-contextual-design.md)) — discover a workflow mismatch in the field rather than after deployment, reject a concept in a paper prototype rather than after building infrastructure. Routing is that idea applied to an uncertainty rather than to a design decision. **Read in full for this repository; its effectiveness claims rest on practitioner experience rather than comparative evidence.**

**3.5 Why every platform claim carries a citation and a date.**

A discovery packet contains claims about what a platform can do, and each one is a claim somebody will build on. They differ from the packet's other claims in one respect that matters for an assessment: **they are checkable today, cheaply, by the candidate, without asking the customer.**

They also decay faster than anything else in the packet. Two published facts from the platform's own selection guidance show the shape of it ([research/06-microsoft-platform/microsoft-foundry-and-copilot-studio-docs.md](/research/06-microsoft-platform/microsoft-foundry-and-copilot-studio-docs.md)). The basic setup for rapid prototyping **lacks network isolation**, while the standard setup provides fine-grained control over data, security and networking — so prototyping topology and production topology are different things and the transition is a deliberate act rather than a configuration discovered late. And one product is **designed for small-scale deployments, such as department-level solutions**, which is a scale claim the vendor publishes about its own product; a packet assuming enterprise rollout on it is outside the design envelope, and the symptoms appear as governance and lifecycle problems rather than as functional failures.

The same family records that the build options are **explicitly not mutually exclusive** and compose through MCP, which is the published fact that makes a documentation server and a Foundry project usable together in one workflow.

**Its limits, and they are severe.** Read through search excerpts rather than full page fetches, so page structure is reconstructed. **Highly perishable**, with product names and feature boundaries actively changing. Single-vendor selection guidance with a commercial frame, offering **no adoption data, no failure-rate data, no cost comparison and no evidence that its recommended mappings produce better outcomes.** Its authority is definitional: it says what the products are, not that choosing them this way works.

**3.6 Why grounding changes what silence means.**

A grounded agent answers from a fetched corpus. Where the corpus covers the question the answer is checkable; **where it does not, a well-behaved grounded agent says so, and an ungrounded one produces a fluent paragraph instead** — with nothing in the output to distinguish the two cases.

That asymmetry is why the in-sentence citation requirement is a mechanism rather than a formality. It converts an absence into an artefact: **"not found in the documentation" becomes a recordable finding** rather than a gap that closes itself. And an unanswerable platform question is an uncertainty, which the same assessment already requires the candidate to route.

The distinction the design is careful about is that **a cited claim is a claim with a provenance, not a claim with a warrant.** Documentation records what a vendor says rather than what a system does; the citation makes the claim's source and date inspectable, and inspectability is the whole of what it buys.

**3.7 Why routing becomes diagnosis once the system exists.**

The routing decisions made in this packet become, later, decisions about where a defect lives. A retrieval-augmented system's failures are stage-specific — corpus, retrieval, context assembly, generation — and the operator's first question should be **which stage failed** rather than which model to change ([research/02-technical-foundations/barnett-rag-failure-taxonomy.md](/research/02-technical-foundations/barnett-rag-failure-taxonomy.md)).

The same source carries a claim this assessment should hold rather than adopt: that **validation is only feasible during operation**, because the space of real queries cannot be adequately anticipated. If that is right, some uncertainties cannot in principle be settled by a discovery packet, and their honest routing is to production observability rather than to a spike — which is a stronger constraint on a stopping rule than anything else in this paper. **Its limits:** an experience report from three deployments; no frequency data, no severity ranking, no controlled comparison; and this repository's note records that the failure-point names are reconstructed rather than quoted.

**3.8 Why a null result is a statement about an instrument.**

One discipline from the measurement literature bears directly on a stopping rule: doing the analysis before the run is what turns "we tested and found nothing" into a statement about the world rather than about the test ([research/03-measurement-evaluation/miller-adding-error-bars-to-evals.md](/research/03-measurement-evaluation/miller-adding-error-bars-to-evals.md)). A stopping rule written in advance is the discovery form of that discipline, and a rule written afterwards has the same defect as a hypothesis formed after the data — it cannot discriminate. **Analytical rather than empirical; its illustrative magnitudes rest on stylised assumptions; no number from it is used here, and a commitment ledger has neither a variance nor a population, so only the discipline transfers.**

**3.9 Why this generates no entrustment evidence.**

Entrustment judgements concern activities a professional may be trusted to perform unsupervised, and they rest on observation of the activity across occasions rather than on inspection of an artefact ([research/08-assessment-epas/ten-cate-epa-framework.md](/research/08-assessment-epas/ten-cate-epa-framework.md)). This assessment inspects a packet and a defence at a higher complexity class. **It therefore produces no entrustment evidence and says so in §7**, which matters because a class-3 occasion at the end of a stage is exactly where a programme is tempted to overclaim what it has seen.

## 4. Session design and its warrant

The assessment is the packet, the rule and the defence.

**The stopping rule is submitted separately and first**, before the candidate opens their evidence, and the ordering is verifiable from the submission. This is the assessment's only genuinely tamper-resistant feature and everything else rests on it.

**The defence is of the rule and not of the finding.** The assessor holds the charter and the rule and nothing else, and asks three published questions: what decision turns on this, what would it cost to be wrong, and what did you stop before finding out. **The third has an answer.** A candidate who says *nothing* has claimed a complete discovery, which is the position the whole stage argues against.

**The absence restoration is a required pass over the packet.** Find every place where the document is smoother than the engagement was — the refusal, the unreachable person, the unanswered question, the contradiction one would like to have resolved — and put each one back. The corpus supplies the motivation: **its case studies all succeed, its only failure narrative is a synthetic composite and fictional by its own admission, and a practice whose artefacts never record a failure produces practitioners whose artefacts never record one either.**

**The agent's role is adversarial and is the stage's closing echo of [S001](/wiki/seminars/S001.md).** It is asked to argue that the discovery was insufficient and the decision should not be taken; what is kept and what is rejected is recorded. An agent that endorsed the packet was not used as an instrument.

**What the platform movement is for, and it is the packet's only externally checkable content.**

Everything else in this assessment is judged against a standard the design wrote. The stopping rule's timing is verifiable, but its content is not marked; the routing is checked against categories this repository inferred; the absences are restored against a judgement of what the engagement was like. **A citation pass is different in kind: whether a page says what the candidate claims it says is a fact about the world.**

The movement therefore does three things the rest of the assessment cannot. It produces **the packet's only claims with an external referent.** It converts a class of uncertainty into a routed item at the moment it is discovered, because a *not found* result is an uncertainty by construction. And it makes the candidate's own confidence legible to them: the claim that comes back unsupported is reliably one they were sure of, which is why the workflow instructs them to include the claims they believe.

**The instructions file is the mechanism and its verification step is the point.** Requiring an in-sentence citation is a specification; observing the instruction text in a request payload is the check that the executor received it. **An instructions file nobody has seen arrive is a file, not a specification** — which is the control-versus-check distinction this programme has been making since [S001](/wiki/seminars/S001.md), applied here to the assessment's own instrument.

**And the movement is where this paper discloses a defect in itself.** This repository holds **no dedicated research note for the documentation server the workflow uses.** Its properties are carried forward from an earlier session rather than verified here. That is precisely the failure the movement teaches a candidate to record, it is recorded, and it is not repaired by inference.

## 5. Predicted failure modes and countermeasures

**The effort rule.** The likeliest failure. A rule expressed as an amount of discovery. *Countermeasure:* the specification item requiring the rule be tied to the decision, and the first defence question.

**The retrospective rule.** *Countermeasure:* separate prior submission, which is the only structural protection and is the reason the assessment is arranged as it is.

**Misrouting.** A feasibility, data-quality or model-behaviour uncertainty supported by interview testimony. *Countermeasure:* the routing requirement and its specification item.

**The smoothed packet.** *Countermeasure:* the absence-restoration pass, plus the specification item requiring a recorded absence.

**The confirmed discovery.** Everything learned agreed with what the candidate brought, and this is reported as success. *Countermeasure:* partial. The specification permits a confirmatory finding provided it is stated as the finding, which makes the outcome legible but does not distinguish a genuinely confirmed hypothesis from a discovery that only asked friendly questions. **The [S016](/wiki/seminars/S016.md) repair is the upstream protection and it is not re-checked here.**

**The citation as decoration.** A candidate attaches a page and a date to every platform claim and never notices that one of the pages does not support the claim it is attached to. *Countermeasure:* none within the movement. A second reader spot-checking citations would catch it and the assessment does not budget for one. **A cited claim is a claim with a provenance, not a claim with a warrant**, and this assessment can see the first and not the second.

**The unanswered claim quietly dropped.** A candidate runs the citation pass, finds three claims unsupported, and removes them from the packet rather than routing them. *Countermeasure:* the absence-restoration pass and specification item 5, which require a recorded absence — but **a claim deleted before the pass leaves no trace to restore**, and the countermeasure runs after the deletion would have happened. Prediction 6 measures it.

**The ungrounded answer accepted.** The agent answers a documentation question from general knowledge and the candidate cannot tell. *Countermeasure:* the in-sentence citation requirement plus the payload verification step. **This is the design's strongest platform countermeasure and it is still only as good as the candidate's willingness to look at a payload once.**

Retrieved by [S021](/wiki/seminars/S021.md), [S033](/wiki/seminars/S033.md), [S066](/wiki/seminars/S066.md) and [S088](/wiki/seminars/S088.md).

The stopping rule returns at [S088](/wiki/seminars/S088.md) as an engagement-level question about what was never established, which is the same instrument at a different scale.

**The citation habit exports separately and is the module's smallest durable export.** *A platform claim carries a page and a date* is retrieved wherever this programme requires a perishable fact to be used — which, given how much of the platform layer decays in months, is most places. The habit it actually installs is narrower than the rule: **notice that a claim you are confident about has no source, and route it rather than dropping it.**

## 7. Assessment and evidence generated

**Seven specification items, published in [S020](/wiki/seminars/S020.md) and repeated here.** Each checked present and adequate, or not.

1. A decision changed, and the packet says which and in what direction. A discovery that confirmed everything is submitted with that stated as its finding.
2. Observation, report and inference are separated throughout the packet.
3. The stopping rule was stated before the evidence that satisfied it, verifiably from the submission.
4. The stopping rule is tied to the decision waiting on it, not to an amount of effort.
5. An absence is recorded — a refusal, a non-advancement, a population not sampled, or a question nobody could answer.
6. At least one uncertainty is routed away from conversation to a mechanism that could settle it.
7. The central claim names what would withdraw it.

**Nothing else is assessed.** Not the number of informants, not whether the customer committed, not whether the finding is correct, not presentation quality, and not whether the stopping rule matches anybody else's.

**Entrustment evidence generated: none.** This is a specification check at a higher complexity class, not an entrustment decision. Those are [S080](/wiki/seminars/S080.md), [S085](/wiki/seminars/S085.md) and [S090](/wiki/seminars/S090.md).

## 8. Objections and rebuttals

**Objection 1: an assessment that declines to mark the rule's content marks almost nothing.**

At its strongest: a candidate may submit a rule that is timed correctly, tied to a decision, and completely unreasonable — stopping when one informant has confirmed the sponsor's view — and pass. The scheme checks the shape of a sentence and its timestamp. Calling that an assessment of judgement, at the programme's first class 3 occasion, is generous to the point of emptiness, and it is the design being frightened of its own lack of warrant.

**Substantially conceded, and it is the strongest objection in this paper.** The scheme is nearly content-free by construction. The rebuttal is only that the alternative is worse: marking the content requires a standard for sufficiency, no source behind this programme supplies one, and a programme that invented one would be doing precisely what it spends this stage criticising. **The declined fix is a rubric of sufficiency levels derived from the design's own judgement.** It is declined because it would be a threshold asserted without warrant, dressed as a mark — and once inside an assessment scheme it would be indistinguishable from a finding to every learner who met it. **The paper would rather assess less and say so.**

**Objection 2: routing is being taught as though the categories were clean, and they are not.**

At its strongest: whether the data supports the system is technical, and it is also organisational, legal and political — who owns it, who may release it, whether anybody will agree. Whether people will adopt something is conversational, and it is also technical, because adoption depends on whether the thing works. A specification item that requires an uncertainty to be routed *away* from conversation invites candidates to make artificially clean assignments in order to satisfy it.

**Conceded.** The categories are not clean and the source itself says the distinction is useful rather than exhaustive, naming organisational change, policy, data rights, safety, security and model evaluation as spanning both. **The declined fix is to require a routing table for every uncertainty with mixed assignments permitted.** It is declined on assessment-cost grounds and not on principle, which is a weaker reason than this paper would like to give. The specification asks for one clearly misrouted uncertainty to be corrected, which is a low bar chosen because it is checkable.

**Objection 3: the citation requirement rewards the appearance of rigour and cannot detect its absence.**

At its strongest: the assessment checks that platform claims carry citations and dates. It does not check that the cited page supports the claim, that the claim is the one the packet depends on, or that the candidate read anything. A candidate who runs the pass mechanically produces a packet that **looks** verified, and a marker inspecting it sees exactly what a genuinely verified packet looks like. Worse, the instrument used to produce the citations is a model, whose failure mode is a fluent wrong answer in the register of a correct one — so the assessment has introduced an additional source of confident error and then made its output the packet's most authoritative-looking content. **The design is aware of this failure class; it names it in the first session of the programme; and it has built an assessment that cannot see it.**

**Conceded, and the last sentence is accurate.** Nothing in this assessment verifies that a citation supports its claim. The rebuttal covers a narrower target than the objection. First, **the movement's most valuable output is the failures rather than the successes** — the *not found* results, which no amount of mechanical execution manufactures and which specification item 6 requires to be routed. Second, the payload-verification step is a genuine check on whether the citation requirement reached the executor at all, and a candidate who skipped it will produce a packet with plausible citations and no evidence that the instruction was ever in force. Third, and weakest: a mechanically produced citation is still a dated pointer, so the claim's decay becomes visible to a later reader even where its accuracy was never established. **That is a real property and it is not rigour.**

**The declined fix is to have a second reader spot-check a sample of citations against their pages.** It is declined on assessment-cost grounds, which is the same weak reason given in objection 2, and this paper flags the repetition rather than dressing it up: **two of three objections are answered partly by what the design cannot afford to check.** Prediction 7 is the only instrument that would show how large the resulting gap is.

## 9. Falsifiable predictions and instrumentation

**Prediction 1.** Most first submissions will contain a stopping rule expressed in units of effort. *Instrument:* the rules, coded *effort* / *decision-linked* / *neither* by a second reader.

**Prediction 2.** Item 6 will be the most frequently failed specification item, and item 3 the least. *Instrument:* per-item pass rates, reported without aggregation.

**Prediction 3.** On the third defence question, most candidates will initially answer *nothing*. *Instrument:* the assessor's record of the first answer given, before any follow-up.

**Prediction 4.** Candidates whose agent was asked to argue for insufficiency will restore more absences than those who used it to review the packet generally. *Instrument:* the prompt record against the count of restored absences. **If indistinguishable, the adversarial framing is decorative and the movement should be rewritten as a checklist.**

**Prediction 5.** Most candidates will find at least one platform claim they were confident about returning *not found*, and those claims will more often concern a limit than a capability. *Instrument:* the citation pass output, coded *supported* / *contradicted* / *not found*, with the not-found items sub-coded *limit* / *capability* / *other*. **If limits and capabilities fail at similar rates, the session's claim that limits are the under-verified class is unsupported and should be dropped.**

**Prediction 6.** A substantial minority of candidates will remove an unsupported platform claim from the packet rather than routing it as an uncertainty. *Instrument:* the packet before and after the citation pass, compared for deletions, against the routed-uncertainty list. **This predicts the failure of §5's dropped-claim countermeasure**, and it is included because the countermeasure runs after the deletion would already have happened.

**Prediction 7.** On a spot-check of a sample of submitted citations against their pages, a non-trivial share will not support the claim they are attached to — and packets with more citations will not have a lower rate than packets with fewer. *Instrument:* a second reader checking a fixed number of citations per packet, on a sample of cohorts only. **This is the direct test of §8's third objection, and it is the prediction this paper would least like to lose.** A confirmed result would mean the citation requirement produces the appearance of verification without the substance, and the requirement would have to acquire a checking budget or be withdrawn.

**Prediction 8.** Candidates instructed to compare their item-3 answer against the taught criterion will produce decision-linked rather than effort-linked stopping rules more often than candidates who read the same material without the comparison prompt. *Instrument:* the rule coding from prediction 1, split within cohort on exercise completion. **The same prediction is made across this module's five papers, so a null across all of them would indict the comparison pattern rather than any one session.**

## What a learner should take from this paper

**What the design claims.** That sufficiency is a function of the decision and the cost of error, that a rule stated afterwards is a description, that some uncertainties cannot be resolved by talking to anybody, and that a platform claim without a page and a date is an uncertainty wearing the clothes of a finding.

**What the evidence licenses.** Almost nothing, and the paper says so twice. No source behind this programme establishes when discovery is enough. One states explicitly that stopping should be governed by the decision, the segment coherence and whether new information is still appearing, and **not by a numeric threshold**; the others give conversation counts as personal heuristics and warn against promoting them. The platform documentation licenses statements about what its products are and **supplies no evidence that choosing them its way works** — no adoption data, no failure rates, no comparison against anything outside its own family.

**The concession worth remembering.** Three. This assessment marks the shape and the timing of a judgement and not the judgement itself, which is preferred to inventing a threshold and giving it the authority of a mark. **Nothing here verifies that a citation supports its claim**, so the requirement may buy the appearance of verification rather than the substance — and two of this paper's three objections are answered partly by what the design cannot afford to check, which is a weak reason repeated. And the paper's own record has the defect it assesses: **the documentation server in the workflow has no verified note in this repository**, and every statement about it should be treated as unverified until one exists.

**The silences that matter, and one of them is this repository's own.** The documentation family offers **no adoption data, no failure-rate data, no cost comparison and no evidence that its recommended mappings produce better outcomes** — its recommendations are reasoned assertions and its authority is definitional. It compares one vendor's options against each other and **never against a non-Microsoft alternative**, so nothing in it establishes that any of these is the right choice, only what each is. It is **the most perishable material in this repository's research set**: product names are actively in flux, one SDK is documented as formerly carrying a different name, and a capability boundary recorded today may be closed tomorrow — which is the whole reason this session's requirement is a citation *and a date* rather than a citation. And then the disclosure this paper owes: **this repository holds no dedicated research note for the Microsoft Learn MCP Server itself.** Its properties as used in the workflow — a remote endpoint exposing documentation search, documentation fetch and code-sample search, requiring no authentication — are carried forward from [S001](/wiki/seminars/S001.md) rather than verified here, its configuration surface was not checked against a dated revision, and **no page describing it was fetched for this paper.** That is the exact defect the assessment teaches a candidate to record in their own packet, it is recorded here rather than repaired by inference, and a reader is entitled to treat every statement about that server in this paper and its session as unverified until it is.
