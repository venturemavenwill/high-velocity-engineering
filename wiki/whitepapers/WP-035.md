## In plain terms

You ran an investigation. Now you write what you found, for somebody who wanted a different answer, in a form that does not tell them what to do.

The last clause is the one candidates fight hardest, and the reason for it is not modesty. **A recommendation absorbs its own evidence.** A reader who disagrees with your conclusion loses access to your observations along with it, because the document was organised around the conclusion rather than around the facts. Separating the two costs nothing at writing time and is nearly impossible to retrofit.

The shape comes from the openly published playbook and has five parts: goal, method, evidence, conclusions, next steps. Its virtue is that evidence and conclusions are different sections, so a reader can take the first and reject the second.

Three things are assessed here that candidates do not expect. **The price of the investigation**, in the units it was actually paid in, including other people's time. **A bound reached without an answer**, which is a pass and not a failure — a time-boxed investigation stops by design rather than by exhaustion, and discovering that a question costs more than it looked is a finding the decision-taker needs. And **the disposal of the spike's code, reported as an act.** Retention reported honestly passes; retention omitted fails, because the rule this module teaches is enforced by disclosure rather than by anybody's resolve at the end of a hard week.

The occasion adds a fourth thing candidates expect even less. **The artefact that produced the evidence is submitted, and it is marked on being no larger than its question.** A declarative agent with an empty tool list and an instruction refusing every request but one is the shape this occasion prefers, and the reason is not economy. **A capability added is attribution lost**: once the artefact can also summarise, also route and also explain, you can no longer say which part of it produced the result you are reporting.

## Abstract

This paper defends the first assessment occasion of Stage 4 and explains why it marks the form, the disclosure and the discipline of a feasibility finding and explicitly not its judgement. The argument is that a finding which recommends destroys the separability of its own evidence, that an investigation whose price is unstated cannot be compared with the alternative that was not run, and that a disposal rule enforced by virtue is not enforced. The occasion installs a five-part finding submitted with its charter, its pre-mortem, its question set and its data record, and closes its specification with the statement that nothing else is assessed.

Three literatures shape how the occasion is run rather than what it contains. **The measurement literature** supplies the requirement that a small run state what it could have detected, since without it *no problem found* and *no problem detectable* are the same sentence. **The narrative-assessment literature** supplies the reason the specification lists elements rather than inviting an overall impression: assessors judge from prose and use scores to confirm, they anchor early, and identical outcomes routinely rest on divergent reasoning. **The entrustment literature** supplies the form of the defence, and in particular the requirement that it include what-if questions, because retrospective explanation is evidence about the case that occurred and counterfactual questioning is the only evidence available about the cases that did not.

The paper concedes that a candidate can pass every element with a perfectly formed finding about an unimportant question; that the material behind the module supplies no basis for marking which question was worth asking; that deferring judgement to [S040](/wiki/seminars/S040.md) is a design choice rather than a solution; and that both assessment literatures it leans on come from medical education, so their transfer to an engineering defence is an extrapolation this programme records rather than defends.

## 1. The problem this session must solve

An investigation ends and its findings enter an organisation. What happens next is determined almost entirely by the shape of the document.

**A finding written as a recommendation is read as a position and is argued with as one.** Its observations become supporting material for a conclusion, and a reader who rejects the conclusion has no reason to read them. The evidence is present and unusable.

**A finding written without its price cannot be compared to anything.** The relevant comparison is never *was this useful* — almost everything is somewhat useful — it is *was this the cheapest way to learn it*, and that question cannot be asked of a document with no cost in it.

**And a finding written without its disposal statement lets spike code enter production at the moment when everybody is most relieved.** The code works, the deadline is close, and the person who would have to object is the person who wrote it.

The occasion's response is to make all three assessed elements, and to make the specification closed so that candidates know exactly what is being marked.

## 2. Entry state and prior knowledge

**Entry state: this-programme-only.** The occasion assembles four earlier artefacts and cannot be attempted without them: the charter from [S031](/wiki/seminars/S031.md), the pre-mortem record from [S032](/wiki/seminars/S032.md), the question set from [S033](/wiki/seminars/S033.md), and the data reality record from [S034](/wiki/seminars/S034.md).

It also assumes [S017](/wiki/seminars/S017.md)'s separation of observation from inference, and this is the occasion where that separation is hardest. A technical investigation produces statements that feel like observations and are conclusions: *the run failed* is an observation; *the latency was unacceptable* and *the extract was incomplete* are conclusions with a standard hidden inside them.

The prior the occasion works against is that a good finding is a helpful one. Candidates arrive wanting to be useful, and being useful looks like telling the reader what to do.

## 3. Theoretical basis

**3.1 Load profile.** The module's evaluative peak. The intrinsic content is low — the candidate has already done the work — and the load is affective: reporting a negative result in writing, to somebody who wanted a different one, without softening it into a recommendation that lets them off.

**3.2 Why evidence and conclusion are separate sections rather than separate paragraphs.** A section boundary is checkable by a reader who was not there. A paragraph boundary is not, and a document with the intention but not the structure degrades within one revision.

**3.3 Why a bound reached without an answer is a pass.** A time box that only counts as satisfied when it produces an answer is a deadline. Marking the unresolved outcome as a pass is what makes the bound real, and it converts an uncomfortable non-result into a reportable fact about the cost of the question.

**3.4 Why retention is disclosed rather than prohibited.** A prohibition against retaining working code is enforced by the person under the most pressure to break it, at the moment of maximum pressure. A disclosure requirement is enforced by a marker. **This is a claim about where enforcement sits, not about whether retention is acceptable**, and [WP-031](/wiki/whitepapers/WP-031.md) §8 concedes the underlying economics have moved.

**3.5 Why the specification is closed.** An open-ended standard is marked against the marker's expectations, which the candidate cannot see. Listing the checked elements and stating that nothing else is assessed makes the occasion a test of the named things rather than of the candidate's ability to guess.

**3.6 Why the specification lists elements rather than inviting an overall judgement.**

The assessment literature this programme's architecture rests on studies what assessors actually do rather than what instruments are supposed to measure, and three of its findings bear directly on this occasion ([research/03-measurement-evaluation/govaerts-narrative-assessment.md](/research/03-measurement-evaluation/govaerts-narrative-assessment.md)).

**Assessors reading an accumulated evidentiary record rely mainly on narrative to form their judgement, and use grades and qualifications to confirm impressions the narrative already produced.** The numbers are not the evidence. This is the strongest available warrant for a programme that records reasoned narrative rather than marks, and it is the reason the defence exists at all.

**Assessors anchor early and elaborate rather than revise.** Having formed a reading, they review the remaining material looking for confirming or disconfirming evidence — and are **inclined to stick to initial judgements even when confronted with seemingly disconfirming evidence**, with final judgements more elaborate than preliminary ones but not substantially different. **A closed specification is the design's countermeasure**: eight named elements each checked present-and-adequate forces the assessor back to the artefact for each one, rather than permitting a single early impression to be elaborated across the whole submission.

**Agreement on the outcome conceals disagreement in the reasoning.** Assessors reached similar pass-and-fail judgements while differing in which evidence they trusted and in what they took competence to be. The recommended repair is **group deliberation with substantiated judgements**, not better scales — standardising the instrument does not standardise the judgement. **This programme does not implement group deliberation at this occasion and §8's third objection is where that is confronted.**

**The limits are substantial.** These are **think-aloud studies from medical education**, the primary one from a single institution with one portfolio type; the method cannot capture fast or automatic processing and carries the risk that participants construct explanations for the researcher. **Transfer to an engineering assessment is this programme's extrapolation.** No figure from that literature is reproduced anywhere in this wiki.

**3.7 Why the defence asks about what did not happen.**

The defence is a short focused conversation after the activity, which is the form the entrustment literature calls an **entrustment-based discussion**. Its defining requirement is that it **probe deep understanding of what was done and include "what-if" questions**, to establish whether the candidate would know what to do in situations that did not arise ([research/08-assessment-epas/ten-cate-epa-framework.md](/research/08-assessment-epas/ten-cate-epa-framework.md)).

**That requirement is what distinguishes a defence from a walkthrough**, and the framework states why it matters: an entrustment decision **implies the acceptance of risk**, because it can never be fully predicted what a person will encounter. Retrospective explanation is evidence about the case the candidate met. **Counterfactual questioning is the only evidence available about the cases they did not.**

The same source supplies the distinction that keeps this occasion honest about its own scope. **Ad-hoc entrustment** is the continuous informal judgement a supervisor makes when the learner's skills appear to match the situation's risk. **Summative entrustment** has the nature of certification, is a decision about future responsibilities, and **"should include an agreement between multiple observers… and multiple observations"**. **This occasion is neither.** It is a specification check with a defence attached, generating specification evidence and no entrustment judgement, and §7 says so.

**The limits.** It is a **primer by the concept's originator**, synthesising a decade of literature: guidance rather than evaluation. Its empirical items are reported from prior studies rather than generated. **It is medical education, and its transfer to engineering is this programme's inference.**

**3.8 Why a small run must state what it could not have detected.**

A feasibility run is an experiment. A reported result needs how many cases it rests on, and where cases arrive in related groups **a naive treatment makes the result look more precise than it is** ([research/03-measurement-evaluation/miller-adding-error-bars-to-evals.md](/research/03-measurement-evaluation/miller-adding-error-bars-to-evals.md)). The discipline the occasion assesses is the one that belongs before the run: **state what difference this could have detected.** Without it, *we found no problem* and *we could not have found this problem* are the same sentence.

Two further disciplines from the same source are taught and not marked. **Paired comparison** on the same cases removes variation the options share and costs nothing. And **suppressing variance by adjusting a setting whose purpose is something else** shifts what is being measured rather than measuring it better.

**That source is analytical rather than empirical**, its illustrative magnitudes rest on stylised assumptions, and it addresses comparing scores rather than choosing what to score. **No number from it appears in this paper or in the session.**

**3.9 Why the artefact must be no larger than its question.**

The hosting fork on the platform this occasion uses is unusually clean. **Prompt agents** use "declarative config in portal or code", are "managed by Foundry with no agent compute", and suit "agents that don't include custom code or require custom hosting"; **hosted agents** run "your code (containerized)" on managed container compute ([research/06-microsoft-platform/microsoft-foundry-and-copilot-studio-docs.md](/research/06-microsoft-platform/microsoft-foundry-and-copilot-studio-docs.md)). **The line between them is whether you are running code**, and for a feasibility artefact that is the only question worth asking.

The design's claim is about attribution rather than about cost. **An artefact that can do more than answer its chartered question produces results that cannot be attributed to that question**, because you can no longer say which capability produced them. The empty tool list and the single-question refusal instruction are the operational form, and they are assessed.

One further published fact is used and is easy to skip past. The **basic setup "lacks network isolation"** and is positioned for rapid prototyping, while the standard setup provides fine-grained control over data, security and networking. **A feasibility artefact built on the prototyping topology is not a production artefact**, and a finding that omits which topology it ran on has made a production claim it cannot support. That is assessed too.

**The limits are heavy.** First-party documentation with a selection-guidance purpose, so **its authority is definitional** — it says what the products are, not that choosing them this way works. **No adoption data, no failure-rate data, no cost comparison and no outcome evidence of any kind.** Read through search-returned excerpts rather than full page fetches, with several comparison tables truncated. **Highly perishable**, with product names and capability boundaries actively changing.

## 4. Session design and its warrant

The seven movements run situation, artefact, standard, failure modes, work, review, limits.

**The specification is the standard movement and it closes with the sentence.** Eight elements, each checked as present and adequate or not, followed by an explicit list of what is not marked — feasibility, code quality, count, sponsor acceptance, presentation, agreement with others. **The exclusions are longer than most candidates expect and they are the point.**

**The negative result is given structural priority.** The work movement instructs the candidate to put the question that came back *no* first, and — where none did — to write the paragraph explaining why an investigation that confirmed everything is a weaker instrument than one that did not. **That paragraph is explicitly said to be worth more than a clean result**, which is a norm statement rather than a marking rule, and it is placed there because the marking rule alone would not shift behaviour.

**The defence is of the method and the price, not the answer.** The assessor holds the charter and nothing else, and asks three questions: what did this cost, what would you do more cheaply now, and what did you stop before finding out. **The third has an answer by construction**, and a candidate who says *nothing* has misunderstood what a bounded investigation is.

**The agent is used adversarially and its use is recorded.** The instruction is to have it argue the investigation was unnecessary. **An agent that congratulated the candidate was not used as an instrument** — [S001](/wiki/seminars/S001.md)'s claim, now in its third register.

**4.1 What the platform workflow is for.**

Not agent-building, which the programme teaches properly in [Stage 5](/wiki/quarters/Q5.md). It exists to put one discipline under observation at the moment it is hardest to hold: **the artefact must stop growing.**

The workflow is arranged so the candidate feels the pull rather than being warned about it. The chartered question requires one capability. Adding a tool would make the artefact more useful. Adding a second question would make it demonstrable. Making it demonstrable would make the defence easier. **Each step is individually reasonable and collectively converts a probe into a prototype**, which is [S031](/wiki/seminars/S031.md)'s failure arriving through a different door.

Three elements carry the design.

**The empty tool list is assessed.** Not because tools are bad, but because attribution is the property being protected: a result produced by an artefact with three capabilities cannot be attributed to the question that authorised one.

**The hosting fork is decided on a single criterion, in writing.** Does answering this question require the candidate's own code? If not, a declarative agent is the artefact. **If it does, the charter's cost line changes and the finding must say so** — which converts a technical choice into a disclosure, in the same register as the disposal statement.

**The topology is recorded.** The documentation states plainly that the fast setup lacks network isolation, so a finding that does not name its topology has silently made a production claim. **This is the only element in the occasion that a candidate can fail while producing an otherwise excellent document**, and it is included because the same omission in a real engagement is discovered at the security review rather than at the assessment.

**Two costs the design accepts.** The workflow requires access to a Foundry project with a model deployment, which not every candidate will hold, and which incurs charge. **A candidate without access should submit the configuration, the intended input set and a documented refusal**, and be marked on elements 1 to 7 and 9 with element 8 assessed on the configuration alone. And the platform surface is highly perishable, so **element 8 is written about the artefact's relation to its question rather than about any product feature**, and should survive the fork being renamed.

## 5. Predicted failure modes and countermeasures

**The recommendation in the conclusion.** The likeliest failure by a distance. *Countermeasure:* a named assessed element that fails on a conclusion beginning *we should*, regardless of the reasoning behind it. **This is deliberately blunt.** A softer rule would be argued around.

**The rescued bound.** The limit was quietly extended. *Countermeasure:* the method section must describe what was actually done, and the charter is submitted alongside; the discrepancy is visible without the candidate having to confess it.

**The confirmed set.** Everything came back yes. *Countermeasure:* the assessed element requiring either a negative answer or an explicit statement that none occurred and why that is suspicious. **The second branch is a genuine pass and candidates do not believe that until they see it marked.**

**The smoothed blocker.** [S034](/wiki/seminars/S034.md)'s blocking absence has acquired a mitigation. *Countermeasure:* an assessed element, and a second check on the same artefact at [S040](/wiki/seminars/S040.md).

**The quiet retention.** *Countermeasure:* the disposal element, which fails on omission rather than on retention.

**The unreadable evidence.** Observations and inferences interleaved. *Countermeasure:* the work movement's sentence-by-sentence marking exercise, run by the candidate on their own draft before submission.

**The artefact that grew.** A tool was added, then a second question, then a demonstration mode. *Countermeasure:* element 8, and the workflow's instruction to keep the configuration hostile to growth. **The countermeasure is a marked element rather than a prevention**, and a candidate who grew the artefact and reported it honestly still fails element 8 — which is the one place in this occasion where honesty is not sufficient, because the defect is in the evidence rather than in the disclosure.

**The prototyping topology presented as production.** *Countermeasure:* element 8's second clause. **This is the countermeasure most likely to be resented**, because the artefact worked and the topology feels like a detail. The documentation's own statement that the fast setup lacks network isolation is what makes it not a detail.

**The overclaimed small run.** A result from a handful of cases is reported without saying what it could have detected. *Countermeasure:* the [S033](/wiki/seminars/S033.md) field carried into the evidence section, and element 2's separability test, which surfaces an unstated threshold as a conclusion in the wrong section. **Indirect, and §9's prediction 6 measures it.**

**The assessor who anchored.** The marker forms a judgement from the opening and elaborates it across nine elements. *Countermeasure:* the closed specification, which requires each element to be checked against the artefact separately. **Partial by construction**, since the literature that identifies anchoring also reports that additional evidence tends to elaborate rather than revise — and this design does not implement the remedy that literature actually recommends. §8's third objection is where that is confronted.

## 6. Retention architecture

Retrieved by [S040](/wiki/seminars/S040.md), [S045](/wiki/seminars/S045.md), [S069](/wiki/seminars/S069.md) and [S088](/wiki/seminars/S088.md).

[S069](/wiki/seminars/S069.md) is the significant long-range target: reporting a result that undermines you is this occasion's affective content, at a point in the programme where the result is about a system in production rather than about an investigation nobody has committed to. **The design's expectation is that the skill transfers poorly**, because the stakes are not comparable, and §9 prediction 4 is written against a related claim.

## 7. Assessment and evidence generated

**This is an assessment occasion.** It generates specification evidence and no entrustment judgement. **It is not a summative entrustment decision**, which the framework this programme's assessment architecture rests on says requires agreement between multiple observers across multiple observations — conditions this occasion does not meet and does not claim to.

**What is assessed.** Nine elements, each checked present and adequate or not:

1. The finding states facts and does not recommend.
2. Evidence is separable from conclusion — a reader can reject a conclusion and still use the evidence.
3. The bound was declared before the work and its outcome reported, including *reached without an answer*, which is a pass.
4. The cost of the investigation is stated, in the units it was actually paid in, including time from people outside the candidate's team.
5. At least one question was answered negatively, or the finding states that none was and says why that should be treated as suspicious.
6. The blocking absences from [S034](/wiki/seminars/S034.md) are carried unsmoothed and are not converted into risks with mitigations.
7. Disposal is reported as an act, with a date; retention reported honestly passes, and retention omitted fails.
8. The submitted artefact is no larger than its chartered question — every capability in its configuration is required by that question — and the finding names the topology it ran on.
9. The finding names the decision it enables and the role who takes it.

**The defence is assessed on three questions** — what this cost, what would be done more cheaply now, and what was stopped before finding out — and on whether the candidate can reason about a situation that did not arise. **The what-if answer is never marked on being right**, because there is no right answer about a case that did not occur.

**Nothing else is assessed.** Whether the thing turned out to be feasible is not marked. The quality of the spike's code is not marked. Whether the artefact worked is not marked. Which model was chosen is not marked. The number of questions in the set is not marked. Whether the sponsor accepted the finding is not marked. The document's presentation is not marked. Whether the conclusions match anybody else's is not marked.

## 8. Objections and rebuttals

**Objection 1: the occasion marks form and refuses to mark judgement, so a candidate can pass it with an impeccably written finding about a question that never mattered.**

At its strongest: the entire value of feasibility work is choosing which uncertainty to spend on. This occasion marks eight properties of a document and not one of them is *was this worth investigating*. A candidate who spikes the interesting part, writes it up beautifully, discloses its price and separates its evidence will score full marks for a fortnight that changed nothing — and will have been taught that the writing is the skill.

**Conceded without qualification.** The design's answer is a deferral rather than a defence: judgement is assessed at [S040](/wiki/seminars/S040.md), where the object of the defence is the price of the whole proof and where an assessor proposes a cheaper alternative and asks what it would have missed. **The declined fix is to add a ninth element marking whether the question was worth asking.** It is declined because nothing in the material behind this module supplies a basis for that mark — the corpus has no cost side anywhere and the playbook has no comparative evidence — so the element would be the marker's opinion wearing a specification's clothing, and the programme's own rule is that a specification states what is actually checked. **The deferral is a design choice and it leaves a real gap for five sessions.**

**Objection 2: prohibiting recommendations produces findings nobody can act on, and the fact-finding rule is a piece of institutional cowardice borrowed uncritically from its source.**

At its strongest: an engineer who has spent a fortnight on a question knows more about it than anybody who will read the report. Withholding their view is not neutrality, it is a refusal to take responsibility — and the reader, who has less information, will make a worse decision. Organisations that separate finding from recommendation do so to protect the finder, not the decision. The playbook states the rule and offers no evidence for it, and this programme has adopted it because it sounds disciplined.

**Partly conceded, and the concession narrows the rule rather than removing it.** The design's position is that the separation is about document structure and not about silence: the next-steps section carries the options and their costs, the candidate may be asked their view in the defence, and the assessor is explicitly permitted to ask for it. **What is prohibited is a conclusions section that has absorbed the evidence**, which is a structural claim rather than a professional one. **The declined fix is to permit a clearly labelled recommendation section after the conclusions.** It is declined because a labelled recommendation is read first and reframes everything above it — and because the candidate who most wants to write one is the one whose evidence is thinnest. **That reasoning is design reasoning and nothing warrants it.**

**Objection 3: the paper cites a literature saying that judgements of this kind should come from group deliberation, and then has one assessor make them alone.**

At its strongest, and this is the sharpest of the three: §3.6 reports, approvingly, that assessors of accumulated evidentiary records **anchor early and stick to initial judgements even when confronted with disconfirming evidence**, and that **agreement on an outcome routinely conceals divergent reasoning** — with the recommended repair being **group discussion with substantiated judgements**, not better instruments. This occasion has a single assessor, holding the charter and nothing else, marking nine elements and conducting a defence. **The design has correctly diagnosed the problem, cited the correct remedy, and implemented a different one**, which is precisely the defect [WP-001](/wiki/whitepapers/WP-001.md) §4.3 records the programme committing once already: citing a mechanism accurately while failing to implement it.

**Conceded, and the parallel is exact rather than rhetorical.** The design's mitigation is the closed specification, which forces the assessor back to the artefact for each of nine elements rather than permitting one impression to be elaborated — and that is a mitigation for anchoring, not for divergent reasoning, which it does nothing about at all. Two assessors marking the same submission for opposite reasons would both record a pass here and the divergence would be invisible.

**The declined fix is to require two assessors and a reconciliation conversation on every submission at this occasion.** It is declined on cost: at cohort scale it roughly doubles the assessment load of a stage that already carries five occasions, and the programme's judgement is that the doubling is better spent at [S040](/wiki/seminars/S040.md), where judgement rather than form is being assessed and where divergence in reasoning is the thing at issue. **The cost of declining is that this occasion's marks are less reliable than its specification's precision suggests**, and a reader who takes the specification's nine elements as evidence of measurement quality is being misled by the artefact's tidiness. **§9's prediction 7 is the only test of how bad this is**, and it requires two markers to run at all.

## 9. Falsifiable predictions and instrumentation

**Prediction 1.** A majority of first submissions will contain at least one conclusion phrased as a recommendation. *Instrument:* the conclusions section, with each sentence coded *fact* / *recommendation* by a second reader.

**Prediction 2.** Cost statements will omit non-team time. *Instrument:* the cost section, coded for whether it includes hours from people outside the candidate's team, against the method section's description of who was involved.

**Prediction 3.** Candidates whose charter declared a two-directional stop rule will report a bound-reached-without-answer outcome more often than those whose charter declared only a target. *Instrument:* the [S031](/wiki/seminars/S031.md) charter's bound clause against this occasion's reported outcome. **This is [WP-031](/wiki/whitepapers/WP-031.md) prediction 3, measured here.**

**Prediction 4.** Candidates who reported a negative result at this occasion will not be more likely to report an unfavourable result at [S069](/wiki/seminars/S069.md). *Instrument:* the two submissions, coded for whether an unfavourable finding is reported unhedged, paired per learner. **This prediction is written to fail in the design's favour and the design expects it to hold**, because the stakes differ by more than the skill can bridge. If it fails, the occasion transfers better than claimed.

**Prediction 5.** A substantial minority of submitted artefacts will carry at least one capability the chartered question does not require, and most of those candidates will be able to justify each addition individually. *Instrument:* the configuration against the charter's question, capability by capability, with the candidate's justification collected at the defence. **The individual justifiability is the prediction's point** — if every addition is defensible and the aggregate is not, the failure is one of accumulation and the countermeasure has to sit at the start rather than at the end.

**Prediction 6.** Findings whose evidence section states what the run could not have detected will place unstated thresholds in the conclusions section more often than findings that omit it. *Instrument:* the two sections, coded by a second reader for misfiled threshold statements, split on presence of the detectability sentence. **This is the only test of whether §3.8's discipline actually improves element 2**, and a null result would mean the two are unrelated and the detectability sentence belongs at [S033](/wiki/seminars/S033.md) alone.

**Prediction 7.** Two assessors marking the same submission independently will agree on the overall pass more often than they agree on which elements failed. *Instrument:* paired independent markings on a sample of submissions, with agreement computed separately on the outcome and on the element pattern. **This is the direct test of §8's third objection**, and if element-level agreement is poor while outcome agreement is good, the specification's precision is cosmetic and the two-assessor fix should be reinstated despite its cost.

**Prediction 8.** A majority of candidates will omit the topology from the finding on first submission, including candidates who recorded it in the configuration. *Instrument:* the finding against the submitted configuration, per candidate. **This predicts the failure of §5's topology countermeasure**, and it is included because a countermeasure whose failure rate is unmeasured is an assumption. **If it holds, the element should move into the configuration's required fields rather than the finding's**, because a candidate who recorded it and did not report it has understood the fact and not the disclosure.

## What a learner should take from this paper

**What the design claims.** That a finding's form determines whether its evidence survives disagreement, that an unpriced investigation cannot be compared, and that a rule enforced by the person under pressure is not enforced.

**What the evidence licenses.** A document shape, published openly by one organisation, with **no comparative evidence that it produces better decisions.** Everything about outcomes is unwarranted.

**The concession worth remembering.** This occasion cannot tell a well-written finding about a trivial question from a well-written finding about the one that mattered. It says so, and it defers the difference to [S040](/wiki/seminars/S040.md). A candidate who notices that gap and closes it themselves has understood the stage.
