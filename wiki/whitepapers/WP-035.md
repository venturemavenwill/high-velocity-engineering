## In plain terms

You ran an investigation. Now you write what you found, for somebody who wanted a different answer, in a form that does not tell them what to do.

The last clause is the one candidates fight hardest, and the reason for it is not modesty. **A recommendation absorbs its own evidence.** A reader who disagrees with your conclusion loses access to your observations along with it, because the document was organised around the conclusion rather than around the facts. Separating the two costs nothing at writing time and is nearly impossible to retrofit.

The shape comes from the openly published playbook and has five parts: goal, method, evidence, conclusions, next steps. Its virtue is that evidence and conclusions are different sections, so a reader can take the first and reject the second.

Three things are assessed here that candidates do not expect. **The price of the investigation**, in the units it was actually paid in, including other people's time. **A bound reached without an answer**, which is a pass and not a failure — a time-boxed investigation stops by design rather than by exhaustion, and discovering that a question costs more than it looked is a finding the decision-taker needs. And **the disposal of the spike's code, reported as an act.** Retention reported honestly passes; retention omitted fails, because the rule this module teaches is enforced by disclosure rather than by anybody's resolve at the end of a hard week.

## Abstract

This paper defends the first assessment occasion of Stage 4 and explains why it marks the form, the disclosure and the discipline of a feasibility finding and explicitly not its judgement. The argument is that a finding which recommends destroys the separability of its own evidence, that an investigation whose price is unstated cannot be compared with the alternative that was not run, and that a disposal rule enforced by virtue is not enforced. The occasion installs a five-part finding submitted with its charter, its pre-mortem, its question set and its data record, and closes its specification with the statement that nothing else is assessed. The paper concedes that a candidate can pass every element with a perfectly formed finding about an unimportant question, that the material behind the module supplies no basis for marking which question was worth asking, and that deferring judgement to [S040](/wiki/seminars/S040.md) is a design choice rather than a solution.

## 1. The problem this session must solve

An investigation ends and its findings enter an organisation. What happens next is determined almost entirely by the shape of the document.

**A finding written as a recommendation is read as a position and is argued with as one.** Its observations become supporting material for a conclusion, and a reader who rejects the conclusion has no reason to read them. The evidence is present and unusable.

**A finding written without its price cannot be compared to anything.** The relevant comparison is never *was this useful* — almost everything is somewhat useful — it is *was this the cheapest way to learn it*, and that question cannot be asked of a document with no cost in it.

**And a finding written without its disposal statement lets spike code enter production at the moment when everybody is most relieved.** The code works, the deadline is close, and the person who would have to object is the person who wrote it.

The occasion's response is to make all three assessed elements, and to make the specification closed so that candidates know exactly what is being marked.

## 2. Entry state and prior knowledge

**Entry state: this-programme-only.** The occasion assembles four earlier artefacts and cannot be attempted without them: the charter from [S031](/wiki/seminars/S031.md), the pre-mortem record from [S032](/wiki/seminars/S032.md), the question set from [S033](/wiki/seminars/S033.md), and the data reality record from [S034](/wiki/seminars/S034.md).

It also assumes [S017](/wiki/seminars/S017.md)'s separation of observation from inference, and this is the occasion where that separation is hardest. A technical investigation produces statements that feel like observations and are conclusions: *the run failed* is an observation; *the latency was unacceptable* and *the extract was incomplete* are conclusions with a standard hidden inside them.

The prior the occasion works against is that a good finding is a helpful one. Candidates arrive wanting to be useful, and being useful looks like telling the reader what to do.

## 3. Theoretical basis

**3.1 Load profile.** The module's evaluative peak. The intrinsic content is low — the candidate has already done the work — and the load is affective: reporting a negative result in writing, to somebody who wanted a different one, without softening it into a recommendation that lets them off.

**3.2 Why evidence and conclusion are separate sections rather than separate paragraphs.** A section boundary is checkable by a reader who was not there. A paragraph boundary is not, and a document with the intention but not the structure degrades within one revision.

**3.3 Why a bound reached without an answer is a pass.** A time box that only counts as satisfied when it produces an answer is a deadline. Marking the unresolved outcome as a pass is what makes the bound real, and it converts an uncomfortable non-result into a reportable fact about the cost of the question.

**3.4 Why retention is disclosed rather than prohibited.** A prohibition against retaining working code is enforced by the person under the most pressure to break it, at the moment of maximum pressure. A disclosure requirement is enforced by a marker. **This is a claim about where enforcement sits, not about whether retention is acceptable**, and [WP-031](/wiki/whitepapers/WP-031.md) §8 concedes the underlying economics have moved.

**3.5 Why the specification is closed.** An open-ended standard is marked against the marker's expectations, which the candidate cannot see. Listing the checked elements and stating that nothing else is assessed makes the occasion a test of the named things rather than of the candidate's ability to guess.

## 4. Session design and its warrant

The seven movements run situation, artefact, standard, failure modes, work, review, limits.

**The specification is the standard movement and it closes with the sentence.** Eight elements, each checked as present and adequate or not, followed by an explicit list of what is not marked — feasibility, code quality, count, sponsor acceptance, presentation, agreement with others. **The exclusions are longer than most candidates expect and they are the point.**

**The negative result is given structural priority.** The work movement instructs the candidate to put the question that came back *no* first, and — where none did — to write the paragraph explaining why an investigation that confirmed everything is a weaker instrument than one that did not. **That paragraph is explicitly said to be worth more than a clean result**, which is a norm statement rather than a marking rule, and it is placed there because the marking rule alone would not shift behaviour.

**The defence is of the method and the price, not the answer.** The assessor holds the charter and nothing else, and asks three questions: what did this cost, what would you do more cheaply now, and what did you stop before finding out. **The third has an answer by construction**, and a candidate who says *nothing* has misunderstood what a bounded investigation is.

**The agent is used adversarially and its use is recorded.** The instruction is to have it argue the investigation was unnecessary. **An agent that congratulated the candidate was not used as an instrument** — [S001](/wiki/seminars/S001.md)'s claim, now in its third register.

## 5. Predicted failure modes and countermeasures

**The recommendation in the conclusion.** The likeliest failure by a distance. *Countermeasure:* a named assessed element that fails on a conclusion beginning *we should*, regardless of the reasoning behind it. **This is deliberately blunt.** A softer rule would be argued around.

**The rescued bound.** The limit was quietly extended. *Countermeasure:* the method section must describe what was actually done, and the charter is submitted alongside; the discrepancy is visible without the candidate having to confess it.

**The confirmed set.** Everything came back yes. *Countermeasure:* the assessed element requiring either a negative answer or an explicit statement that none occurred and why that is suspicious. **The second branch is a genuine pass and candidates do not believe that until they see it marked.**

**The smoothed blocker.** [S034](/wiki/seminars/S034.md)'s blocking absence has acquired a mitigation. *Countermeasure:* an assessed element, and a second check on the same artefact at [S040](/wiki/seminars/S040.md).

**The quiet retention.** *Countermeasure:* the disposal element, which fails on omission rather than on retention.

**The unreadable evidence.** Observations and inferences interleaved. *Countermeasure:* the work movement's sentence-by-sentence marking exercise, run by the candidate on their own draft before submission.

## 6. Retention architecture

Retrieved by [S040](/wiki/seminars/S040.md), [S045](/wiki/seminars/S045.md), [S069](/wiki/seminars/S069.md) and [S088](/wiki/seminars/S088.md).

[S069](/wiki/seminars/S069.md) is the significant long-range target: reporting a result that undermines you is this occasion's affective content, at a point in the programme where the result is about a system in production rather than about an investigation nobody has committed to. **The design's expectation is that the skill transfers poorly**, because the stakes are not comparable, and §9 prediction 4 is written against a related claim.

## 7. Assessment and evidence generated

**This is an assessment occasion.** It generates specification evidence and no entrustment judgement.

Eight elements, each checked present and adequate or not: the finding states facts and does not recommend; evidence is separable from conclusion; the bound was declared before the work and its outcome reported; the cost of the investigation is stated; at least one question was answered negatively or the absence is flagged; the blocking absences from [S034](/wiki/seminars/S034.md) are carried unsmoothed; disposal is reported as an act; and the finding names the decision it enables and the role who takes it.

**Nothing else is assessed.** Whether the thing turned out to be feasible is not marked. The quality of the spike's code is not marked. The number of questions in the set is not marked. Whether the sponsor accepted the finding is not marked. The document's presentation is not marked. Whether the conclusions match anybody else's is not marked.

## 8. Objections and rebuttals

**Objection 1: the occasion marks form and refuses to mark judgement, so a candidate can pass it with an impeccably written finding about a question that never mattered.**

At its strongest: the entire value of feasibility work is choosing which uncertainty to spend on. This occasion marks eight properties of a document and not one of them is *was this worth investigating*. A candidate who spikes the interesting part, writes it up beautifully, discloses its price and separates its evidence will score full marks for a fortnight that changed nothing — and will have been taught that the writing is the skill.

**Conceded without qualification.** The design's answer is a deferral rather than a defence: judgement is assessed at [S040](/wiki/seminars/S040.md), where the object of the defence is the price of the whole proof and where an assessor proposes a cheaper alternative and asks what it would have missed. **The declined fix is to add a ninth element marking whether the question was worth asking.** It is declined because nothing in the material behind this module supplies a basis for that mark — the corpus has no cost side anywhere and the playbook has no comparative evidence — so the element would be the marker's opinion wearing a specification's clothing, and the programme's own rule is that a specification states what is actually checked. **The deferral is a design choice and it leaves a real gap for five sessions.**

**Objection 2: prohibiting recommendations produces findings nobody can act on, and the fact-finding rule is a piece of institutional cowardice borrowed uncritically from its source.**

At its strongest: an engineer who has spent a fortnight on a question knows more about it than anybody who will read the report. Withholding their view is not neutrality, it is a refusal to take responsibility — and the reader, who has less information, will make a worse decision. Organisations that separate finding from recommendation do so to protect the finder, not the decision. The playbook states the rule and offers no evidence for it, and this programme has adopted it because it sounds disciplined.

**Partly conceded, and the concession narrows the rule rather than removing it.** The design's position is that the separation is about document structure and not about silence: the next-steps section carries the options and their costs, the candidate may be asked their view in the defence, and the assessor is explicitly permitted to ask for it. **What is prohibited is a conclusions section that has absorbed the evidence**, which is a structural claim rather than a professional one. **The declined fix is to permit a clearly labelled recommendation section after the conclusions.** It is declined because a labelled recommendation is read first and reframes everything above it — and because the candidate who most wants to write one is the one whose evidence is thinnest. **That reasoning is design reasoning and nothing warrants it.**

## 9. Falsifiable predictions and instrumentation

**Prediction 1.** A majority of first submissions will contain at least one conclusion phrased as a recommendation. *Instrument:* the conclusions section, with each sentence coded *fact* / *recommendation* by a second reader.

**Prediction 2.** Cost statements will omit non-team time. *Instrument:* the cost section, coded for whether it includes hours from people outside the candidate's team, against the method section's description of who was involved.

**Prediction 3.** Candidates whose charter declared a two-directional stop rule will report a bound-reached-without-answer outcome more often than those whose charter declared only a target. *Instrument:* the [S031](/wiki/seminars/S031.md) charter's bound clause against this occasion's reported outcome. **This is [WP-031](/wiki/whitepapers/WP-031.md) prediction 3, measured here.**

**Prediction 4.** Candidates who reported a negative result at this occasion will not be more likely to report an unfavourable result at [S069](/wiki/seminars/S069.md). *Instrument:* the two submissions, coded for whether an unfavourable finding is reported unhedged, paired per learner. **This prediction is written to fail in the design's favour and the design expects it to hold**, because the stakes differ by more than the skill can bridge. If it fails, the occasion transfers better than claimed.

## What a learner should take from this paper

**What the design claims.** That a finding's form determines whether its evidence survives disagreement, that an unpriced investigation cannot be compared, and that a rule enforced by the person under pressure is not enforced.

**What the evidence licenses.** A document shape, published openly by one organisation, with **no comparative evidence that it produces better decisions.** Everything about outcomes is unwarranted.

**The concession worth remembering.** This occasion cannot tell a well-written finding about a trivial question from a well-written finding about the one that mattered. It says so, and it defers the difference to [S040](/wiki/seminars/S040.md). A candidate who notices that gap and closes it themselves has understood the stage.
