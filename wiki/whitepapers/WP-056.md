## In plain terms

The governance regime this module is built on opens with the right question. **Can this be solved without AI at all?**

It is asked here, in an assurance stage, for two reasons. **It needs the system model** — a comparison against an alternative is not writable until you can say what the thing is made of. And this is where the programme's other requirements about records converge, because the artefact this session produces is a record rather than a judgement.

**The judgement was made much earlier, at [S008](/wiki/seminars/S008.md).** This session is not the first asking and does not pretend to be. It exists because a judgement with no findable record cannot be revisited, and this particular decision will be revisited: **the alternative you rejected is the first thing anybody reaches for when the system disappoints.**

Two elements do the real work.

**The alternative must be designed from scratch, not described from the present.** The practice material behind this programme has a rule worth borrowing and inverting — do not automate the existing workflow, ask how you would do this from scratch given the tooling. **The same discipline applies to the thing you are comparing against**, and a non-AI option that is the current process with better forms is a straw figure the author built themselves.

**And the rejection must name evidence that could have appeared while the decision was still open.** *If the model had not worked* is available only after the money is spent. A rejection no evidence could have reversed is not a rejection, which is [S001](/wiki/seminars/S001.md)'s claim about engagements, applied to technology choice.

One structural fact frames the whole session and the paper states it rather than implying it. **The organisation asking this question is usually the organisation selling the answer.**

## Abstract

This paper defends placing the standing first question of a responsible-AI regime inside an assurance stage rather than at the start of an engagement, and defends requiring a fully designed non-AI alternative rather than a comparison against the status quo. The argument is that the judgement is made early and the record is made here, that a record is what makes the decision revisitable when the system disappoints, and that a comparison is not writable before the system model exists. The session installs a technology-free problem statement, a from-scratch alternative, a cost comparison carrying the categories the source corpus omits entirely, a statement of evidence that could have changed the decision while it was open, and a disclosure of the delivery organisation's own commercial interest. The paper concedes that an artefact produced at this point in an engagement will usually be written to defend a decision already taken, that the disclosure element may be unacceptable to a real employer, and that no source available establishes that asking the question changes anything.

## 1. The problem this session must solve

A delivery organisation arrives at an engagement holding a solution category, and every structure around it converts that into a requirement.

**The practice corpus behind this programme demonstrates the mechanism unusually clearly.** Its organising thesis is excellent — a delivery can meet every agreed criterion, use good architecture and ship to production **and still produce nothing of value** — and everything short of realised benefit is reclassified as activity. But its readiness criteria are composed almost entirely of customer dispositions, and the objection it prepares its engineers against most heavily is that the tooling improved and delivery did not speed up. **Its prepared answer reattributes the shortfall to the customer's own organisational constraints.**

**A model whose failure explanation is always the customer's readiness has arranged its evidence so that a disappointing result confirms rather than challenges the thesis.** That arrangement is not dishonest and nobody built it deliberately; it is what happens when the people who assess fit are the people who deliver.

The second problem is that the question, asked casually, is unanswerable. *Could this be done without AI?* invites *yes, but worse*, and there the matter rests. **The question only bites when the alternative has been designed to the same fidelity**, because only then can it be priced, argued with, and preferred.

## 2. Entry state and prior knowledge

**Entry state: this-programme-only.** The session requires the system model from [S051](/wiki/seminars/S051.md), because a comparison needs something to compare, and it retrieves [S003](/wiki/seminars/S003.md)'s problem behind the request, [S008](/wiki/seminars/S008.md)'s judgement about simpler technology and [S033](/wiki/seminars/S033.md)'s discipline of a feasibility question that has an answer.

**The prior it works against is that the decision is settled.** It is settled. The session is not trying to reopen it; it is producing the artefact that lets somebody else reopen it later, and a cohort that misreads this will treat the exercise as pointless because they cannot change the outcome.

## 3. Theoretical basis

**3.1 Load profile.** Low intrinsic load, high interpersonal load. The difficulty is writing a credible case against the work your team is being paid for, in a document your team will read.

**3.2 Why the alternative must be designed rather than described.** A described alternative cannot be priced, cannot be shown to fail, and cannot be preferred. **Designing it to the same fidelity is the only way to make the comparison symmetric**, and asymmetric comparisons always favour the option that was described in detail.

**3.3 Why the cost categories are named explicitly.** The corpus behind this programme contains **no cost side anywhere** — every claim in it is a gross benefit, with staffing, compute, the cost of the discovery activity itself and opportunity cost all absent. A learner who inherits that habit will produce the same asymmetry. Naming the categories converts an omission into a visible blank.

**3.4 Why the disconfirming evidence must have been available while the decision was open.** Evidence that arrives after commitment cannot have informed it, and a rejection resting on such evidence is a prediction dressed as a criterion. **This is the same distinction [S040](/wiki/seminars/S040.md) drew about proof: evidence obtained before a commitment can change it, and evidence obtained after cannot.**

**3.5 Why the commercial interest is disclosed.** An assessment of whether a technology is necessary, written by the party that sells it, with no disclosure, is structurally the same artefact as a readiness rubric composed of customer dispositions. **Naming the relationship does not neutralise it; it lets a reader discount appropriately**, which is all any disclosure ever does.

## 4. Session design and its warrant

The seven movements run situation, artefact, standard, failure modes, work, review, limits.

**Opening item 1 is a filter.** A problem statement that does not survive having every product name removed was a solution statement, and a learner who discovers this in Stage 6 has discovered it late but not too late.

**Opening item 2 is asked before instruction because the failure is instructive.** Most learners will offer the status quo, notice on being asked for components that it has none, and thereby learn what a designed alternative is without being told.

**Opening item 5 asks who benefits and whether they were in the room**, which is [S007](/wiki/seminars/S007.md)'s question aimed at the delivery side rather than the customer side. It is the only place in the programme where it is turned around.

**The review movement is blind by construction.** The reviewer does not know which option the author prefers, and the signals are stripped. **The second question — what would change your mind — is the real instrument**, because a comparison a reader cannot reason with is advocacy in an analysis's clothing.

**The agent is briefed to argue for the alternative** and the learner is instructed to read its case on the assumption that it is right. That instruction is unusual in this programme and is deliberate: the learner's own incentives all run the other way, and neutral reading is not available to them here.

## 5. Predicted failure modes and countermeasures

**The status quo as alternative.** The likeliest failure. *Countermeasure:* the components-operator-failure-mode requirement in the standard, and the warning sign — your alternative has no components.

**The straw alternative.** *Countermeasure:* the blind review, which is the only mechanism here that does not depend on the author's good faith. **It is a weak countermeasure**, because a reviewer given two designs cannot tell that one was weakened deliberately.

**The unpriced comparison.** *Countermeasure:* the named cost categories, checked column by column.

**The retrofitted question.** *Countermeasure:* the requirement that disconfirming evidence be of a kind that could have appeared during the engagement. **This is a detection rather than a prevention**, and §8 concedes that the session cannot prevent it.

**The capability answer.** *Countermeasure:* the standard's portability test — a justification that fits a different problem unchanged is not a justification.

**The unowned interest.** *Countermeasure:* the disclosure element. §8 concedes that a real employer may not accept it.

## 8. Objections and rebuttals

**Objection 1: asking this in Stage 6 produces a justification rather than a decision, and the artefact will be cited as evidence that the alternative was considered when it was not.**

At its strongest: the money is spent, the team is assembled, the customer is committed. **Every artefact produced at this point is written by somebody who knows the answer**, and this one will be filed and later quoted — *we assessed the non-AI option* — as though a genuine comparison had occurred. That is worse than not asking, because it manufactures the appearance of due diligence and inoculates the decision against future challenge.

**Substantially conceded, and it is the sharpest objection to this session.** Three partial answers. The judgement is not made here — it was made at [S008](/wiki/seminars/S008.md), in Stage 1, when it was cheap — and this session produces the record rather than the decision, which is a smaller and more honest claim. The disconfirming-evidence element makes retrofitting detectable, because a rejection resting on evidence only available after commitment is visibly post hoc to any second reader. And the blind review removes the author's framing from the one moment the comparison is tested. **The declined fix is to delete this session as duplicative of [S008](/wiki/seminars/S008.md).** It is declined because a judgement with no findable record cannot be revisited, and this decision is revisited by default: the rejected alternative is the first thing anybody reaches for when the system disappoints, and at that moment the existence of a dated, priced, honestly disconfirmable rejection is worth more than the original judgement was. **The residual risk — that the record is written to defend rather than to inform — is real, is not eliminated, and is what §9 prediction 2 measures.**

**Objection 2: requiring an engineer to disclose their own organisation's commercial interest is naive, and the element trains a behaviour no employer will accept.**

At its strongest: a delivery organisation will not sign out an artefact stating that it recommended a technology it sells. The element therefore guarantees one of two outcomes — the artefact is not produced, or the sentence is removed before it leaves the building — and a curriculum requirement that cannot survive contact with an employer has taught nothing except how to be uncomfortable.

**Conceded, and the design's answer is narrow.** The element requires the relationship to be *named*, not characterised as corrupting: *this organisation supplies the recommended technology* is a statement of fact, is standard in several professional disclosure regimes, and is not an accusation. **The declined fix is to drop the element.** It is declined because an assessment of whether a technology is necessary, produced by the party selling it with no disclosure, is structurally the artefact this session exists to critique — and a programme that teaches learners to detect that arrangement cannot require them to construct one. **The friction is real and this programme cannot resolve it.** A learner who finds the sentence removed by their organisation has learned something the session could not otherwise teach, and [S060](/wiki/seminars/S060.md) accepts a record of that removal in its place.

## 9. Falsifiable predictions and instrumentation

**Prediction 1.** Most first alternatives will describe the current process rather than a from-scratch design. *Instrument:* the submitted alternative, coded *designed* / *partially designed* / *status quo* by a second reader, using the presence of components, an operator and a failure mode.

**Prediction 2.** Most disconfirming-evidence statements will name evidence available only after commitment. *Instrument:* each statement coded *available during the engagement* / *available only afterwards* / *absent*.

**Prediction 3.** Cost comparisons will price the non-AI alternative more completely than the option being built. *Instrument:* a category-by-category presence check across both columns by a second reader, using the five named categories.

**Prediction 4.** Blind reviewers will be unable to state what would change their mind for most submissions. *Instrument:* the review movement's second question, recorded per learner as *answered* / *not answered*. **If this fails, the comparison format is doing more work than the design expects and the movement's first question could be dropped.**

## What a learner should take from this paper

**What the design claims.** That the alternative must be designed to be comparable; that a rejection no evidence could have reversed is not a rejection; and that a record, unlike a judgement, can be revisited by somebody who was not there.

**What the evidence licenses.** Nothing. **No source available to this programme establishes that asking this question changes any outcome**, and the regime that asks it publishes no evidence that any assessment ever changed a system.

**The concession worth remembering.** You will write this artefact knowing the answer. The design's only real defence is that somebody will read it later under different pressure, and that what you leave them determines whether they can act.
