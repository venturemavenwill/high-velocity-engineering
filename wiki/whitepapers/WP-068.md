## In plain terms

You were handed a model. It has a name, a structure, cells to fill, and a workbook that runs a simulation. Whatever comes out will be quoted with the framework's name attached.

**The framework asserts nothing.**

That is not the criticism anybody expects and it is the whole session. This module's source contains **no claimed multiples, no claimed savings and no efficiency figures.** Its metric catalogue is a taxonomy in which not one entry is populated with an observed value, a benchmark or a typical range. Its case-studies section is reserved and empty. **It contains no unwarranted magnitudes because it contains no magnitudes at all** — and it ships models that produce them.

**Figures produced downstream carry the authority of a named framework while resting entirely on elicited inputs, and because the framework asserts nothing they cannot be traced back to any claim in it.** There is no proposition to check them against. Asked where the number came from, the honest answer is *the model*. Asked what the model says, the honest answer is *nothing*.

**That is a worse failure than an unwarranted magnitude, which is at least locatable and refutable.**

The session's repair is the discipline [S054](/wiki/seminars/S054.md) installed for a security attribute table, moved into a financial model. **Every number carries its provenance beside it**: measured means an instrument produced it and you can name the instrument; elicited means a person supplied it and you can name the person; assumed means you chose it; inherited means it came from an example. **Uncertainty relocated is uncertainty preserved; uncertainty stripped is a guess wearing a fact's clothes.**

And there is one prohibition harder than any other in the stage. **Specimen answers become benchmarks whether or not anyone intends them to.** The corpus's question bank ships illustrative response menus containing figures, presented as the plausible range of customer answers; an engineer who has internalised them will hear a real customer's number as high or low against a scale nobody validated. **No such figure is reproduced anywhere in this repository**, and reproducing them to demonstrate the hazard would install the hazard.

## Abstract

This paper defends teaching the untraceable output as a distinct and more dangerous failure than the unwarranted magnitude, and defends an exercise whose designed outcome is a check that cannot be completed. The argument is that a framework asserting nothing supplies no proposition against which its own outputs can be tested; that a model's authority travels with its name while its inputs do not travel at all; and that a provenance mark placed in an appendix reaches only the reader who was already suspicious. The session installs a four-way provenance classification placed beside each number, a sensitivity statement naming the input the output follows, an attempted traceability check reported including its failure, a prohibition on simulating over an unsourced distribution, and the marking of every inherited figure as an illustration. The paper concedes that the classification scheme is this design's invention, that the traceability check cannot come out any other way against this framework, and that the session has no example of a framework against which it would succeed.

## 1. The problem this session must solve

An unwarranted number can be found and argued with. **An untraceable number cannot.**

The distinction is not rhetorical. A figure asserted in a document has a location, a date, an author and a claim. **You can go to it, read what it says, notice that it cites nothing, and refuse it.** Every property that makes it bad also makes it addressable.

A figure produced by an apparatus that asserts nothing has none of those. It has a provenance chain that terminates in a workshop, a name that implies a body of knowledge, and no parent claim anywhere. **Challenged, it does not defend itself and it does not have to** — the challenge has nowhere to land, because there is no proposition in the framework the figure could contradict.

There is a second problem which is why the first one persists. **The framework's emptiness reads as rigour.** A document that declines to assert magnitudes looks disciplined, and it is disciplined in one specific respect: it has not over-claimed. **The absence of a claim is being read as the presence of restraint**, when what it actually produces is an instrument that cannot be checked.

And a third, which travels furthest. **Illustrative figures become norms.** They appear in specimen answers and threshold catalogues, unattributed, presented as what a plausible response looks like — and a reader who absorbs them acquires a scale they cannot cite and will use it to characterise real numbers without noticing that a comparison has occurred.

The session's response is to mark every input, run the check, and report that it stopped.

## 2. Entry state and prior knowledge

**Entry state: this-programme-only.** The session operates on the cost model from [S064](/wiki/seminars/S064.md), the baseline record from [S066](/wiki/seminars/S066.md) and the attribution statement from [S067](/wiki/seminars/S067.md).

**It depends completely on [S054](/wiki/seminars/S054.md).** A learner who has traced a value being inferred, its marker stripped, its justification dropped at assembly, and the same value then counted as evidence by a scorer, has already met this session's mechanism in a different medium. **The session names the inheritance explicitly** so that the learner recognises rather than relearns.

It assumes [S029](/wiki/seminars/S029.md), where criteria were stated before options were compared, because the sensitivity statement is that ordering applied to a model's inputs.

**The prior it works against is that a model is a calculation.** Learners treat the arithmetic as the artefact. **The arithmetic is the least interesting part and it is almost always correct** — the corpus's computed percentages are reproducible and their formulas are given, and every input is a judgement, so the precision is spurious.

## 3. Theoretical basis

**3.1 Load profile.** Intrinsic load is low; the classification is four categories and the check is one attempt. **The load is affective and it lands late**, when a learner realises the model they were given cannot be checked and that they will have to say so to people using it.

**3.2 Why four categories rather than two.** Measured and not-measured would be enough for arithmetic and is not enough for accountability. **Elicited, assumed and inherited fail differently**: an elicited value has a person who can be re-asked, an assumed value has an author who can be argued with, and an inherited value has neither and is the most dangerous of the three because it looks like the others. Collapsing them hides the one that needs the mark most.

**3.3 Why the mark goes beside the number.** The output travels and the input sheet does not. **A provenance record placed anywhere other than on the circulating page is a record for the reader who was already going to ask**, and that reader was not the risk. This is identical to [S067](/wiki/seminars/S067.md)'s asymmetry-disclosure rule and the two sessions place it the same way deliberately.

**3.4 Why sensitivity comes before argument.** A model has many inputs and one that matters. **Identifying it converts a general anxiety about provenance into a specific claim about a specific number**, which is arguable. In most models of this kind the output is most sensitive to an elicited input, and that sentence belongs at the top of the document.

**3.5 Why an unsourced distribution forbids the simulation.** A simulation over a distribution nobody chose does not become evidence by being sampled many times; **it becomes a precise representation of the guess.** The source recommends a stochastic method for quantities it never quantifies, which is the clearest available instance of the failure, and the prohibition is the minimal response to it.

**3.6 Why the failed check is the work.** A learner asked to trace an output to a claim will discover there is no claim. **Reporting that as the finding rather than as an obstacle is the entire transferable skill**, because it is the report they will have to write in industry about a model nobody has questioned.

## 4. Session design and its warrant

The seven movements run situation, artefact, standard, failure modes, work, review, limits.

**Opening item 2 — which claim in the framework could your output contradict — is the instrument, and it has no answer.** It is asked before instruction so that the learner reaches the emptiness themselves rather than being told about it. **A learner who is told the framework asserts nothing has learned a fact about one framework; a learner who looked for a proposition and could not find one has learned a procedure.**

**The work movement's second step is the designed failure.** Trace one output figure back as far as it goes and write down where it stopped. **For this framework it stops immediately**, and §8 concedes that this makes the exercise unfalsifiable in its own right.

**The fifth step hunts the learner's own inherited expectations** rather than the model's inherited values, which is the harder and more useful direction. It asks where each belief about a normal value came from — and the honest answer is usually a specimen answer or a threshold catalogue read years earlier.

**The review movement splits the audience deliberately.** One reader gets the figure alone, one gets the provenance sheet alone, and the gap between what each would do is the argument for putting the marks on the page that travels. **That is an argument the learner produces rather than receives**, which is why the movement is shaped as an experiment rather than as a demonstration.

## 5. Predicted failure modes and countermeasures

**Provenance in an appendix.** The likeliest failure, because it is what every methodology convention encourages. *Countermeasure:* the standard's requirement that the mark be beside the number, the review movement's split test, and the [S070](/wiki/seminars/S070.md) element checking the page that will be forwarded.

**The check reported as an obstacle.** The learner treats the missing proposition as a gap in their work rather than as the result. *Countermeasure:* the standard states that the failed check is the work, and the [S070](/wiki/seminars/S070.md) element requires the check to be reported including where it could not be completed.

**The inherited figure used as a benchmark.** *Countermeasure:* the marking requirement, and the warning sign — you described a real customer's figure as high or low and cannot say against what.

**The simulation run anyway.** *Countermeasure:* the prohibition, stated as a prohibition rather than as advice, because the workbook makes running it the path of least resistance.

**The classification that collapses.** A learner marks everything elicited or everything assumed. *Countermeasure:* the sensitivity statement, which forces one input to be examined closely enough that its category has to be right.

## 8. Objections and rebuttals

**Objection 1: the traceability check cannot come out any other way, so it is a demonstration dressed as an exercise.**

At its strongest: the session sends a learner to test whether a framework asserts something, having already established in its own text that it does not. **The result is known to the designer, known to the paper, and stated in the module page before the learner begins.** An exercise whose outcome is fixed teaches the conclusion and not the method, and a learner who runs it has performed a ritual. **Worse, the session has no example of a framework where the check succeeds**, so a learner leaves knowing what failure looks like and having never seen success — which means they cannot recognise it, and may report failure against a framework that would have passed.

**Conceded without qualification, and the session states it in its own limits movement rather than confining it here.** The rebuttal is only about what the exercise leaves behind: **the procedure — take an output, find the claim it could contradict, follow it, record where it stopped — is portable to any model a learner is handed**, and the fact that this instance terminates at the first step does not make the steps unavailable elsewhere. **The declined fix is to supply a contrasting framework that would pass the check.** It is declined because this programme has not found one. **That is a real gap in the source coverage rather than a design decision, and it should be closed if such a framework is ever read into this repository.** The session asks the learner what a framework would have to look like to pass, and cannot answer its own question.

**Objection 2: requiring provenance marks beside every number will make the model unreadable, and an unreadable model is not circulated, which removes whatever value it had.**

At its strongest: a commercial value document is read by people who will not tolerate annotation. **Marks beside every figure make the page look uncertain, and a page that looks uncertain is replaced by a summary somebody else writes** — at which point the provenance is stripped by a different hand and the session has achieved the opposite of its intention. The appendix convention exists for a reason, and the reason is that it keeps the discipline alive inside a document people will actually read.

**Conceded in part, and the rebuttal narrows the requirement rather than defending the strong form.** What the element needs is that **the classification is visible on the page that circulates**, which a compact mark satisfies — a single-character convention, a colour, a footnoted key. It does not require a paragraph per cell, and the session should say so more clearly than it does. **The deeper answer is that the objection's alternative is the failure mode the whole module describes**: a clean page whose numbers rest on a workshop is precisely the artefact that travels furthest and can be checked least. **The declined fix is to permit the classification in an appendix where the audience requires a clean page.** Declined because the audience always requires a clean page, so the exception would consume the rule. **The readability cost is real and is accepted.**

## 9. Falsifiable predictions and instrumentation

**Prediction 1.** Most candidates will attempt an answer to opening item 2 and will name something that is not a claim. *Instrument:* the submitted opening answers, coded *identifies that no claim exists* / *names a non-claim* / *absent* by a second reader.

**Prediction 2.** Most models will be most sensitive to an elicited input. *Instrument:* the sensitivity statement's named input, cross-referenced with its provenance classification.

**Prediction 3.** Most candidates will place provenance marks in an appendix at first submission. *Instrument:* the location of the classification, coded *beside the number on the circulating page* / *elsewhere in the document* / *separate sheet*.

**Prediction 4.** Most candidates will be unable to say where their own expectation of a normal value came from. *Instrument:* the fifth work step's output, coded *source identified* / *source not identified* per expectation named. **If most candidates can identify sources, the specimen-answer hazard is not operating in this cohort and the fifth step should be reduced to a check rather than a hunt.**

## What a learner should take from this paper

**What the design claims.** That an untraceable figure is harder to refuse than an unwarranted one; that provenance placed off the circulating page is provenance withheld; and that illustrative figures become benchmarks in the reader rather than in the document.

**What the evidence licenses.** Nothing about decisions. **The framework's emptiness is class 1** — verified from a complete reading — and everything built on it is class 3, including the four-way classification, which is this design's invention. **This is where the corpus is weakest and where the teaching is least warranted, and the session says so in its own text.**

**The concession worth remembering.** Your check will fail, and it was always going to. **Ask what a framework would have to look like for it to succeed, and notice that neither this session nor this programme can show you one.**
