## In plain terms

You were handed a model. It has a name, a structure, cells to fill, and a workbook that runs a simulation. Whatever comes out will be quoted with the framework's name attached.

**The framework asserts nothing.**

That is not the criticism anybody expects and it is the whole session. This module's source contains **no claimed multiples, no claimed savings and no efficiency figures.** Its metric catalogue is a taxonomy in which not one entry is populated with an observed value, a benchmark or a typical range. Its case-studies section is reserved and empty. **It contains no unwarranted magnitudes because it contains no magnitudes at all** — and it ships models that produce them.

**Figures produced downstream carry the authority of a named framework while resting entirely on elicited inputs, and because the framework asserts nothing they cannot be traced back to any claim in it.** There is no proposition to check them against. Asked where the number came from, the honest answer is *the model*. Asked what the model says, the honest answer is *nothing*.

**That is a worse failure than an unwarranted magnitude, which is at least locatable and refutable.**

The same shape has a purer specimen, and it is the session's anchor. An openly published agent configuration corpus, read at a pinned commit, holds **evaluation machinery**: graded behavioural checks, conformance lanes, baseline-equivalence comparisons, validators, test suites, eval drivers. Real engineering, done carefully. **It holds no committed result corpus** — the storage location is an empty placeholder. And its own prose uses the vocabulary of demonstrated benefit.

**The machinery is real and it is not a result.** That sentence is the session's central skill, and it needs teaching because the mistake is not made by careless readers. **It is made by everybody, including the authors** — an apparatus that could produce evidence feels like evidence, and nothing in a repository tree announces the difference.

The session's repair is the discipline [S054](/wiki/seminars/S054.md) installed for a security attribute table, moved into a financial model. **Every number carries its provenance beside it**: measured means an instrument produced it and you can name the instrument; elicited means a person supplied it and you can name the person; assumed means you chose it; inherited means it came from an example. **Uncertainty relocated is uncertainty preserved; uncertainty stripped is a guess wearing a fact's clothes.**

And there is one prohibition harder than any other in the stage. **Specimen answers become benchmarks whether or not anyone intends them to.** The corpus's question bank ships illustrative response menus containing figures, presented as the plausible range of customer answers; an engineer who has internalised them will hear a real customer's number as high or low against a scale nobody validated. **No such figure is reproduced anywhere in this repository**, and reproducing them to demonstrate the hazard would install the hazard.

**This paper applies the same test to itself in §3.12, and fails it.**

## Abstract

This paper defends teaching the untraceable output as a distinct and more dangerous failure than the unwarranted magnitude, and defends an exercise whose designed outcome is a check that cannot be completed.

The argument runs in five steps. **First**, having no evidence divides into two classes that behave differently: an asserted magnitude with no method is locatable, dateable and refutable, while a figure produced by an apparatus that asserts nothing has no parent claim and offers a challenger nowhere to land. **Second**, the second class has a purer form than a financial model — an openly published, carefully built configuration corpus containing substantial evaluation machinery, no committed results, and benefit vocabulary in its own prose — and that corpus is the session's anchor because nothing about it is careless. **Third**, the platform's own evaluation documentation has the same shape one layer up: a complete, buildable, well-documented harness publishing no agreement with human judgement, no calibration and no threshold rationale. **Fourth**, machinery cannot be converted into evidence by inspection; the only route in this repository runs through alignment against a human-graded subset, and even that is complicated by criteria that cannot be fully specified before outputs are seen. **Fifth**, an apparatus emitting a number without an interval has not established whether it found anything.

The session installs a machinery inventory and a results inventory searched with equal effort, a vocabulary audit, a five-bucket classification of every conclusion, a four-way provenance classification placed beside each number, a sensitivity statement, an attempted traceability check reported including its failure, a prohibition on simulating over an unsourced distribution, and the marking of every inherited figure as an illustration.

The paper concedes that the traceability check cannot come out any other way against this framework and that the programme has no example of one where it would succeed; that the provenance marking imposes a real readability cost; that using a vendor's most candid published artefact as the specimen risks teaching that candour is punished; and that **this repository is itself an instance of the pattern, carrying hundreds of falsifiable predictions of which none has been measured.**

## 1. The problem this session must solve

An unwarranted number can be found and argued with. **An untraceable number cannot.**

The distinction is not rhetorical. A figure asserted in a document has a location, a date, an author and a claim. **You can go to it, read what it says, notice that it cites nothing, and refuse it.** Every property that makes it bad also makes it addressable.

A figure produced by an apparatus that asserts nothing has none of those. It has a provenance chain that terminates in a workshop, a name that implies a body of knowledge, and no parent claim anywhere. **Challenged, it does not defend itself and it does not have to** — the challenge has nowhere to land, because there is no proposition in the framework the figure could contradict.

There is a second problem which is why the first one persists. **The framework's emptiness reads as rigour.** A document that declines to assert magnitudes looks disciplined, and it is disciplined in one specific respect: it has not over-claimed. **The absence of a claim is being read as the presence of restraint**, when what it actually produces is an instrument that cannot be checked.

A third is the one this platform generation has made ubiquitous. **Evaluation machinery has become cheap to build and results have not become cheap to produce.** A team can stand up graders, harnesses, conformance lanes and dashboards in a fortnight. Producing a defensible comparison against a stated alternative, with an interval, on a population that resembles the one they care about, takes far longer and is nobody's sprint goal. **The predictable equilibrium is an organisation with an impressive apparatus and no findings, describing itself in the language of findings** — not from cynicism but because the apparatus felt like the hard part and was.

And a fourth, which travels furthest. **Illustrative figures become norms.** They appear in specimen answers and threshold catalogues, unattributed, presented as what a plausible response looks like — and a reader who absorbs them acquires a scale they cannot cite and will use it to characterise real numbers without noticing that a comparison has occurred.

The session's response is two inventories, a vocabulary audit, a five-bucket classification, provenance marks beside the numbers, and a check that is run and reported as stopped.

**It depends completely on [S054](/wiki/seminars/S054.md).** A learner who has traced a value being inferred, its marker stripped, its justification dropped at assembly, and the same value then counted as evidence by a scorer, has already met this session's mechanism in a different medium. **The session names the inheritance explicitly** so that the learner recognises rather than relearns.

It assumes [S029](/wiki/seminars/S029.md), where criteria were stated before options were compared, because the sensitivity statement is that ordering applied to a model's inputs. And it assumes [S049](/wiki/seminars/S049.md), where the same pinned corpus was read for what a passing check is worth — **the learner already knows that a check's colour and a check's consequence are unrelated, and this session generalises that from one check to a whole apparatus.**

**The prior it works against is that a model is a calculation.** Learners treat the arithmetic as the artefact. **The arithmetic is the least interesting part and it is almost always correct** — computed percentages of this kind are reproducible and their formulas are given, and every input is a judgement, so the precision is spurious.

## 3. Theoretical basis

**3.1 Load profile.** Intrinsic load is low; the classification is four categories, the buckets are five and the check is one attempt. **The load is affective and it lands late**, when a learner realises the model they were given cannot be checked and that they will have to say so to people using it.

**3.2 The two failure classes, and why only one has a handle.**

This is the design's organising distinction and it is class 3 reasoning. Its warrant is what it explains: **every specimen in this session's material is one class or the other, and the recognisable class is the one everybody has been trained on.** An asserted magnitude with no method is locatable, dateable, sourceable and refutable. A figure emitted by an apparatus that asserts nothing is none of those.

The uncomfortable corollary is stated in the session and is worth repeating here. **The document that behaves worse — the one that asserts figures it cannot support — leaves its reader in a better epistemic position** than the document that asserts nothing and ships a model. That inversion is why the session exists, and it is the reason a curriculum cannot teach magnitude-hunting alone.

**3.3 What the pinned corpus warrants, and what it does not.**

The session's anchor is an openly published agent configuration corpus read at a pinned commit ([research/06-microsoft-platform/hve-core-repository.md](/research/06-microsoft-platform/hve-core-repository.md)). **Its warranted claims are architecture, configuration and observed-execution claims**: what files exist, what they declare, how they are packaged, and what the maintainers state about their own limits.

Those claims are substantial. Four separated artefact kinds with distinct application rules. Authoring source, packaging manifest and generated output kept apart, with the generated tree declared non-canonical. A gated phase workflow with explicit return paths. **Durable on-disk state under stable identifiers declared authoritative over session memory**, which buys provenance and restartability. A review subsystem decomposing orientation from judgement, scaling depth to assessed risk, fixing the lenses and normalising severity so two reviewers produce comparable findings. Distinct evaluation lanes and a full validator suite.

**And its own documented honesty is the reason it is a teaching object rather than a target.** It states that its behavioural checks are regex-graded and **test output shape and not correctness**; that conformance is **advisory by default** and lacks established calibration; that baseline equivalence covers a narrow contract with unresolved work; that **a check can be advisory at one tier while a scheduled run treats the same check as authoritative**; and, in its transparency statement, that the project ships no model and no safety classifier, that host behaviour varies, and that human oversight remains necessary.

**Then the finding, which was produced by a deliberate search rather than by inference.** At the pinned revision there is no committed controlled study, no calibrated baseline, no time-to-delivery dataset, no defect-rate comparison, no human-rated outcome dataset and no learner study. **The evals produce artefacts at run time; the repository stores none of them as evidence, and the storage location is an empty placeholder.** The note's own framing is the sentence the session is built on: **the limit is sharper here than for a prose handbook, because this repository *contains* evaluation machinery, and machinery invites the inference that results exist.**

**And the prose uses causal and outcome vocabulary anyway.** The note records benefit words appearing without support, and states plainly that **the gap between the mechanism claims the source warrants and the benefit claims it does not is the single most useful thing about it.**

**Its limits are extreme and are the session's own limits.** Perishability is total on paths, counts, plugin names and package versions, all of which moved during the reading. **Host and model dependence is absolute** — a result observed on one client and model is not a result about the corpus. Scale will overwhelm a small team. A single vendor's opinion about agent configuration is not the field. **And absence at one commit is not proof of absence in the project**, which the note states and this paper repeats because a cohort finding committed results later must revise the session rather than defend it.

**3.4 Why the platform's evaluation documentation is the same shape one layer up.**

The framework documentation is definitional and accurate about what the tooling computes ([research/06-microsoft-platform/agent-framework-and-evaluations-docs.md](/research/06-microsoft-platform/agent-framework-and-evaluations-docs.md)): three core types, two evaluator tiers, three conversation split strategies, repetitions for non-determinism, a four-category evaluator catalogue, and pass-or-fail output carrying a reason and a threshold.

**And it publishes no agreement rate with human judgement for any evaluator, no calibration data and no rationale for any default threshold.** Most of these evaluators are model-judged.

**Read as an instance rather than as a complaint, that is the session's pattern at platform scale.** A team can stand up the harness, produce a dashboard, and hold no basis whatever for believing the scores correspond to what a competent human would say. **No agreement figure is asserted anywhere in this paper or the session**, and a cohort that finds one published should treat the session's opening as due for revision.

**The documentation's own limits:** read through search excerpts rather than full fetches, with structure reconstructed; most agent evaluators are preview; one consulted page is an explicitly superseded variant.

**3.5 Why the two inventories are searched with equal effort.** An absence is evidence only if the search was competent, and the commonest defective audit is one that looked hard for the apparatus and glanced for the findings. **Requiring the results search to be described — what would have counted, where it was looked for, what came back — is what converts a keyword search into an audit**, and it is the step §9's prediction 2 expects most candidates to skimp.

**3.6 Why four categories rather than two.** Measured and not-measured would be enough for arithmetic and is not enough for accountability. **Elicited, assumed and inherited fail differently**: an elicited value has a person who can be re-asked, an assumed value has an author who can be argued with, and an inherited value has neither and is the most dangerous of the three because it looks like the others. Collapsing them hides the one that needs the mark most.

**3.7 Why the mark goes beside the number.** The output travels and the input sheet does not. **A provenance record placed anywhere other than on the circulating page is a record for the reader who was already going to ask**, and that reader was not the risk. This is identical to [S067](/wiki/seminars/S067.md)'s asymmetry-disclosure rule and the two sessions place it the same way deliberately.

**3.8 Why sensitivity comes before argument.** A model has many inputs and one that matters. **Identifying it converts a general anxiety about provenance into a specific claim about a specific number**, which is arguable. In most models of this kind the output is most sensitive to an elicited input, and that sentence belongs at the top of the document.

**3.9 Why an unsourced distribution forbids the simulation.** A simulation over a distribution nobody chose does not become evidence by being sampled many times; **it becomes a precise representation of the guess.** The source recommends a stochastic method for quantities it never quantifies, which is the clearest available instance of the failure, and the prohibition is the minimal response to it.

**3.10 Why machinery cannot be converted into evidence by inspection.**

The obvious repair, once the distinction is seen, is to validate the machinery. The human-computer-interaction literature on this is exact about the difficulty ([research/03-measurement-evaluation/shankar-evalgen.md](/research/03-measurement-evaluation/shankar-evalgen.md)). Using a model to grade a model **relocates the trust problem rather than solving it**: generated evaluators "simply inherit all the problems of the LLMs they evaluate, requiring further human validation." The repair offered is alignment — have a person grade a subset, then prefer the implementation whose verdicts best reproduce that person's grades. **That is the only route in this repository from an apparatus to an evidentiary basis, and it costs human labour that no amount of engineering removes.**

The finding that makes it harder still is named: **criteria drift** — "users need criteria to grade outputs, but grading outputs helps users define criteria." A person cannot fully specify what counts as good before seeing outputs, because seeing outputs is how they discover which dimensions vary and matter. The sharper form is that some criteria are **dependent on the particular outputs observed**, which "raises serious questions for approaches that assume the independence of evaluation from observation of model outputs."

**So an apparatus built before any output was seen is measuring criteria its authors could not yet have known were the right ones.** That is a second, independent reason machinery is not evidence, and it would hold even if every check were perfectly calibrated.

**Its limits:** the drift evidence is qualitative, establishing that the phenomenon is real and consequential rather than how prevalent or how large; the user study is small by construction; the tool is a research prototype. **It addresses criteria alignment and says nothing about statistical validity.** The cost the paper does not resolve is stated in its own limits and is inherited here: **if criteria are revised after seeing outputs, those outputs cannot cleanly serve as a held-out evaluation set.**

**3.11 Why an apparatus with no interval has not told you anything.**

The statistical treatment this module rests on attacks the convention these specimens exemplify: results reported with a highest-number-is-best mentality, in which a leading figure is highlighted "but not necessarily to test that result for any kind of statistical significance" ([research/03-measurement-evaluation/miller-adding-error-bars-to-evals.md](/research/03-measurement-evaluation/miller-adding-error-bars-to-evals.md)). Two conclusions bear directly. **A comparison of two systems on a small evaluation set frequently cannot distinguish them at all.** And **where items arrive in related groups rather than independently, an uncorrected interval is too narrow**, so the measurement looks more precise than it is.

**The transfer is one sentence: an apparatus that emits a number and no interval has not told you whether it found anything.**

**Its limits:** analytical rather than empirical, with most illustrative magnitudes resting on stylised assumptions; valid only where the items sample the population you care about; **and statistical validity says nothing about construct validity — a tight interval around a meaningless metric is still meaningless.** No figure from it is reproduced here.

**3.12 Why the external number cannot rescue you, and why this design applies the test to itself.**

The move a learner will make when their own apparatus yields nothing is to borrow a published figure. The evaluation book forecloses it: **"Public benchmarks will help you filter out bad models, but they won't help you find the best models for your application"** ([research/03-measurement-evaluation/huyen-ai-engineering.md](/research/03-measurement-evaluation/huyen-ai-engineering.md)) — with benchmark saturation, assumed contamination, and leaderboards omitting expensive benchmarks for cost reasons all recorded alongside. **Its limits:** a practitioner book, heavily citational, much of its most useful material resting on unattributed interviews, and its illustrative mappings are shapes rather than measurements. No magnitude from it appears here.

And the borrowed number has a governance problem one layer further away. An audit of the leaderboard that had become the de facto public ranking found its scores distorted by undisclosed platform policy: private testing with retraction produced **"biased Arena scores due to selective disclosure of performance results"**, concluding in **"overfitting to Arena-specific dynamics rather than general model quality"** ([research/03-measurement-evaluation/singh-leaderboard-illusion.md](/research/03-measurement-evaluation/singh-leaderboard-illusion.md)). **Its allocation figures are external estimates by its own account, its findings are specific to one platform at one time, and nothing in it establishes that models are not improving.** No figure from it is reproduced here.

**Then the test turned inward.** Every whitepaper in this programme closes with falsifiable predictions, each naming an instrument. **There are hundreds. Not one has been measured.** The predictions are written, the instruments named, the coding schemes specified and the artefacts defined — **which is machinery, and is not a result.** [WP-090](/wiki/whitepapers/WP-090.md) §8 concedes it directly, predicting that only a small fraction of this wiki's own predictions will ever be measured.

**Hold that beside the pinned corpus's empty results placeholder and notice that they are the same object.** §8's fourth objection is where the concession is examined rather than displayed.

**3.13 Why the failed check is the work.** A learner asked to trace an output to a claim will discover there is no claim. **Reporting that as the finding rather than as an obstacle is the entire transferable skill**, because it is the report they will have to write in industry about a model nobody has questioned.

**3.14 Why the opening items are unscaffolded, and why exercise 3 requires comparison.** The items are a generation phase: learners attempt a problem they have not been taught, fail, and are then taught, with the failed attempt improving later learning even when wrong ([research/04-professional-formation/sinha-kapur-productive-failure.md](/research/04-professional-formation/sinha-kapur-productive-failure.md)). **Guidance during generation reduces the failure rate and reduces learning**, which is why the release rule gates §§4, 5 and 9; and **consolidation must explicitly prompt comparison**, which exercise 3 supplies. **The evidence is school, university and laboratory work with no adult-professional study in its reference list**, and the extrapolation is conceded at [WP-001](/wiki/whitepapers/WP-001.md) §8.

**3.15 Why a second, independent instance was added, and why it is stronger than the first.**

The pinned corpus is a good specimen with one weakness as a teaching object: **the learner has to notice the absence unaided.** The machinery is visible, the empty results placeholder is not, and a candidate who never thought to look for results will not discover that there are none. That is realistic and it is also slow.

**The added instance removes the noticing problem, because the vendor names the metrics itself.** The documentation for the work-data access layer states that its servers undergo **continuous evaluation measuring accuracy, latency and reliability**, and **publishes no value for any of the three** — no figure, no range, no target, no series, no methodology, no cadence, no date ([research/06-microsoft-platform/microsoft-iq-intelligence-layer.md](/research/06-microsoft-platform/microsoft-iq-intelligence-layer.md)). **The learner is not searching for an absence in the abstract. They are searching for three named things, and finding none of them, which is a bounded task with a determinate answer.**

**The design's judgement is that this makes it the better exercise and the corpus the better anchor**, so both are kept. The corpus supplies the full shape — an inventory long enough to be genuinely impressive beside a results location that is genuinely empty — which is what makes the finding land physically rather than argumentatively. The vendor page supplies a version any learner can run in minutes, on an engagement, on a page a customer has already read. **One teaches the pattern; the other makes it portable.**

**Two further instances are carried because a pattern seen once is an anecdote.** The same layer **asserts rate limiting and quantifies no limit**, which is the identical shape in an operational field rather than a quality one — and is the version that will actually hurt somebody, because the ceiling is discovered in production. And the governance surface documents continuous evaluation of agent responses in detail while **publishing no evidence that it catches anything a manual review would not**, its stated benefit being **a claim about what the feature is for rather than a finding about what it does** ([research/06-microsoft-platform/foundry-control-plane-and-governance.md](/research/06-microsoft-platform/foundry-control-plane-and-governance.md)). **Four independent instances across four products, and not one number between them.**

**The symmetry constraint from §3.3 applies unchanged and is the part most likely to be got wrong.** A learner who concludes from the absence that the layer is unreliable has committed this session's error in the opposite direction and fails the exercise exactly as hard as one who concludes it is reliable. **The evidence supports neither. The finding is *unknown*, and the professional act is stating an unknown as an unknown** — which is harder than either alternative, because both alternatives are sentences and an unknown is a refusal to write one.

**What this licenses and what bounds it.** It licenses a claim about an absence on a dated reading, checkable by anybody with a browser. **It licenses nothing about whether the evaluation is happening, how well, or how the product performs**, and nothing about frequency — no survey exists of how often documentation asserts measurement without publishing results, and none is asserted. **The source is the most perishable in this repository and must not be pooled**: definitional product documentation containing no measurement sits beside one evidential-but-weak engineering evaluation, and **its own pages contradict each other on licensing, layer membership, storage and topology, so no single page can be trusted alone.** Substantial parts were not read; one named member of the family has no documentation behind it at all; **no retrieval quality measure, coverage statement or freshness bound is published for any layer in it**, which means the three unpublished metrics are the norm rather than the exception. **Every number in it is forbidden downstream and none appears in this paper or in the session.**

## 4. Session design and its warrant

The movements run situation, artefact, material, workflow, standard, failure modes, exercises, limits.

**Opening item 2 — which claim in the framework could your output contradict — is the instrument, and it has no answer.** It is asked before instruction so the learner reaches the emptiness themselves rather than being told about it. **A learner who is told the framework asserts nothing has learned a fact about one framework; a learner who looked for a proposition and could not find one has learned a procedure.**

**4.1 Why the machinery inventory is written first, and generously.**

The sequencing is the design's most deliberate choice. **The learner is asked to be genuinely impressed before being asked to search for results**, and the instruction is explicit that a short machinery list would make the finding trivial. Two reasons. The finding only lands if the apparatus is real — an audit of a shoddy system teaches that shoddy systems are shoddy. **And a learner who has spent half an hour admiring the engineering will feel the second inventory's emptiness rather than merely note it**, which is the difference between a fact retained and a disposition installed.

**4.2 Why the failing job is found rather than shown.** The pinned revision is not all-green. The session says so and does not say where. **Learning it from a page teaches a fact; finding it in the record teaches how much looking a mostly-green summary requires**, which is the normal condition of every dashboard a supplier will show them.

**4.3 Why the vocabulary audit is two columns and no commentary.** The word in one column, what would have to exist for it to be warranted in the next. **The format forbids editorialising**, which matters because the learner's instinct is to write an indictment. The corpus's authors were not cynical; they had built serious work and described it in the vocabulary available for describing serious work. **A mechanical audit survives that observation and an accusation does not.**

**4.4 Why five buckets rather than a pass-or-fail judgement.** Configured control, executable check, observed result, outcome evidence, unsupported inference. **The fifth is where the learning is**, and the corpus note says so directly. A classification with no entries in the fifth bucket is an audit that has not been performed, and the standard says that too.

**4.5 Why the fifth exercise turns the instrument on this programme.** It would be possible to teach the whole session without it. **The design includes it because a curriculum that exempts itself from its own test has taught the test as a thing done to other people's work.** The exercise's stated purpose is not humility: it is to establish that the error is structural, committed here by people who knew the distinction and stated it in every paper.

**4.6 Why the last workflow step attacks the audit's own classification.** The prompt asks the model to argue items out of the unsupported-inference bucket and to find the item wrongly placed in observed-result. **The second half is the diagnostic**; the first half exists so the learner sees how easy the argument is to make.

## 5. Predicted failure modes and countermeasures

**Machinery counted as evidence.** The failure the session is named after. *Countermeasure:* the two inventories on one page, and §4.1's sequencing.

**The asymmetric search.** The learner looks hard for the apparatus and glances for the findings. *Countermeasure:* the requirement to describe the results search — what would have counted, where it was looked for, what came back. **§9's prediction 2 predicts this countermeasure failing.**

**Provenance in an appendix.** Likely, because it is what every methodology convention encourages. *Countermeasure:* the standard's requirement that the mark be beside the number, and the [S070](/wiki/seminars/S070.md) element checking the page that will be forwarded.

**The check reported as an obstacle.** The learner treats the missing proposition as a gap in their work rather than as the result. *Countermeasure:* the standard states that the failed check is the work, and the [S070](/wiki/seminars/S070.md) element requires the check to be reported including where it could not be completed.

**The benefit word absorbed.** The learner starts using the corpus's own vocabulary. *Countermeasure:* the two-column vocabulary audit, and the warning sign — a verb in your summary is doing work no artefact in your inventory supports.

**The inherited figure used as a benchmark.** *Countermeasure:* the marking requirement, and the warning sign — you described a real customer's figure as high or low and cannot say against what.

**The simulation run anyway.** *Countermeasure:* the prohibition, stated as a prohibition rather than as advice, because the workbook makes running it the path of least resistance.

**The classification that collapses.** A learner marks everything elicited or everything assumed. *Countermeasure:* the sensitivity statement, which forces one input to be examined closely enough that its category has to be right.

## 7. Assessment and evidence generated

No entrustment evidence. **The programme's entrustment decisions begin at [S080](/wiki/seminars/S080.md).**

Four elements of this session's artefact are checked at [S070](/wiki/seminars/S070.md): that every number carries a provenance classification beside it with a source and a date; that the machinery and results inventories are both present with the results search described; that the traceability check is reported including where it could not be completed; and that any inherited figure is marked as an illustration rather than as a benchmark.

**Whether any number is correct is not assessed**, and the specification says so, because **nothing available to this programme could check one.**

The elements are written to be checkable on the page rather than as judgements about understanding, following the narrative-assessment finding that assessors reason from prose and use scores to confirm ([research/03-measurement-evaluation/govaerts-narrative-assessment.md](/research/03-measurement-evaluation/govaerts-narrative-assessment.md)). **Single-institution medical-education research using mock portfolios; think-aloud methods cannot capture fast processing; transfer is an argument rather than a given.**

## 8. Objections and rebuttals

**Objection 1: the traceability check cannot come out any other way, so it is a demonstration dressed as an exercise.**

At its strongest: the session sends a learner to test whether a framework asserts something, having already established in its own text that it does not. **The result is known to the designer, known to the paper, and stated in the module page before the learner begins.** An exercise whose outcome is fixed teaches the conclusion and not the method, and a learner who runs it has performed a ritual. **Worse, the session has no example of a framework where the check succeeds**, so a learner leaves knowing what failure looks like and having never seen success — which means they cannot recognise it, and may report failure against a framework that would have passed.

**Conceded without qualification, and the session states it in its own limits section rather than confining it here.** The rebuttal is only about what the exercise leaves behind: **the procedure — take an output, find the claim it could contradict, follow it, record where it stopped — is portable to any model a learner is handed**, and the fact that this instance terminates at the first step does not make the steps unavailable elsewhere. **The declined fix is to supply a contrasting framework that would pass the check.** It is declined because this programme has not found one. **That is a real gap in the source coverage rather than a design decision, and it should be closed if such a framework is ever read into this repository.** The session asks the learner what a framework would have to look like to pass, and cannot answer its own question.

**Objection 2: requiring provenance marks beside every number will make the model unreadable, and an unreadable model is not circulated, which removes whatever value it had.**

At its strongest: a commercial value document is read by people who will not tolerate annotation. **Marks beside every figure make the page look uncertain, and a page that looks uncertain is replaced by a summary somebody else writes** — at which point the provenance is stripped by a different hand and the session has achieved the opposite of its intention. The appendix convention exists for a reason, and the reason is that it keeps the discipline alive inside a document people will actually read.

**Conceded in part, and the rebuttal narrows the requirement rather than defending the strong form.** What the element needs is that **the classification is visible on the page that circulates**, which a compact mark satisfies — a single-character convention, a colour, a footnoted key. It does not require a paragraph per cell, and the session should say so more clearly than it does. **The deeper answer is that the objection's alternative is the failure mode the whole module describes**: a clean page whose numbers rest on a workshop is precisely the artefact that travels furthest and can be checked least. **The declined fix is to permit the classification in an appendix where the audience requires a clean page.** Declined because the audience always requires a clean page, so the exception would consume the rule. **The readability cost is real and is accepted.**

**Objection 3: using the vendor's most candid published artefact as the specimen punishes candour and teaches teams to publish less.**

At its strongest, and this is the objection the design finds most morally uncomfortable. **The pinned corpus is unusually honest.** It states that its own graders test shape rather than correctness. It states that conformance is advisory and uncalibrated. It ships a transparency note conceding that it provides no model, no classifier, and that human oversight remains necessary. **Almost nothing else in the industry does any of that**, and a curriculum that selects it as the specimen for a session about unwarranted benefit claims is holding up the most self-critical artefact available as the cautionary example. **The predictable lesson a practitioner draws is that publishing your limits gets you used as a teaching object, while publishing nothing gets you left alone** — which is precisely the wrong incentive, and the session's own argument about honest reporting at [S069](/wiki/seminars/S069.md) makes it worse rather than better.

**Partly conceded, and the concession changes how the session must be delivered rather than whether it runs.** Three answers. **The session says repeatedly that nothing about the corpus is careless and that this is why it is a good teaching object** — a straw specimen would teach the lesson worse, because the failure being taught is one that survives competence and candour. **The corpus's honesty is presented as the finding rather than the fault**: the two sentences a learner is most likely to remember are the corpus's own statements about its graders and its tiers. **And the session applies the same audit to this repository in its fifth exercise and to this paper in §3.12**, so the specimen is not being asked to carry a standard the design exempts itself from. **The declined fix is to use a constructed example instead of a real corpus.** It is declined for [WP-049](/wiki/whitepapers/WP-049.md) §8's reason and a sharper one here: **a constructed example cannot exhibit the thing that makes this pattern important, which is that the machinery is genuinely good.** The incentive risk is real and is accepted, and a facilitator who presents this session as an exposure rather than as an autopsy has broken it.

**Objection 4: this paper's self-audit buys absolution cheaply.**

At its strongest: §3.12 announces that this repository carries hundreds of unmeasured predictions and calls its own prediction register machinery. **That is a confession with no consequence.** The register remains. The predictions remain unmeasured. The paper continues, in the very next section, to make design claims resting on nothing but argument, and it will close with more predictions of its own that will also go unmeasured. **Naming a defect is not addressing one**, and a design that performs self-criticism while changing nothing has discovered that the appearance of rigour is available at a discount — which is exactly the charge this session lays against benefit vocabulary. **The self-audit is benefit vocabulary in a hair shirt.**

**Conceded outright. This is the sharpest objection in the module and the paper has no rebuttal it believes.** Three notes, none of which is a defence. The register is not costless: **it is the artefact that makes this objection formulable**, and a reader can enumerate exactly what this programme has failed to measure because the programme wrote it down. That is a lower bar than measurement and it is not zero. **The declined fix is to delete the prediction register on the grounds that unmeasured predictions are decoration.** It is declined because deleting it would remove the only mechanism by which the failure is visible, and would leave the design's claims exactly as unwarranted while looking cleaner — **which is the non-closure failure this module exists to name.** The other declined fix is to measure them, and it is declined on capability rather than principle: **the programme has never been run, so its instruments have never had an occasion.** That is an excuse and this paper records it as one. **A reader is entitled to weigh §3.12 as the session's own specimen rather than as its author's humility, and §9's prediction 6 is written so that a cohort can find out which.**

## 9. Falsifiable predictions and instrumentation

**Prediction 1.** Most candidates will attempt an answer to opening item 2 and will name something that is not a claim. *Instrument:* the submitted opening answers, coded *identifies that no claim exists* / *names a non-claim* / *absent* by a second reader.

**Prediction 2.** Most results searches will be reported as an outcome without being described as a search — no statement of what would have counted as a result and where it was looked for. *Instrument:* the audit artefact, coded for the presence of a `search_performed` block with a non-empty `looked_for` list. **This predicts the failure of §5's countermeasure against the asymmetric search**, and it is included because a countermeasure whose failure rate is unmeasured is an assumption.

**Prediction 3.** Most models will be most sensitive to an elicited input. *Instrument:* the sensitivity statement's named input, cross-referenced with its provenance classification.

**Prediction 4.** Most candidates will place provenance marks in an appendix at first submission. *Instrument:* the location of the classification, coded *beside the number on the circulating page* / *elsewhere in the document* / *separate sheet*.

**Prediction 5.** Most candidates will be unable to say where their own expectation of a normal value came from. *Instrument:* the sixth exercise's output, coded *source identified* / *source not identified* per expectation named. **If most can identify sources, the specimen-answer hazard is not operating in this cohort and the exercise should be reduced to a check rather than a hunt.**

**Prediction 6.** A majority of candidates, asked in the fifth exercise to classify this repository's own prediction register, will place it in *observed result* or *outcome evidence* rather than in *machinery*. *Instrument:* the exercise submission, coded by bucket. **This is the direct test of §8's fourth objection**: a confirmation means the self-audit is not transferring, and that §3.12 is being read as the design's humility rather than as its specimen.

**Prediction 7.** Candidates instructed to compare their item-2 answer against the taught criterion will produce a correctly-shaped traceability report more often than candidates who read the criterion without the comparison prompt. *Instrument:* a within-cohort split on whether exercise 3 was completed, with reports coded *names a proposition* / *states no proposition exists* / *neither*. **This is the session's only direct test of the mechanism §3.14 rests on**, and a null result would mean the comparison requirement is ceremony.

## What a learner should take from this paper

**What the design claims.** That the presence of measurement machinery is routinely mistaken for the presence of measurements; that an untraceable figure is harder to refuse than an unwarranted one; that provenance placed off the circulating page is provenance withheld; and that illustrative figures become benchmarks in the reader rather than in the document.

**What the evidence licenses.** Nothing about decisions. **The pinned corpus's architecture and its own documented limits are class 1 — read at a commit, with the absence produced by a deliberate search — and absence at one revision is not proof of absence in the project.** The value framework's emptiness is class 1 from a complete reading of material no public reader can check. Everything built on either is class 3, including the four-way classification and the five buckets, which are this design's inventions. **This is where the corpus is weakest and where the teaching is least warranted, and the session says so in its own text.**

**No magnitude appears anywhere in this paper**, including from the openly published handbook whose four unsupported figures are recorded as specimens and reproduced nowhere.

**The concession worth remembering.** Your check will fail, and it was always going to. **Ask what a framework would have to look like for it to succeed, and notice that neither this session nor this programme can show you one** — and then read §8's fourth objection, where this paper fails its own test and says so.
