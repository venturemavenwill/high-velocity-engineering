## In plain terms

Reusable starting points carried between engagements are named, in the practice corpus behind this programme, as one of four things that must hold together if accelerated delivery is to produce value rather than activity.

**That is the entire treatment.** Nothing describes how one is built, what it must carry, how it is versioned, who owns it, when it is retired, or whether reuse ever produced a better result than starting again. It is a pillar with nothing underneath it, and this session is written into the gap.

There are two reasons to be more careful here than enthusiasm suggests.

**The first is that a starting point is precisely the instrument that turns step-skipping into acceleration.** The rule holding this whole practice together is stated as a headline: use the tooling to make each step better, not to skip steps. The customer-facing material's proudest anecdote is a prototype generated on the first day from a template, with no discovery activity described at all. **Read against the method, the flagship story is step-skipping presented as speed, and neither document notices.** You are about to build the thing that produced that contradiction.

**The second is residue.** The same corpus is unusually good on this in a neighbouring context: reusing an environment after a project closes is named a policy violation rather than an inefficiency, explicitly because the asset is bound to a project identity. **That instinct is correct and is applied to infrastructure and not to artefacts.** A repository carries a customer's thresholds, schemas and assumptions exactly as an environment carries their data, and it travels more easily.

## Abstract

This paper defends treating a reusable asset as a set of claims requiring provenance and retirement, rather than as a head start. The argument runs in four parts. **First**, a starting point is at its most attractive when its provenance is freshest and at its most dangerous when it is reused, because the artefact does not degrade while the reason for it does. **Second**, the corpus that names reuse as a pillar supplies no mechanism for it — no provenance requirement, no versioning rule, no ownership, no retirement criterion, and no evidence that reuse ever produced a better outcome than starting again. **Third**, the same corpus's best available instinct about residue is applied to environments and withheld from artefacts, although a repository carries a customer's assumptions exactly as an environment carries their data and travels far more easily. **Fourth**, the session's platform anchor supplies the missing discipline in a form a learner can execute: a public agent configuration corpus read at a pinned commit, whose note records the revision, the package version, the host, the model, the date and the fact that paths and counts moved while the note was being written. **Pinning does not slow the decay; it makes the decay visible**, which is exactly what makes reuse safe and exactly what its absence makes unsafe.

The session produces a reuse ledger with four fields per item, a pin block, and a retirement list carrying at least one entry with a stated cost. The paper concedes that no source behind this programme describes such a ledger; that inferring the practice from an omission is a weak move this programme has now made in three separate stages; that the nearest thing to evidence about reuse in the corpus points against it; and that the discipline it proposes may simply be unaffordable, which §8's first objection states at its strongest and does not answer.

## 1. The problem this session must solve

Three properties make a reusable asset unusually hostile to careful handling.

**Its provenance decays faster than its usefulness.** A threshold set on a Thursday afternoon works, and continues to work, long after everybody who could say what it was tuned against has moved on. An evaluation set assembled by a selection rule nobody wrote down keeps returning results. **The artefact does not degrade; the reason for it does**, and nothing in the artefact signals which state it is in.

**Its value is described in units that reward reuse.** A starting point is talked about in elapsed time saved, and elapsed time saved is the measure the corpus's own thesis says is activity rather than impact. **An asset justified by time is an asset nobody will ask harder questions about.**

**Its blind spots become the next engagement's blind spots, invisibly.** Where a single source drives a generated process, that source's gaps are the process's gaps, and no step records the source's version, age or coverage anywhere in any output. A stale input produces confident, well-formatted, out-of-date results carrying no signal that this happened. **That mechanism is documented for a knowledge catalogue and applies without modification to a starter repository.**

The session's response is not a quality bar. It is a set of four questions per item that have answers, and a requirement to retire something.

## 3. Theoretical basis

**3.1 Load profile.** Intrinsic load is moderate and the concepts are few, but the germane load is unusually high because every judgement is a per-item judgement made against an artefact the learner authored. **The design spends its surplus on the separation in 3.2 and buys that by supplying the specimen rather than asking learners to find one.** This is a design judgement and carries no external warrant.

**3.2 Why provenance is a property of the item and not of its author.** *Written by a senior engineer* records who to ask and, once they have left, records nothing. *Tuned against the returned records from a named period, by the method in this decision record* survives the person. **The test is whether the row still functions when everybody named in it is unavailable**, which is the ordinary case at the point of reuse.

The platform anchor is the worked demonstration. The note at [research/06-microsoft-platform/hve-core-repository.md](/research/06-microsoft-platform/hve-core-repository.md) records the corpus at a named commit with a package version, a host, a model, a date and a build state — and states that file counts, paths, plugin names and collection membership all moved while the note was being written. **A pin does not arrest decay. It converts an unanswerable question about currency into an arithmetic one.** The note's own limit is that only the mechanism layer is quotable without a date; the paths are not, and no outcome evidence exists at that revision.

**3.3 Why problem-specific and customer-specific must be separated per item.** The distinction is not obvious and cannot be made at the level of a repository. A field structure that everyone at the operator believes a regulator set, and that one person thinks a predecessor set to match an old system, is one organisation's inheritance wearing a standard's clothes. **Carrying it forward exports somebody's unexamined belief as a design.**

**3.4 Why a staleness signal must be observable rather than scheduled, and where the vocabulary comes from.** The platform documentation supplies the distinction more cleanly than any prose could. The Learn knowledge service is documented as refreshing incrementally after content updates with a full refresh once a day, and the note at [research/06-microsoft-platform/learn-mcp-server-docs.md](/research/06-microsoft-platform/learn-mcp-server-docs.md) records the consequence exactly: **cadence bounds nothing about latency.** The same source documents that deprecated content redirects to the next best appropriate content, so a fetch of a known-good address may silently return something else. **A citation resolves to a redirect target, not to a document**, which is the general form of every stale reuse.

*Review annually* is an intention held by nobody in particular. *This breaks when the platform's default changes, and the pipeline fails on the day it does* is a signal the receiving team receives whether or not they were looking. This is the same preference the programme applies to withdrawal at [S074](/wiki/seminars/S074.md): a state change beats a person remembering. **The documentation's limits: first-party, definitional throughout, evidential nowhere, and extremely perishable.**

**3.5 Why a carried evaluation set is the hardest row in the ledger.** The human-computer-interaction literature on evaluation design names the problem directly: **criteria drift** — users need criteria to grade outputs, but grading outputs is how they discover what the criteria should be ([research/03-measurement-evaluation/shankar-evalgen.md](/research/03-measurement-evaluation/shankar-evalgen.md)). Some criteria appear to depend on the particular outputs observed, which the authors say raises serious questions for approaches assuming evaluation is independent of observing model outputs.

The consequence for reuse is sharp and is not a matter of hygiene. **A set assembled during one engagement encodes criteria its authors could not yet have known were the right ones**, and carrying it to a different problem carries that encoding silently. The set may still be useful; what may not travel is the belief that a result from it means what a result from a specified set would mean. **The note's limits: qualitative evidence establishing that the phenomenon is real rather than how often, a small user study by construction, and a research prototype. Take the drift concept, not the interface.**

**3.6 Why last time is evidence about last time.** Accumulated practice explains least in domains with unstable conditions, delayed feedback and contested criteria ([research/04-professional-formation/macnamara-deliberate-practice-limits.md](/research/04-professional-formation/macnamara-deliberate-practice-limits.md)) — which is a description of engagement work. **No magnitude from that source is reproduced here or anywhere in this wiki**; it was read from the official abstract, its underlying studies are correlational and range-restricted, and its professions estimate is the least stable of the set. The direction is what the design uses: repetition compounds where rules are stable and feedback is fast, and reuse is not automatically improvement.

**3.7 Why at least one retirement is mandatory, and why disposability survives convenience.** The default is to carry, because carrying is free at the moment of decision and costly later, to somebody else. Requiring a retirement forces the ledger to have been read; requiring its cost to be stated forces it to have been a decision rather than a tidy-up. **The corpus's own best mechanism agrees**: retirement is expressed there through collection membership and metadata rather than inferred from a file's age, which is the only treatment of retirement as a first-class state available anywhere in this programme's sources.

And spike code was discarded at [S031](/wiki/seminars/S031.md) because it was written to answer a question and not to be maintained. **That reason has not changed because the code is now available and the next engagement is in a hurry.** A starting point assembled from artefacts that were deliberately disposable has laundered their status.

**3.8 The property a pin cannot capture, and the difference between a gap and a structural exception.**

§3.2 states that a pin is the whole of a starting point's safety. **That is too strong, and a second platform family falsifies it in a way worth recording rather than smoothing over.**

An agent development toolchain's documentation records that **seven named products and four role names in one family have all been renamed**, with old names surviving in addresses, package names and prose — sometimes on the same page as the new ones ([research/06-microsoft-platform/agents-toolkit-and-sdk-family.md](/research/06-microsoft-platform/agents-toolkit-and-sdk-family.md)). More consequentially: **one component ships under two package names with two different commands, both appearing on live documentation pages, both presented as current.** This is not deprecation with a redirect, where an old name resolves and something announces the change. It is two current names at once, with nothing to distinguish them.

**A pin answers *what was true, and when*. It does not answer *what was it called to the reader*** — and where two names are simultaneously live, that is not a property of the artefact at all. So §4's pin block gains three lines: the name used, the alternative live name where one exists, and whether a published support window exists. **The third is the one most often skipped and hardest to reconstruct**, because *no announced end* is an absence of information rather than a commitment to continue, and a dependency with no published end date has no staleness signal available to it.

The same source supplies a second correction. **Its version tables are floors rather than facts** — accurate to one build of one editor extension, with the source itself recording that some templates ship an older version than the table asserts. **A version table written by a person is an observation**, and a starter repository that copies one has converted a snapshot into a specification. §4's ledger therefore records the table's kind alongside its contents.

**And a well-behaved counter-example, because the session would otherwise teach only suspicion.** A cloud AI platform's construct for binding tools once behind a single endpoint publishes its own coverage table, distinguishing capabilities that work both through the binding and directly, capabilities available only through it, and capabilities that cannot go through it at all **because execution happens client-side** ([research/06-microsoft-platform/foundry-agent-build-and-runtime.md](/research/06-microsoft-platform/foundry-agent-build-and-runtime.md)). **A gap may close; an exception rooted in where something runs will not**, and §4 requires the ledger to distinguish them and to record coverage as *unknown* where the supplier is silent — which is a better row than an empty one.

**Three limits, and §8 carries the first.** **The extended pin block is this design's invention**: no source establishes that recording a name, an alternative and a support window improves reuse, and none measures how often the ambiguity misleads anybody. **No published effect of the tool-binding construct on any outcome exists** — the argument for it is architectural and no measurement of cost, accuracy or quality accompanies it. And verification depth in the toolchain source is uneven and recorded per page: some pages fetched in full, roughly thirty read only as search excerpts, and two policy documents not fetched at all, **so any criteria quoted from them are the subset the excerpts returned and must never be presented as a complete list.**

## 4. Session design and its warrant

**The two thresholds are the session's centre and they cannot be carried as values.** Their provenance is a person's Thursday. The learner must decide what may travel instead — the fact that a threshold is required at that point, the method for setting one, or the observation that would justify a value — and each of those is a different row with different consequences for the receiving team.

**The evaluation set is the harder case and is deliberately second.** Its selection rule was never written down, so the set encodes a rule nobody can state. A set may still be useful under those conditions; what may not travel is the belief that a result from it means what a result from a specified set would mean.

**The retirement is chosen adversarially: the learner picks the item they will most resent retiring.** A retirement chosen for convenience teaches nothing, and the cost statement is what makes the decision legible.

**The closing question is fixed:** what happens if the receiving team uses the item without doing the re-establishment step? A row whose answer is *they would notice* must say how. A row whose answer is *they would not* is not finished.

## 5. Predicted failure modes and countermeasures

**Carrying by default.** *Countermeasure:* the mandatory retirement, and the reading that a ledger matching the repository has not been read.

**Provenance replaced by authorship.** *Countermeasure:* the field is assessed per row against the survives-the-person test.

**The unprovenanced constant carried as a default.** *Countermeasure:* the threshold rows, which cannot be completed with a value.

**Value described in elapsed time.** *Countermeasure:* the ledger has no field for it, and a learner who supplies one has answered a question nobody asked.

**Residue.** *Countermeasure:* partial. The session names the parallel with the environment rule and relies on the learner to apply it. **There is no mechanical check here, and this is the failure mode most likely to survive the session**, because residue is invisible to the person who put it there.

**The invisible catalogue.** No version, age or coverage recorded anywhere. *Countermeasure:* the staleness-signal field, which is the only field with no acceptable empty answer.

## 8. Objections and rebuttals

**Objection 1: this makes reuse so expensive that nobody will do it, which loses the pillar's entire value.**

At its strongest: the whole argument for carrying assets between engagements is that it is cheap. A ledger requiring provenance, a separation judgement, a re-establishment step and a staleness signal for every item costs more than rebuilding the asset, and it will be produced once, filed, and never updated — at which point the receiving team has a stale ledger describing a stale asset, which is worse than an asset with no ledger, because the ledger will be believed.

**This is conceded in part and the mitigation is real but incomplete.** The ledger has four fields on purpose, and the session's requirement is a row per carried item rather than per file. The retirement requirement exists to shrink what is carried rather than to document all of it. **The fix that was considered and declined** was to require the ledger only for items the receiving team must not trust — the unprovenanced ones. It was rejected because deciding which items those are is exactly the judgement the ledger exists to force, and a ledger that documents only the items you already knew were doubtful documents nothing. **The objection is entitled to say that a discipline nobody can afford is a discipline nobody will practise, and this paper cannot show that this one is affordable.**

**Objection 2: the residue argument treats a repository as though it were an environment, and they are not alike.**

At its strongest: the environment-reuse prohibition exists because an environment holds a customer's data, credentials and network position, and the consequences of carrying those across a boundary are immediate and legal. A starter repository holds code and configuration, most of which is generic by construction, and applying the same prohibition to it is a category error dressed as rigour — it will produce elaborate cleansing rituals for material that was never sensitive.

The rebuttal is narrow and concedes the frame. **The residue this session is about is epistemic rather than legal**: a threshold tuned against one customer's data, a schema inherited from one customer's predecessor system, an assumption about how a workforce behaves. Those do not create a disclosure problem and they do create a correctness problem, because they will be applied to a different population without anybody deciding to. **The parallel is with the reason for the environment rule — that an asset is bound to the engagement that produced it — rather than with its consequences.** Whether that reasoning holds for artefacts is this design's argument and nobody's evidence.

**Objection 3: pinning is ceremony, because nobody ever goes back to the pin.**

At its strongest: a pin block is written once, at the moment of greatest enthusiasm, and never consulted again. The receiving team does not diff the pinned revision against the current one; they clone, run, and find out. **A field that is filled in and never read is worse than an absent field, because it creates the appearance of currency management where none exists** — and the platform note this session rests on proves the point against itself, since the corpus it pins moved during the writing of the note and no consumer was notified.

**This is conceded, and it is the objection this paper finds hardest.** The mitigation is that the pin's value is realised at exactly one moment — when something has gone wrong and somebody asks what the artefact was when it worked — and that moment is common enough to pay for a cheap field. That is an assertion about frequency and **this paper has no measurement of it.** **The declined fix was to require a scheduled pin review**, and it was declined for the reason 3.4 gives: a scheduled review has no observer, and replacing an unobservable field with an unobservable ritual is not an improvement. The honest position is that the pin is a bet that the retrospective question gets asked, and §9's fifth prediction is written to lose that bet if it is wrong.

## 9. Falsifiable predictions and instrumentation

**Prediction 1.** Most provenance entries on a first attempt will name a person or a team rather than an observation or a record. *Instrument:* the ledgers, coded per row against the survives-the-person test.

**Prediction 2.** Learners will more often produce an observable staleness signal for items bound to a platform than for items bound to a customer's process. *Instrument:* the signal field, coded observable or scheduled, cross-tabulated with item type.

**Prediction 3.** The retirement chosen will more often be an item of low value than the item the learner most resents retiring, despite the instruction. *Instrument:* the retirement, against the learner's own stated resentment ranking collected separately.

**Prediction 4.** Few learners will reach for the environment-reuse parallel without prompting. *Instrument:* the artefacts and the session record, checked for the reference before the facilitator supplies it. **If most do reach for it, the retrieval from [S074](/wiki/seminars/S074.md) is stronger than the design assumed** and the session can afford to spend its time elsewhere.

**Prediction 5.** A majority of learners will complete the pin block and will not use it again — no learner will re-check a pinned fact against the current head unless exercise 2 compels it, and among those who do, most will report the discrepancy as an inconvenience rather than as the finding. *Instrument:* the workflow record, coded for whether a pinned fact was re-checked outside exercise 2, and the learner's own written characterisation of any discrepancy found. **This predicts the failure of the session's own countermeasure**, and it is included because §8's third objection cannot be answered by argument. If it holds, the pin block is ceremony and the session should stop teaching it as discipline and start teaching it as forensics.

**Prediction 6.** Ledgers will carry more rows for artefacts than for defaults — configuration values, thresholds and timeouts inherited without an owner will be under-represented relative to their presence in the repository. *Instrument:* a count of ledger rows by item type against an independent inventory of the same repository, taken by a second reader. **If this holds, the unprovenanced constant survives the exact instrument built to catch it**, which would mean the ledger's granularity requirement is set at the wrong level.

**None of these has been measured.** No cohort has run this session, and no instrument named above has been applied to anybody.

## What a learner should take from this paper

**What the design claims.** That a name is not a provenance; that a reused asset with no staleness signal will be used stale and confidently; and that a starting point is the instrument by which step-skipping is mistaken for acceleration.

**What the evidence licenses.** Nothing about whether reuse helps. The pillar is named in the corpus and given no mechanism; the residue instinct is verified in a neighbouring context and not applied to artefacts by anybody but this design.

**The concession worth remembering.** The only thing in the corpus that resembles evidence about reuse points the other way: on a mature codebase, generated work reused outdated patterns and ignored the architecture's constraints, and the workaround was to make failing tests the specification. **That sits in speaker notes while the reuse claims sit on slides.**

## Evidence status

**Verified in this repository.** That reusable starting points carried between engagements are named as one of four things that must hold together for the delivery model to produce impact rather than activity, and that no mechanism, provenance requirement, versioning rule, ownership or retirement criterion for them appears anywhere; that the corpus states as a headline rule that the tooling must be used to make each step better rather than to skip steps, and that its customer-facing material's flagship anecdote generates a prototype on the first day from a template with no discovery activity described, with neither document noticing the contradiction; that reusing an environment after a project closes is named a policy violation rather than an inefficiency, explicitly because the asset is bound to a project identity; that the model claims the tooling amplifies the operator rather than levelling the field; and that the one honest note about a mature production codebase records generated work reusing outdated patterns and ignoring architectural constraints, with failing tests adopted as the specification, sitting in speaker notes against contrary headline claims. All recorded at [research/05-fde-craft/microsoft-accelerated-delivery-practice.md](/research/05-fde-craft/microsoft-accelerated-delivery-practice.md) on a complete reading. **Also verified: that where a single knowledge source drives a generated process, no stage records that source's version, age or coverage anywhere in any output, so a stale source produces confident, well-formatted, out-of-date results with no signal that this happened** — recorded at [research/05-fde-craft/microsoft-agentic-threat-modelling.md](/research/05-fde-craft/microsoft-agentic-threat-modelling.md).

**Cited from general knowledge, not verified here.** That an artefact's provenance decays faster than its apparent usefulness; that a value with no recorded origin becomes a default through repetition; and that documentation scheduled for review rather than triggered by an observable change is reviewed late or not at all. That criteria for judging output are discovered by looking at output rather than specified in advance, and that accumulated practice explains least in domains with unstable conditions and delayed feedback — the notes for both are held here and were consulted for direction and mechanism only. **No rate, proportion or magnitude is asserted for any of these, here or anywhere in this wiki.**

**Design reasoning with no external warrant.** The reuse ledger and its four fields; the pin block and its contents; provenance defined as a property of the item rather than of its author; the per-item separation of customer-specific from problem-specific content; the observable staleness signal; the mandatory retirement with a stated cost and the instruction to choose the most resented one; the extension of the environment-residue reasoning to repositories; the prohibition on carrying a threshold as a value; the judgement in §8's third objection that the retrospective question is asked often enough to justify the field; and all six predictions in §9.

**Grounded in vendor documentation, with its version and its silence recorded.** The platform anchor is an openly published agent configuration corpus, recorded at [research/06-microsoft-platform/hve-core-repository.md](/research/06-microsoft-platform/hve-core-repository.md) at a named commit with its package version, host, model, date and build state — which was **not all-green**. **The silences that matter, and they are the reason this session exists.** The corpus publishes **no committed controlled study, no calibrated baseline, no time-to-delivery dataset, no defect-rate comparison, no human-rated outcome dataset and no learner study** at the pinned revision; the storage location for results is an empty placeholder, and its own prose nonetheless uses benefit vocabulary. It publishes **no provenance requirement for anything it ships, no coverage statement, no currency statement and no staleness signal** — a consumer cannot tell from any artefact whether it was current last month or last year, what it was validated against, or whether anybody still maintains it. Where a date exists at all it is a commit date, which records when a file was touched and not when its contents were last true. The Learn documentation **bounds no indexing lag**, publishes **no rate limit value, window or retry guidance** although it confirms limits exist, and publishes **no measurement whatever that grounding improves an answer**. **The engineering handbook demonstrates the same silence in the most legible form available**: its pages are individually dated, the dates run across several years, a large band share a date that looks like a bulk edit rather than a review, and nothing in its navigation distinguishes a maintained page from an abandoned one. If any publisher of a reusable asset ships a currency or coverage statement with it, this session's argument weakens and this paper should record the version that did it.

**And their silences.** **Verification depth in the toolchain source is uneven and stated per page**: some fetched and read in full, roughly thirty read only through search-returned excerpts with structure reconstructed, and two policy documents not fetched at all — criteria quoted from those are the subset the excerpts returned and are explicitly not a complete gate. **Its ergonomics vocabulary appears without measurement anywhere**, and none of it may travel into teaching material. **Nothing in it states how often the two-live-names ambiguity misleads a reader, or how a reader is expected to resolve it**; the source's own instruction is to pin the version, record the date, assess the reasoning and never assess the command, which is the discipline §4 adopts. On the second source, **no published effect of the tool-binding construct, or of its neighbours, on any outcome exists** — the arguments are architectural and plausible and no measurement of token cost, selection accuracy or retrieval quality accompanies them — and **the wider source publishes numeric guidance in quantity with no study, curve or citation behind any of it**, which is why every figure in it is forbidden downstream and none appears in this paper or its session.
