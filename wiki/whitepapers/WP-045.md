## In plain terms

This is the stage's first assessment occasion and it marks something narrower than the title suggests.

**Whether the increment works is not the subject.** The subject is whether you can say where its boundary was, who moved it, when, and what you let past when the date stopped moving.

Three assessed elements are unusual and they are the ones that matter.

**Every departure from the slice definition must be marked and dated.** A departure discovered at shipping is recorded as a departure, not as a revision. The design's position is that **an undated exclusion was not a decision, it was an outcome**, and dating is the cheapest available test of which one happened.

**The capacity statement travels unrevised.** A statement that can be edited at assembly will be, and the softened version is always the one that circulates.

**The what-this-does-not-establish page must be specific.** Not *this is an early increment* — named inferences: that it works at the volume described, that it behaves on data nobody saw, that the recipient's colleagues would use it. **That page is the pack's most valuable one and the first deleted before circulation**, which is why it is marked here as it was at [S040](/wiki/seminars/S040.md).

The defence is adversarial in one specific way. **The assessor names something in your increment and asks why it was not an exclusion.** A candidate who can only answer *because it was needed* has not drawn a boundary. A candidate who concedes it could have been excluded and says what that would have cost has answered properly, and that is a pass.

## Abstract

This paper defends closing the delivery module with an assessment of the boundary of an increment rather than of the increment. The argument is that a delivery is distinguished from a result by whether its exclusions were decided, that dating is the only cheap evidence of decision available, and that the artefacts most likely to be softened at assembly — the review's unread boundary, the capacity statement, the refusals — are exactly the ones that carry the stage's content. The occasion installs an eight-part increment pack assembled from four prior artefacts, defended against an assessor holding only the proof pack and the slice definition, and closes its specification with the statement that nothing else is assessed. The paper concedes that the occasion cannot mark whether the boundary was well drawn, that no source supplies a basis for such a mark, and that requiring exclusions to be dated rewards learners with better note-keeping as much as learners with better judgement.

## 1. The problem this session must solve

An increment has shipped. Two forces now act on the account of it, and they act in the same direction.

**The first is assembly.** A pack is written after the fortnight, by somebody who knows how it came out, from records made under pressure. Every rough edge has an obvious smoothing available: the blocker that was worked around becomes a decision, the review that was cut short becomes a review, the refusal that was actually a deferral becomes a refusal.

**The second is the increment's own political effect.** The corpus behind this stage records it accurately: working software is hard to argue with, so resistance dissolves in front of a running increment in a way it does not in front of a document. **That is true, and it is the mechanism by which an increment ends an argument without settling the disagreement.** A pack assembled after a successful demonstration inherits the room's agreement and records it as consensus.

The occasion's response is to assess the properties those two forces destroy — dates, unread boundaries, refusals, unrevised statements — and to make the defence about the boundary rather than about the outcome.

## 2. Entry state and prior knowledge

**Entry state: this-programme-only.** The occasion assembles four artefacts and cannot be attempted without them: the slice definition from [S041](/wiki/seminars/S041.md), the scope ledger from [S042](/wiki/seminars/S042.md), the specification from [S043](/wiki/seminars/S043.md), and the review record and capacity statement from [S044](/wiki/seminars/S044.md). The commitment from [S039](/wiki/seminars/S039.md) is what the increment is answerable to.

The prior the occasion works against is that shipping is the achievement. Candidates arrive expecting the increment's existence to carry the assessment, and the design's first move is to make the increment's contents unassessed.

## 3. Theoretical basis

**3.1 Load profile.** The module's evaluative peak. The intrinsic content is retrospective; the load is in defending a small thing in front of somebody entitled to say it should have been smaller.

**3.2 Why a dated exclusion is a decision.** Intent is unobservable and reconstructible. A date is neither. **The date does not prove the reasoning was good; it proves the reasoning happened before the outcome**, which is the only property of a decision that survives hindsight.

**3.3 Why the capacity statement must be unrevised.** [S044](/wiki/seminars/S044.md) produced it under a specific pressure. Any subsequent edit is made with knowledge of how the fortnight ended, and the edit direction is predictable. **Freezing the artefact preserves an estimate made in ignorance of its outcome**, which is a different instrument from one made after.

**3.4 Why the recipient must have been able to refuse.** This is [S041](/wiki/seminars/S041.md)'s requirement re-checked at the point where it can be verified rather than asserted, and it is re-checked because an increment's recipient frequently changes between definition and delivery, always in the direction of somebody more available and less able to decline.

**3.5 Why nothing about correctness is marked.** No source behind this stage supplies a basis for marking whether a slice was right, whether an exclusion was safe, or whether an increment was adequate. **The corpus reports no failed delivery and no post-deployment record; the playbook offers no comparative evidence.** An element marking those things would be the assessor's opinion in a specification's clothing, and [WP-040](/wiki/whitepapers/WP-040.md) §8 established the precedent for declining it.

## 4. Session design and its warrant

The seven movements run situation, artefact, standard, failure modes, work, review, limits.

**Opening item 2's second clause is the occasion's centre of gravity.** *On what day did you decide it?* Almost every candidate can name an exclusion; far fewer can date one, and the dating exercise is the work movement's first and longest step because most of the dates are not written down anywhere.

**The assessor holds only the proof pack and the slice definition.** They have not seen the ledger, the specification, the review record or the capacity statement before the defence, which means those four artefacts have to carry themselves.

**The specification is closed and its exclusions are long**: not whether the increment works, not whether the recipient was pleased, not code quality, not counts of anything, not whether the slice was right, not whether the exclusions should have been excluded, not presentation.

**Two elements have escape clauses rather than thresholds.** An increment received only by the delivery team is submitted with that stated as its finding; a review record in which everything was read is checked against its own time budget. **Both are passes**, and stating that in the session prevents candidates fabricating the harder-looking answer.

**The defence's adversarial move is the assessor naming a candidate for exclusion.** A candidate who concedes and prices the concession passes. **This is stated in the session in advance**, for the same reason [WP-040](/wiki/whitepapers/WP-040.md) §4 states its analogue: a candidate who suspects that conceding is a trap will defend work they know was unnecessary.

## 5. Predicted failure modes and countermeasures

**The tidy pack.** The likeliest failure. *Countermeasure:* the unrevised capacity statement, the requirement that the review record still say what was not read, and the standard's warning sign — the pack reads better than the fortnight went.

**The undated exclusion.** *Countermeasure:* the dating exercise as the first step of the work movement, and the assessed element requiring departures to carry dates.

**The internal recipient.** *Countermeasure:* the assessed element requiring the recipient to have been in a position to refuse, with the escape clause that makes the honest answer submittable.

**The retrospective specification.** Tests present, written after. *Countermeasure:* the element requiring the pack to *show* that tests arrived in the same change rather than assert it, and the standard's warning sign — no check in the pack has ever been seen red.

**The increment that ended the argument.** Everyone agreed once it ran and nobody recorded what they had disagreed about. *Countermeasure:* the standard's warning sign only. **This is the design's weakest countermeasure** — there is no assessable trace of an argument that stopped — and §8 does not defend it.

**The softened capacity statement.** *Countermeasure:* the unrevised requirement, which is checkable against the [S044](/wiki/seminars/S044.md) submission and is therefore the specification's most mechanical element.

## 7. Assessment and evidence generated

**This is an assessment occasion.** It generates specification evidence and no entrustment judgement — the programme's entrustment decisions begin at [S080](/wiki/seminars/S080.md).

Nine elements, each checked present and adequate or not: every departure from the slice definition is marked and dated, with a departure discovered at shipping recorded as a departure rather than a revision; the recipient is named and was in a position to refuse, or the pack states an internally received increment as its finding; tests arrived with the code in the same change, shown rather than asserted; the review record still says what was not read, and a record in which everything was read is checked against its stated time budget; something was refused, in the ledger or the review, and the pack names it; the capacity statement is unrevised and is reconciled against what the increment produced; the untestable residue has a holder and the pack says whether that person looked; nothing in the pack asserts a magnitude that was not measured by a named instrument; and the what-this-does-not-establish page exists and names inferences rather than gesturing at limits.

**Nothing else is assessed.** Whether the increment works is not marked. Whether the recipient was pleased is not marked. The quality, elegance or size of the code is not marked. The number of items shipped, refused or recorded is not marked. Whether the slice was the right slice is not marked. Whether the exclusions should have been excluded is not marked. The pack's presentation is not marked.

## 8. Objections and rebuttals

**Objection 1: marking dates rather than judgement assesses note-keeping, and will select for candidates with good administrative habits over candidates with good engineering instincts.**

At its strongest: two candidates make identical decisions for identical reasons. One keeps a running record and one does not. The specification marks the first as having decided and the second as having drifted, and the difference between them is clerical. Worse, the fix is trivially gameable — a candidate who learns that dates are marked will produce dates — so the element selects for candidates who read the specification carefully, which is not the competence being assessed.

**Substantially conceded, and it is the sharpest objection to this occasion.** Two partial answers. Dating is required against the ledger, the review threads and the specification, which are artefacts produced during the fortnight for other reasons and are therefore harder to backfill coherently than a standalone date column. And the design's claim is deliberately weak: §3.2 says the date proves the reasoning happened before the outcome, not that it was good. **The declined fix is to mark the quality of the exclusion reasoning instead.** It is declined because no source behind this stage supplies a basis for it — the corpus reports no failed delivery, the playbook offers no comparative evidence — so the element would be marker opinion, and the design would rather assess something narrow and real than something broad and invented. **The gameability is accepted and disclosed.**

**Objection 2: freezing the capacity statement assesses a candidate's earlier self and punishes learning.**

At its strongest: a candidate who wrote a poor capacity statement at [S044](/wiki/seminars/S044.md) and understands the problem far better by [S045](/wiki/seminars/S045.md) is required to submit the poor one. That is the opposite of what a formative sequence should do, and it teaches candidates to write cautious, unfalsifiable statements at [S044](/wiki/seminars/S044.md) because they will be held to them.

**Conceded in part.** The mitigation is that the element requires the unrevised statement **plus a reconciliation** against what actually happened, so the learning is submitted alongside the estimate rather than instead of it, and a candidate whose reconciliation shows they now understand the problem better has satisfied the element well. **The declined fix is to allow the statement to be revised with the original retained in an appendix.** It is declined because an appendix is not read at a defence and the revised version becomes the operative one, which is the exact softening the element exists to prevent. **The incentive towards cautious statements at [S044](/wiki/seminars/S044.md) is real**, and [WP-044](/wiki/whitepapers/WP-044.md) §9 prediction 2 is the instrument that would detect it.

## 9. Falsifiable predictions and instrumentation

**Prediction 1.** Most candidates will be unable to date a majority of their exclusions from records made during the fortnight. *Instrument:* the exclusion list's date column, with each date classified *evidenced from a contemporaneous artefact* / *asserted* / *absent* by a second reader.

**Prediction 2.** Capacity statements will be reconciled optimistically — candidates will conclude that supply was adequate more often than their change volumes support. *Instrument:* the reconciliation against the pack's own change record, by a second reader.

**Prediction 3.** A substantial number of packs will name a recipient different from the one in the [S041](/wiki/seminars/S041.md) slice definition, and the substitution will be towards somebody less able to refuse. *Instrument:* the two recipient fields, paired per candidate, with the change classified *more able to refuse* / *less able* / *equivalent*.

**Prediction 4.** Candidates who concede the assessor's proposed exclusion will not score lower on any other element than those who defend against it. *Instrument:* element-level marks, paired against the concession outcome. **If this fails, the rule that conceding is a pass is not operating**, and the assessor briefing rather than the specification is at fault.

## What a learner should take from this paper

**What the design claims.** That an undated exclusion was not a decision; that a statement which can be edited at assembly will be; and that an increment which ends an argument has not settled a disagreement.

**What the evidence licenses.** Nothing about whether the boundary was well drawn. No source behind this stage supplies a benchmark for that and the occasion does not invent one.

**The concession worth remembering.** This occasion assesses something narrow and real in preference to something broad and invented, and the narrow thing is partly clerical. If you leave able to tell a well-drawn boundary from a well-documented one, you have acquired something the specification could not test.
