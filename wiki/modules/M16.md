# M16 · Leaving Them More Capable

**Quarter:** [Q8](/wiki/quarters/Q8.md) · **Sessions:** S076–S080 · **Complexity class:** 5
**Strands:** PE · ME
**Platform anchor:** one in every session, and every one is executed by the recipient rather than described to them — custom instructions and agent configuration files at [S076](/wiki/seminars/S076.md), GitHub Copilot agent mode worked in pairs at [S077](/wiki/seminars/S077.md), Foundry hosted agents and their operating burden at [S078](/wiki/seminars/S078.md), Microsoft Agent Framework evaluations run on the recipient's own credentials at [S079](/wiki/seminars/S079.md), and the whole stack executed unaided at [S080](/wiki/seminars/S080.md)
**Assessment:** [S080](/wiki/seminars/S080.md) ⊘ — an entrustment decision, not a specification check

## This module is written past its source, and says so on every page

The aspiration exists in the practice corpus behind this programme. Capable people are named as one of the things that must hold together for accelerated delivery to produce anything, and the corpus states once — as a reflection inside a narrative about business value, not as a step in any procedure — that the customer should be left holding the cost-modelling capability because they will need it after the team disengages.

**That is the whole of it.** There is no mechanism for producing capability in the customer's engineers, no measure of whether it was produced, no evidence that it ever was, and no place in any exit procedure where somebody is asked. An organisation that has mechanised the teardown of its own assets down to a register-triggered state change has left the transfer of capability as an unrecorded intention.

So this module is **design reasoning with no external warrant**, in the same class as Stage 1 and by the same route: it is inferred from an omission. That is a weak basis and the objection is conceded in every paper. It is included because the alternative — a programme that teaches an engineer to build something inside somebody else's organisation and never asks whether that organisation can keep it — makes a stronger implicit claim on less.

## The module's central difficulty

Capability is invisible until it is tested, and testing it is socially expensive.

A learner can produce a training plan, deliver it well, and be received warmly by people who cannot do the thing afterwards. Nothing in the room signals the gap. The recipient nods, the demonstration works, the questions are polite, and the failure surfaces months later when the delivery team is gone and cannot see it.

**The module's answer is to make capability something observed rather than delivered.** Its central instrument is a rehearsal in which the recipient operates the system and the learner is present, watching, and forbidden from helping. That is uncomfortable for everyone, which is why it does not happen by default, and it is the only thing in the module that produces evidence rather than intention.

## The five sessions

| Session | Title | What the learner does | What the learner produces |
|---|---|---|---|
| [S076](/wiki/seminars/S076.md) | Capability is not documentation | Separates being told, being willing, knowing how, being able and continuing, and locates the receiver on that sequence | A capability statement per receiving group |
| [S077](/wiki/seminars/S077.md) | Teaching while delivering | Binds capability transfer to real backlog items instead of an end-of-engagement training week | A teaching plan that names what the learner will not do |
| [S078](/wiki/seminars/S078.md) | The dependency you created | Inventories the dependencies the engagement manufactured, and disposes of each | A dependency disposal record |
| [S079](/wiki/seminars/S079.md) | Proving they can run it | Runs a supervised rehearsal, does not intervene, and records what the recipient could not do | A rehearsal record with an observed incapacity in it |
| [S080](/wiki/seminars/S080.md) ⊘ | A transfer the recipient signs | Defends the decision to leave, against an assessor who argues the recipient is not ready | An entrustment judgement, formed on accumulated observation |

The sequence is a narrowing from intention to evidence. S076 asks what the recipient must be able to do. S077 asks when that capability is built, and answers *during the work, not after it*. S078 asks what the engagement did to make the recipient less able rather than more. S079 tests it. S080 decides.

## Why the module is class 5

Nothing here has a specified method, and two of the sessions require the learner to act against their own immediate interest — S077 requires them to hand real work to somebody slower, under a fixed date, and S079 requires them to watch a rehearsal go wrong without rescuing it.

**The class is not about technical difficulty.** It is about the absence of a rule that resolves the choice. Whether to intervene in a failing rehearsal is a judgement the learner has to make live, having written down beforehand the only condition under which they will.

## What this module borrows, and the warrant it borrows with

The distinctions between being told, being willing, knowing how and being able come from a **practitioner change model published by the consultancy that owns its trademark and sells training and certification in it.** Its supporting research is that firm's own benchmarking survey work; no independent evaluation of the model is cited anywhere in the book.

**That warrant licenses direction and mechanism only.** It licenses no ordering strength, no success rate, no magnitude, and no claim that using the model improves outcomes. The distinctions are worth teaching because they are real, they are routinely conflated, and separating them changes what a team does. They are not evidence.

**One correction travels with the model wherever this programme uses it.** A widely repeated summary of it holds that effort spent past the blocking element is wasted. **The book does not say that**, and this repository withdrew the claim on reading it. The author's own formulations are conditional — that a given effort will not by itself produce the change, or will not be productive until the blocking element moves — with a single absolute restricted to knowledge in the absence of willingness. Any use of the model in this module carries the conditional form.

Two further items from that source are used and are worth naming because they are directly reusable: that a closed feedback channel makes a broken system look like a failing operator, and that some people, for some changes, never acquire the ability. Both are direction and mechanism only.

The module also uses one idea about regression — that declaring a change finished is the mechanism by which it comes undone, and that people who never agreed with it wait for exactly that moment. That comes from a practitioner book whose author states plainly that it cites no external sources and reports what he has seen and concluded. It carries the same licence: direction and mechanism, no magnitude, and no timescale.

## Assessment contribution

**[S080](/wiki/seminars/S080.md) is an entrustment decision.** It produces an ordinal supervision level with a mandatory narrative, formed from observation accumulated across S071–S079 rather than from a single occasion, and it does not check a specification.

The judgement is about one thing: **whether this learner can be trusted to decide when a system may be left with somebody else.** Not whether their handover was good. Not whether their recipient was capable. Whether the learner's own reading of the recipient's readiness was defensible, and whether they would have said so if the answer had been no.

See [Assessment Architecture](/wiki/program/03-Assessment-Architecture.md) for the scale and the rater discipline.

## Predicted failure modes for the module as a whole

- **Training as the reflex.** Every capability gap acquires a session. *Countermeasure:* S076 requires the learner to state which element is blocking before proposing any intervention, and a willingness problem answered with a training plan fails the statement.
- **The rescued rehearsal.** The learner intervenes, because watching somebody struggle with something you could fix in a moment is genuinely hard. *Countermeasure:* S079 requires the intervention condition to be written before the rehearsal begins, and treats any intervention outside it as the finding rather than as a failure of the exercise.
- **Capability located in one person.** The recipient organisation acquires exactly one capable individual, and the learner records the group as capable. *Countermeasure:* S076 assesses per group and S079 requires the rehearsal to be run by the person who will actually hold the responsibility, not the person most interested in it.
- **Dependency read as expertise.** Being needed is flattering, and the flattery makes the dependency invisible. *Countermeasure:* S078 requires disposal decisions, and treats documenting a dependency as not having disposed of it. **There is no countermeasure available for the case where remaining needed is commercially rewarded**, which is the ordinary case, and the module names it rather than solving it.
- **The signature mistaken for the evidence.** A recipient signs and the learner treats the signature as proof of capability. *Countermeasure:* S080 separates them explicitly — a signature records willingness to accept, and only the rehearsal records ability — and a learner who cannot hold that distinction under challenge has not met the entrustment bar.

## The evidence position

**Class 3 throughout, with two borrowed distinctions at class 2.**

The module's existence, its sequence, the rehearsal instrument, the dependency-disposal move, the preference for removing a dependency over documenting it, and the separation of signature from evidence are all this design's own arguments.

The knowledge-versus-ability distinction, the willingness-cannot-be-manufactured distinction, the closed-feedback-channel mechanism and the regression-after-declared-victory mechanism are **direction and mechanism only, from commercially interested practitioner sources with no independent evaluation.**

**No magnitude, rate, proportion or timescale is asserted anywhere in this module, and none may be inherited from its sources.**
