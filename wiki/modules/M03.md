# M03 · Representing Structure

**Quarter:** [Q2](wiki/quarters/Q2.md) · **Seminar days:** [S011](wiki/seminars/S011.md)–[S015](wiki/seminars/S015.md) · **Complexity class:** 1, rising to 2 at S015
**Strands:** CF · MS · SY
**Platform anchor:** Microsoft Foundry embedding deployment as the module's fourth representation · Azure MCP Server, read-only
**MCP servers:** Microsoft Learn MCP Server · Azure MCP Server (Read only = true)

---

## The central difficulty

Data structures are taught as a catalogue. Students learn a list of structures, a table of asymptotic costs, and an implicit instruction to pick the one whose row looks best. The catalogue is real knowledge and it does not answer the question students actually face, which is: **this caller, doing these operations, at this frequency, with these constraints — what should I give them?**

The gap has a specific shape. Asymptotic cost is a property of a structure; usefulness is a property of a structure *relative to a usage pattern*. A student who has only the first cannot reason about the second, and defaults to habit — a hash table for everything, or whatever the standard library makes easiest.

The module's target is the second question. Every day asks what a representation makes cheap, and at what price, and for whom.

## The fourth representation

Three of the module's four representations are built on the machine. The fourth is a vector from a Foundry embedding deployment, and it is in the module for four reasons rather than for novelty.

**It is the representation students will use most and interrogate least.** By Q6 they will be building retrieval systems on it. If it arrives first as a capability that finds similar things, the choices inside it become invisible before anyone examines them.

**It makes every cost term in the module physical.** Its construction cost is a bill, its per-operation cost is a round trip, its batch has a boundary that no item in the batch has, and its throttle manufactures a tail. S012 and S013 both use it as their limiting case for exactly this reason.

**Its configuration is a set of decisions the platform makes for you.** Which model, how many dimensions, which similarity function, what the returned score actually is. The module's whole argument is that a representation is a choice with named costs; this is the representation where refusing to choose is the easiest thing to do and looks like nothing at all.

**And it is irreversible in a way the others are not.** You cannot upgrade between Foundry embedding models — moving from one to another means regenerating every vector. A first-year student meets a representation decision they cannot cheaply undo, in week eleven, on a real system.

The claim the module hands forward from it is one sentence: **similar is a decision somebody made, not a property of text.** [S016](wiki/seminars/S016.md), [S051](wiki/seminars/S051.md), [S056](wiki/seminars/S056.md), [S057](wiki/seminars/S057.md) and [S058](wiki/seminars/S058.md) all assume it has landed.

## Why this module sits here

**It requires M01's stance and supplies M04's vocabulary.** S003 established that a representation discards; M03 establishes what it discards *in exchange for*. S007 established that a seam's cost is the cost of changes crossing it; M03 applies the same reasoning to data. And M04's memory and contention material is unintelligible without a working model of what a data structure costs to traverse.

**It is where the S001 sealed prediction is opened.** S011 sits at a ten-week gap from S001, which is the longest interval the programme can construct in its first two quarters, and the design wanted the opening to fall on a day with content rather than on a ceremonial occasion.

**It ends at the class boundary.** S015 is the first day where the student chooses, and placing that transition inside a module rather than between modules means the class 1 scaffolding is still present when the choice is first made.

## Task-class variability plan

The module varies **the dimension along which cost is measured**, which is a deliberate choice: four days varying the structure would teach four structures, while four days varying the cost dimension teach one method.

- **S011** varies operation mix. One dataset, four representations, several access patterns, and a different winner for each.
- **S012** varies what a comparison costs — integers, strings, structured records, a call to an embedding deployment — which turns a constant factor into the deciding term and then into the whole term.
- **S013** varies the time horizon, which is the only day in the module where the *same* structure has two different correct cost answers.
- **S014** varies structural shape, and is where S008's recursion becomes the working method rather than a topic.
- **S015** varies the caller, which is the class-2 move.

The platform instance varies with it rather than being bolted on: the same embedding deployment is a representation at S011, a comparison at S012, a source of tails at S013, a graph at S014 and a candidate at S015. **One service, five different questions asked of it**, which is the module's own thesis applied to its own platform anchor.

## Load profile

Moderate throughout, rising at S013 and S015.

S013 is the module's high point, because amortised reasoning requires holding a sequence of operations as a single object while also reasoning about individuals — genuine element interactivity that does not decompose. It is placed third, at the module's midpoint, with S012 and S014 as lower-load neighbours.

S015 carries evaluative rather than intrinsic load: it is the first choice the student must defend, and the discomfort is the transition, not the material.

The module's standing load reductions are three. **The dataset is the same across S011, S012 and S015**, so students meet one substrate dataset in week eleven and are still using it in week fifteen. **The endpoint, deployment name and credential are supplied and pre-verified on every day**, so no student spends the module's time on authentication; provisioning is Q4's work and the module says so. And **no day requires the student to build a measurement harness**, which is S029's work.

The load the module accepts and does not manage is a service's run-to-run variance, sixteen days before ME101 supplies any framework for it. WP-011 and WP-012 both record this as forced by the timetable rather than chosen, and it is worse with a network call than it was without one.

## Module-level predicted failure modes

**Catalogue reversion.** The most likely outcome. Students absorb the days as five more entries in the structure catalogue and continue choosing by habit. Countermeasure: every day's phase 5 requires the cost claim before the choice, and S015's specification fails any justification that names a structure without naming an operation mix.

**Asymptotic fixation.** Students treat asymptotic cost as the whole answer and dismiss constant factors. Countermeasure: S012's varying comparison cost is constructed so that the asymptotically worse structure wins on the realistic case, and S016 completes the argument.

**The S013 horizon collapse.** Students conclude either that amortised cost is the real cost and worst-case is pedantry, or the reverse. Countermeasure: S013's phase 3 supplies a caller for whom each answer is correct, and the day's artefact requires both.

**S015's choice becomes arbitrary.** Without a criterion, "defend your choice" degenerates into post-hoc rationalisation. Countermeasure: the caller is real, has met the students, and states their own operation mix — so the criterion comes from outside the student. S015 step 4 additionally runs the four representations against the derived mix, so the rejected alternatives are rejected on measurement rather than on assertion.

**Acceptance of agent output.** The module's newest failure mode and the one it is least equipped to detect. A student who directs Copilot competently, reads the result, finds it plausible and ships it has produced an artefact indistinguishable from one they reasoned about — and four of the module's five days now end in exactly that kind of artefact. Countermeasures: every day's intervention record fails when empty unless a specific, named detectability route is demonstrated, and each of the five routes is concrete rather than generic — a similarity assertion with an independently known answer at S011, an oversized batch at S012, a recorded 429 at S013, a hand-computed graph at S014, a checkable vocabulary rule at S015. **The residual risk is real and the module states it: a student who intervenes once, in a way the artefact demonstrates, has cleared the bar and may still have accepted everything else.** Whether the bar is high enough is unknown, and the programme's structural answer is the unassisted segments at [S010](wiki/seminars/S010.md), [S045](wiki/seminars/S045.md) and [S090](wiki/seminars/S090.md) rather than anything in this module.

**Platform fascination.** Students find the service more interesting than the cost model, spend the module's time on endpoints and error codes, and finish able to call an embedding API and unable to say when they should not. Countermeasure: no day assesses anything about the platform. Every specification's checked elements are cost claims, predictions, reconciliations and caller-facing statements, and the perishability register on every day separates the assessed claim from the instance carrying it — printed, not implied.

## Assessment contribution

**No entrustment judgements.** Specification items:

- **S011** — a cost claim per operation for four representations of one dataset, with the measurement that adjudicates it.
- **S012** — a comparison-cost analysis where the constant factor decides, with the crossover point located.
- **S013** — both worst-case and amortised claims, each with a caller for whom it is the relevant one.
- **S014** — a problem restated as a structural one, with the recursion from S008 applied.
- **S015** — a representation chosen for a named caller, with the operation mix, the cost claim, the rejected alternatives and what the choice costs the caller.

**Every one of the five additionally requires a prompt record, an intervention record and a frontier statement**, per the [Copilot Practice Standard](wiki/program/12-Copilot-Practice-Standard.md). These are checked elements of the specification and are listed as such on each day. Nothing else is assessed; in particular no day assesses the agent's output quality, no day assesses its phase 1 Copilot prediction, and [S014](wiki/seminars/S014.md)'s two demonstrated platform cases are excluded explicitly.

Dispositions observed: **humility** at S013 and S015, where a defensible choice can still be wrong.
