# M17 · Raising the Floor

**Quarter:** [Q9](/wiki/quarters/Q9.md) · **Sessions:** S081–S085 · **Complexity class:** 6
**Strands:** PE · ME
**Platform anchor:** none, in any session of this module
**Assessment:** [S085](/wiki/seminars/S085.md) ⊘ — an entrustment decision, not a specification check

## What the module is about

Something was delivered and handed over. This module asks the three questions that decide whether it was worth doing, and it asks them in the order in which they go wrong.

**Did it mature, and how would anybody other than you know?** A level is easy to record and hard to re-derive. The practice corpus behind this programme contains maturity instruments, and the closest thing to a worked example of one — a generated assessment inside an agent pipeline — grades a system by counting properties in a table that an earlier stage in the same pipeline filled in. The finding is not that the instrument is bad. It is that **a level and the evidence for a level are different objects, and only one of them survives the departure of the person who claimed it.**

**Is it used, and by whom, doing what?** Adoption cannot be instructed. It is not produced by training, it is not evidenced by a provisioned account, and the party best placed to measure it is the party that wants the answer to be yes.

**Does it hold?** Momentum decays. The corpus says so once, as practitioner judgement, and proposes a reinforcing cadence as the remedy — a cadence it also sells. This module teaches the diagnosis and the interest together, because separating them is the professional act.

## The module's central difficulty

**Every claim in this module is a claim about a change over time, made without a baseline, by somebody with a stake in it.**

That is not a rhetorical framing. It is the literal condition of the supplied situation, and it is the ordinary condition of the work. The maturity level was entered from memory by a member of the delivery team. The usage report counts accounts. The reinforcement proposal was drafted by the person whose utilisation depends on it. None of those people is dishonest and all three artefacts are worthless as evidence.

**The module's answer is re-derivation.** Not a better claim, not a more careful claim, not a claim with more caveats — a claim somebody else could reproduce, from artefacts they can reach, without the claimant in the room. That instrument is borrowed from the one place in the practice corpus where it appears in good order: a security-status stage that will not accept an assertion that something is implemented, and requires a file and a location instead.

## The five sessions

| Session | Title | What the learner does | What the learner produces |
|---|---|---|---|
| [S081](/wiki/seminars/S081.md) | Maturity as an evidenced level | Takes the recorded level apart, dimension by dimension, and asks what each one was scored against | A re-derivation procedure, and the dimensions it cannot reach |
| [S082](/wiki/seminars/S082.md) | Adoption you cannot mandate | Distinguishes provisioned from used, and locates each receiving group on the readiness sequence | An adoption position with the evidence that would change it |
| [S083](/wiki/seminars/S083.md) | Momentum, and why it decays | Diagnoses the decay, then writes the version of the remedy that earns nobody anything | A reinforcement design, and its conflict-of-interest statement |
| [S084](/wiki/seminars/S084.md) | Each iteration leaves it better | Re-runs closure against the estate and decides what the regenerated items mean | A re-run finding, with the items that will never close named |
| [S085](/wiki/seminars/S085.md) ⊘ | A maturity claim with evidence | Lodges a claim, a re-derivation procedure and a sealed prediction; somebody else executes it | An entrustment judgement, formed on accumulated observation |

The sequence narrows from a claim to its reproduction. S081 asks what the level rests on. S082 asks whether anybody is using the thing the level describes. S083 asks whether that will still be true later, and what it costs to make it so. S084 asks what a re-run of the evidence actually reports, including the part of it that will never come back clean. S085 hands the whole apparatus to somebody else and watches whether it survives.

## Why the module is class 6

Because nothing in it has a method, and the learner is required to act against a reading of the evidence that would flatter them.

Three of the five sessions ask the learner to produce a conclusion that is worse than the one already recorded: a level lower than the one entered at close, an adoption position that says the second site has stopped, a closure re-run that reports an item which has never closed and will not. **There is no rule that resolves any of those, and each has a plausible reading in the opposite direction.** The class is about the absence of a rule, not about technical difficulty.

## What this module borrows, and the warrant it borrows with

**From the practice corpus, verified in this repository and carrying no external warrant of its own:** that maturity instruments exist in this practice and are self-assessed; that a generated assessment can score a system on properties an earlier stage of the same process inserted; that a rubric's proxy checks can be satisfiable by naming a technology nobody has deployed; that no calibration of any such score against human judgement exists anywhere in the material; and that momentum is asserted to decay without a deliberate reinforcing rhythm. **All of these are what one organisation writes down. None is evidence that any of it is true.**

**From the change-management literature, direction and mechanism only.** That being told is not the same as being aware; that awareness does not produce willingness; that knowing how is not the same as being able; that a closed feedback channel makes a broken system look like a failing operator; and that declaring a change finished is itself the mechanism of its regression, with the people who never agreed with it waiting for that moment.

Both sources are commercially interested practitioner accounts with no independent evaluation. **They license no ordering strength, no success rate, no proportion, no magnitude and no timescale, and none is asserted anywhere in this module.**

**One correction travels with the first of them.** A widely repeated summary holds that effort spent past the blocking element is wasted. The book does not say that, and this repository withdrew the claim on reading it. Its own formulations are conditional; the single absolute is narrower and concerns knowledge in the absence of willingness. **The conditional form is the only form used here.**

## Assessment contribution

**[S085](/wiki/seminars/S085.md) is an entrustment decision.** It produces an ordinal supervision level with a mandatory narrative, formed from observation accumulated across S081–S084 rather than from a single occasion, and it does not check a specification.

The judgement is about one thing: **whether this learner can be trusted to say what a claim about improvement actually rests on** — including when the honest answer damages a position they have already taken.

The occasion's instrument is a re-derivation executed by somebody else against a prediction the learner lodged in advance. **It is deliberately not a challenge dialogue**, and the reason is recorded in [WP-085](/wiki/whitepapers/WP-085.md) §8: this programme has repeated one assessment device across several occasions, and repeating it again would have made the fourth consecutive entrustment occasion turn on the same move.

See [Assessment Architecture](/wiki/program/03-Assessment-Architecture.md) for the scale and the rater discipline.

## Predicted failure modes for the module as a whole

- **The level defended rather than re-derived.** The learner argues for the recorded level instead of specifying how somebody else would reach it. *Countermeasure:* S081 assesses the procedure, not the level, and a procedure whose only step is *ask the delivery team* fails on its face.
- **Provisioning read as adoption.** An account exists, therefore a person uses it. *Countermeasure:* S082 requires the evidence to be an observed act with a date, and requires the learner to name the group whose usage they cannot see.
- **The reinforcement that is also an invoice.** A correct diagnosis arrives attached to a proposal that happens to be chargeable, and the learner never notices the coincidence. *Countermeasure:* S083 requires the free version of the remedy to be written first, and treats the paid version as an escalation that has to be argued for.
- **The re-run treated as the finding.** A closure re-run reports items, the learner reports the items, and nothing is decided. *Countermeasure:* S084 requires each regenerated item to be classified as a real gap, an invisible fix, or a criterion that cannot see the fix — and requires the third class to be named as a defect in the criterion rather than in the estate.
- **Improvement asserted from a single observation.** Something got better once, and the learner reports a trend. *Countermeasure:* every session in this module requires the name of whoever is responsible for establishing causation, which is the requirement carried forward from [S067](/wiki/seminars/S067.md). **A claim whose attribution is assigned to nobody is not a claim.**

## The evidence position

**Class 3 throughout, with one borrowed mechanism at class 2 and one verified description of practice.**

This design's own arguments: that a level nobody else can re-derive is not a level; the re-derivation procedure as an artefact; the sealed-prediction instrument at S085; the separation of a free reinforcement design from a chargeable one; the three-way classification of regenerated closure items; and the position that adoption evidence must be an observed act rather than a provisioned capability.

**Verified as description, not as finding:** what the practice corpus contains and omits, including its maturity instruments, its self-assessment, its uncalibrated generated scores, its single momentum claim and its complete absence of outcome, quality and sustainment evidence.

**Direction and mechanism only:** the readiness distinctions and the regression-after-declared-victory mechanism, from commercially interested practitioner sources with no independent evaluation.

**No magnitude, rate, proportion, percentage or timescale is asserted anywhere in this module, and none may be inherited from its sources.**
