# M02 · Saying No, and What It Costs

**Quarter:** [Q1](/wiki/quarters/Q1.md) · **Sessions:** S006–S010 · **Complexity class:** 2
**Strands:** PE · ME
**Platform anchor:** one in every session — Microsoft Purview DSPM for AI at [S006](/wiki/seminars/S006.md), Azure AI Search security trimming at [S007](/wiki/seminars/S007.md), the serverless-runtime, Foundry and Copilot Studio build-path comparison at [S008](/wiki/seminars/S008.md), the Azure API Management AI gateway with provisioned throughput at [S009](/wiki/seminars/S009.md), and Microsoft Agent Framework evaluations at [S010](/wiki/seminars/S010.md)
**Assessment:** [S010](/wiki/seminars/S010.md) ⊘

## The module's central difficulty

[M01](/wiki/modules/M01.md) makes refusal available. This module tries to make it expensive, because a refusal that costs nothing is a preference wearing a decision's clothes.

The failure this module exists to prevent is the mirror image of the one before it. A learner who has been taught to look for disqualifying conditions will find them everywhere, will conclude that scepticism is the sophisticated posture, and will decline work that should have proceeded — at no cost to themselves, because nothing in an exercise punishes a false refusal. **Refusal is only a judgement if the alternative has been priced**, and pricing the alternative is most of what this module does.

The second difficulty is that the things which should stop an engagement are mostly invisible at the point the decision is made. They are held by people who were not invited, sit inside constraints nobody has tested since they were inherited, or take the form of a simpler option that no one in the room has an incentive to raise.

## The five sessions

| Session | Title | What the learner does | What the learner produces |
|---|---|---|---|
| [S006](/wiki/seminars/S006.md) | Constraints you cannot see yet | Classifies every constraint by how much it can move, and records who said so | A frozen/fluid register |
| [S007](/wiki/seminars/S007.md) | Who is not in the room | Finds the affected parties who were absent and states their strongest objection | An absence map |
| [S008](/wiki/seminars/S008.md) | When simpler technology would do | Builds the case for the simplest sufficient option, then argues past it or does not | A sufficiency finding |
| [S009](/wiki/seminars/S009.md) | The cost of doing nothing | Prices the alternative, including the cost of refusing | A do-nothing statement |
| [S010](/wiki/seminars/S010.md) ⊘ | A refusal you can defend | Delivers a refusal to a sponsor who argues back | A defended refusal |

The order matters and is not arbitrary. S006 and S007 find the grounds. S008 removes the easiest reason to proceed. S009 removes the easiest reason to decline. S010 is the only session in the stage where a learner has to say the thing out loud to somebody who does not want to hear it.

## Why the module is class 2

Task specified, method chosen by the learner. **This is the first point in the programme where nobody is told how to do the work**, and the change is deliberate: sizing an unmeasured cost and constructing a refusal are both tasks where the choice of method is the substance of the judgement.

The register at S006 and the map at S007 name the columns and nothing else. The finding at S008 does not say how sufficiency is to be established. The statement at S009 does not say what counts as a baseline — and a learner who declares that no baseline exists, and says who would have to produce one, has done the work correctly.

## Assessment contribution

**No entrustment evidence.** One assessment occasion, [S010](/wiki/seminars/S010.md), with a closed specification, run against [Brief B](/wiki/quarters/Q1.md).

The specification checks the mechanics of a refusal: that the triggering condition is the one previously written or a dated amendment to it, that an observation rather than a preference triggered it, that an alternative course is offered specifically enough to be started, that the cost to the customer is stated, and that the learner answers the sponsor's objection rather than restating the decision.

**The sponsor's reaction is not marked.** A refusal the sponsor accepts is worth exactly what a refusal they reject is worth, and learners are told so before the exercise rather than after it.

## Predicted failure modes for the module as a whole

- **Refusal as reflex.** The most likely failure and the one the module is built against. *Countermeasure:* S009 requires the cost of the alternative to be sized before S010 permits a refusal, and the S010 specification requires that cost to appear in the refusal itself.
- **The costed refusal that is still free.** A learner prices the alternative in a paragraph nobody would act on. *Countermeasure:* partial. The specification requires the cost to be an event with a frequency and a bearer, which is harder to fabricate than a category — but a determined learner can fabricate one, and no check here catches it.
- **The convenient freeze.** At S006, the constraints filed as immovable turn out to be exactly those whose testing would require an uncomfortable conversation. *Countermeasure:* every register entry must name the person who is the source of the belief, which makes the untested ones visible without accusing anyone.
- **Ventriloquism at S007.** The absent party's objection is stated in a form the learner can already answer. *Countermeasure:* the objection must be written in that party's terms and read aloud by somebody who was not its author, which is unreliable and better than nothing.
- **The straw simple option.** At S008 the simple option is written so that it loses. *Countermeasure:* the stated failure of the simple option must be specific and observable; a generality fails the standard. This catches lazy versions and not deliberate ones.
- **Performance at S010.** The refusal is delivered as theatre to a peer who has no reason to push. *Countermeasure:* the sponsor role is briefed to concede nothing and to make one reasonable-sounding offer that does not address the condition. Whether that is enough is unknown.

## The evidence position

**Class 3 throughout, and this module is further past its source than [M01](/wiki/modules/M01.md) is.**

The practice corpus supplies the frozen/fluid classification and the observation that constraint is not an excuse for inaction, which is the most transferable original idea in it. It supplies the first question of a published responsible-AI assessment — whether the problem can be solved without AI and whether simpler technology would suffice — which is the substance of S008. It supplies, negatively, the material for S009: a value framework that instructs the reader to establish a baseline and never exhibits one being established, and that assigns responsibility for establishing causation to nobody.

**What it does not supply is any instance of the module's actual subject.** There is no declined engagement, no exclusion criterion and no worked refusal anywhere in it. S010 has no source at all and its paper says so without qualification.
