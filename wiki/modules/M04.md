# M04 · Discovery Beyond the Workshop

**Quarter:** [Q2](/wiki/quarters/Q2.md) · **Sessions:** [S016](/wiki/seminars/S016.md)–[S020](/wiki/seminars/S020.md) · **Complexity class:** 2, rising to 3 at [S020](/wiki/seminars/S020.md)
**Strands:** PE · ME
**Platform anchor:** one in every session — Microsoft Agent Framework evaluations at [S016](/wiki/seminars/S016.md), Azure AI Search and its hybrid retrieval and ranking at [S017](/wiki/seminars/S017.md) and [S018](/wiki/seminars/S018.md), provisioned throughput for Foundry models at [S019](/wiki/seminars/S019.md), and the Microsoft Learn MCP Server with Foundry at [S020](/wiki/seminars/S020.md)

## The central difficulty

[M03](/wiki/modules/M03.md) taught an instrument that the practice corpus builds well. M04 takes apart one that it builds badly, and the badness is more instructive than the goodness.

The corpus ships a large practitioner question bank for customer discovery. It is not careless work. It opens with a mandatory breadth-first block before any detailed enquiry — an independent rediscovery of the rule against examining detail inside a problem category before establishing that the category matters at all, and enforced structurally rather than merely advised. Its prior-attempt questions are textbook. It asks threshold questions about what would be good enough. Several of its questions are genuinely behavioural: a recently shipped feature and where it stalled, how often tests are switched off under deadline, whether security has ever actually blocked a release, what happens when the one person who knows is away.

**And it is unusable as written**, for reasons that are diagnosable one at a time against published interviewing method. That combination — competent, experienced, and structurally incapable of producing a disconfirming answer — is the module's whole subject.

## The four repairs and the three additions

The module is organised as work on the instrument rather than as a tour of its defects. Grouped by what the learner has to do about them.

**Repairs — things the instrument asks, that it should not ask that way.**

- **It asks the customer to endorse the interviewer's own solution**, in every section, asked by the person who has come to deliver that solution. This is the central prohibition in the question-quality literature and it is present here as the template rather than as an occasional slip. Repaired at [S016](/wiki/seminars/S016.md).
- **It ships specimen answers with nearly every question.** Defensible as preparation for a novice; damaging in the room, because appending examples narrows the response before it is given. The specimens disproportionately name the frictions the method happens to address. Repaired at [S016](/wiki/seminars/S016.md).
- **It is entirely self-report.** Nobody is asked to demonstrate a deployment, to share a screen during a pipeline run, or to set up an environment while being watched. Repaired at [S017](/wiki/seminars/S017.md).
- **It never consolidates, and ships an artefact that prevents consolidation** — a lookup table converting one person's single remark directly into a prescribed intervention. Repaired at [S018](/wiki/seminars/S018.md).

**Additions — things the instrument never asks at all.**

- **There is no data block.** Nothing about where data comes from, who may see it, whether it is correct or labelled, or whether anybody consented. Added at [S017](/wiki/seminars/S017.md) and carried into the packet at [S020](/wiki/seminars/S020.md).
- **There is no question asking what result would show it had not worked.** Added at [S016](/wiki/seminars/S016.md), and it is the question the module cares most about.
- **There is no question asking who loses if it succeeds**, in a practice whose commercial material carries an explicit headcount claim. Added at [S016](/wiki/seminars/S016.md), retrieving the same question from [S001](/wiki/seminars/S001.md).

## The figures problem, quarantined

The question bank's specimen answers contain numbers — change sizes, review turnarounds, coverage figures with a before-and-after target, feedback thresholds, deployment failure rates, rework proportions, requirement lead times.

They are unattributed, and they are presented as the plausible range of customer answers. **That makes them function as norms whether or not anybody intended it.** An engineer who has read them will hear a real customer's number as high or low against a scale nobody validated, and will never notice that the scale came from an illustration.

**No figure from that material is reproduced in this module, in this programme, or anywhere in this repository**, and the reason is exactly this mechanism. The lesson is the mechanism, not the numbers, and it can be taught without them. [S016](/wiki/seminars/S016.md) requires the learner to strip every specimen from a supplied set before doing anything else, and to say what each specimen would have made them expect.

## Why the module sits here

**It needs [M03](/wiki/modules/M03.md) in front of it.** A learner who has facilitated a room knows why specimen answers exist: the room goes quiet, the question lands badly, and a prepared example rescues it. The specimens are not stupidity, they are a solution to a real problem in the room, and a learner who has felt that problem produces a better repair than one who has not.

**It supplies [Stage 3](/wiki/quarters/Q3.md).** A requirement is a business statement about somebody's situation; a requirement derived from a leading question is a business statement about your own product. The chain from [S016](/wiki/seminars/S016.md) to [S021](/wiki/seminars/S021.md) is direct.

## The class 3 hinge

[S020](/wiki/seminars/S020.md) is where the learner first sets a success criterion rather than receiving one.

Through [S016](/wiki/seminars/S016.md)–[S019](/wiki/seminars/S019.md) the criterion is given: the question is repaired when it can no longer be answered flatteringly, the observation is adequate when told, taught and done have all been collected, the finding is consolidated when it names its population and preserves its contradiction, the ask has succeeded when a commitment was made or explicitly refused. Each of those is checkable by someone else.

**"Enough discovery" is not.** It depends on the decision waiting on it, on what a wrong answer would cost, and on what else could resolve the uncertainty more cheaply. The learner states a stopping rule, states it before the evidence that satisfies it, and defends both — and the defence is the assessed work. A stopping rule written after the evidence arrived is a description, not a rule.

## Load profile

Rising throughout. [S018](/wiki/seminars/S018.md) is the intrinsic high point, because consolidation requires holding several cases as distinct objects while also reasoning about what is common to them, and that does not decompose into simpler steps. [S020](/wiki/seminars/S020.md) is the evaluative high point and the load there is the class transition rather than the material.

## What the module hands forward

**Observation, report and inference are three different things, and an artefact that does not keep them apart cannot be checked.** That distinction is installed at [S017](/wiki/seminars/S017.md), enforced at [S018](/wiki/seminars/S018.md), assessed at [S020](/wiki/seminars/S020.md), and assumed by [S022](/wiki/seminars/S022.md), [S066](/wiki/seminars/S066.md) and [S088](/wiki/seminars/S088.md).
