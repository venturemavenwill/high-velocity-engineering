## The quarter's theme

Q1 asked what a program does and how you would know. Q2 asks what it costs, and introduces the first genuinely uncomfortable idea in the programme: **there is no such thing as a correct choice of representation or resource policy, only a choice with its costs named.**

The quarter is where complexity class advances. M03 remains at class 1 — the task is specified and the method given — while M04 moves to class 2, where the student chooses the method. The transition is deliberate and is placed at the point where the student has enough cost vocabulary to choose on grounds rather than by preference.

Q2 is also where the S001 sealed prediction P2 is opened, at S011, at a ten-week gap.

## What the platform anchor is for

Q1's toolchain was Copilot and the Learn MCP Server: an agent, and a way to check what it told you. Q2 adds the first Azure service the students call and the first Azure estate they read, and both are chosen because they make this quarter's argument concrete rather than because they are impressive.

**A Foundry embedding deployment is a representation with a price.** It is the only representation in M03 whose construction cost is a line on a bill, whose per-operation cost is a network round trip, and whose configuration — which model, how many dimensions, which similarity function — is decided by a default if the student does not decide it. Every claim M03 makes about representation choice is true of it, and it is the one representation students would otherwise never think to interrogate. **Similar is a decision somebody made, not a property of text** is installed at [S011](/wiki/seminars/S011.md) and is inherited by S016, S051, S056, S057 and S058.

**The Azure MCP Server is read-only in this quarter, and the students set that themselves.** The option exists, it is documented, and it is **off by default** — which is the quarter's cheapest and most durable lesson about defaults. Students read subscriptions, resource groups, resources, deployments and index definitions. They change nothing. Writing begins in Q4.

Both are perishable and both are marked. Each day carries a perishability register separating its durable claim from the instance carrying it, and the instances are re-verified before every offering per the [Durable and Perishable Register](/wiki/program/09-Durable-and-Perishable-Register.md).

## What advances to complexity class 2

At class 1 the method is given. At class 2 **the student chooses the method and must defend the choice**. The distinction is visible in three places:

- **S015** is the hinge. The task is specified — serve this caller — and for the first time the representation is not.
- **S016 to S019** each present multiple viable policies with different cost profiles and require a choice.
- **S020** requires a quantitative argument, made before observation, defended to someone who will act on it.

What does *not* advance is the success criterion. At class 2 the student is told what counts as success; choosing the criterion is class 3 and arrives at M07.

## Substrate role

Q2 is the first quarter where students touch the live system rather than observing it, and the first where every seminar day ends in something that runs.

- **S011 and S015** use real substrate data structures and a real caller.
- **S016 and S019** use the substrate's actual latency and throughput measurements, unretouched.
- **S020** produces a capacity argument about a real forthcoming load change, which staff will compare against what actually happens — and the comparison is reported back to the cohort in Q3.

**Every day in M03 ships a build to a disposable environment**, directed through Copilot agent mode, and every build carries a prompt record, an intervention record and a frontier statement. No student change reaches production in Q2; production changes begin at S035, and Azure writes begin in Q4.

**What is demonstrated rather than operated, and therefore not assessed.** At [S014](/wiki/seminars/S014.md), two of the four platform cases — varying an index's `efSearch` and its `m` — are performed by staff, because both edit an index definition and this quarter holds the Azure MCP Server in read-only mode. The day states this to students and the specification does not assess those cases. It is the quarter's only such exclusion and it is recorded here so that the boundary is visible at quarter level rather than only on the day.

## Entrustment occasions

**None.** Per the [Assessment Architecture](/wiki/program/03-Assessment-Architecture.md), the first entrustment judgement is made in Q3. S020 is assessment-bearing and produces a specification decision and a dispositions observation only.

The reason is stated plainly: an entrustment judgement in Q2 would still be substantially a judgement about what the student arrived with.

## The frontier map begins here

Every day in this quarter opens with a sealed prediction about what Copilot will do and where it will be wrong, and closes by opening it. The cohort's accuracy across the ten days is the programme's first running measurement of frontier calibration, its reader is next year's cohort, and it is carried through the [Durable and Perishable Register](/wiki/program/09-Durable-and-Perishable-Register.md).

The predictions are deliberately about different properties: at S011 what the agent silently accepts, at S012 which term it optimises, at S013 whether it distinguishes a transient error from a permanent one, at S014 whether it states a shape before writing code, at S015 which of your own instructions it satisfies in letter only. **None of them is about whether the agent can do the task.** By week eleven that question is not interesting and the design does not ask it.

## The retrieval position

Q2 is the first quarter where spacing is genuinely active. S011 retrieves S003 at a seven-week gap and S001 at ten; by S020 the schedule is drawing on material from four to nineteen weeks back across two modules.

The known weakness stated at [Q1](/wiki/quarters/Q1.md) — that early days retrieve almost entirely from the preceding fortnight — is resolved here, and the design's first meaningful test of the spacing claim is the S011 opening of the S001 P2 prediction.

## What Q2 must achieve for Q3 to be possible

1. **A working cost vocabulary.** Q3's algorithms and measurement work assumes that "what does this cost" is a question the student asks unprompted.
2. **Comfort with irreducible trade-off.** M05's approximation material and M06's measurement material both require accepting that a choice can be defensible and wrong.
3. **A quantitative argument made and adjudicated.** S020 is the rehearsal that S030's first entrustment-adjacent measurement defence depends on.
4. **Class 2 established as normal.** Choosing a method and defending it must stop being remarkable before S021 asks students to choose between paradigms.
5. **An embedding understood as a representation rather than a capability.** Q3's retrieval work and Q6's vector search both assume the student already knows that a similarity score is a chosen function with a chosen threshold. If that has not landed by S015, Q6 has to teach it under time pressure and against a folk model the student has by then used for four quarters.
6. **A working habit of grounding before asserting.** Every day in this quarter requires a platform claim to be checked against the Learn MCP Server rather than against the agent's memory. Q3's evaluation material is unteachable to a cohort that trusts what it is told.

## Cross-quarter commitments this quarter creates

Stated here so that a later quarter can be held to them.

- **S013 defers the retry-and-queueing mechanism to [S019](/wiki/seminars/S019.md)**, at a two-week gap, and S019 must resolve caller C explicitly rather than in passing.
- **S011 and S012 defer the constant-factor explanation to [S016](/wiki/seminars/S016.md)**, one and two weeks later.
- **S011, S012 and S013 all use measurement as an arbiter before [S026](/wiki/seminars/S026.md) teaches what a measurement is.** S026 is required to open by revisiting these three days as instances of what it is about to teach, and both WP-011 and WP-012 record that this connection is listed in S026's retrieval sources and is not yet built into its design.
- **S014 defers approximate-search accuracy to [S057](/wiki/seminars/S057.md) and [S058](/wiki/seminars/S058.md).** The day teaches that an approximate answer fails as a traversal rather than as an arithmetic and asserts no recall figure; those days must supply the measurement.
- **S013 opens the concealment question and does not answer it**, for thirteen weeks to [S050](/wiki/seminars/S050.md) and roughly two years to [S088](/wiki/seminars/S088.md).
