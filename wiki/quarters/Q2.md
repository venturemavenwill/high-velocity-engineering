## The stage's question

[Stage 1](/wiki/quarters/Q1.md) asked whether the work should happen. Stage 2 asks what the work actually is.

Those are not the same question and the gap between them is where most delivery goes wrong. A customer who has been qualified has told you something. What they told you is a request, filtered through their own account of their situation, shaped by the tools they already have and delivered to someone they know has come to sell them something. **Discovery is the discipline of finding out what is true underneath that.**

The stage runs in two halves that argue with each other.

**[M03](/wiki/modules/M03.md) teaches the workshop as an instrument.** The practice corpus behind this programme is unusually good here: it has a lifecycle, readiness gates, facilitation technique, a catalogue of difficult situations each with prescribed handling *and prohibitions*, named antipatterns with observable warning signs, and a facilitator wellness document that reads as an incident record written in the grammar of advice. This is hard-won operational knowledge of a kind that is almost never written down, and the module teaches it as content.

**[M04](/wiki/modules/M04.md) takes the same corpus's discovery instrument apart.** Its question bank asks the customer to endorse the interviewer's own solution, in every section, asked by the person who has come to deliver it. It ships specimen answers that narrow the response before it is given. It contains no observation of any kind. It never consolidates across informants, and it ships an artefact that prevents consolidation by converting one person's single remark into a prescribed intervention. It has no questions about data — where it comes from, who may see it, whether it is correct, whether anyone consented. It never asks what result would show the thing had *not* worked. And it never asks who loses if it succeeds.

**Both halves are the same organisation.** A practice can be operationally excellent and evidentially naive at the same time, in the same documents, in the same register. Holding both readings at once is the skill this stage exists to produce.

## Why this stage was written without a platform anchor, and why that was reversed

**The original position, at its strongest.** This stage once declared that it anchored on nothing, and the argument behind that was not a shrug. The organisation whose practice it draws on marks each item of its own delivery workflow as automated, assisted or manual, and **marks its discovery work manual** — the conversations about success measures, security expectations and monitoring maturity are recorded as live facilitation, not as anything an agent performs. That is the organisation's own judgement about its own work, made by people with a commercial interest in the opposite answer, and it was the closest thing to warrant this stage had. Set beside the same corpus's proudest customer-facing anecdote — a prototype generated on the first day from a reusable starting point, with no discovery activity described at all — the conclusion drawn was that **acceleration which removes discovery is not acceleration, because it moves faster toward an unexamined destination.** Platform content is also the most perishable material in this repository, and a stage bound to a vendor product dates with the product.

**What actually happened.** Each stage reached its own judgement independently and on its own reasoning. Six of the nine argued themselves out of an anchor, and the aggregate — seventy-nine of the ninety sessions carrying no platform workflow at all — was nobody's intention and contradicted the programme's own stated purpose. No stage argued for that total. Every stage helped produce it.

**The defect was the level, not the reasoning.** A per-stage judgement cannot see the aggregate it is producing, and no stage page was ever asked to read the other eight. That finding generalises past this repository — a decision delegated to the unit best placed to argue it is a decision nobody is positioned to total — and it is worth more to a reader than the original argument was.

**The resolution.** Anchors are now assigned centrally, as a designed spine across all ninety sessions, and every session in this stage carries one. The perishability concern is handled where it belongs rather than by abstention: in each session's `## Perishable content in this session` table, which pairs a durable claim with the version-bound instance illustrating it, and in [09 · Durable and Perishable Register](/wiki/program/09-Durable-and-Perishable-Register.md). **The discovery caution survives intact as content rather than as an omission.** Agents still appear in these ten sessions as a named temptation as well as an instrument — the consolidation you did not do, the informant you simulated, the polished artefact that suppresses honest feedback — and each paper still says so.

## What advances to complexity class 3

At class 2 the learner chooses the method and defends the choice. At class 3 **the learner also chooses what would count as success.**

The stage holds at class 2 through nine sessions because the criterion is supplied: a workshop has succeeded when a commitment was made or refused, and a question has been repaired when it can no longer be answered flatteringly. Those are given.

[S020](/wiki/seminars/S020.md) removes the criterion. Its question is *when is discovery enough?*, and there is no supplied answer — the learner states a stopping rule, states it before the evidence that satisfies it, and defends both. That is the class boundary, and it is placed at the end of the stage rather than the beginning because a stopping rule written by someone who has not yet been in a difficult room is a guess.

## The confidentiality discipline, stated once

The practice material behind this stage is **access-controlled internal material belonging to a customer-embedded engineering organisation within a large technology vendor.** This repository is public.

**No organisation, programme, site, team, tool, checklist, individual, customer or partner is named anywhere in these twenty-two files, and no internal material is linked.** One case study in the source names an industrial customer; it is not named here and its identifying detail is not carried. The stage teaches the durable structure in original prose and refuses the labels. The standing rule is recorded at [research/99-source-register/source-register.md](/research/99-source-register/source-register.md).

**This is a real constraint on what the stage can teach.** A reader cannot check the paraphrase against the source, and no claim in this stage can be externally warranted by it. Where a claim rests on that corpus alone, the papers say so and classify it accordingly.

## What this stage does not establish

Nothing here shows that better discovery produces better outcomes.

The practice corpus reports no failed engagement, no declined customer beyond a single instance, no post-deployment record and no sustainment data. The three interviewing sources this stage holds it against — a contextual design method, a field-interviewing manual and a customer-conversation handbook — are all practitioner syntheses. **All three state plainly that they establish no effect and no threshold.** Each is a diagnostic vocabulary and a set of inspectable behaviours, and that is what this stage teaches.

**The strongest honest claim available is negative:** a discovery instrument that cannot produce a disconfirming answer will not produce one, and several named properties of the corpus's instrument guarantee that. Learners should hold the positive claims — that observation beats self-report, that consolidation beats anecdote, that commitment beats enthusiasm — as mechanisms worth testing rather than as findings.
