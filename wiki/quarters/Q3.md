## The quarter's theme

Q3 asks what you can establish and what you must concede.

M05 works the limits of computation: some problems are hard, some are intractable, and an engineer's duty when a problem is intractable is to say so rather than to keep trying. M06 works the limits of evidence: a measurement is an instrument applied to a question, both can be wrong, and a number licenses far less than it appears to.

The two halves are the same lesson in different domains, and the quarter's structure makes that explicit. **The competence being built is knowing the boundary of what you can claim — and saying where it is, out loud, to someone who wants you to claim more.**

Q3 is also where the programme makes its **first entrustment judgement**.

## The quarter's platform

This is the quarter where the argument stops being about problems on paper.

Every M05 day runs against **one Azure AI Search index** built by the students in week 21 and kept for the module, and every M06 day runs against **Foundry evaluators** applied to that index's output. The choice is not decorative. A search index is the cleanest place in the whole Microsoft AI Platform to meet M05's subject, because it offers the same query four ways — full-text BM25, approximate nearest neighbour, exhaustive nearest neighbour, and a fusion of them — and **one of those four is exact and the other three are not, and the service will not tell you which one you are getting unless you ask.**

The two anchors interlock in a way that decides the quarter's shape.

- **Azure AI Search supplies the tractability material.** An exhaustive nearest-neighbour scan returns the true answer and is documented as computationally intensive; an approximate one returns a fast answer that is usually the same and sometimes is not. That is S021 through S023's subject in a form a student can run.
- **The same service supplies its own ground truth.** The documentation names exhaustive nearest-neighbour search as the way to build the ground-truth set against which an approximate configuration's recall is measured. **A day that would otherwise have to assert its adjudicator can execute it.**
- **Foundry's evaluators supply the measurement material,** and they supply it with a defect the programme wants: `document_retrieval` computes its metrics arithmetically against human labels and needs no model, while `retrieval` asks a language model to judge and needs a model deployment. Two evaluators of the same construct, one deterministic and one not, in the same product.
- **No agreement-with-human data is published for any Foundry evaluator.** Every judged score this quarter produces is therefore **uncalibrated**, students are taught to say so in exactly those words, and ME101 spends S026 through S029 on what that sentence costs.

**Every seminar day in this quarter carries a build.** The day's whole-task phase produces something that runs against the index, authored principally by prompting GitHub Copilot in agent mode, and the artefact carries a prompt record, an intervention record and a frontier statement per the [Copilot Practice Standard](/wiki/program/12-Copilot-Practice-Standard.md). A day with no platform anchor and no build does not exist in this programme.

**Everything in this section is perishable and the quarter says so on every day.** Parameter defaults, score ranges, candidate-window sizes and preview boundaries are the vehicle. The durable claims — a method is a hypothesis, a guarantee has a form, a reduction has a direction, an impossibility is owed to the requester, a promise must be checkable — are what the specifications assess.

## The first entrustment judgement

**S030 produces the programme's first entrustment judgement**, and it is deliberately narrow.

The entrustable activity is not one of the three terminal activities. It is a bounded precursor: **conduct a measurement of a stated quantity and report what it does and does not establish.** The judgement is made on the ordinal supervision scale and the expected level at this point is 2a — the student can do this with the supervisor readily available and checking the work.

The reason a judgement is made now rather than earlier is stated in the [Assessment Architecture](/wiki/program/03-Assessment-Architecture.md): before Q3, a judgement would be substantially a judgement about what the student arrived with. Twenty-nine seminar days and two assessed defences later, it is not.

★ **S024 is retrieved by [S050](/wiki/seminars/S050.md), [S055](/wiki/seminars/S055.md) and [S087](/wiki/seminars/S087.md).** Its frame — recognise the impossibility, and deliver it to the person who asked at the moment you hold it — is load-bearing for two more years and does not change in this revision. What changes is that the impossibility is now one a student watches an agent attempt.

## Substrate role

Q3 is the first quarter where student work informs decisions.

- **S021 through S023** build and configure the module's Azure AI Search index over a real substrate corpus, and the configuration the cohort converges on is the one the substrate's retrieval feature is built against next quarter.
- **S024 and S025** produce a stated-guarantee recommendation about a real substrate algorithm that staff act on or decline, with reasons given. From this revision the recommendation covers the index's retrieval configuration, and the S024 request that cannot be satisfied is a real schema request from the substrate's backlog.
- **S029 and S030** design and run a measurement of a real substrate property that nobody has measured, and the result enters the system's documentation with the student's name and their stated limitations attached.

Students still do not change production code. That begins at S035. **The index is not production**: it is a disposable index in a course-owned search service, rebuilt each offering, and S024's rebuild request is genuinely executable there — which is the point, because a student who has performed a drop-and-rebuild knows what they are asking a production team to accept.

## The Q2 follow-through

**The S020 load change has happened.** In week 22 the cohort is shown what occurred, each student's recorded claim is compared against it, and the distribution of errors is discussed — unattributed, and with the assumption each student identified shown alongside.

This is the programme's first long-horizon prediction adjudication and it is scheduled as a full session inside SY102's successor, not as an aside.

## What Q3 must achieve for Q4 to be possible

1. **A measurement vocabulary that is actually used.** Q4's concurrency and requirements work both assume that students ask what an observation establishes.
2. **Comfort with intractability.** M07's partial failure material and M08's requirements material both require accepting that some things cannot be guaranteed.
3. **A first entrustment judgement on record,** so that the scale, the narrative substantiation and the rater discipline are familiar before they carry weight.
4. **The stated-guarantee habit.** S025's discipline — every heuristic ships with what it promises and what it does not — is assumed from S034 onward.
5. **A working retrieval configuration and the vocabulary to argue about it.** Q4's gateway work meters a service the students can now describe, and [IS203](/wiki/program/06-Course-Catalogue.md)'s vector and hybrid material in Year 2 is a return to this index at a higher complexity class, not a first encounter.
6. **The sentence "this score is uncalibrated," used without prompting.** Q5 onward is full of judged numbers. A student who has not learned to say this in Q3 will accept one in Q5.

## The quarter's running frontier measure

Every day in Q3 seals a Copilot prediction at phase 1 and opens it at phase 8: *what will the agent do with this, and where will it be wrong?* The cohort's hit rate on both halves — predicting that it errs, and naming the error — is recorded across the ten days and read by the following year's cohort through the [Durable and Perishable Register](/wiki/program/09-Durable-and-Perishable-Register.md).

**The rate is a sample and the quarter says so**, per instrumentation rule 3: one cohort, one index, one corpus, on tasks staff selected because a known failure was available. It describes these ten days and not the agent.
