# Reading List — Assessment and EPAs

Grouped by the question each source answers. Access status is recorded so that nothing is cited beyond what was actually read.

## What unit should be assessed, and what should the output look like?
- **Olle ten Cate, "A primer on entrustable professional activities"**, *Korean Journal of Medical Education* 2018; 30(1):1-10. DOI 10.3946/kjme.2018.76. PMCID PMC5840559. **Open access (CC BY-NC), full text read via Europe PMC.** The seven-part description template, the five supervision levels with expanded sub-levels, the four evidence types, the multiple-observer summative standard, the entrustment-based discussion, the three professionalism characteristics, and the 20-40 programme sizing guidance. This is the single most load-bearing source in the folder.
- **ten Cate, "Nuts and bolts of entrustable professional activities"**, *Journal of Graduate Medical Education* 2013; 5(1):157-158. DOI 10.4300/jgme-d-12-00380.1. PMCID PMC3613304. **Metadata verified; text not read.** The original short statement of the concept; the 2018 primer supersedes it for practical purposes.

## What will go wrong when humans make these judgements, and what controls help?
- **Oudkerk Pool, Govaerts, Jaarsma and Driessen, "From aggregation to interpretation: how assessors judge complex data in a competency-based portfolio"**, *Advances in Health Sciences Education* 2018; 23(2):275-287. DOI 10.1007/s10459-017-9793-y. PMCID PMC5882626. **Open access (CC BY 4.0), full text read.** Narrative primacy, the three-phase acquire-organise-integrate process, anchoring on initial judgements, and the three sources of divergence beneath identical pass/fail outcomes.
- **Govaerts, Van de Wiel, Schuwirth, Van der Vleuten and Muijtjens, "Workplace-based assessment: raters' performance theories and constructs"**, *Advances in Health Sciences Education* 2013; 18(3):375-396. DOI 10.1007/s10459-012-9376-x. PMCID PMC3728456. **Open access (CC BY); abstract and introduction verified, full text not read end to end.** Immediate person-schema formation, seventeen inter-related performance dimensions, idiosyncrasy unrelated to expertise, and the second-hand 29% to over 50% variance range.

## How does judgement-based assessment actually run inside a semester?
- **CMU 17-645 / 17-445 / 17-745, Machine Learning in Production**, `https://mlip-cmu.github.io/` and `https://mlip-cmu.github.io/f2026/`. **Open, read directly; all course materials Creative Commons at github.com/mlip-cmu.** Oral verification as a rubric item and as in-lab demonstration, specification grading with no partial credit, the sixteen-token economy, opt-in participation, peer rating on team citizenship, and the permissive-except-in-conversation AI policy.
- **Kaestner et al., "Teaching Software Engineering for AI-Enabled Systems"**, arXiv:2001.06691. **Listing metadata verified.** The published design rationale behind the course above; read this before adapting its mechanics rather than merely copying them.
- **"Machine Learning in Production"**, MIT Press, **open access** at `https://mlip-cmu.github.io/book/`. The course's companion text; the chapter mapping in the schedule shows which assessment sits on which content.

## How much confidence does the evidence actually support?
- **Miller, "Adding Error Bars to Evals: A Statistical Approach to Language Model Evaluations"**. **Full text read.** Standard errors, clustered standard errors, variance reduction, paired comparison, and power analysis — the source of the clustering ratios and the ≈969-question calculation that together argue for coarse ordinal reporting.
- **Zheng et al., "Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena"**. **Abstract and listing metadata verified.** The four judge biases and the over-80% human-human agreement ceiling.
- **Shankar et al., EvalGen**. **Abstract and listing metadata verified.** Criteria drift and the requirement that validators be validated.
- **Angelopoulos et al., "Prediction-Powered Inference"**. **Abstract and listing metadata verified.** The principled combination of a small expensive labelled set with a large cheap predicted set.
- **Husain, "Your AI Product Needs Evals"**. **Full article read on the open web.** The three-level evidence ladder and the plateau symptoms.
- **Thakur et al., BEIR**. **Abstract and listing metadata verified.** Out-of-distribution generalisation as the real target, and the robust-simple-baseline discipline.
- **Singh et al., "The Leaderboard Illusion"**. **Abstract and listing metadata verified.** Access asymmetry inside a measurement system as a fairness problem distinct from the metric.

Full note in [research/07-accreditation-exemplars/abet-cac-criteria-and-student-outcomes.md](/research/07-accreditation-exemplars/abet-cac-criteria-and-student-outcomes.md).
- **CS2023, ACM/IEEE-CS/AAAI Computer Science Curricula**, `https://csed.acm.org/`. DOI 10.1145/3664191. **Site pages read; three sectioned PDFs could not be extracted.** The vision statement's argument that skills and professional dispositions matter as much as knowledge. Full note in [research/07-accreditation-exemplars/cs2023-competency-and-disposition-materials.md](/research/07-accreditation-exemplars/cs2023-competency-and-disposition-materials.md).

- **Berkeley LLM Agents MOOC**, `https://llmagents-learning.org/f24` — its five-tier certificate structure is an interesting alternative to graded assessment, though it grades on completion rather than judgement. ## What this folder should answer
- **Which activities should be entrusted.** Provisionally answered: a bounded ship/no-ship evaluation decision, a system handover, and a stakeholder risk communication, with the remaining candidates merged, demoted to observed practices, or folded into a dispositions layer. Not yet ratified — see [epa-research-notes.md](/research/08-assessment-epas/epa-research-notes.md).
- **What the supervision scale should be.** Answered: the expanded sub-level scale rather than the bare five points, targeting level 3b or 3c at course completion, with anchors written in supervising-engineer idiom.
- **What evidence each activity needs.** Answered in form: multiple observers, multiple observations, and coverage across direct observation, longitudinal observation, case-based discussion and product evaluation, with mandatory narrative substantiation and an entrustment-based discussion including what-if questions. Not yet answered in practice, because assessor capacity is unquantified.
- **Still unanswered:** whether the course can produce a substrate with genuine failure modes; how many independent assessor-occasion pairs the staffing supports; and how oral assessment is accommodated. The last of these is addressed by no source in this folder.
