# Reading List — Accreditation and Exemplars

Grouped by the question each source answers. Access status is recorded so that anyone reproducing this folder knows what can be read directly and what is behind a barrier.

## What faculty committees and accreditors will expect
- **ABET, Criteria for Accrediting Computing Programs, 2025-2026.** `https://www.abet.org/accreditation/accreditation-criteria/criteria-for-accrediting-computing-programs-2025-2026/`; PDF at `https://www.abet.org/2025-2026_cac_criteria/`. Open. Read Definitions, Criterion 3, Criterion 5, and the Computer Science Program Criteria. **Section V, Proposed Changes, is the most important part for this course** — professional dispositions and the comprehensive-project requirement, first applied in the 2026-27 review cycle. Note that the 2024-2025 URL returns 404.
- **ACM/IEEE-CS/AAAI, Computer Science Curricula 2023 (CS2023).** `https://csed.acm.org/`; DOI 10.1145/3664191. Open. Read the vision statement in full — it is short and it is where the disposition argument is made. The knowledge-areas index gives the coded area list. The final report contains a section on Generative AI and the Curriculum.

## What exemplar course structures look like
- **CMU 17-645 / 17-445 / 17-745, Machine Learning in Production.** `https://mlip-cmu.github.io/` and the Fall 2026 offering at `https://mlip-cmu.github.io/f2026/`. Open; all materials Creative Commons at `https://github.com/mlip-cmu`. The single most useful exemplar in this folder. Read the grading, token, participation, oral-verification and AI-policy sections closely.
- **Christian Kaestner and Eunsuk Kang, "Machine Learning in Production."** MIT Press, open access at `https://mlip-cmu.github.io/book/`; print at `https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262049726/`. The course's companion text; chapter references appear throughout the CMU schedule.
- **Stanford CS336, Language Modeling from Scratch, Spring 2025.** `http://cs336.stanford.edu/spring2025/`; latest offering at `http://cs336.stanford.edu/`. Open. Read the prerequisites and honour-code sections for the minimal-scaffolding and AI-autocomplete arguments.
- **Berkeley Large Language Model Agents MOOC, Fall 2024.** `https://llmagents-learning.org/f24`; Spring 2025 iteration at `https://llmagents-learning.org/sp25`. Open. Read the coursework and certificate section for the five-tier structure.

## How to justify oral defence and project-based assessment
- **Olle ten Cate, "A primer on entrustable professional activities."** *Korean Journal of Medical Education* 2018; 30(1):1-10. DOI 10.3946/kjme.2018.76. Open access, CC BY-NC; full text via Europe PMC, PMCID PMC5840559. The entrustment-based discussion and the multiple-observer evidence standard are the load-bearing passages.
- **Andrea Oudkerk Pool, Marjan Govaerts, Debbie Jaarsma, Erik Driessen, "From aggregation to interpretation: how assessors judge complex data in a competency-based portfolio."** *Advances in Health Sciences Education* 2018; 23(2):275-287. DOI 10.1007/s10459-017-9793-y. Open access, CC BY 4.0; PMCID PMC5882626. The source of the finding that assessors rely on narrative and use grades only for confirmation.
- **Marjan Govaerts, Margje Van de Wiel, Lambert Schuwirth, Cees Van der Vleuten, Arno Muijtjens, "Workplace-based assessment: raters' performance theories and constructs."** *Advances in Health Sciences Education* 2013; 18(3):375-396. DOI 10.1007/s10459-012-9376-x. Open access; PMCID PMC3728456. The source of the 29-50% idiosyncratic-variance range and the finding that idiosyncrasy is unrelated to expertise.

## Design rationale behind the exemplars
- **Christian Kaestner and Eunsuk Kang, "Teaching Software Engineering for AI-Enabled Systems."** arXiv:2001.06691. Open. The published rationale for the CMU course design; useful when justifying a similar structure to a committee.
- **Annotated bibliography on software engineering for AI-enabled systems.** `https://github.com/ckaestne/seaibib`. Open. A maintained entry point to the wider literature.

## Retrieved but unavailable
These were sought and could not be read. Nothing in this folder asserts their contents.
- **CS2023 sectioned PDFs** — `1.1-Executive-Summary.pdf`, `1.4-Introduction-to-Competency-Framework.pdf`, `2.2-Characteristics-of-Graduates.pdf` on csed.acm.org. All three failed content extraction. These would confirm the competency formula, the named dispositions, core-hours figures and the exact knowledge-area count.
- **Govaerts' most-cited works, all paywalled** — "Validity in work-based assessment: expanding our horizons" (*Medical Education* 2013); "Twelve Tips for programmatic assessment" (2015); "Seeing the 'black box' differently: assessor cognition from three research perspectives" (2014); "Broadening perspectives on clinical performance assessment" (2007); "The reliability of multisource feedback in competency-based assessment programs" (2015); "The use of programmatic assessment in the clinical workplace: a Maastricht case report" (2012).
- **ten Cate, "Nuts and bolts of entrustable professional activities."** *Journal of Graduate Medical Education* 2013; 5(1):157-158. DOI 10.4300/jgme-d-12-00380.1. The more frequently cited short piece, but not open access; metadata only.

## Adjacent reading, not core
- **ABET Program Criteria for Cybersecurity, Data Science, Information Systems and Information Technology.** In the same 2025-2026 document. Worth skimming for the Data Science lifecycle framing (acquisition, management, preparation and integration, analysis, model development and deployment, visualisation and communication), which is closer to this course's subject matter than the CS criteria are.
- **Later CS336 and CMU offerings.** Both courses are re-run; comparing successive offerings shows which mechanisms the instructors kept and which they dropped, which is more informative than any single snapshot.

## What this folder should answer
- What a computing accreditation body actually requires, in its own words, and what it is about to require.
- What the discipline's own curricular guidance says about dispositions and behaviour.
- What a rigorous, project-based, orally verified course looks like in practice, from courses that are running.
- How to defend judgement-based assessment against the reliability objection without overclaiming.
