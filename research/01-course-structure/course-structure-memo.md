# Course Structure Memo

Date: 2026-07-25

## Summary
The current package is strong as a Microsoft enablement track, but a degree-level course needs a durable six-strand structure:

1. Foundations
2. Measurement and evaluation
3. Architecture and operations
4. Adversary and safety
5. Professional formation
6. FDE / engagement craft

## Key finding
Evaluation science should be the spine, not a side module. Every assignment should ship an evaluation artifact before it ships a feature.

## What the course spine should probably be
- Weeks 1-3: foundations and systems basics
- Weeks 4-6: inference economics and evaluation workflow
- Weeks 7-8: retrieval, architecture, and orchestration
- Weeks 9-10: safety, security, and adversarial failure modes
- Weeks 11-12: FDE craft, handover, and oral defense

## Gaps to resolve
- Whether the six strands should stay separate or compress into fewer modules
- Whether the EPA set should stay at 12 or shrink
- What should be durable theory versus perishable platform detail
